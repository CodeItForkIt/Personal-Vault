# Outer Dominances (Era 1)

On the very fringes of the Empire, the Farmost Dominance, Crescent Dominance, and Remote Dominance are generally less hospitable than their Inner counterparts. To the east, the Crescent Dominance is parched by vast deserts, and to the west, the coastal dominances are besieged by storming seas. The Southern Islands are lush but promise danger and prosperity in  equal measure.

Far from the Inner Dominances, the Final Empire’s control wanes in the Outer Dominances. Though some nobles successfully exploit their estates and business, skaa rebellions are common. The burnlands at the edges of the dominances are home to nomadic communities and bandit groups composed of rebels and fugitive skaa.

The Lord Ruler’s koloss armies are stationed at the edges of the Outer Dominances, and they’re particularly active in the burnlands, where they come into conflict with those who resist his order.

#### Outer Dominances Names

Axshon, Colette, Cartoux, Egon, Farlene, Hoxs, Janier, Mioux, Oskar, Renli, Seren, Seaban, Thrice, Ulla, Yuem.

#### Outer Dominances Expertise

Choose one of the Outer Dominances; you can choose this expertise more than once to reflect knowledge of additional dominances. As a character with an Outer Dominances expertise, you know the names of the lesser nobles claiming power over your chosen dominance, and you know the industries they favor. You know about the reality of the koloss, including the size of the Lord Ruler’s armies, and you may have witnessed their ferocity firsthand—but you’re well aware the Final Empire’s control isn’t absolute and pockets of resistance exist on its borders.

Choose how proficient you are in writing Imperial. If you chose the Farmost Dominance, Remote Dominance, or Southern Islands, you know how to safely navigate the coastal seas; if you chose the Crescent Dominance, you know how to safely navigate the deserts.
