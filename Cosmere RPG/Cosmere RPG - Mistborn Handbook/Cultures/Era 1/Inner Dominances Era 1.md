# Inner Dominances (Era 1)

At the populous core of the Final Empire are the Central, Northern, Western, Eastern, and Southern Dominances. Mostly flat and with relatively “mild” weather, the Inner Dominances host some of the world’s largest cities, including Luthadel, Urteau, and Fadrex.

The cultural hegemony of the nobility over the skaa defines life in these regions. Though nobles vie against one another in Luthadel, their power is largely unchecked in their own ancestral lands. The noble families (or sometimes, the Cantons of the Steel Ministry) are responsible for the upkeep and exploitation of their estates. With noble and Ministry control so secure, skaa rebellions very rarely take hold.

The Inner Dominances are ideal for agriculture, and skaa are worked cruelly to keep the fields clear of ash. The dominances also specialize in their own trades; for example, the Northern Dominance, where House Venture holds sway, is known for its rich coal mines.

#### Inner Dominances Names

Anture, Dammel, Farene, Galea, Japes, Keliera, Lian, Mantare, Nomoron, Parteau, Quindees, Seloux, Taradare, Vendex.

#### Inner Dominances Expertise

Choose one of the five Inner Dominances; you can choose this expertise more than once to reflect knowledge of additional dominances. As a character with an Inner Dominances expertise, you know the names of the Great Houses and lesser nobles who hold power in that dominance, as well as the important industries in that dominance. You also know the names of the local Ashmounts and other notable landmarks such as the Pits of Hathsin or the Black Lake. As such, you can easily navigate the roads and canals in your chosen dominances. Choose how proficient you are in writing Imperial. You can also speak or understand dialects from your dominance, such as Eastern street slang.
