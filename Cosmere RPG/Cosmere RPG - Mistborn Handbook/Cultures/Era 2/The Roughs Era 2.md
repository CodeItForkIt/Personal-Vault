# The Roughs (Era 2)

Past the mountain ranges of the Elendel Basin, the Roughs are a swath of frontier land that’s desolate by comparison. Harsh weather, scant resources, and a sparse population have shaped the people into hardy homesteaders, conniving industrialists, bold prospectors, and desperate criminals. Mining companies hold most of the wealth, and they operate company towns, send raw materials into the Basin, and maintain bounty rolls to discourage banditry.

Some koloss clans trade with settlements. Other clans raid and fight with the companies, towns, and among themselves.

Most residents grew up in the Basin and moved to the Roughs, whether to seek better prospects or to run away from something. Some families in the Roughs have been around for generations. Ethnically, the people are similar to those of the Basin, but there is a smaller percentage of Terris and a much larger percentage of koloss-blooded people. Roughs trousers, button-up shirts, and widebrimmed hats are common for all genders. Some people wear long dusters, and many decorate those coats with colorful patterns.

#### Roughs Expertise

As a character with the Roughs expertise, you’re familiar with rail lines and mining businesses, and you know the names of major population centers. Even if you’re not trained in survival, you have the basic skills necessary to find water or recognize natural landmarks.

Choose whether you’ve spent time in the Northern Roughs, the Southern Roughs, or both. You know the names of the smaller settlements in your region, and you understand the relative power of local businesses. Choose the education you’ve received as well as how proficient you are in speaking and writing skaa. Regardless of your religious beliefs, you’re familiar with the basic tenets of Survivorism, Sliverism, and the Path.
