# Terris Enclaves (Era 2)

Several Terris enclaves exist across the Elendel Basin, though the largest and most influential is the Village in Elendel. Enclaves are overseen by their Synod, usually a council of elders, but each enclave has different methods of choosing leaders. Traditional Terris culture encourages submission, meekness, and respectful dignity. In addition, the Terris greatly value scholarship and education, focusing both on practical sciences and spiritual health.

Terris skin tones range from pale to dark. Terris are usually taller than non-Terris, and they have a variety of hair colors, tending more toward dark. Some people wear their hair in braids, while others shave their heads. Traditional Terris adorn themselves with many pieces of jewelry, and they wear the tinningdar—a robe with V-shaped designs down the chest. Even nontraditional Terris tend to incorporate V shapes in their clothing choices.

#### Terris Enclave Names

Arathwyn, Gindanwyl, Hexaldweth, Idashim, Kanderwyl, Kenlew, Kwathendwyl, Marshek, Nessenwy, Nilzan, Razendal, Sazzek, Sebaam, Tellingwym, Tessen, Vedlip, Vezlanaze, Vorch, Vwadenar.

#### Terris Enclaves Expertise

As a character with the Terris Enclaves expertise, you know the names of noteworthy Terris teachers, elders, and Synod members. You can navigate Terris customs and gain access to Terris libraries and records. Even if you’re not a Ferring, you’re familiar with Feruchemy and can recite the individual Feruchemical metals and the attributes each Ferring can store.

Choose a Terris enclave where you’ve spent time. You know its businesses, schools, and specialized areas of scholarship. Choose the education you’ve received as well as how proficient you are in speaking and writing Terris. Decide how proficient you are in Terris scholarship and meditative practices. Regardless of your religious beliefs, you’re familiar with Harmony and the ancient Terris religion.
