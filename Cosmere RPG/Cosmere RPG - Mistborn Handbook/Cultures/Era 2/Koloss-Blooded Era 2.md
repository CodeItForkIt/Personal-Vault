# Koloss-Blooded (Era 2)

Born with at least one koloss or koloss-blooded parent, koloss-blooded children grow up in the worlds of both peoples. Family, community, and individual authenticity are of the utmost importance in koloss-blooded culture. Koloss clans in the Roughs send their children to city communities to connect with family members, just as koloss-blooded families send their children to koloss clans to learn their heritage.

Most settlements in the Elendel Basin have at least a small koloss-blooded population, but larger cities such as Elendel, Bilming, and New Seran have several koloss-blooded neighborhoods. The Roughs have dozens of koloss clans as well as relatively large koloss-blooded populations—including some majority koloss-blooded towns.

#### Koloss-Blooded Names

Augurd, Bailen, Boly, Dav, Dylan, Erma, Hadeline, Karoline, Larson, Lizzie, Lunille, Martin, Moe, Mollie, Nax, Persephonine, Ray, Roscoe, Tillie, Willium.

#### Koloss Clan Names

Calder, Chennant, Douglan, Dramal, Hinnaird, Hudowen, Hukainly, Kobarr, Kogarroch, Kovlair.

#### Koloss-Blooded Expertise

As a character with the Koloss-Blooded expertise, you know the names of local koloss clans and major kolossblooded neighborhoods or towns. You can acquire services and navigate customs in both koloss clans and koloss-blooded communities.

Choose a koloss or koloss-blooded community where you’ve lived. You know that community’s territory, leaders, families, and history. Choose the education you’ve received as well as how proficient you are in speaking and writing skaa. Regardless of your religious beliefs, you’re familiar with the basic tenets of Survivorism, Sliverism, and the Path.
