# Malwish (Era 2)

The Malwish nation is home to one of five peoples who populate the Southern Continent of Scadrial. They’re a militant culture with advanced technology and a deep regard for propriety and Metalborn.

Three centuries ago, the Malwish people suffered the Ice Death. During Harmony’s work to restore Scadrial to the state it was in before the Lord Ruler, he cooled the southern climate, causing great hardship. After the Malwish civilization collapsed, the Sovereign arrived and brought them life-saving technology, which they’ve since expanded on. The Malwish worship the Jaggenmire, continuing to practice their religion from before the Sovereign’s arrival. Currently, the Malwish are working diligently to unite the Southern nations under one banner—their own.

The Malwish have a variety of skin tones and hair colors. Each Malwish wears a painted and decorated wooden mask, usually red. People change their masks as they follow fashion trends or interests, and they might change their mask’s style or ornamentations when they change professions or mark achievements.

The Malwish use hand gestures to convey emotions and moods, and younger Malwish may raise their mask to emphasize a certain emotion or statement.

#### Malwish Names

Andrid, Astrine, Brigine, Dagmar, Dagnus, Eihannes, Ellis, Folse, Hendrik, Hilda, Indraal, Jarika, Julavil, Kaanus, Kadri, Osval, Preida, Sveikki, Ulvis, Vilmmes.

#### Malwish Expertise

As a character with the Malwish expertise, you know the landscape of Southern Scadrial and the mask customs of your people, and you may have at least one mask of your own. You’re also familiar with medallion and airship technology. Choose how proficient you are in speaking and writing Malwish. Regardless of your religious beliefs, you’re familiar with the basic tenets of the Jaggenmire.
