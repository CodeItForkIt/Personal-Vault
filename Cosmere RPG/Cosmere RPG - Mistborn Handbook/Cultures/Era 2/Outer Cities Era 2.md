# Outer Cities (Era 2)

The outer cities encompass large population centers such as Bilming and New Seran as well as small settlements such as Doriel, Drypost, Dryport, and Garmet. Most outer cities elect their own leaders, and for generations they’ve followed Elendel’s lead in the areas of policy, economics, and law enforcement. In the past few years, however, independence movements have gained popularity.

The fashion and disposition of small communities tends toward practical choices. Bilming is a center for industrial advancement, and Bilminger fashion features brightly colored and eccentric clothing choices. New Seran fashion features staid and proper suits and dresses, especially railroad-stripe suits and pastel dresses.

#### Outer Cities Expertise

As a character with the Outer Cities expertise, you’re familiar with the industries of the Basin, particularly the logistics of shipping and transport. Choose a city or region in the Elendel Basin that your character has lived in; you can choose this expertise more than once to reflect knowledge of additional regions. You’re familiar with the imports and exports of this area, and you know how to find businesses and noteworthy landmarks there. Choose the education you’ve received as well as how proficient you are in speaking and writing skaa and High Imperial. Regardless of your religious beliefs, you’re familiar with the basic tenets of Survivorism, Sliverism, and the Path.
