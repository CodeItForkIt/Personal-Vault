# High Society

In the Final Empire, nobles conduct business and politics during lavish balls, at dining tables, and in smoking rooms. After the Catacendre, inequalities remain rife, though they’re not so strictly enforced; even in Harmony’s world, many nobles cling to leadership, and a new class of industrialists and bankers has emerged into high society.

You needn’t be nobility to choose this expertise. You might simply know how to move in the world of the affluent, or you may have meticulously studied your enemy.

#### High Society Expertise

As a character with the High Society expertise, you’re well-versed in the etiquette, protocol, and fashions of the rich and noble. You know the names of local nobility and how their influence extends over business and politics. You can almost always score an invitation to a ball or similar event.
