# Underworld

The exploitation of the skaa has driven the emergence of a thriving underworld. Rebels and revolutionaries make their plans from the shadows, while crews of thieves and scammers try to earn their rightful share. Strife and inequality are common even after the Catacendre, when the machinations of the rich and powerful force the poor (or lure the opportunistic) into the criminal lifestyle.

#### Underworld Expertise

As a character with the Underworld expertise, you know or can quickly discover where local authority lies—whether it’s considered legitimate or not. You can almost always get in touch with thieves, gangs, and crews, even if it’s through a contact of a contact. You can understand underworld codes, such as smugglers’ signs and thieves’ cants. You can easily find a fence or a buyer for stolen goods, though the risk is commensurate with the reward.
