# Wayfarer

There have always been Scadrians who live a life of travel, driven by occupation, exile, or tourism. You could be a nomad from the burnlands traveling inward to spur rebellion, a gentlefolk scholar looking for wonders far from home, a drifter from the Roughs searching for a new home, or even a Malwish explorer keen to see Elendel. Whatever your reason, your life of travel has made you familiar with navigation and transport in your era, and with the dangers of the road.

#### Wayfarer Expertise

As a character with the Wayfarer expertise, you know international geography and can read national and international maps. You’re adept at navigating cultural exchanges between people from distant lands. You’ve learned slang and similar cultural touchstones from across Scadrial, which help you earn trust and aid. You can identify wildlife and livestock from across the world.
