# Kandra Homeland

During the reign of the Lord Ruler, the kandra Homeland is an underground haven with sprawling tunnels, large caverns, and cozy chambers. Its mysterious people are ruled by the ancient First Generation, though the Second Generation handles most day-to-day administration.

The kandra follow a set of laws called the First Contract, which emphasize dedication to the Lord Ruler, reverence for earlier generations of kandra, the legal rights of all kandra, and more. The terms of kandra Contracts in the Final Empire also prohibit killing humans and require the kandra to return to the Homeland if their contract holder dies or if the kandra breaks their Contract.

After the Catacendre, the kandra live among humans in the Elendel Basin, and the kandra Homeland becomes a place to visit rather than live.

#### Kandra Homeland Expertise

The Kandra Homeland expertise is generally only available to kandra characters. If your character has another ancestry but has lived or worked closely with the kandra, your GM might give you permission to choose this expertise.

As a character with the Kandra Homeland expertise, you know the names of the First Generation as well as the areas of expertise and responsibilities of the Second Generation. You understand the terms and requirements of the First Contract and other secrets of the kandra history, including the information in the “Kandra Ancestry” section earlier in this chapter.

If playing in Era 1, choose how experienced you are with activities outside the Homeland. If playing in Era 2, you’re familiar with kandra activities, businesses, and leisure spots, and you’re familiar with modern kandra customs and rules. Choose the education you’ve received as well as how proficient you are in speaking and writing Imperial/skaa and Terris. Regardless of your religious beliefs, you’re familiar with the Terris gods, Preservation and Ruin; if you’re playing after the Catacendre, you’re also familiar with Harmony.
