| Sveikki tests **Light Weaponry** to perform a trick with a knife to impress bar patrons. | DC 14 | Her skill clearly impresses the audience. | She fumbles the knife, dropping it midway through her performance. | Sveikki uses the distraction to draw closer to the spy she’s been shadowing. | Sveikki nicks herself in the process, taking 1d4 keen damage. |
| Doug tries to recall the penalty for impersonating a constable by testing **Lore**. | DC 20 | She knows this would incur a steep fine and likely imprisonment. | She doesn’t remember and would need to look it up at an official building. | She happens on a local festival with free food and entertainment. | The official buildings are closed for an official event, and most constables are off duty. |

| Nilzan listens closely to check if anyone’s following them through the alley by testing **Perception**. | DC 15 | Behind them, Nilzan hears two sets of footsteps—one light, one heavy—which pause when Nilzan slows their stride. | They don’t hear any unusual noises nearby. | They find a guard post up ahead. | A hurried passerby rounds the corner and collides with Nilzan. Nilzan loses 2 focus and falls Prone. |
| On the eve of an important job, Larker tests **Persuasion** to ask to borrow an untested, one-of-a-kind prototype invention. | DC 22 | The inventor allows her to borrow the prototype and provides strict instructions. | She isn’t permitted to requisition the experimental invention. | She’s offered a lesser, but better-tested, device. | The inventor expects this grand favor to be returned in the future. |
