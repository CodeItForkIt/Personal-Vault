  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Cadmium Allomancy]]

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Chromium Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Copper (power, talent tree):** Copper is burned to hide the use of Allomancy in an area around the Allomancer. Burning copper also grants immunity to the emotional Allomancy of zinc and brass.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Copper Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Duralumin (power, talent tree):** Duralumin is burned to expend the Allomancer’s own Allomantic reserves, manifesting their power in an explosive burst.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Duralumin Allomancy]]

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Electrum Allomancy]]

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Gold Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Iron (power, talent tree):** Iron is burned to Pull nearby metals toward the Allomancer.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Iron Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Nicrosil (power, talent tree):** Nicrosil is burned to expend the Allomantic reserves of someone the Allomancer touches, manifesting the target’s power in an explosive burst.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Nicrosil Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Pewter (power, talent tree):** Pewter is burned to enhance the Allomancer’s strength, agility, and endurance.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Pewter Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Steel (power, talent tree):** Steel is burned to Push nearby metals away from the Allomancer.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Steel Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Tin (power, talent tree):** Tin is burned to enhance the Allomancer’s senses.

  [[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Tin Allomancy]]

- **Allomancy, Zinc (power, talent tree):** Zinc is burned to enflame emotions in others.
