# Another Secret

By reading this book, you’ve taken the first step of your journey into Scadrial. Some readers might be content simply to let the story continue in their imagination, bolstered by a deeper understanding of this beloved fantasy world. Others might be inspired to build characters and dream up complex backstories regardless of whether they join a game.

For those of you who wish to take the next step into playing the Cosmere RPG, a world of adventure awaits. You can continue your journey by finding other players to join in the fun!

## Gather Your Party

The Cosmere RPG was designed to be approachable for a wide range of players, including those unfamiliar with the Mistborn novels. Most groups will include players who are experiencing Scadrial for the first time. When recruiting such players, you can pitch it as “a dark fantasy world with a unique magic system.” You can also engage players by explaining that this RPG is part of the broader cosmere, a universe where each world has its own history, culture, and magic system.

## Existing Roleplayers

For fans of other tabletop RPGs, the Cosmere RPG offers a balance of traditional rules and narrative innovations. The core mechanic of d20-based skill tests is familiar to most players, but other features (flexible paths, the plot die, and the order of combat) may feel fresh. Whether a player prefers tactical dungeon delving or character-driven improvisation, the Cosmere RPG can support different preferences and playstyles.

## New Roleplayers

This RPG was also designed for Mistborn fans who aren’t familiar with tabletop roleplaying. If this is your first tabletop RPG, consider beginning with the adventure in the *Mistborn Starter Set*, which introduces different gameplay concepts in each scene. By the time you finish that adventure, your group should have a good grasp of the system. After a couple more sessions, you should be familiar enough with the rules to understand higher-level mechanics like Allomancy and Feruchemy.

## Gather Your Crew

Once you’ve found your players, it’s time to plan your first game. Beforehand, take time for a “session zero” to make sure everyone is on the same page about the game’s content, tone, and safety tools, as discussed in [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/14 - Ch 13 Gamemastering|chapter 13]]. (Though a live session is optimal, this type of discussion can also take place via email or messaging.) If players are newer to RPGs, a session zero can also be a good time to help players build their characters. Allow at least an hour for a session zero.

To prepare for the session, make sure you’re familiar with the rules. Visit [CosmereRPG.com](https://www.cosmererpg.com/) to print out quick reference sheets for players. If you’re playing with battle maps, be ready with miniatures or tokens and printed maps or dry-erase mats. Prepare a comfortable place to play and gather together.

Once everyone is ready to play, it’s time for your first full gameplay session! You can meet in person, via video, or by using VTT (virtual tabletop) software. For your first session, it’s a good idea to set aside a couple of hours (or more) and to keep an eye on the time. The GM’s role is to keep the action moving forward and make sure every player has a chance to contribute. Don’t expect your first session to be perfect, but as long as everyone stays engaged, it should be a memorable experience!

## Further Reading and Resources

Aside from this handbook, you can find many resources to enrich your RPG experience. The Mistborn novels aren’t required reading, but they can add depth and excitement to your understanding of the world.

The *Mistborn World Guide* is a great way for GMs and players to immerse themselves in Scadrial. Though the adversary profiles at the back of that book offer gameplay spoilers that players should generally avoid, anyone involved in a game can benefit from the other setting information and lore. For players who intend to read the novels and haven’t done so, just keep in mind that the World Guide contains many spoilers for both eras. The *Welcome to Scadrial* booklet is a shorter alternative that describes both eras while avoiding spoilers about the story.

When it comes to looking up specific lore on the fly, we know of no better resource than the [Coppermind.net](https://www.coppermind.net) wiki. Maintained by the official Brandon Sanderson fan site, [17thShard.com](https://www.17thshard.com), the Coppermind is a great way to look up specific details about any location, individual, or item in Scadrial and beyond. Members of the 17th Shard provided crucial advice and lore expertise during the creation of this game, and the Coppermind is an invaluable resource for anyone who wants to learn more about the works of Brandon Sanderson.

Finally, this is a living RPG that’ll continue to grow with new resources. Visit [CosmereRPG.com](https://www.cosmererpg.com) to find preview content, digital supplements, and our online character creation tool.

## Beyond Mistborn

*There’s always another secret.*

The mist-shrouded world of Scadrial could provide enough material to support years of adventure across multiple campaigns. However, Scadrial is just one world in an entire universe of magic, conflict, and characterdriven storytelling.

The *Mistborn Handbook* is part of the larger Cosmere RPG. This system already includes the war-torn world of Roshar, and it’s expanding to include adventure across other worlds with their own unique magic systems. Everything within the Cosmere RPG is written for cross-compatibility. A party of worldhoppers could include a Radiant, an artifabrian, a Twinborn, and a sand master… and their adventures could take them across the cosmere.

Scadrial, a world undergoing massive change, is developing toward exploring beyond their planet and stumbling into a universe-wide conflict. Who are the heroes when the champions of different worlds collide? Who will emerge victorious? That story becomes your story in the Cosmere RPG, and there’s always another secret, so visit [CosmereRPG.com](https://www.cosmererpg.com/) to continue your journey!
