# Pounce

The Actor Name jumps to an unoccupied space up to their movement rate away. If they land within 5 feet of an enemy, the Actor Name makes a Claw attack against them. On a hit, if the target is Medium or smaller, the Actor Name knocks them [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]].
