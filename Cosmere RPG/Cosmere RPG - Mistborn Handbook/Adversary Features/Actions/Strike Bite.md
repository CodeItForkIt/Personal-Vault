# Strike: Bite

**Attack** +4; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 4 Keen average`, if the target is Large or smaller, the Actor Name can spend 1 focus to knock the target Prone, then move up to 10 feet while dragging the target behind them.
