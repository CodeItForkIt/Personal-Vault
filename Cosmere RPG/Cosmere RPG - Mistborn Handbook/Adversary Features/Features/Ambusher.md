# Ambusher

At the start of each scene, if the Actor Name isn’t [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Surprised|Surprised]], they can make a *Test: `skill=stl`* against the highest Spiritual defense among their enemies. On a success, the Actor Name takes a fast turn before any other characters (this doesn’t count as their turn this round).
