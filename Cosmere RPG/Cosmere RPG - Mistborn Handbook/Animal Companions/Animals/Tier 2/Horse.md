# Horse

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** large  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 4, Intellect 0, Willpower 1, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 25/25, Focus 3/3, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 3, ATH 3, PRC 2

### Strike: Hooves
*1 act*

**Attack** +6; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + 6 Keen average`

### Charge
*2 act*

The Horse moves up to their movement rate in a straight line without triggering Reactive Strikes. During this movement, the Horse can pass through the spaces of Medium and smaller enemies, trampling them underfoot. Each Medium or smaller enemy whose space the Horse enters must succeed on a *Test: `dc=15 skill=ath`* or be knocked [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]] and take `2d6 Impact`.

### Kick
*2 act*

**Attack** +6; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + 6 Impact average`, if the target is Medium or smaller, they're pushed back 10 feet.

### Beast of Burden
The Horse’s carrying capacity is 500 lb.
