# Snake

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** small  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 3, Intellect 0, Willpower 0, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 11/11, Focus 2/2, Investiture 0  
**Deflect:** 2  
**Skills:** AGI 1, PRC 2, SUR 1

### Sleek
The Snake counts as one size smaller when squeezing through smaller spaces.

### Scales
Increase the Snake’s deflect value by +2.

### Strike: Bite
*1 act*

**Attack** +4; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 4 Keen average`, if the target is Large or smaller, the Snake can spend 1 focus to knock the target Prone, then move up to 10 feet while dragging the target behind them.

### Nimble
The Snake can move through enemy spaces and can end their turn in the same space as a willing character. While occupying the same space as another character, the Snake can’t be grazed and attacks against the Snake gain a disadvantage.

### Enhanced Senses
The Snake gains an advantage on non-attack tests that rely on sight.

### Minion
The Snake’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.
