# Cat

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** small  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 3, Intellect 0, Willpower 0, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 11/11, Focus 2/2, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 1, STL 2, PRC 2, PRS 2, SUR 1

### Enhanced Senses
The Cat gains an advantage on non-attack tests that rely on sight.

### Furtive Climber
Climbing and sneaking don’t cause the Cat to become Slowed.

### Strike: Bite
*1 act*

**Attack** +4; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 4 Keen average`.

### Minion
The Cat’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Nimble
The Cat can move through enemy spaces and can end their turn in the same space as a willing character. While occupying the same space as another character, the Cat can’t be grazed and attacks against the Cat gain a disadvantage.
