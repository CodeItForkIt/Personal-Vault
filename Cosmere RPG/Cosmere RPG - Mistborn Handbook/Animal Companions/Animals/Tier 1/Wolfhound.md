# Wolfhound

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 3, Intellect 0, Willpower 1, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 15/15, Focus 3/3, Investiture 0  
**Senses:** 40 ft.  
**Skills:** AGI 1, ATH 2, INM 2, PRC 2, SUR 2

### Strike: Bite
*1 act*

**Attack** +4; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 4 Keen average`, if the target is Large or smaller, the Wolfhound can spend 1 focus to knock the target Prone, then move up to 10 feet while dragging the target behind them.

### Pack Instincts
While within 5 feet of an ally, the Wolfhound can use the Gain Advantage action as 0.

### On the Hunt
After an enemy within 30 feet of the Wolfhound falls [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]], the Wolfhound moves up to half their movement rate toward them.

### Enhanced Senses
The Wolfhound gains an advantage on non-attack tests that rely on sight.
