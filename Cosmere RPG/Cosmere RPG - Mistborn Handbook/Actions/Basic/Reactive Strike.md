# Reactive Strike

As an enemy retreats, you use the opening to attack. When an enemy voluntarily leaves your reach, you can use this reaction and spend 1 focus to make a melee weapon attack or unarmed strike against the enemy’s Physical defense. You can’t use this reaction to attack a character who moves instantaneously (such as a Radiant using the surge of **Transportation** from the Stormlight Handbook).
