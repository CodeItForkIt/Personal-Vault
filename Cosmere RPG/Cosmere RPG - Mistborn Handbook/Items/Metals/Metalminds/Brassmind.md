# Brassmind

*Stores warmth*

Each metalmind can hold a maximum number of charges equal to 2 + your ranks in Feruchemy plus any additional modifiers granted by talents.

**Max Charges**: 2 + FER + Other Modifiers

*\*Max charges must be configured manually at this time.*
