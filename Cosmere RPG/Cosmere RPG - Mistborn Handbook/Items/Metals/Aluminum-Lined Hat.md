# Aluminum-Lined Hat

Popular among the wealthy and fashionable of Era 2, an aluminum-lined hat has aluminum bound within it to block Allomantic influence. While wearing this hat, you can’t be affected by Invested Arts that influence your thoughts or emotions. (This hat is a tier 1 reward.)
