# 2-Boxing Coin

Imperial coins, colloquially called boxings, are larger—about twice the size of clips—and typically made of more precious metals like gold. A single boxing is, of course, worth 1 bx. Before the Catacendre, there were 2- and 5-boxing coins, and more denominations were introduced in Era 2.
