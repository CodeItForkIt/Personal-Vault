# Anesthetic (5 doses)

**Price** 30 bx; **Weight** 1.5 lb.;

Usually made with ether, you can apply a dose of anesthetic ointment to an injury after a short or long rest to reduce its remaining recovery time by `1d4` days. Each injury can be treated with anesthetic only once per day.
