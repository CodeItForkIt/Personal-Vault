# Poison (potent, 1 dose)

**Price** 60 bx; **Weight** 0.2 lb.;

Often manufactured using gurwraith or the bark of climbing vines like birchbane, poison comes in single-dose glass vials. As 1, you can pour it into food, liquid, or a container, or you can apply the poison to a melee weapon or a piece of ammunition you’re holding. When you do, the GM may require you to succeed on a *Test: `skill=stl`* to avoid discovery. When a character ingests the poison or is hit by an attack using the poisoned weapon or ammunition, the poison is expended, and the character suffers the following effects:

**Potent Poison.** The target must succeed on a *Test: `dc=16 skill=ath`* test or take `3d10 Vital` and be [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]] for 1 hour. While [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]] in this way, the target is [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Immobilized|Immobilized]].
