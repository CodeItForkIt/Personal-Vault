# Net (hunting)

**Price** 2 bx; **Weight** 5 lb.;

This net of thick rope can ensnare animals or enemies. While holding this net, you can use 1 to make an *Test: `skill=ath`* against the Physical defense of a Large or smaller character within 15 feet of you. On a success, the character is [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Restrained|Restrained]] and knocked Prone. That character or another who can reach them can use the Interact action to make a *Test: `dc=15 skill=ath`*, freeing the trapped character on a success. If a character is [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Restrained|Restrained]] by multiple nets, they must be freed from each separately.
