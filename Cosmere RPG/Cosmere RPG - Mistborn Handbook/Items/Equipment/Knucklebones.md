# Knucklebones

**Price** 5–10 bx; **Weight** 0.5 lb.;

Street toughs and enforcers in Scadrial’s big cities love using knucklebones. Brass knuckles are common, but when fighting Allomancers, the more expensive wooden variety is worth every coin.

You can wear knucklebones on your hand instead of wielding a weapon in that hand. When you make an unarmed attack using that hand, increase the size of each unarmed damage die by one size (see the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/17 - Appendix 2 Tables|Unarmed Damage]] table earlier in this chapter). For example, damage of 1 (no die roll) becomes 1d4, 1d8 becomes 1d10, and so on.
