# Chain (thin, 1 foot)

**Price** 10 bx; **Weight** 0.5 lb.;

This thin chain is often used in jewelry, usually made of precious metals in fine links. This chain can be broken by succeeding on a *Test: `dc=20 skill=ath`* or by dealing 5 damage to it.
