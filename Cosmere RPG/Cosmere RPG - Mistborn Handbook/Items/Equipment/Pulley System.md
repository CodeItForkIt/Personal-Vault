# Pulley System

**Price** 50 bx; **Weight** 12 lb.;

Most commonly used by engineers and construction workers, this system contains four pulley wheels, 30 feet of rope, and a hook. When you have time to set up a pulley apparatus while attempting to lift an object, you treat the object as being a quarter of its normal weight.
