# Oil (1 flask)

**Price** 0.5 bx; **Weight** 1 lb.;

Oil generally comes in a 1-pint ceramic flask. Oil is flammable and can be used to fuel oil lanterns, to create dangerous terrain in a space, or as an improvised weapon (sling). When you hit or graze a target with this improvised weapon, you coat the target in oil in addition to the normal damage. After the attack, regardless of the result, the ceramic flask breaks and any unused oil is lost.
