# Musical Instrument

**Price** 5–100 bx; **Weight** 0.5–20 lb.;

Instruments are often used for enjoyment, passing time, or busking for clips. When playing an instrument, you can determine the quality of your performance by making a test using a related skill (such as *Test: `skill=agi`* for a stringed instrument, *Test: `skill=ath`* for drums, or *Test: `skill=dis`* for an instrument with many keys). If you have an expertise in that instrument, you gain an advantage on the test.
