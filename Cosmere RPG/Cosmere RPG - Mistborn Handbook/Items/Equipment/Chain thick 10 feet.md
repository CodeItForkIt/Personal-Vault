# Chain (thick, 10 feet)

**Price** 10 bx; **Weight** 10 lb.;

This sturdy chain can be used in securing cargo, shackling prisoners, and other situations where strength is essential. This chain can be broken by succeeding on a *Test: `dc=30 skill=ath`* or by dealing 15 damage to it.
