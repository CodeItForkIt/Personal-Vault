# Rope (50 feet)

**Price** 15 bx; **Weight** 5 lb.;

Generally made of hemp fibers, rope is generally sold in 50-foot lengths that can be cut and tied to change the rope’s length as needed. It can be cut by dealing 2 damage directly to it, or it can be ripped apart with a *Test: `dc=20 skill=ath`*.
