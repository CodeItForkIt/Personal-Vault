# Tonic (1 dose)

**Price** 2 bx; **Weight** 0.2 lb.;

A pungent tincture formulated of secret ingredients. In Era 1, many nobles enjoy a daily spoonful of Pewterheart Tonic, and in Era 2, countless happy customers swear by the efficacy of Sparkle Tonic. (Meanwhile, others swear it’s just the placebo effect.) Either way, a character can consume a dose after a short rest to recover and 1 focus.
