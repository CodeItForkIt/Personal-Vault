# Freechair (Era 2)

**Price** Free or 500 bx; **Weight** 12 lb.;

Adapted from airship technology, a freechair is a mobility device that levitates and moves using an iron medallion and a Steelpushing primer cube. The operator of a freechair has the same movement rate as other characters (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/04 - Ch 3 Character Statistics#Speed|Speed]]” in chapter 3), and the freechair doesn’t require an action to activate.

Though freechairs are available for purchase, disabled characters can obtain one for free. In Era 1, freechair technology is unknown, but characters can use a wheelchair with the same movement rate.
