# Manacles

**Price** 5 bx; **Weight** 6 lb.;

These metal restraints, which come with a key, can bind the wrists or ankles of a Large or smaller character. They can be escaped with a successful *Test: `dc=25 skill=agi`*, broken by succeeding on a *Test: `dc=25 skill=ath`* or by dealing 15 damage to them, or lockpicked by succeeding on a *Test: `dc=20 skill=thv`*. At the GM’s discretion, manacles that require higher DCs may be available at higher prices.
