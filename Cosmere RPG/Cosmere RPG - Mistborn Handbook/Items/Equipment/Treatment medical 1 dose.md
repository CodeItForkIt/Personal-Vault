# Treatment (medical, 1 dose)

**Price** 5 bx; **Weight** 0.2 lb.;

Scadrian doctors treat ailments with a variety of remedies, generally made from botanicals such as garlic, parsley, willow, and cypress. During a short rest, you can use a dose of such a treatment to cure a character of one of the following conditions, given that its source isn’t a permanent injury: [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Disoriented|Disoriented]], [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] (reduces the penalty by 1), or [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]].
