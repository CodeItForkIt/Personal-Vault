# Grappling Hook

**Price** 5 bx; **Weight** 4 lb.;

A grappling hook is typically attached to the end of a rope to aid in scaling or descending from walls and cliffs. A securely anchored grappling hook grants an advantage on Agility and Athletics tests made to climb using its attached rope.

You can use an Interact action to attach the grappling hook to an anchor within your reach without making a test. Alternatively, you can throw and attach it to a suitable anchor within 20 feet of you by succeeding on an *Test: `skill=agi`* or *Test: `skill=ath`* (DC set by the GM).
