# Mistcoat (Era 2)

**Deflect Value** 1; **Traits** Presentable; **Expert Traits** Unique;

**Price** Reward only; **Weight** 10 lb.;

An homage to the mistcloaks of the Final Empire, the mistcoats of Era 2 are ankle-length coats that divide into tassel-like strips just above the waist. Like mistcloaks, each mistcoat is specially commissioned for its wearer. They’re designed to be simultaneously protective and fashionable, and a well-worn mistcoat can even mimic some of the effects of the legendary mistcloaks.

**Unique Traits.** If you have the Mistcoat specialist expertise, add the following special expert trait:

**Flowing Tassels.** While wearing this armor in an area that obscures vision, such as in darkness or mist, you gain an advantage on Stealth tests you make against characters whose primary sense is obscured by that area. Additionally, those characters gain a disadvantage on Perception tests against you.
