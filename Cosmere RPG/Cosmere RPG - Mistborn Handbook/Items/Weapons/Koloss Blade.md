# Koloss Blade

**Damage** 1d12 impact; **Range** Melee [+5]; **Traits** Cumbersome [6], Dangerous, Deadly, Two-Handed, Unique; **Expert Traits** Unique: loses Dangerous trait, damage becomes 1d20 impact;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy; **Price** Reward only; **Weight** 200 lb;

Koloss blades are massive and outrageously heavy chunks of metal—usually iron—roughly smelted in the shape of a sword. They’re nearly as long as the koloss who wield them are tall—usually 7 to 8 feet—and about as wide as most humans are broad. After generations of being wielded with brute strength to literally crush opponents, these blades are typically blunted, chipped, and dented, often with jagged edges.

**Unique Trait.** Koloss blades have the following unique trait:

**Unwieldy.** Due to its awkward construction, attacks with the koloss blade can’t graze.
