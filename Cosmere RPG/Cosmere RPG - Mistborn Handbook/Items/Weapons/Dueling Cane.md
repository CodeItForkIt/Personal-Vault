# Dueling Cane

**Damage** 1d8 impact; **Range** Melee; **Traits** Discreet, Unique; **Expert Traits** Offhand;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy or Light; **Price** 40 bx; **Weight** 3 lb;

A favorite of hazekillers, dueling canes are wooden canes crafted to withstand combat despite appearing as a standard walking cane. Their weight and size are precisely designed to accommodate a wide variety of fighting styles.

**Unique Trait.** Dueling canes have the following unique trait:

**Flexible Fighting.** You can use either the Heavy Weaponry or Light Weaponry skill with a dueling cane.
