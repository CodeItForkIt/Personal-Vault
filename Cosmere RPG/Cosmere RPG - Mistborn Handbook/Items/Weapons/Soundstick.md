# Soundstick

**Damage** 1d4 impact; **Range** Melee; **Traits** Fragile, Unique; **Expert Traits** Offhand;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Light; **Price** 100 bx; **Weight** 0.5 lb;

Soundsticks are wooden sticks designed to produce a loud and jarring noise when struck; they’re especially effective when banged against another soundstick. Often utilized by hazekillers, these weapons are particularly effective at overloading the tin-enhanced senses of Tineyes and Windwhisperers.

**Unique Traits.** Soundsticks have the following unique trait:

**Ear-Splitting.** While you wield this weapon, you can use 1 to make a *Test: `skill=lwp`*, comparing the result to the Spiritual defense of each character (other than yourself) whose senses range includes your space. In addition to potentially overstimulating characters with supernatural hearing, each character you succeed against takes `1d6 Vital` and becomes [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Disoriented|Disoriented]] until the end of their next turn. If you make this test while wielding two soundsticks, you gain an advantage against characters with supernatural hearing, and on a success against such a character, they also become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]] until the end of their next turn.
