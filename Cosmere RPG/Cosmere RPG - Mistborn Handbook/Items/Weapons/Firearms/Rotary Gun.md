# Rotary Gun

**Damage** 3d4 keen; **Range** Ranged [20/60]; **Traits** Cumbersome [6], Dangerous, Loaded [20], Pierce, Two-Handed, Unique; **Expert Traits** Unique: loses Dangerous trait;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy; **Price** 2,500 bx; **Weight** 170 lb;

A rotary gun has an array of barrels set on a rotating circular base, typically connected to an ammunition magazine or belt. By manually cranking a lever, rounds are fed into the barrels and fired at an incredible rate.

Especially in urban population centers, law enforcement officers (such as the constabulary) keep a close watch for hazardous items like this. Unless you have a patron (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/09 - Ch 8 Goals and Rewards|Reward Categories]]” in chapter 8) who authorizes you to acquire, transport, or wield a rotary gun, it’s incredibly difficult to purchase—and if caught with it, you’re liable to be arrested and prosecuted.

**Unique Traits.** Rotary guns have the following unique traits:

**Fire Velocity.** You aren’t limited in the number of Strike actions you can make with a rotary gun on your turn. However, each time you attack using a rotary gun, the test gains a number of disadvantages equal to the number of previous attacks you made with it on that turn. For example, when you make your third rotary gun attack in a single turn, your attack test gains two disadvantages.

**Unwieldy Weight.** Due to its sheer weight and recoil, it’s incredibly difficult to use a rotary gun without anchoring it on a stable surface. While the rotary gun isn’t anchored, it has the Cumbersome [6] trait. You or another character who can reach it can use the Interact action to anchor the rotary gun on a horizontal stable surface; each time you do this, the value of the Cumbersome trait decreases by 2. For example, if you Interact in this way twice, the rotary gun becomes Cumbersome [2]. If an anchored rotary gun is moved from its surface for any reason, it is unanchored and again becomes Cumbersome [6].
