# Runaway Kit

**Weapons, Armor, and Currency:** None

**Equipment:** Ragged clothing

**Snap:** You Snapped long before the average person, discovering your latent Allomantic abilities, and you’ve already been experimenting with your powers. When you acquire the key talent for a Metalborn path that grants Allomancy (such as Mistborn, Misting, or Twinborn), whether during character creation or at a later level, you immediately complete that talent’s initial “Train Your Power(s)” goal.
