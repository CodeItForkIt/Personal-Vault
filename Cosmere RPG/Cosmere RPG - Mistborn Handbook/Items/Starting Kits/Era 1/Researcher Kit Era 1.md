# Researcher Kit (Era 1)

**Weapons:** One knife (Era 1)

**Armor:** Uniform

**Equipment:** Backpack with common clothing, an ink pen, a bottle of ink, 20 sheets of paper, an oil lantern, a flask of oil, a reference book on a topic of your choice (approved by the GM), and a lock and key

**Currency:** A pouch containing `3d12` boxings (Era 1)

**Additional Expertise:** You gain the Literature utility expertise. If you already have this expertise, you instead gain one cultural or utility expertise of your choice. This expertise doesn’t count against your expertises chosen at character creation.
