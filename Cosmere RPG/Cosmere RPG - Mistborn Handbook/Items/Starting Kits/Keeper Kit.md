# Keeper Kit

**Weapons, Armor, and Currency:** None

**Equipment:** Fine clothing (robes)

**Metalmind:** Whether you’re a Keeper yourself or someone carrying on their traditions hundreds of years in the future, you learned about your Feruchemical abilities early, and have already acquired your first metalmind. When you acquire the key talent for a Metalborn path that grants Feruchemy (such as Ferring, Feruchemist, or Twinborn), whether during character creation or at a later level, immediately complete that talent’s initial “Construct Your Metalmind(s)” goal.
