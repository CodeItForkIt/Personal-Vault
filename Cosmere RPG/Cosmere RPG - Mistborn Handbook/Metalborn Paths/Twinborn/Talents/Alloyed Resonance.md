# Alloyed Resonance

**Activation:** **\***

*As your two Invested Arts interact, you learn to harness their unique resonance, augmenting each power with the other and experimenting with their new effects.*

When making a test to use an Allomantic power, you can spend 1 metalmind charge to gain an advantage on that test. Additionally, when making a test to use a Feruchemical power, you can spend 1 Investiture to gain an advantage on that test.

When you do so, your other Metallic Art may augment and enhance the power you’re using in creative and interesting ways. Work with your GM to figure out how your two powers synergize.
