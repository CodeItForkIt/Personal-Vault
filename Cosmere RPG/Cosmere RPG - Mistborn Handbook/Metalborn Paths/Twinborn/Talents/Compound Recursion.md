# Compound Recursion

**Activation:** **1**

*Burning a piece of your metalmind fills your body with a rush of the power you usually store, allowing you to restore a bit of that power to your metalmind, keeping it full and creating a feedback loop.*

While under the effects of your [[Metalborn Paths/Twinborn/Talents/Compounder|Compounder]] talent, spend 1 Investiture or more to add an equal number of charges to your metalmind.
