# Compounder

**Activation:** **1**

*By burning a piece of your charged metalmind, you instantaneously unlock a massive reserve of Feruchemical power, allowing you to use it at a rate normally impossible for other Feruchemists.*

You can use the [[Actions/Metallic Arts/Allomancy/Drink Vial|Drink Vial]] action to consume a small piece of the metalmind you gained from your [[Metalborn Paths/Twinborn/Talents/Twinborn Heritage|Twinborn Heritage]]. When you do, you experience the following effects:

- You recover Investiture up to your maximum, as usual for that action.

- That metalmind’s maximum charges are permanently reduced by 1.

- Until the end of the scene or until you become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Depleted|Depleted]] of that metalmind’s power, you can fuel that Feruchemical power by spending Investiture as if it were metalmind charges.

To use the [[Actions/Metallic Arts/Allomancy/Drink Vial|Drink Vial]] action in this way, you either must have previously removed a piece of your metalmind (as 1) and placed it in a vial, or you must already have that piece within your body (such as through ingesting it, or through using the [[Metalborn Paths/Twinborn/Talents/Embedded Metalmind|Embedded Metalmind]] talent to embed your metalmind in your body).

Additionally, you can forgo the benefits of a long rest to repair a metalmind whose maximum charges were reduced by this talent. To do so, you must have adequate access to the needed metal (as determined by the GM). For common metals, and for rare metals you have a recurring supply of, the metalmind regains the ability to store the usual number of charges. For other rare metals, you can restore 2 charges per pound of metal expended in the repair.
