# Twinborn

*Waxillium flared his steel and tapped his iron metalmind… He increased his weight a hundredfold… then Pushed outward from himself… He became the epicenter of an expanding ring of force. Tables toppled, chairs scraped against the floor, and people [...] were caught up in it, shoved away from him.*

The Twinborn of Era 2 have both Allomantic and Feruchemical heritage, allowing them to wield both an Allomantic power and a Feruchemical power. These powers needn’t be of the same metal—and they usually aren’t. Due to the vast number of combinations of the sixteen metals, there’s incredible diversity among Twinborn, and in the three hundred years since the Catacendre, each combination has appeared no more than a handful of times.

Because they wield two distinct powers, Twinborn often experience resonances—interactions between powers that create effects greater than the sum of their parts (see the upcoming “[[06 - Ch 5 Metalborn Paths|Exploring Resonances]]” sidebar). In some cases, a resonance simply improves the flexibility of the individual powers. For example, Waxillium Ladrian can manipulate his weight with Iron Feruchemy, granting him precise control over the relative strength of his Steelpushes. Other resonances can manifest like an entirely new power. For example, Miles Dagouter is a Compounder who uses the same metal for both Allomancy and Feruchemy, allowing him to burn small pieces of his metalmind to dramatically amplify its effects.

## Compounders

*Waxillium fired a single quick shot… He nailed Miles right in the left eye socket… Miles stumbled, and Waxillium shot again, hitting him in the forehead… Miles’s eye grew back, and the head wound was gone in an eyeblink… Even getting shot in the eye hadn’t slowed him much… How did you fight someone who was virtually immortal?*

A Compounder is an extremely rare Twinborn whose Allomantic and Feruchemical metals are the same. These characters can uniquely use Allomancy to burn their metalmind (or more commonly, a shaving of it), and in so doing, greatly multiply the effects of the Feruchemical trait they stored therein. This allows Compounders to achieve feats far beyond Feruchemy’s usual capabilities.

The Compounder talent in this path grants you the ability to burn your metalminds; you’re encouraged to get creative with the incredible feats this allows you to achieve.

The Lord Ruler of the Final Empire was the first Compounder, using atium to remain eternally youthful for nearly a thousand years. The “Iconic Twinborn” section describes another notorious Compounder, Miles “Hundredlives” Dagouter. Through Compounding his Gold Feruchemy, Miles could near-instantaneously recover from seemingly fatal wounds.

For most Feruchemists, the powers automatically adjust to keep their users safe from harmful side effects. For example, an Iron Ferring’s body compensates so they’re not crushed under their own increased weight, and a Brass Ferring doesn’t harm themself with any but the most extreme fluctuations in body temperature. However, Compounding creates such intense bursts of power that it removes Feruchemy’s usual “safety rails,” and most Compounders must carefully control their power if they don’t want to harm themselves or others.

## Twinborn History

During the Final Empire, the Lord Ruler tried to prevent the existence of Twinborn by instituting horrific breeding programs, and he unfortunately succeeded in that goal. He believed the potential power combinations of Twinborn—and especially of Compounders—posed the greatest risk to his iron-fisted control of Scadrial.

However, these programs didn’t completely eliminate Feruchemical heritage from the Terris people. After the Lord Ruler’s death and the new beginnings offered by the Catacendre, many Terris people began to mingle with the rest of the Northern Scadrian population. Due to this blending of Allomantic and Feruchemical lineages, Twinborn eventually began to appear across Northern Scadrial.

Many Twinborn find themselves pulled between two different worlds. No matter their love for wherever they call home, many Twinborn also are drawn toward the secretive Terris enclaves. Generally closed to those without Terris blood, these enclaves offer many Ferrings and Twinborn the opportunity to master their Feruchemical abilities.

## Building a Twinborn

The following “[[06 - Ch 5 Metalborn Paths#Iconic Twinborn|Iconic Twinborn]]” section provides example builds for characters inspired by the novels. Also consider these general tips for building an effective Twinborn:

**Attributes.** Your attributes largely depend on which Allomantic and Feruchemical powers you choose. For example, to increase your Investiture pool for **Allomancy**, invest in **Awareness** or **Presence**. If a power often calls for **Allomancy** tests (like [[Brass Allomancy|Brass Allomancy]]), you might prioritize **Willpower**. If a power often uses your **Feruchemy** modifier (like [[Brass Feruchemy|Brass Feruchemy]]), you might prioritize **Intellect**. A power that stores a particular attribute (like [[Electrum Feruchemy|Electrum Feruchemy]]) typically benefits from investing in that attribute. Meanwhile, if a power lends itself to combat (like [[Pewter Allomancy|Pewter Allomancy]]), consider **Strength** or **Speed** to empower your weapon attacks.

**Heroic Paths.** Most Twinborn not only invest in their Metalborn path and its powers, but they supplement it with heroic talents. It’s a good idea to consider the build advice from any heroic paths of interest.

**Skills.** Most Twinborn prioritize ranks in **Allomancy** and **Feruchemy**. Also consider investing in mundane skills, such as those that are prerequisites for Metalborn talents or heroic talents you’re interested in.

## Twinborn Key Talents

After the Catacendre, the genes that enabled Allomancy and Feruchemy blended over time, and some people began to discover they could wield both Metallic Arts. People can discover these abilities at any time during or after puberty, revealing powers previously unknown to them. Choosing this talent represents your character discovering their heritage and unlocks access to the Twinborn tree.

**Starting Skill: Discipline.** If you choose Twinborn as your starting path, gain a free skill rank in **Discipline**.
