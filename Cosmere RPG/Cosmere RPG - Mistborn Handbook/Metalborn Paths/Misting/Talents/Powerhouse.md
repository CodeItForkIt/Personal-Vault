# Powerhouse

**Activation:** **8**

*Whether through practiced skill or pure talent, when you burn metal, your raw power and efficiency exceeds that of most Allomancers.*

When determining the Metallic Art range of your Allomantic powers, you count as having one additional rank in **Allomancy**.

Additionally, when you maintain an Allomantic power’s effect, reduce that Investiture cost by 1 (to a minimum of 1 Investiture).
