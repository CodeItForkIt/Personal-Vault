# Misting Snap

**Activation:** **\***

*You Snap, gaining one Allomantic power. At first, you can only use a rudimentary version of your power—and you might not even be aware you’re doing it. But after practice or training, you can master your power.*

When you acquire this talent, you Snap, becoming a Misting and gaining the following benefits:

- You gain access to Investiture, beginning with a maximum Investiture of 2 + your **Awareness** or **Presence** (whichever is higher). You can now use the [[Drink Vial|Drink Vial]] action.

- You gain the **Allomancy** skill on your character sheet, starting with 1 rank in it. It uses your **Willpower** attribute.

- Choose one Allomantic power available in your era (see “[[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Metals of Scadrial]]” in chapter 6). You can only use the nascent version of your chosen power for now (unless you choose [[Atium Allomancy|Atium Allomancy]]).

Additionally, you gain the Metalborn goal “Train Your Power” (unless your power is [[Atium Allomancy|Atium Allomancy]]). After completing this goal, you gain its reward: You unlock the full version of your power and can choose talents from its talent tree in chapter 6.
