# Instinctual Tap

**Activation:** **\***

*It has become raw instinct to tap your favorite metalmind at the first sign of danger.*

After a long rest, you can choose a Feruchemical power you’ve unlocked through either your Metalborn path or a Hemalurgic spike. When a scene begins, you can use that power’s Tap action before the first character’s turn (no action required), spending charges as usual.

This benefit ends when you use this talent to choose a different power.
