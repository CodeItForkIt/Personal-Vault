# Feruchemist Heritage

**Activation:** **\***

*You discover your Feruchemical heritage, gaining all Feruchemical powers. At first, you can only use your powers rudimentarily with whatever metals you have on hand. After crafting proper metalminds, you can unlock your full powers.*

When you acquire this talent, you discover your heritage as a Feruchemist, gaining the following benefits:

- You gain the **Feruchemy** skill on your character sheet, starting with 1 rank in it. It uses your **Intellect** attribute.

- You gain all Feruchemical powers available in your era (see “[[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Metals of Scadrial]]” in chapter 6). You can only use the nascent versions of these powers for now.

- After you acquire metalminds for your powers, you can simultaneously wear a number of metalminds up to your Intellect score (minimum of 1).

Additionally, choose one pure metal and its paired alloy (see “[[07 - Ch 6 Metallic Arts|Metals of Scadrial]]” in chapter 6) or choose the metal atium. You gain the Metalborn goal “Construct Your Metalminds” for these two powers. After completing this goal, you gain its reward: You gain a metalmind for both, unlock the full version of these two powers, and can choose talents from their talent trees. At the GM’s discretion, you can then gain the “Construct Your Metalminds” goal for another pair (or for the metal atium), continuing until you’ve unlocked all your powers.
