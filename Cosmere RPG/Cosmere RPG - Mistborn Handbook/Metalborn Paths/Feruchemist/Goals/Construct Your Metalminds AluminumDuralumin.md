# Construct Your Metalminds (Aluminum/Duralumin

After completing this goal, you gain its reward: You gain a metalmind for both, unlock the full version of these two powers, and can choose talents from their talent trees. At the GM’s discretion, you can then gain the “Construct Your Metalminds” goal for another pair (or for the metal atium), continuing until you’ve unlocked all your powers.
