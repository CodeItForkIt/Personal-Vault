# Expanded Metalminds

**Activation:** **8**

*Through careful crafting and skilled storing, you can store more of your Feruchemical power into a metalmind before hitting your personal limits.*

When you acquire this talent, the maximum number of charges for each power’s metalmind increases by a number equal to your tier. When your tier increases by 1, this maximum does as well.
