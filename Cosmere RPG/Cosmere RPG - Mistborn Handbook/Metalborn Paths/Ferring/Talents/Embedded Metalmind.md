# Embedded Metalmind

**Activation:** **\***

*Metal embedded in your body is much harder to affect with Allomancy—allowing you to protect your metalmind from others while leaving it accessible for your use.*

After a long rest, you can embed one of your metalminds in your skin as an earring or other adornment. While embedded, only characters with at least 6 ranks in an Invested skill can affect the metalmind with those Invested powers. Additionally, depending on where you embed the metalmind, it’s likely to be overlooked during normal searches (at the GM’s discretion).

The metalmind remains embedded until you use this talent again or until it’s otherwise removed.
