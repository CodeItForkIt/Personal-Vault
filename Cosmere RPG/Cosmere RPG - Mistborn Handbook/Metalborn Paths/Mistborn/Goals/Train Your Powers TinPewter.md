# Train Your Powers (Tin/Pewter)

After completing this goal, you gain its reward: You unlock the full version of these two powers and can choose talents from their talent trees. At the GM’s discretion, you can then gain the “Train Your Powers” goal for another pair, continuing in this way until you’ve unlocked all your powers.
