# Instinctual Burn

**Activation:** **\***

*It has become raw instinct to burn your favorite metal at the first sign of danger.*

After a long rest, you can choose an Allomantic power you’ve unlocked through either your Metalborn path or a Hemalurgic spike. When a scene begins, you can use that power’s Burn action before the first character’s turn (no action required), spending Investiture as usual.

This benefit ends when you use this talent to choose a different power.
