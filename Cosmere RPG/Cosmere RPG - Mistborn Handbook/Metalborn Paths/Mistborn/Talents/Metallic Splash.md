# Metallic Splash

**Activation:** **\***

*Burning multiple metals in concert has become second nature. You weave the uses of your metals together, augmenting one with a touch of others.*

When you make a test to use an Allomantic power, you can O spend to activate the nascent versions of another two powers whose metals you have access to (without spending Investiture, no action required).
