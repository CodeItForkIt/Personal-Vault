# Selective Burn

**Activation:** **\***

*You can feel separate wells of power within you for each metal you consume, allowing you to single one out and burn it while saving the others for later.*

Before you spend Investiture for an Allomantic power, you can choose to become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Depleted|Depleted]] of that power instead of spending Investiture. When you do, that power has the same effect as if you’d spent Investiture equal to your Metallic Art limit.
