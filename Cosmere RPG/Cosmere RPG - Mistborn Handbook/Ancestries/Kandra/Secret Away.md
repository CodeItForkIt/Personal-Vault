# Secret Away

**Activation:** **8**

*You can form a slit in your muscles and hide a foreign object inside, keeping it hidden from pat-downs—and from the effects of Allomancy.*

You can use an [[Actions/Basic/Interact|Interact]] action to retrieve or store an object in an unseen compartment in your body, or you can allow a character who can reach you to use that action and do so instead.

This compartment can hide a single object up to the length of your forearm. Only characters with at least 6 ranks in an Invested Art can affect the hidden object with those Invested powers.
