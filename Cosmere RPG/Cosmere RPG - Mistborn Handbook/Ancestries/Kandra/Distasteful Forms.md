# Distasteful Forms

**Activation:** **8**

*Most kandra find it distasteful to take on the form of creatures other than humans. However, enterprising kandra like yourself have realized the utility of other forms.*

When you use your [[Kandra Disguise|Kandra Disguise]], you can ingest and assume the form of any Small or Medium character, not just humans. While in that form, you also gain any of the following benefits that are innate to their natural body: weapons, deflect value, movement rates, and senses. However, your bones are very vulnerable; if that form grants you a deflect value, you can’t reduce impact damage by that value.
