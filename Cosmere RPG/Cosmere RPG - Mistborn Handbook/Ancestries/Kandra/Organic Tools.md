# Organic Tools

**Activation:** **8**

*Your malleable form is useful not only for imitating people— you can also mold it into unusual shapes that function as lockpicks or other tools.*

You can quickly reshape your appendages into tools, allowing you to pick locks or perform other delicate manipulations. Additionally, you gain an advantage on **Thievery** and **Crafting** tests that can benefit from such tools.
