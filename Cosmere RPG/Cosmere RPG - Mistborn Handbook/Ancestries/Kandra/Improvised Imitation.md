# Improvised Imitation

**Activation:** **8**

*You improvise better than most kandra, and through a few simple observations, you can uncannily extrapolate a person’s usual mannerisms.*

If you carefully observe a character for one scene prior to ingesting their bones, you can convincingly imitate their mannerisms, and you gain an advantage on **Deception** tests regarding information you lack from their past. However, you still need the usual month of research to perfectly imitate a character without making **Deception** tests.
