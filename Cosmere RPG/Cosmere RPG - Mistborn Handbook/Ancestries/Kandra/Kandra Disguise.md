# Kandra Disguise

**Activation:** **\***

*You can ingest the bones of a human to perfectly assume their form—and if you first study them before their death, you can also masterfully imitate their mannerisms.*

You can forgo the usual benefits of a long rest to instead spend that time ingesting the body of a Small or Medium human. When you do, you assume that character’s form for as long as their bones remain inside you. While in this form, you experience the following effects:

- Temporarily replace your **Strength**, **Speed**, and **Awareness** attributes with those of the character you ingested (though your kandra Blessing still applies), and update all statistics that rely on that attribute (as listed in that attribute’s section in chapter 3). If this increases your maximum health, your current health increases by the same number. If this decreases your maximum health, your current health doesn’t change unless it would exceed your new maximum.

- When you suffer an injury that would break or remove a bone, you can’t heal the bone, so this injury is permanent for as long as you maintain this form. If you shed the form’s bones, you lose the injury, but if you reassume the form, you regain that permanent injury.

- You’re physically indistinguishable from the character whose bones you wear, even to people who knew them well. Additionally, if you observe or otherwise research the character for a month before their death, you can perfectly imitate their mannerisms, and when having general conversations with people who knew them, you don’t need to make **Deception** tests to pass as that character. However, if you discuss incredibly specific or private information that wouldn’t have come up in your research, the GM might require you to succeed on a **Deception** test to avoid suspicion.

As 1, you can choose to shed this form’s bones and return to your natural form. If you save these bones, you can later use them to reassume that form as if ingesting a body for the first time.
