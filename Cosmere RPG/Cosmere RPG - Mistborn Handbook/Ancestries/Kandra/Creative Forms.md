# Creative Forms

**Activation:** **2**

*Through generations of practice, you can creatively shape your muscles instead of just imitating the creature you ingested.*

Make minor creative alterations to the form from your [[Kandra Disguise|Kandra Disguise]] talent, such as changing your facial features and weight, so you no longer resemble the original form of the bones you’re using.

If you attempt to imitate a specific person in this way, you must succeed on a *Test: `skill=dec`* to avoid suspicion when interacting with someone who knew them. However, if you first spend the usual time researching the person you’re imitating, you gain an advantage on this test.
