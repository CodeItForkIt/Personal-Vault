# Unchecked Size

**Activation:** **8**

*Though you don’t grow as large or unchecked as a full koloss, you’re far larger and stronger than humans, and you even loom over many of your fellow koloss-blooded.*

When you acquire this talent, your **Strength** increases by 1, and your maximum attribute score for **Strength** increases by 1. Additionally, you count as Large for all purposes other than the amount of space you occupy.
