# Koloss-Blooded

The koloss-blooded people are descended from the koloss of the Final Empire. After the Catacendre, the koloss became able to have children with each other and with humans; these children are born without spikes (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/03 - Ch 2 Origins#History of the Koloss|History of the Koloss]]”), and thus they’re koloss-blooded instead of full koloss. The child of a koloss-blooded person is also koloss-blooded.

Koloss have blue skin and red eyes, but their kolossblooded descendants have a range of skin tones, from mottled shades of gray to various hues of blue. Kolossblooded people aren’t inherently stronger or bigger than humans, but they have the capacity to increase their strength and size through intensive physical conditioning.

In the “[[Mistborn Handbook/03 - Ch 2 Origins|Culture]]” section of this chapter, the [[Mistborn Handbook/03 - Ch 2 Origins|Koloss-Blooded]] expertise describes the koloss-blooded culture and how these people live throughout the Elendel Basin and the Roughs. You can learn even more about koloss-blooded people in chapter 2 of the *Mistborn World Guide*.

### History of the Koloss

During Era 1, the koloss served as the Lord Ruler’s most dangerous troops, patrolling the Outer Dominances, deploying against rebellions, and keeping fearful nobles in line. Created with four iron Hemalurgic spikes, koloss have incredible physical strength, improved resilience, and an enhanced drive toward violence. Koloss continue to grow throughout their lives, reaching up to twelve feet tall.

After the Catacendre, Harmony modified the koloss, turning them into a self-perpetuating species. Due to the natural degradation of the ancient Hemalurgic spikes used to create koloss, modern koloss are less spiritually affected by the transformation process.

In Era 2, koloss-blooded people live throughout the Elendel Basin and the Roughs. Those in the Roughs tend to have more immediate family connections with koloss clans. Those in the Basin often congregate in shared communities or neighborhoods in the larger cities.

### Becoming Koloss

In their early teens, most koloss-blooded youths must decide if they’ll accept spikes and become a koloss, or if they’ll refuse spikes and remain koloss-blooded. This is an exciting moment for a koloss clan or koloss-blooded community, as the youth is choosing to shape the spiritual and physical makeup of their people. Whether the youth chooses spikes or refuses them is a cause for celebration—the clans value a choice made honestly more than the result of that choice.

However, this transformation isn’t restricted to the young. An older koloss-blooded person—or even a human—might choose to take koloss spikes and become a full koloss. PCs could discover spikes during their adventures, or they could receive them as the culmination of a patronage and relationship with a koloss clan. However, if a PC takes these spikes, they become a koloss NPC.

### Choosing Koloss-Blooded Ancestry

If you choose the koloss-blooded ancestry, your size is Medium, and you gain the following benefits at the specified levels:

**Koloss Attributes (Level 1).** Your maximum attribute score for **Strength** increases by 1, and during step 3 of character creation, you can put up to 4 attribute points into **Strength** (instead of 3).

**Koloss Stamina (Level 1).** You gain the [[Ancestries/Koloss-Blooded/Koloss Stamina|Koloss Stamina]] (Koloss-Blooded Key) talent from the Koloss-Blooded talent tree.

**Unique Talent Tree (Level 1).** Each time you choose a new talent, you can choose it from the Koloss-Blooded tree (in addition to the other trees you have access to).

**Ancestry Bonus Talents (Level 6, 11, 16, and 21).** Each time you reach a new tier (as indicated on the [[Mistborn Handbook/17 - Appendix 2 Tables|Character Advancement]] table in [[Mistborn Handbook/02 - Ch 1 Character Creation|chapter 1]]), you again gain a bonus talent. You must choose it from the Koloss-Blooded tree or from any heroic path (see [[Mistborn Handbook/05 - Ch 4 Heroic Paths|chapter 4]]).

As usual, you must meet the prerequisites for these talents (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/05 - Ch 4 Heroic Paths#Advancing Through a Path|Advancing Through a Path]]” in chapter 4).
