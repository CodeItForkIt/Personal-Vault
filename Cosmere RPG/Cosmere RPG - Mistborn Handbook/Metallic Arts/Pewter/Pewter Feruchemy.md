# Pewter Feruchemy

Through Pewter Feruchemy, you can store strength in your pewtermind, becoming scrawny and weak while you do. When you later tap that strength, your size and muscle mass increase—and consequently, so do your power and bulk. Pewter Feruchemists, also known as Brutes, wear pewter metalminds that stretch to accommodate their wearer at any size.
