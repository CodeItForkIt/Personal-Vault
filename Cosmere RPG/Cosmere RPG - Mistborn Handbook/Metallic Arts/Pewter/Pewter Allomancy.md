# Pewter Allomancy

Pewter Allomancy significantly increases your physical capabilities and your constitution. While burning pewter, it’s common to adopt a fighting style that uses your enhanced strength and speed to good advantage. These styles often center around grappling, unarmed combat, or combat with improvised weapons, leading to these Allomancers being known by the monikers of Pewterarm and Thug.

Burning pewter prevents you from feeling the effects of exhaustion, allowing you to exert your body and push your limits for long periods of time; this is commonly known as pewter dragging. The moment you stop burning pewter, though, you’re immediately hit with the full force of your exhaustion.
