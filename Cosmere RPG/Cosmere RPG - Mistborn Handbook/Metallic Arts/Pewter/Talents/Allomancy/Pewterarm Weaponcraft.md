# Pewterarm Weaponcraft

**Activation:** \*

*You wield your pewter-enhanced brute strength with greater finesse, able to better apply it to your weapons.*

When you make a melee weapon attack while burning pewter or tapping strength from a pewtermind, instead of dealing the usual damage, you can instead deal the damage for an unarmed attack. (You must choose to do so before rolling, and the weapon’s usual damage type and traits still apply.)
