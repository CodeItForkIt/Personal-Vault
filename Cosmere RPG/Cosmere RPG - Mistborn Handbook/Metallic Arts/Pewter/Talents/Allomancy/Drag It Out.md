# Drag It Out

**Activation:** \*

*When grievously wounded, you can continue the fight until either your pewter reserves or your body finally gives out.*

When you’re reduced to 0 health while burning pewter, you can choose to ignore the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition (though you still suffer injuries as usual). When your burn ends, you become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] if you’re still at 0 health.

While you’re at 0 health and ignoring the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition, you also become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] [−1] each time you take damage.
