# Proactive Nutrition

**Activation:** \*

*You’ve identified foods that hasten your mental and physical recovery, and you draw them from your bendalloymind to give yourself an edge in time of need.*

When you make a test, you can spend 1 charge from your bendalloymind to reroll one die (other than the plot die) that rolled a number lower than your ranks in **Feruchemy**.
