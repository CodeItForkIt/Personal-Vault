# Drop of Prevention

**Activation:** 8

*You’ve learned you can store certain medicines in bendalloy minds, keeping a different one for each medicine; later, you can deploy these medicines directly into your bloodstream.*

While storing nutrition, you can eat an ingestible curative, such as a ***[[Tonic 1 dose|tonic]]*** (see “[[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/08 - Ch 7 Items|Equipment]]” in chapter 7), storing it and gaining a charge in your bendalloymind without experiencing the curative’s effect. When you gain a charge in this way, note which curative is connected to that charge.

When you later spend that charge, you gain the effects of the connected curative (instead of sustaining yourself).
