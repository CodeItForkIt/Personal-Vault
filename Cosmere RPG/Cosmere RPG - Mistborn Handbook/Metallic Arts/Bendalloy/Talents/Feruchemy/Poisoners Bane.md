# Poisoner's Bane

**Activation:** 8

*You’ve learned to introduce your body to minuscule amounts of a poison stored in your bendalloymind, building up your tolerance to potentially deadly toxins.*

While storing nutrition, you can eat an ingestible ***[[Items/Equipment/Poison effectual 1 dose|poison]]*** (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/08 - Ch 7 Items|Equipment]]” in chapter 7) or similar toxin, storing it and gaining a charge in your bendalloymind without suffering the poison’s effect. When you gain a charge in this way, note which poison is connected to that charge. (At the GM’s discretion, some poisons can’t be stored in this way.)

When you later spend that charge, you can temporarily tolerate the connected poison (instead of sustaining yourself). Until the end of the scene, you automatically succeed on all tests to resist that poison.
