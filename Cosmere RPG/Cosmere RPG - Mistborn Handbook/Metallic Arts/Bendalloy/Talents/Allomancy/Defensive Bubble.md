# Defensive Bubble

**Activation:** 8

*Within your bendalloy bubble, the quicker passage of time aids you in noticing and dodging assaults from outside.*

While in a bendalloy bubble, you gain a bonus to each of your defenses against effects that originate outside the bubble. The bonus equals the Investiture spent to burn bendalloy for that bubble.
