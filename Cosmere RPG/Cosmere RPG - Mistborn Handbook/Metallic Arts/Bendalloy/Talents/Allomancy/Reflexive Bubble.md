# Reflexive Bubble

**Activation:** r

*You can create a time bubble at a moment’s notice, especially in response to danger.*

Before a character acts in a way that could be affected by the creation of a time bubble, you can use this reaction and spend 1 focus to **Burn Bendalloy** or **Burn Cadmium** (spending Investiture as usual to create the bubble).

If you’re reacting to an attack, you must use this talent before the attack test is rolled. That attack is then made through your bubble’s surface.

For example, you could create a time bubble before a character influences you or an ally, before they target you or an ally with a ranged attack, or before they enter or leave your potential bubble’s area.
