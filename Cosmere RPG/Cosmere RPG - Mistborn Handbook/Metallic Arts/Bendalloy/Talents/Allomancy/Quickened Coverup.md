# Quickened Coverup

**Activation:** \*

*Your bendalloy bubble makes your actions so speedy that you can cover for your mistakes before anyone notices.*

During an endeavor, when you fail a test while in a bendalloy bubble, you can spend 1 focus to gain C instead of counting that failure toward the endeavor’s resolution.
