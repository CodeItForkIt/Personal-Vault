# Bendalloy Feruchemy

Bendalloy Feruchemy allows you to store nutrition and hydration in your bendalloymind. In doing so, you can eat or drink indefinitely without losing your appetite, then later you can tap that sustenance to draw it directly into your body, enabling you to go without food and water for extended periods.

Beyond those commonly understood effects, bendalloy Feruchemists—known as Subsumers—can use this power for much more than they might realize. A skilled Subsumer learns that nearly anything they consume can be stored in a bendalloymind. For example, medicine can be stored then tapped in time of need; alcohol can be stored to stay sober while drinking to apparent excess; and poisons can be stored then released in controlled doses, granting metabolic resistance against them.
