# Bendalloy Allomancy

Bendalloy Allomancy forms a time bubble around you, within which time passes more quickly than outside the bubble. From the perspective of people outside the bubble, those inside appear to move so rapidly they blur, earning bendalloy Allomancers the name of Sliders.
