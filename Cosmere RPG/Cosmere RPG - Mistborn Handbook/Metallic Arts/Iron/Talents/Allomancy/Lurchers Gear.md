# Lurcher's Gear

**Activation:** 8

*You advantageously redirect attacks into your protective gear.*

While you’re burning iron and either wielding a Defensive weapon or wearing armor with a deflect value of 3 or higher, it’s easier for you to evade attacks that use metal weapons you sense with this power. Increase your deflect value against those attacks by your ranks in **Allomancy**, and ignore the **Pierce** weapon trait for those attacks.
