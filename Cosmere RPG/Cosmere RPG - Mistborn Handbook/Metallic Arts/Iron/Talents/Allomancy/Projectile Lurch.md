# Projectile Lurch

**Activation:** r

*You can redirect metal projectiles meant for your allies to yourself.*

Before an ally within your Metallic Art range is attacked with a metal projectile you sense with this power, you can use this reaction and spend 1 Investiture to change the target of the attack to yourself. You must do so before learning the result of the attack test.
