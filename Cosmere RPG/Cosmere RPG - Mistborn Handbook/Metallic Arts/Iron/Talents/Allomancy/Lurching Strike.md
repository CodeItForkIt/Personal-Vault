# Lurching Strike

**Activation:** \*

*The momentum of your Ironpulls augments the strength of your blows.*

When you use your **Ironpull** to make a Lurching Crash attack, you can do so by making a melee weapon attack or an unarmed attack (instead of an Allomancy attack). When you do, add the damage dice of your Lurching Crash to the weapon’s usual damage.
