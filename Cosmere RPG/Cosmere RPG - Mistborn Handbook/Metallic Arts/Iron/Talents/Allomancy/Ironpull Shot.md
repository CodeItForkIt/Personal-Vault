# Ironpull Shot

**Activation:** \*

*You can Ironpull objects with such force that they become deadly projectiles.*

When you **Ironpull** an object toward you while there’s a character in its path, you can make a ranged **Allomancy** attack against that character’s Physical defense, rolling `1d4 Impact`. That roll uses your Metallic Art die, so its size increases with your ranks in **Allomancy**; at 2 ranks, roll 1d6 instead of 1d4, and so on. (This attack counts as a weapon attack with a metal projectile.)

When you attack in this way, you don’t gain a disadvantage for an enemy being within 5 feet of you, and if you previously used the [[Actions/Basic/Brace|Brace]] action, this attack doesn’t end those benefits.

The GM can spend C to cause you to graze yourself with this attack, unless you’re benefiting from the [[Actions/Basic/Brace|Brace]] action.
