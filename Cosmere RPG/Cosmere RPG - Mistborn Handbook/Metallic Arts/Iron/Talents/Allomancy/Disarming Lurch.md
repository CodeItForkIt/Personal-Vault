# Disarming Lurch

**Activation:** 8

*You can prevent nearly any metal attack from striking true.*

You can also use your [[Projectile Lurch|Projectile Lurch]] on attacks that target you (instead of an ally), and on attacks that use metal weapons (instead of metal projectiles). When you do, make an *Test: `skill=all`* opposed by the attack roll. If you succeed, you become the target of the attack (regardless of whether you were within its range), and after the attack resolves, the weapon is pulled from the attacker’s hand into your space. If you fail, the attack is resolved as usual against the original target.
