# Critical Mass

**Activation:** \*

*You can tap extraordinary amounts of weight from your ironmind all at once.*

When you **Tap Weight**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. The effects stack for each charge spent, but they only last until the end of your next turn instead of the usual duration.

For example, if you weigh 200 pounds, you can spend 2 charges to increase your body weight by up to 400 pounds (for a final weight of 600 pounds), you’re considered to be two sizes larger, and your melee attacks gain 2 advantages.

Before this weight tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more from your ironmind as 0.
