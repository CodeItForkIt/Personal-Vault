# Light on Your Feet

**Activation:** 8

*By becoming lighter, you can achieve acrobatic feats you can only dream of at your full weight.*

While storing weight, gain the following benefits:

- You gain an advantage on non-attack tests that use your **Speed** attribute.

- When you move, you can jump a horizontal distance up to your movement rate, or a vertical distance up to half your movement rate, without succeeding on an **Athletics** test.
