# Iron Feruchemy

Iron Feruchemy enables you to store your physical weight in your ironmind. Charging your ironmind temporarily reduces your weight, while tapping the ironmind increases your weight. Your body naturally compensates for any increased weight, allowing you to easily move as if you were your normal weight.

By tapping a great deal of weight at once, a skilled Skimmer—as iron Feruchemists are known—can multiply their usual weight by many times, possibly becoming heavy enough to break a surface supporting them or to become nigh immovable.
