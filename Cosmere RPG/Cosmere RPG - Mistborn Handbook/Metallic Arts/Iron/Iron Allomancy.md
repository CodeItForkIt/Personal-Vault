# Iron Allomancy

Iron Allomancy allows you to see blue lines extending from your center of mass to that of nearby metal objects. You can Ironpull those sources of metal, moving either you or the object toward the other (depending on which is bulkier). The lurching movement of iron Allomancers and their metal projectiles has earned them the name of Lurchers. Most Lurchers carry wooden shields to catch any metal projectiles they redirect toward themselves.
