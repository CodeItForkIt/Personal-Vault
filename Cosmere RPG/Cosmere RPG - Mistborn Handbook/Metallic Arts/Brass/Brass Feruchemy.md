# Brass Feruchemy

Brass Feruchemy enables the storing of your body’s warmth in your brassmind. While storing, you reduce your body temperature, preventing overheating and even partially protecting you from open flames. Conversely, when tapping warmth, your raised body temperature can prevent many adverse effects of cold climates; this latter ability is why brass Feruchemists are called Firesouls.

A Firesoul’s body generally compensates for changes in body temperature, avoiding harm to themself even when hot enough to harm others with close contact. However, super-heating to radiate scorching heat can be dangerous even to the Firesoul, since this power only regulates internal temperature, not external.
