# Brass Allomancy

Brass Allomancy subtly dampens the emotions of people nearby, encouraging them to act against those emotions while believing it’s their idea. This has earned brass Allomancers the name of Soothers. For example, you might Soothe anger to deescalate a riot, or Soothe attentiveness to evade a door guard’s notice. Skilled Soothers can even affect multiple emotions at once. However, Soothers can’t sense emotions, so learning to mundanely read emotional states is essential.
