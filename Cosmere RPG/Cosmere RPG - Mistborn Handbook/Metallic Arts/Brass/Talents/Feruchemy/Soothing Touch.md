# Soothing Touch

**Activation:** \*

*You can warm or cool others with a Feruchemical touch, allowing you to numb their pain or loosen tense muscles.*

While storing or tapping warmth, when a willing ally within your reach rolls a recovery die, you can add your ranks in **Feruchemy** to the result.

Additionally, after a long rest in which you forgo recovery to store in your metalminds, you can choose an ally within your reach who has a temporary injury. That ally can choose one temporary injury and reduce its recovery time by a number of days equal to your ranks in **Feruchemy**.
