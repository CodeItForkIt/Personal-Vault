# Living Furnace

**Activation:** \*

*By tapping a great deal of warmth at once, you radiate scorching waves of heat that affect everyone around you, even yourself.*

When you **Tap Warmth**, you can spend 1 charge or more from your brassmind, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, you experience the following effects:

- You only tap warmth until the end of your next turn, instead of that action’s usual duration.

- In addition to that action’s usual effects, you also exude ambient heat in a radius equal to 10 feet × the number of charges you spent to **Tap Warmth**. At the end of each of your turns, each character (including you) in that area takes energy damage equal to your Feruchemy modifier, even if they also took damage from touching you.

Before this warmth tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more from your brassmind as 0.
