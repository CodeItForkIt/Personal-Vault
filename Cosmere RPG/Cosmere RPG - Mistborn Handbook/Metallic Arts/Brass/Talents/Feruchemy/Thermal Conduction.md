# Thermal Conduction

**Activation:** \*

*You can conduct your Brass Feruchemy’s heat through metal, making it glow red hot.*

Once per turn while tapping warmth, after you and another character touch each other, you can deal them energy damage equal to your **Feruchemy** modifier (no action required). You can do so not only when one of you touches the other’s body directly, but also when one of you touches the other with a metal melee weapon or similar metal object.
