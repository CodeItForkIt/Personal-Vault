# Searing Palm

**Activation:** 2

*You’ve practiced channeling your tapped heat into a striking surface.*

While tapping warmth, use this action to either [[Actions/Basic/Grapple|Grapple]], [[Actions/Basic/Shove|Shove]], or make an [[Actions/Basic/Unarmed Attack|unarmed attack]] against the Physical defense of a character within your reach. On a successful [[Actions/Basic/Grapple|Grapple]] or [[Actions/Basic/Shove|Shove]], or on a hit or graze, deal an extra `1d4 Energy` to the target. That extra damage uses your Metallic Art die, so its size increases with your ranks in **Feruchemy**; at 2 ranks, roll 1d6 (instead of 1d4) and so on.
