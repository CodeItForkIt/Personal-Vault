# Shadow Mastery

**Activation:** 8

*You continuously hone your skill in reading and reacting to atium’s many shadows, increasing your grace while burning the God Metal.*

When you acquire this talent, it amplifies the effects of your **Burn Atium** action:

- The static d20 value for your tests now increases by your ranks in **Allomancy**.

- The static d20 value for enemy tests now decreases by your ranks in **Allomancy**.

If these effects would set a d20 to any value higher than 20 or lower than 1, instead add an additional Opportunity to your test, or add an additional Complication to the enemy’s test. For example, if you have 6 ranks in **Allomancy**, **Burn Atium** would increase your d20 value from 15 to 21—so instead, set your d20 value to 20, and add two Opportunities to the test (one for rolling a natural 20, and one for this talent increasing your d20 beyond its maximum roll of 20).
