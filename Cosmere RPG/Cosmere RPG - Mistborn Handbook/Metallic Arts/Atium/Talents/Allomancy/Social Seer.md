# Social Seer

**Activation:** 8

*It’s difficult to watch for subtle social cues in the shadows, but with enough practice, you can even pick out facial expressions and use them to inform your interactions.*

While burning atium, you can affect tests that mentally or spiritually interact with a character (such as tests against Cognitive defense and Spiritual defense), in addition to physical interactions.
