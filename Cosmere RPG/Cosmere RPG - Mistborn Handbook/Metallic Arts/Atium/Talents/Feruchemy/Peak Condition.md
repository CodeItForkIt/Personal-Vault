# Peak Condition

**Activation:** 1

*You’ve learned which physical age enables your body to operate at its best, and you easily change your age to suit your needs in the moment.*

If your chronological age category is Middle-Aged or older, spend 2 charges from your atiummind to become physically Young and gain the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Enhanced|Enhanced]] [+1 to all attributes] condition until the end of your next turn.

Before this effect ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 2 charges from your atiummind as 0.
