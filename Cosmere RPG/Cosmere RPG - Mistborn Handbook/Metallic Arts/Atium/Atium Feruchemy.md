# Atium Feruchemy

Through Atium Feruchemy, you can store your youth in your atiummind. While charging your atiummind, your body becomes physically older. Conversely, while tapping it, your body becomes physically younger. Based on how quickly you decide to use the stored youth, you can revert yourself to a much younger age, possibly even to childhood. Though an atium Ferring has never been known to exist, Terris scholars have dubbed them Timewinders.

When you use your atiummind to change your physical age, your mental faculties and physical capabilities can be affected, though your overall personality, knowledge, and skills are unaffected. It’s not possible to store or tap so much youth that it kills you or removes you from existence, but due to the significant impact of either extreme, users of this power must take great care not to make themselves too young or old. However, by testing these boundaries, a curious Timewinder can gain insight into the length of their natural lifespan by storing the maximum possible amount of youth.

If a Timewinder discovers their power when young and stores vast quantities of youth for their entire life, then as they enter their later years, they can constantly tap atium to appear significantly younger than their chronological age.
