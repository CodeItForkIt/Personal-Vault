# Chromium Allomancy

Chromium Allomancy allows you to touch Metalborn or other characters who have Invested abilities, instantly depleting their reserve of power and leaving only a sudden coldness. This ability to sap power has earned chromium Allomancers the name of Leechers. On Scadrial, it’s common practice to employ a Leecher as part of a crew, in preparation for inevitably encountering other Metallic Art users.
