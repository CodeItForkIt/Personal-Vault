# Chromium Feruchemy

Chromium Feruchemy grants the ability to store spiritual Fortune in your chromiummind. Fortune, a type of spiritual awareness, encompasses the whole cosmere. While charging your chromiummind, you lose touch with this cosmic intuition, removing the sense that everything is connected. While tapping your chromiummind, you instead feel the threads of fate move, and you can follow this cosmic undercurrent to certain places and people of forthcoming significance. A Spinner—as chromium Feruchemists are called—who taps Fortune can’t tell the future or understand destiny in its entirety. However, they can identify critical times to act, and they excel at interpreting how to inspire and coordinate others.
