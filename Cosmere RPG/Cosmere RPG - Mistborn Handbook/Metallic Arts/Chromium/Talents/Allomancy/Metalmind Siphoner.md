# Metalmind Siphoner

**Activation:** 8

*You can brute force your way through metalminds to Leech them of their power.*

When you Leech an object with charges, it loses charges equal to your Metallic Art die roll (instead of half that many charges).

Additionally, you can Leech a target’s metalmind even if they don’t have 0 Investiture (but as usual, they must be storing or tapping it).
