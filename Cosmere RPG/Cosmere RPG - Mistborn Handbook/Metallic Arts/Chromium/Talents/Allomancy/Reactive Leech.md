# Reactive Leech

**Activation:** r

*You adeptly Leech nearby enemies before they have a chance to use their power.*

Before a character within your reach uses an Invested ability you’re aware of (such as an Allomancer burning metals, or a Feruchemist tapping their metalmind), you can use this reaction to target them with **Burn Chromium**, spending Investiture as usual.

If this depletes them of the resources needed to use the triggering ability, the ability doesn’t resolve and any actions spent on it are lost.
