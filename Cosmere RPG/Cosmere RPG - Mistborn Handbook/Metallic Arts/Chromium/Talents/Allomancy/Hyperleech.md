# Hyperleech

**Activation:** \*

*Through training, your Leeching becomes much more potent.*

When you burn chromium, you can spend additional Investiture, up to your Metallic Art limit. Roll that number of additional Metallic Art dice when determining how much Investiture or how many charges you Leech.
