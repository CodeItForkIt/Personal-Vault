# Simultaneous Leeching

**Activation:** \*

*Training has granted you the speed and precision to Leech two targets at the same time.*

When you burn chromium, you can Leech two targets within your reach (instead of one). If both targets are unwilling, raise the stakes on your **Allomancy** test, and compare the result against the Physical defense of both targets. If you succeed against both targets, you spend Investiture only once, and you use the same Metallic Art die roll for both targets.
