# Draining Grasp

**Activation:** \*

*Instead of waiting for opportunities to Leech your enemies, you make your own.*

After you succeed on a test using a physical skill (such as a weapon attack, or a Thievery test to swipe their metal vials) against a character within your reach, you can spend O or 2 focus to target them with **Burn Chromium** (no action required). You automatically succeed on the **Allomancy** test against their Physical defense.
