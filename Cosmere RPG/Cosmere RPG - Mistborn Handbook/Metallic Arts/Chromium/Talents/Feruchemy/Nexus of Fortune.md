# Nexus of Fortune

**Activation:** \*

*By tapping a large amount of Fortune at once, you find yourself at the center of climactic events.*

When you **Tap Fortune**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, forgo the usual effects of that action, and instead gain the following benefits until the end of your next turn:

- Raise the stakes on every test you make.

- Reroll the plot die when you roll a blank.

- Your Opportunity range and Complication range both expand by a number equal to 1 + the charges you spent to tap Fortune.

Before this Fortune tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more as 0.
