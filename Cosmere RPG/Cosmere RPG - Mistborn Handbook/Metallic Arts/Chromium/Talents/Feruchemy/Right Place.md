# Right Place

**Activation:** \*

*The landscape of the cosmere is vast and complex. Luckily, you know your way around these parts and can efficiently get where you’re going.*

When a scene begins, before the first character’s turn, you can spend 1 charge or more from your chromiummind, up to your Metallic Art limit. For each charge you spend, immediately use the [[Actions/Basic/Move|Move]] action (no action required); this movement doesn’t trigger reactions or other abilities. You then start the scene in your new location.
