# Not Quite Yet

**Activation:** r

*Fortune awaits, but now isn’t the time. You feel fate stalling as you spin new threads.*

When the GM raises the stakes on one of your tests while you’re tapping Fortune, you can use this reaction to instead **Store Fortune** and make the test without the stakes raised. When you do, you gain 1 charge in your chromiummind.
