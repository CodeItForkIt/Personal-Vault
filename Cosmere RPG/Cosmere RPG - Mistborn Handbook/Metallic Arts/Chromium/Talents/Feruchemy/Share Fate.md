# Share Fate

**Activation:** r

*The threads of fate are stronger when woven together. You intuitively feel a connection to others and glean meaning from your interactions.*

Before a willing ally you can sense makes a test while you’re tapping Fortune, you can use this reaction and spend 1 charge from your chromiummind to raise the stakes.
