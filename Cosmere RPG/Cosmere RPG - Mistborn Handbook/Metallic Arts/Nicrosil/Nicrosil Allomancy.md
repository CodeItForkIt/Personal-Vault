# Nicrosil Allomancy

Nicrosil Allomancy allows you to touch a Metalborn (or other Invested character) who’s using an Invested ability, causing their entire reserve of power to instantly and explosively expend itself. These bursts have earned nicrosil Allomancers the name of Nicrobursts. These bursts are difficult to control, especially if your target is caught unawares, but their potency can allow your target to surpass the usual limitations of their power.
