# Bursting Grasp

**Activation:** \*

*You can seamlessly Nicroburst people while you grapple or pickpocket them.*

After you succeed on a test using a physical skill against a character within your reach (such as a weapon attack, or a **Thievery** test to swipe their metal vials), you can spend O or 2 focus to target them with [[Proactive Nicroburst|Proactive Nicroburst]] (no action required). You automatically succeed on the **Allomancy** test against their Physical defense.
