# Assisted Burst

**Activation:** 8

*You’ve trained with your allies, giving them experience with your nicrosil bursts so they can control that explosive power.*

When you **Burn Nicrosil** to burst an ally’s Invested ability, they gain the following benefits:

- They aren’t required to roll a plot die (though they can still do so if they wish).

- They don’t have to choose the maximum possible size, range, and number of targets.

- They have greater control over what extraordinary effects occur in place of or in addition to the ability’s usual effects; they’re encouraged to work with the GM to resolve these creative uses of their ability.
