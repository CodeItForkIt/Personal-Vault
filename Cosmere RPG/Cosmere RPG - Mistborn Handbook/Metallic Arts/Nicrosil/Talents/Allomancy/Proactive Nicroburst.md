# Proactive Nicroburst

**Activation:** 1

*You can burst powers when it best suits your strategy, instead of simply reacting in the moment.*

Choose a character within your reach, and spend 1 Investiture or more, up to your Metallic Art limit, to burst that many ongoing Invested abilities of your choice that are being used by a single character within your reach. As usual, if the target is unwilling, you must first succeed on an *Test: `skill=all`* against their Physical defense.

Each chosen ability bursts as though you used your **Burn Nicrosil** when the ability was initially activated. If more than one of the chosen abilities use the same resource, that resource is split evenly between those abilities.
