# Unexpected Burst

**Activation:** \*

*You don’t show your hand as a Nicroburst until you’re ready to wreak havoc on your enemies.*

The first time in each scene that you successfully burst an enemy’s Invested ability, you can add a disadvantage to the plot die roll they make for your burst. If you spend C from that roll to make them lose control of the ability, you have slightly greater influence on how the burst is resolved.
