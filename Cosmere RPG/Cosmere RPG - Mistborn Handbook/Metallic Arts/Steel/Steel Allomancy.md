# Steel Allomancy

Steel Allomancy allows you to see blue lines extending from your center of mass to that of nearby metal objects. You can Steelpush those sources of metal, moving either you or the object away from the other (depending on which is bulkier). Because steel Allomancers can launch small metal objects at deadly speeds, many people call them Coinshots.
