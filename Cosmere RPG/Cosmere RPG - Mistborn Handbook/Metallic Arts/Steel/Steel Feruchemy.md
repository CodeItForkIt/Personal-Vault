# Steel Feruchemy

Steel Feruchemy enables you to store physical speed in your steelmind. While storing, you move lethargically, as if trying to make your way through molasses. By tapping speed, you can instead move many times faster than usual—and if you tap enough speed at once, you can potentially move too fast for the eyes of observers to follow.

Known as Steelrunners, steel Feruchemists must be cautious of friction and wind resistance while running. However, their bodies do partially compensate for the increased speed, enabling them to withstand the force of acceleration and to process their surroundings as if moving at a normal speed.
