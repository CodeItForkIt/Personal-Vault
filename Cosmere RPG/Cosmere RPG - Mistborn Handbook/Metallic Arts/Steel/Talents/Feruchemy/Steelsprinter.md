# Steelsprinter

**Activation:** \*

*You tap speed to sprint ahead of your group, allowing you to take action before they arrive.*

Before a scene begins, you can spend 1 charge from your steelmind to arrive at the destination early. If you do so in combat and take a fast turn on the first round, you gain an additional 2. If you do so in a conversation or endeavor, you can make an additional contribution on the first round.
