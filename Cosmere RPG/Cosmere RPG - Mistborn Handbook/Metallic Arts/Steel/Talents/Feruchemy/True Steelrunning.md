# True Steelrunning

**Activation:** \*

*By tapping much speed at once, you become a blur.*

When you **Tap Speed**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, you experience the following effects until the end of your next turn (instead of the usual effects and duration):

- You become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Enhanced|Enhanced]] [**Speed**]. The bonus to this attribute equals 1 + the charges you spent to tap speed.

- On each of your turns, you can [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Move|Move]] as 0, up to a number of times equal to the charges you spent to tap speed.

Before this speed tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more from your steelmind as 0.
