# Bubble Shield

**Activation:** 1

*By weakly Steelpushing in all directions, you gain some protection from metal projectiles.*

Spend 1 Investiture to create a **Steelpush** bubble around you until the end of your next turn. For the duration, when you’re targeted by a ranged attack that uses a metal projectile you can sense with this power, the attack gains a disadvantage, and grazing you costs an additional 1 focus.

Before this effect ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 Investiture as 0.
