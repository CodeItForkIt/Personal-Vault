# Coin Scattershot

**Activation:** \*

*You launch a fistful of coins, bullet casings, or other tiny metal baubles in a concentrated spray.*

Before you use your **Steelpush** to make a Shoot Coin attack, you can spend additional Investiture, up to your Metallic Art limit, to grant the attack the **Blast** weapon trait. This trait has a radius of 5 feet per additional Investiture spent.
