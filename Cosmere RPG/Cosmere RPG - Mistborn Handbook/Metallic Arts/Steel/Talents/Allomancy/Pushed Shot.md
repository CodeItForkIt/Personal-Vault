# Pushed Shot

**Activation:** \*

*You increase the power of your ranged weapons by Steel pushing your ammunition as it flies at your target.*

When you use your **Steelpush** to make a Shoot Coin attack, you can do so using a ranged weapon that fires metal projectiles you can sense with this power (instead of making the usual Allomancy attack). When you make this attack, use the weapon’s range but increase its short and long ranges by 20 feet per Investiture spent, and add the damage dice of your Shoot Coin to the weapon’s damage.
