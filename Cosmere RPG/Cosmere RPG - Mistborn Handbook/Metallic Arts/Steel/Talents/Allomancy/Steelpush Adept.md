# Steelpush Adept

**Activation:** 8

*Steelpushing has become as intuitive to you as moving your own body.*

You can use your **Steelpush** action more than once per turn. Each time after the first that you **Steelpush** on a turn, you must spend 1 focus (in addition to any other costs).

Additionally, when you make a ranged attack while you have 1 Investiture or more, you count as having stable footing.
