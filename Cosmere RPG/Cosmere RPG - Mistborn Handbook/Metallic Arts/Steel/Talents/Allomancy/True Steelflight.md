# True Steelflight

**Activation:** 1

*You’ve mastered the art of Steelpushing on tiny metal objects, such as coins or bullet casings, to affix them to surfaces and fluidly propel yourself through the air.*

Spend 1 Investiture to gain a 40-foot flying rate until the end of your next turn. While flying, a stable structure or the ground must always be within your Metallic Art range.

Before this effect ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 Investiture as 0.
