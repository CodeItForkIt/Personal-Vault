# Aluminum Allomancy

From the perspective of Scadrians, Aluminum Allomancy has no effect unless you can also access other Metallic Art powers… and even if you can, its effect is considered purely negative. Burning aluminum wipes your own reserve of all metals—including the effects of any metalmind you’re tapping—instantly expending the power with no effect. Thus, aluminum Mistings are deemed Aluminum Gnats, cursed with a power that’s considered worse than useless.

However, those who’ve visited other worlds in the cosmere understand that this power has applications in defending against other Invested Arts, especially Surgebinding. While burning aluminum, you wipe all Investiture from your system—and in so doing, you also end Invested effects from external sources. This renders you completely immune to some of the cosmere’s most dangerous abilities. For this reason, worldhoppers call these Allomancers Voids.
