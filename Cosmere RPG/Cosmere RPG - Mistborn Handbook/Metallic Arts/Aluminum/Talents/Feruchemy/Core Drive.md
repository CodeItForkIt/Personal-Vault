# Core Drive

**Activation:** 8

*By grounding yourself to your most quintessential elements, you more easily focus on your true priorities.*

While tapping Identity, you recover 1 focus at the end of each of your turns during which you acted in accordance with your purpose, obstacle, or one of your goals (at your GM’s discretion).
