# Unshakeable Quintessence

**Activation:** \*

*Tapping a large amount of Identity at once allows you to resist nearly any influence in the cosmere.*

When you **Tap Identity**, instead of spending 1 charge as usual, you can instead spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, forgo the usual effects of that action, and instead gain the following benefits until the end of your next turn:

- When you resist influence, reduce the cost by a number equal to 1 + the charges you spent to tap Identity.

- Your Spiritual defense increases by a number equal to 2 + twice the charges you spent to tap Identity.

- Attacks against your Spiritual defense can’t graze you.

Before this Identity tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by  spending 1 charge or more as 0.
