# Aluminum Feruchemy

Aluminum Feruchemy allows you to store your spiritual Identity in your aluminummind. Throughout both eras of Mistborn, those who wield this power—colloquially called Trueselves—don’t truly understand what they’re doing when they store in or tap their aluminumminds, but experimentation has allowed them to identify some basic effects.

While storing Identity, you become more susceptible to outside influence, especially to Invested abilities like Soothing and Rioting. While tapping Identity, it becomes incredibly difficult to influence you through either Invested or mundane means. It’s believed that if a Trueself tapped enough Identity from their metalmind, they could hold firm even against the will of gods.
