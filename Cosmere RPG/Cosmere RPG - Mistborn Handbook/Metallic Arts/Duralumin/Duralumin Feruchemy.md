# Duralumin Feruchemy

Through Duralumin Feruchemy, you can store spiritual Connection. When charging your duraluminmind, your existing Connections weaken and it’s harder to form new ones. This dulls the bonds of friendship and reduces strangers’ awareness of you. Conversely, while tapping your duraluminmind, you become more magnetic and charismatic, able to form relationships with ease. This ability is largely why duralumin Feruchemists are called Connectors.
