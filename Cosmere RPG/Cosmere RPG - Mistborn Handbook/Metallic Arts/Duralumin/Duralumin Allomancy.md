# Duralumin Allomancy

When burned while using another power, Duralumin Allomancy flares through your entire Investiture reserve in an instantaneous burst. Such duralumin bursts are difficult to control, but their potency can allow you to surpass the usual limitations of other powers.

Duralumin Allomancy has no benefit unless you can access another Invested ability, leading to duralumin Mistings being dubbed Duralumin Gnats.
