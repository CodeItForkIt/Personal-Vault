# Individual Connection

**Activation:** \*

*You’ve refined your control over tapping and storing Connection, enabling you to create lingering bonds with those you choose.*

When you **Store Connection**, you can choose one or more characters you can sense, exempting them from the effects of your weakened Connection. You still count as an ally to those characters.

Additionally, when you gain a language with **Tap Connection**, you continue to know it until you use that action to gain a different language.
