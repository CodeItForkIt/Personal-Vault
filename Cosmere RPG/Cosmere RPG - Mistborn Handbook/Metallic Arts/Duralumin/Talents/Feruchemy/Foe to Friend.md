# Foe to Friend

**Activation:** 8

*Through manipulating Connection, you turn even foes into allies.*

It costs you one fewer 1 to use your [[Metallic Arts/Duralumin/Talents/Feruchemy/Instant Bond|Instant Bond]].

Additionally, once per scene when you use [[Metallic Arts/Duralumin/Talents/Feruchemy/Instant Bond|Instant Bond]], you can instead target a Minion enemy instead of a non-enemy. On a success, the target counts as your companion, but only until the end of the scene, instead of until after a long rest.
