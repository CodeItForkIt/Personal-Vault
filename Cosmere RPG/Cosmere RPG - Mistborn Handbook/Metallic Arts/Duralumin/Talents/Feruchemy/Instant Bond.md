# Instant Bond

**Activation:** 3

*Using tapped Connection, you quickly forge new relationships or greatly strengthen existing ones.*

Spend 2 charges from your duraluminmind to make a *Test: `skill=fer`* against the Spiritual defense of a non-enemy NPC you can sense and influence within your Metallic Art range. On a success, they become your friend or grow deeper in their relationship with you until after a long rest.

For example, a character might temporarily become your companion or patron (see “[[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/09 - Ch 8 Goals and Rewards|Reward Categories]]” in chapter 8), or if they already are one, their tier might temporarily increase. You’re encouraged to use this talent creatively and work with the GM to resolve the effects.

When this effect ends, the character chooses how friendly they wish to be with you, taking into account how you and your allies treated them for the duration.
