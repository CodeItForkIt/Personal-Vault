# Controlled Burst

**Activation:** 8

*You’ve trained to master the massive power of your duralumin bursts.*

When you **Burn Duralumin**, you gain the following benefits:

- You aren’t required to roll a plot die (though you can still do so if you wish).

- You don’t have to choose the maximum possible size, range, and number of targets.

- You have greater control over what extraordinary effects occur in place of or in addition to the ability’s usual effects; work with your GM to resolve these creative uses of your power.
