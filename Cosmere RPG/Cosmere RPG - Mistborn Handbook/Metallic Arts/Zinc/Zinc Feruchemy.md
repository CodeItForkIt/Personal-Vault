# Zinc Feruchemy

Zinc Feruchemy enables you to store mental speed in your zincmind. While storing, your thoughts slow and it takes you longer to process and recall information. While tapping, you can more rapidly absorb information, plan ahead, and reach accurate conclusions, while the world around you feels as if it’s in slow motion.

Zinc Feruchemists, also known as Sparkers, quickly learn that tapping mental speed comes with an unusual side effect. Thinking so quickly burns extra calories, causing a Sparker to rapidly become hungry.
