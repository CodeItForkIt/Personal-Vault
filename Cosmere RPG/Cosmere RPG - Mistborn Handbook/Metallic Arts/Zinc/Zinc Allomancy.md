# Zinc Allomancy

Zinc Allomancy subtly enflames the emotions of others nearby, encouraging them to act on those emotions while believing it’s their idea. This has earned zinc Allomancers the name of Rioters. For example, you might Riot impulsiveness at a ball so a noble commits a reputation-ruining faux pas, or Riot trust in strangers to gain their aid. With practice, you can even Riot multiple emotions at once. However, Rioters can’t sense emotions, so learning to mundanely read emotional states is essential.
