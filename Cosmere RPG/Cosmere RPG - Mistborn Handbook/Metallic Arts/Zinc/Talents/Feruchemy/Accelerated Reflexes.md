# Accelerated Reflexes

**Activation:** 1

*You’ve learned to tap speed in a way that quickens your reactions to many stimuli in close succession.*

While tapping speed or mental speed, spend 1 charge from your corresponding steelmind or zincmind to quicken your reflexes until the start of your next turn. For the duration, at the start of each character’s turn other than your own, you gain an additional r that lasts until the end of that turn.
