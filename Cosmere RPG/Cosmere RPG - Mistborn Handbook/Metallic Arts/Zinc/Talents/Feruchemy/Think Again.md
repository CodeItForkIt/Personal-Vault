# Think Again

**Activation:** 1

*Your enhanced mental speed often allows you to double up on thought processes.*

While tapping mental speed, spend 1 focus to use an action you’ve already used this turn. That action must cost 1 (but you don’t need to pay that cost again; just pay the cost of activating this talent), and as part of that action, you must make a test using your **Awareness**, **Intellect**, or **Willpower** attribute (such as [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Gain Advantage|Gain Advantage]], [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Use A Skill|Use a Skill]], or an action requiring an **Allomancy** test).
