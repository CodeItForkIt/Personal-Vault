# Quick Thinking

**Activation:** 0

*You can think so fast while tapping your zincmind that you can bear the cognitive load of multiple people.*

While tapping mental speed, spend 1 charge from your zincmind to gain 2, which you can only use for the following actions: [[Actions/Basic/Ready|Ready]], [[Actions/Basic/Recover|Recover]], or any action that requires a test using your **Awareness**, **Intellect**, or **Willpower** attribute.

During a conversation or endeavor, you can instead use both 2 to make an additional contribution for the round, but you’re limited to the above actions when you do.
