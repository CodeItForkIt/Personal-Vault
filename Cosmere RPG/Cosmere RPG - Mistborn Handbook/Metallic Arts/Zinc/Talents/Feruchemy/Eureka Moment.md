# Eureka Moment

**Activation:** 1

*By tapping mental speed, you easily detect details others miss.*

Spend 1 charge from your zincmind to have an epiphany about the current scene. At the GM’s discretion, you might learn something interesting, suspicious, out of the ordinary, or otherwise worth further investigation, whether about your environment or about a character within it. If the GM determines there’s nothing worth learning, you instead gain an Opportunity.
