# Subtle Manipulation

**Activation:** 1

*Your Allomantic touch on emotions has become so subtle that you can use them with little risk of discovery.*

Spend 1 Investiture to gain the following benefits until the end of your next turn:

- Gain an advantage on tests to influence characters within your Metallic Art range, including on **Allomancy** tests to make targets unaware of your emotional Allomancy.

- After a Minion or Rival within your Metallic Art range resists your influence, they lose an additional 2 focus.

Before this effect ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 Investiture as 0.
