# Overwhelming Aggression

**Activation:** 0

*You Riot an emotion so strongly that those feelings completely overtake the person.*

Spend 2 Investiture and choose a character you’re Rioting who has 0 focus, forcing this target to indiscriminately attack a nearby character. On each of the target’s turns for the duration of your Rioting, they must use all their actions to move toward and attack the character nearest them. If multiple characters are the same distance away, randomly choose a target.
