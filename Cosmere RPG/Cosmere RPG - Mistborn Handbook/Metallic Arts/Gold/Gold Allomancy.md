# Gold Allomancy

Gold Allomancy causes you to see shadowy projections of yourself. These “gold shadows” represent possible versions of you if you’d made different choices, earning gold Allomancers the name of Augurs. Each time an Augur burns gold, the shadows they see are typically different versions than before.

Augurs can simultaneously experience not only their own vision and thoughts, but also the vision and thoughts of their shadows from alternate pasts—though doing so causes intense nausea. Gold Allomancy is widely regarded as useless, but trained Augurs can use gold shadows to greater effect; instead of simply glimpsing what might’ve been, they can use these glimpses to gain greater clarity on their own lives.

In Era 1, it was commonly known that Mistborn could burn gold—but until near the end of the era, the existence of gold Mistings remained a secret to all but the Steel Ministry.
