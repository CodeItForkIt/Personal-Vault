# Gold Feruchemy

Through Gold Feruchemy, you can store health in your goldmind. While you do, you become sicklier and prone to injury, experiencing symptoms like a runny nose, fever, weakness, and chills. When you later tap that stored health, you can heal at accelerated rates—and even heal from wounds that would normally be fatal. Because of these powers of recovery, gold Feruchemists are known as Bloodmakers.
