# Subconscious Bloodmaking

**Activation:** 0

*Your enemies thought being dead would be enough to stop you. They were wrong.*

While you either are [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] or have been dead for no longer than a minute, you can **Tap Health** as 0. If you’re dead when you use this action, you return to life, you become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] with 0 health, and then you gain **Tap Health’s** benefits.

You can use this free action even if you’re [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] or otherwise prevented from using actions.
