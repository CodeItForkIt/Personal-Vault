# Instant Regeneration

**Activation:** 2

*By tapping an enormous amount of stored health at once, you can recover from even seemingly fatal wounds.*

Once per scene, spend 5 charges from your goldmind to immediately recover all your health, heal from any number of your injuries, and end the effects of any number of diseases, drugs, and poisons on yourself.
