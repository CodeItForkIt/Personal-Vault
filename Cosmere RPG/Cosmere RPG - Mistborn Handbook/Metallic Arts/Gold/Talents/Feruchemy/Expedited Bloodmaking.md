# Expedited Bloodmaking

**Activation:** 1

*Your practice with gold Feruchemy allows you to make better use of your stored health.*

Spend 1 charge or more from your goldmind, up to your Metallic Art limit. For each charge you spend, recover health equal to 1d8 + your **Feruchemy** modifier. That roll uses your Metallic Art die, so its size increases with your ranks in **Feruchemy**; at 4 ranks, roll 1d10 (instead of 1d8), and so on.
