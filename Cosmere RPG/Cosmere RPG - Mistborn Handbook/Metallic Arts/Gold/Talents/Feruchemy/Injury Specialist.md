# Injury Specialist

**Activation:** \*

*When you tap your goldmind, healing from previously sustained injuries is no sweat.*

Each time **Tap Health** allows you to end the effects of a disease, drug, or poison on yourself, you can instead either heal from a temporary injury (with no additional cost), or you can spend an additional 1 charge to heal from a permanent injury.
