# Reflexive Bloodmaker

**Activation:** r

*It’s not the size of the Feruchemist in the fight; it’s the size of the goldmind on the Feruchemist.*

Before you become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]], you can use this reaction to **Tap Health** and remain conscious instead. When you do, you also immediately recover health equal to your ranks in **Feruchemy**.

If you use this reaction before dropping to 0 health, you drop to 1 health instead of 0, you don’t suffer an injury or become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]], and then you gain **Tap Health’s** benefits.
