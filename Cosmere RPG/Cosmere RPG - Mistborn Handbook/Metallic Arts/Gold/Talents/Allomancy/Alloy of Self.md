# Alloy of Self

**Activation:** \*

*Instead of briefly glimpsing through your shadow’s eyes, you spend hours reflecting on a shadow and its unique perspectives. This lends you meaningful insights on both your present and future.*

You can forgo the usual benefits of a long rest to instead spend that time reflecting on a gold shadow to seek insights into your current situation. Spend 1 Investiture and roll a plot die to determine the effect:

**Opportunity.** Describe how the insights from the gold shadow can help you advance one of your character’s goals. If the GM is satisfied with this explanation, advance that goal by one milestone. You can only do so once per goal.

**Complication.** After thinking in circles for hours, you become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] [−1].

**Blank.** Nothing happens.
