# Power Nap

**Activation:** 8

*You learn to tap significantly more wakefulness at once.*

When you **Tap Wakefulness**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. For each charge you spend, you immediately gain the recovery benefits of a short rest.

For example, if you spend 2 charges, roll two recovery dice and add the total to your current health, current focus, or a combination of the two.
