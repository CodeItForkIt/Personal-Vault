# Focused Sentry

**Activation:** 1

*You can tap wakefulness to gain a short burst of intense concentration.*

Spend 1 charge from your bronzemind to become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Focused|Focused]] until the end of your next turn (or until you begin storing wakefulness).

Before this effect ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge from your bronzemind as 0.
