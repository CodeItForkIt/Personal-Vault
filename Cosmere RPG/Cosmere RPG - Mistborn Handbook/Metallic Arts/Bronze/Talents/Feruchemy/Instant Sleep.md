# Instant Sleep

**Activation:** 2

*By tapping an extraordinary amount of wakefulness at once, you can compress a night of sleep into a single moment.*

Once per scene, spend 5 charges from your bronze mind to immediately reduce your [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] penalty and recover health and focus as if you just finished a long rest.
