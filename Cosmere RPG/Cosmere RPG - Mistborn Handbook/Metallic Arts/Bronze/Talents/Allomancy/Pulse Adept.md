# Pulse Adept

**Activation:** 8

*You discern metal pulses instinctively, able to distinguish them at a moment’s notice*.

When you begin Seeking an effect, you automatically learn what power caused it without making a test.

Additionally, while Seeking an effect, you can make a *Test: `dc=15 skill=all`* as 1 to determine how the effect’s power is being used. On a success, you learn the power’s effects, as well as the location of any targets of that power who are within your Metallic Art range.
