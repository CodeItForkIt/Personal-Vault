# Cosmere Seeker

**Activation:** 8

*You’ve learned to listen for the subtlest of rhythms, from Allomancy and Feruchemy to abilities from worlds beyond Scadrial.*

While burning bronze, you can detect the use of nearly any Invested ability, including the following:

- Effects that spend or regain charges instead of Investiture (such as Feruchemical metalminds).

- Effects that are generally difficult to detect (such as the Illumination surge).

- Effects like summoning or dismissing a Shardblade.

- Singers attuning to a rhythm (you can identify which rhythm it is).
