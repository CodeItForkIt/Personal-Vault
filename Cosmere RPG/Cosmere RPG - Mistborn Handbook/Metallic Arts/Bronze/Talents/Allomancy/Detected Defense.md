# Detected Defense

**Activation:** 8

*You process metal pulses so quickly that you can instinctively work around them.*

While you’re burning bronze, when you detect an enemy who’s using an Invested ability, they either gain a disadvantage on the next test they make against you before the start of your next turn, or you gain an advantage on the next test they force you to make before the start of your next turn, whichever occurs first.
