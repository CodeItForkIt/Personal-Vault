# Bronze Feruchemy

You can use Bronze Feruchemy to store wakefulness in your bronzemind; this is the only known Feruchemical trait that can be stored while sleeping. When you rest while storing, you do so in a near-comatose state, but this “sleep” isn’t fully restorative, and it’s incredibly difficult to wake from. As a result, most users of this power only store while resting in safety. Particularly skilled users can even store while awake, though they feel drowsy and fatigued, as if sleep-deprived.

Conversely, when you tap your bronzemind, you become more alert and focused. This power is primarily used to stay awake for extended periods without deleterious effects. Bronze Feruchemists are often called Sentries because tapping wakefulness can instantly revitalize the body and restore concentration.
