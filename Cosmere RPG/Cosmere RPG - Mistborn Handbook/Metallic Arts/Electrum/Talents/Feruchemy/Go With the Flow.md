# Go With the Flow

**Activation:** 8

*Storing determination makes you more passive and persuadable by others—but you’ve learned that this agree ability can have its own advantages, aiding you in finding common ground and understanding perspectives you might otherwise reject.*

While storing determination, resisting influence costs you an additional 1 focus, but you gain an advantage on **Insight** and **Persuasion** tests.
