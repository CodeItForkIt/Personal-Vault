# Endless Agreeability

**Activation:** \*

*Your openness and apparent harmlessness make people unguarded in your presence, even when you aren’t actively trying to loosen their lips.*

While storing determination, when you choose not to resist a character’s influence, you recover 2 focus. When you do so, you also learn a key piece of information about that character, such as their purpose, obstacle, or goal.
