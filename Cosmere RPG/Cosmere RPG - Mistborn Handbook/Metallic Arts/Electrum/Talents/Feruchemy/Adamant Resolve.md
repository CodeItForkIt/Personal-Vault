# Adamant Resolve

**Activation:** \*

*You draw deeply on your reserves of determination to push through hardship, disappointment, and even physical injury.*

When you **Tap Determination**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, you gain the following effects until the end of your next turn, instead of the usual effects and duration:

- You become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Enhanced|Enhanced]] [**Willpower**]. The bonus to this attribute equals 1 + the charges you spent to tap determination.

- Choose temporary injuries you’re suffering, up to a number equal to 1 + the charges you spent to tap determination. You ignore the effects of those injuries (but the injuries still exist and apply a penalty to injury rolls).

Before this determination tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more from your electrummind as 0.
