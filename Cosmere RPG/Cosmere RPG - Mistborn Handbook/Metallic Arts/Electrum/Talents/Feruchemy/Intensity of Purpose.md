# Intensity of Purpose

**Activation:** \*

*When you feel strongly convicted, your allies rally around you as a bastion of determination, and even your bitter foes feel the futility of attempting to sway you.*

When you make a test while tapping determination, you can spend O to demonstrate your supernatural conviction. You and each ally you can influence becomes [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Determined|Determined]], and each enemy you can influence becomes [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] [−1].
