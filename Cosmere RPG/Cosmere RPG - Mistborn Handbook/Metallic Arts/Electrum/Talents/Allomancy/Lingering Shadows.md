# Lingering Shadows

**Activation:** \*

*You’ve trained to see shadows of events farther in the future.*

When you maintain your electrum burn, you can spend additional Investiture, up to your Metallic Art limit, to keep that many of your current electrum shadows for an additional round (in addition to rolling new shadows).
