# Theoretical Exercise

**Activation:** \*

*You’re exceptionally skilled in reading the possibilities in your electrum shadows, guiding your decisions almost as if you’d already experienced all possible outcomes.*

Once per round, after you use an action or reaction and resolve its effects, you can spend 3 Investiture to undo it. You regain any resource (such as actions, focus, and Investiture) you spent on the undone action or reaction, and all its effects are reversed. You can then attempt the action or reaction again, use a different action or reaction, or choose to not act at all.
