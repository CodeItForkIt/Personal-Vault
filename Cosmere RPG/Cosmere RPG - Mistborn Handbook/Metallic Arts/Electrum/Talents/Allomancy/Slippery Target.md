# Slippery Target

**Activation:** 8

*You might not be invincible, but your strengthened Oracle abilities make you difficult to harm.*

While you’re burning electrum, attacks can’t graze you, and [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Reactive Strike|Reactive Strikes]] against you gain a disadvantage.
