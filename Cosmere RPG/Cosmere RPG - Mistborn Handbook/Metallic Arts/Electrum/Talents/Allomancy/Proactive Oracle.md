# Proactive Oracle

**Activation:** \*

*You not only scan your shadows for external danger, but you watch for the subtle differences between actions that drive your own successes and failures.*

When you make a test that affects a character within your Metallic Art range, you can spend an electrum shadow to replace the test’s d20 roll with the shadow’s recorded result. You can replace that number after you see your die roll and apply any advantages or disadvantages, but you must do so before resolving the effects of the test.
