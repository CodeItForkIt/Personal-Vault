# Electrum Feruchemy

Electrum Feruchemy enables you to store determination in your electrummind. While storing, you might seem directionless and unfocused, but you can also see things from perspectives you might otherwise reject. And tapping the pool of willpower in your electrummind can grant you intense bursts of determination. The highs (and lows) experienced by electrum Feruchemists have earned them the name of Pinnacles.
