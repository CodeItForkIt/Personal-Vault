# Electrum Allomancy

Electrum Allomancy causes you to see “electrum shadows” projecting from you, representing many immediate futures possible for you. This ability to scan the future has led to electrum Allomancers being called Oracles. However, you can only see yourself and how your shadow reacts to possible events. Since you can’t see who or what causes your shadow’s reactions, it’s hard for electrum Allomancers to read these futures without practice.

The shadows granted by electrum aren’t considered nearly as useful as atium shadows—however, because you can glimpse potential futures, burning electrum renders atium ineffective against you.
