# Boosted Oxygenation

**Activation:** \*

*You draw more deeply on your stored breath to push yourself beyond your limits.*

When you **Tap Breath**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, you reduce the focus cost of r and 0 by a number equal to the charges you spent to tap breath (instead of reducing the cost by 1).

Before this breath tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more as 0.
