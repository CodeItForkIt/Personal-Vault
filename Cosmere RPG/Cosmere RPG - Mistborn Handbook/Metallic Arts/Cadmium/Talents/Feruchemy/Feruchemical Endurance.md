# Feruchemical Endurance

**Activation:** 1

*You know how to apportion the oxygen into your blood not only to speed recovery, but also to shrug off pain as it builds.*

While you’re [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Afflicted|Afflicted]] or [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Disoriented|Disoriented]], you can spend 1 charge from your cadmiummind to remove that condition.
