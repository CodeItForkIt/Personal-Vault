# Deprivation Training

**Activation:** 8

*Through frequently storing breath, you’ve acclimated to environments with less oxygen, such as high-altitude regions. This boosts your endurance and increases the effectiveness of tapping your breath.*

The duration of your **Tap Breath** action doubles (to twice your ranks in **Feruchemy**).

Additionally, while you’re not tapping or storing breath, the first time in each scene that you use r or 0 with a focus cost, reduce that cost by 1.
