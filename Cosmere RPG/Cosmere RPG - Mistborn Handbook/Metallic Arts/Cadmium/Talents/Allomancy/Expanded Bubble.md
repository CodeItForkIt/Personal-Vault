# Expanded Bubble

**Activation:** \*

*You’ve trained to expand the area of your time bubbles so you can affect even more people at once.*

When you **Burn Bendalloy** or **Burn Cadmium**, you can spend an additional 1 Investiture to create the time bubble in a 25-foot area (instead of 15). This expanded bubble also costs an additional 1 Investiture to maintain.
