# Cadmium Allomancy

Cadmium Allomancy forms a time bubble around you, within which time passes more slowly than outside the bubble. From the perspective of people outside the bubble, those inside appear to move in slow motion—or even appear to be at a complete standstill. These “pulses” of sudden deceleration earned cadmium Allomancers the name of Pulsers.
