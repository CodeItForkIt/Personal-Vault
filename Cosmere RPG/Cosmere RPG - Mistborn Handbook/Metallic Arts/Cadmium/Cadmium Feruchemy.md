# Cadmium Feruchemy

Through Cadmium Feruchemy, you can store breath. You first fill your cadmiummind through rapid breathing, then later tap it to release oxygen directly into your blood. This can enable exceptional feats of concentration and endurance. Gaspers, as cadmium Feruchemists are known, use this power to speed physical recovery, energize themselves at critical moments, or simply stop breathing for a time while otherwise acting normally.
