# Expanded Coppercloud

**Activation:** 8

*You’re especially skilled in creating large copperclouds.*

The maximum radius of your coppercloud increases by 5 × your ranks in **Allomancy**. For example, at 2 ranks, the radius increases by 10, giving you a maximum radius of 20 feet.
