# Covert Coordination

**Activation:** \*

*Your crew acts with practiced precision, confident in your coppercloud and guided by your expertise.*

Before an ally within your coppercloud makes a **Stealth** test or a test with raised stakes, you can spend 1 focus to grant them an advantage on that test.
