# Efficient Smoker

**Activation:** 8

*You almost never stop burning copper.*

The duration of your **Burn Copper** doubles (to a number of rounds equal to twice your ranks in **Allomancy**). Additionally, while burning copper, you can change the size of your coppercloud as 0.
