# Indexed Coppermind

**Activation:** \*

*You organize your coppermind so well that you can recall stored memories with the greatest of ease.*

When you **Tap Memory**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. You recall the memories connected to each of those charges.
