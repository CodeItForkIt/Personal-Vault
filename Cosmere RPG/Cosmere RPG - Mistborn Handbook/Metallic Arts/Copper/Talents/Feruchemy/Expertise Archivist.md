# Expertise Archivist

**Activation:** 8

*In addition to raw memories, you can store entire areas of expertise, then later recall them and consider them with a fresh mind.*

You can use **Store Memory** to store one of your expertises as if it were a memory of an event. You lose that expertise for as long as its charge is stored.

When you later use **Tap Memory** to recall that expertise, you gain an advantage on related cognitive and spiritual tests until the end of the scene.
