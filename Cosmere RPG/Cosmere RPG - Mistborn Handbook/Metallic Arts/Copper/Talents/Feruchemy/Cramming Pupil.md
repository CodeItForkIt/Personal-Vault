# Cramming Pupil

**Activation:** \*

*After focusing intently on a topic, you can store your temporary-but-impressive knowledge in your coppermind before your memory has a chance to fade.*

After a short or long rest during which you spent at least 1 hour performing an activity that—given enough downtime—would eventually grant an expertise in a related topic (such as studying a reference book or training with an ally who has that expertise), you can store that expertise in your coppermind as a temporary expertise.

A temporary expertise follows the same rules as an expertise stored with [[Metallic Arts/Copper/Talents/Feruchemy/Expertise Archivist|Expertise Archivist]], except that when you recall it with **Tap Memory**, you only gain that expertise until the end of the scene. Before you lose that temporary expertise, you can spend 2 focus to store it back in your coppermind, as though using **Store Memory**.
