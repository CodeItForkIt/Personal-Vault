# Keeper of Knowledge

**Activation:** 8

*You’ve trained to master both your biological mind and your copperminds, increasing your capacity beyond usual mortal limits.*

When you acquire this talent, you gain the following benefits:

- Your coppermind’s maximum number of charges increases by 5.

- Your **Intellect** increases by 2, and your maximum attribute score for Intellect also increases by 2.

- As usual, increasing your **Intellect** by 2 also grants you two new expertises.
