# Copper Allomancy

Copper Allomancy creates an invisible cloud around you that prevents others (such as Seekers burning bronze) from detecting Investiture used by anyone inside your coppercloud. Copper Allomancers thus bear the name of Smokers (or Copperclouds). In addition to this obscuring effect, Smokers burning copper are immune to all but the most powerful emotional Allomancy.

On worlds other than Scadrial, copperclouds can interact with and block other Invested Arts in profound ways. For example, they can prevent Rosharan singers from attuning to the rhythms of Roshar, impede an Awakener’s life sense, and interfere with a secretspren’s ability to sense surges.
