# Copper Feruchemy

Copper Feruchemy allows you to store and retrieve memories from your coppermind. After storing a memory, you’re left with only a vague recollection of what the memory concerned. You can later recall that memory, with no loss of accuracy, by tapping it from your coppermind. Through carefully using this power, knowledge can be preserved indefinitely; accordingly, copper Feruchemists are called Archivists.

Unlike traits stored with other Feruchemical powers, stored memories aren’t returned to your mind after you stop storing; each memory is forgotten, recoverable only by tapping the coppermind in which you stored it. If your coppermind is lost or destroyed, so are any memories contained within it.
