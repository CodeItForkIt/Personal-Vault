# Wasing About Not Seeing

**Activation:** 0

*You increase your control of different sensory inputs, focusing on a single sense and tuning out others.*

While burning tin, spend 2 focus and choose a sense. Until the end of your next turn (or until you stop burning tin), you experience the following effects:

- The chosen sense becomes your primary sense, and you lose all other senses.

- Your senses range quadruples.

- Once on each of your turns, when you make a test that relies on the chosen sense (as determined by the GM), you can choose to gain an Opportunity on that test.

Before this effect ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 2 focus as 0.
