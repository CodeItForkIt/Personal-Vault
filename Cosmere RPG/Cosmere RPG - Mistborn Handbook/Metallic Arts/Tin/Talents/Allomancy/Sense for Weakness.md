# Sense for Weakness

**Activation:** 8

*Your enhanced senses make you a vital asset when it comes to observing enemies in combat.*

While burning tin, when you successfully [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Gain Advantage|Gain Advantage]] with either **Deduction** or a skill that uses the **Awareness** attribute, you also learn the target’s current health and their Physical, Cognitive, and Spiritual defense values. Additionally, your next attack against them on this turn deals an extra `1d4` damage. That extra damage uses your Metallic Art die, so its size increases with your ranks in **Allomancy**; at 2 ranks, roll 1d6 (instead of 1d4), and so on.
