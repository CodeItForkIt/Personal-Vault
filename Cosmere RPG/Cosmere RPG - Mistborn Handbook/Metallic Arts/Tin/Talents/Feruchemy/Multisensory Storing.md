# Multisensory Storing

**Activation:** \*

*You’ve learned to store multiple senses simultaneously.*

When you **Store Sense**, instead of only storing one sense, you can store a number of senses up to your tier, gaining a separate charge for each. For each additional sense you’re storing, you again become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Diminished|Diminished]] [**Awareness** −1]. For example, while storing two senses, you become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Diminished|Diminished]] [**Awareness** −2] and gain 2 charges per scene.
