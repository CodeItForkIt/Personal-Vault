# True Windwhispering

**Activation:** \*

*By tapping a large amount of sense at once, your senses become so finely tuned that you can detect even the most minute of stimuli.*

When you **Tap Sense**, instead of just spending 1 charge, you can spend any number of charges, up to your Metallic Art limit. When you do, choose a number of senses up to the charges you spent to tap sense. You experience the following effects until the end of your next turn (instead of the usual effects and duration):

- You become [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Enhanced|Enhanced]] [**Awareness**]. The bonus to this attribute equals 1 + the charges you spent to tap sense.

- You gain an advantage on all tests that rely on any sense you’re tapping (GM’s discretion).

- Each sense you’re tapping becomes a supernatural sense (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/04 - Ch 3 Character Statistics#Supernatural Senses|Supernatural Senses]]” in chapter 3), and they can all count as your primary sense (making it incredibly difficult for your senses to become obscured).

Before this sense tapping ends, you can maintain it for the same duration by spending 1 charge or more from your tinmind as 0.
