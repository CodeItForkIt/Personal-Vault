# Reflexive Windwhisperer

**Activation:** r

*You can react to unpleasant sensory input with great speed.*

Before an action or other effect occurs that could be affected by storing one of your senses, you can use this reaction to **Store Sense**. Until the end of this turn, you gain an advantage on tests to resist the triggering effect, and that effect’s source gains a disadvantage on tests against you caused by that effect.

For example, you could use this reaction to reduce the impact of a loud noise, a bright light, an overwhelming scent, or another sensory effect that could cause you to become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Disoriented|Disoriented]].
