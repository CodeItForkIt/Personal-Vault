# Tinpoint Accuracy

**Activation:** 8

*Your enhanced senses aid you in making long-range attacks with great precision.*

While burning tin or tapping sense from a tinmind, you gain the following benefits:

- The short and long range of your ranged attacks increases by your senses range.

- When you make a ranged attack against a target you can sense within 5 feet of your ally, you can choose not to raise the stakes.
