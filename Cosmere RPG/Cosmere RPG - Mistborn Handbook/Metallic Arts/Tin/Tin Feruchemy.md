# Tin Feruchemy

Tin Feruchemy grants you the ability to store your senses—typically hearing, sight, smell, taste, and touch—in your tinmind. Your chosen sense is dampened while you store it and enhanced while you tap it. Tin Feruchemists are known as Windwhisperers due to their ability to hear whispers from across a crowded room.
