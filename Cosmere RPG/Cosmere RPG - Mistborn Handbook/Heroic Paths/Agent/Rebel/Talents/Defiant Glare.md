# Defiant Glare

**Activation:** **1**

*The spark of rebellion burns bright in you, undermining your enemy’s authority and wearing them down.*

Spend 2 focus to make an *Test: `skill=ins`* against the Spiritual defense of a character you can influence. On a success, if your target wasn’t already [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]], they become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] [−2].
