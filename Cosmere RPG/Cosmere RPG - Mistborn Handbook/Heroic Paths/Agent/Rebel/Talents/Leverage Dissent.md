# Leverage Dissent

**Activation:** **8**

*Your voice is clear through the commotion of a crowd, convincing others to rise up and take action.*

When you roll a Complication on the plot die, add your ranks in **Leadership** to the bonus you receive.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, gain a utility expertise in Public Speaking.
