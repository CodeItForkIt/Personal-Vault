# Through the Grapevine

**Activation:** **\***

*With connections in high and low places, you gather information and reinforcements that could prove invaluable to your cause.*

After a long rest, you can make an *Test: `skill=ins`* to collect information about a person or group of your choosing. The GM sets the DC based on how secretive and obscure your subject is. On a success, the GM shares two pieces of information that are true. On a failure, the GM shares two pieces of information, one true and the other false, without identifying which is which.
