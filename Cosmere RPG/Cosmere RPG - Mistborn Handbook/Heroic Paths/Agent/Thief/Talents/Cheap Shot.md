# Cheap Shot

**Activation:** **1**

*You know exactly where to strike to leave your foe reeling in pain. Some might not view it as sporting, but it certainly works.*

While you have a hand free, spend 1 focus to make an [[Unarmed Attack|unarmed attack]] using **Thievery** against the Cognitive defense of an enemy of your size or smaller, raising the stakes when you do. On a hit, the target also becomes [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]] until the end of their next turn. On a hit or graze, you can also spend an Opportunity to steal one item on the target’s person that they aren’t holding or wielding (such as an Allomancer’s vial of metals). The exact item you can steal is up to the GM’s discretion.
