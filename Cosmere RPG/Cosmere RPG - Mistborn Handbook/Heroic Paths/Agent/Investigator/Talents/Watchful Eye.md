# Watchful Eye

**Activation:** **r**

*Allies can be an asset or a liability, so you keep an eye on them for their protection and yours.*

When a willing ally you can influence within 20 feet of you rolls a plot die, you can use this reaction to use [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Agent/Opportunist|Opportunist]] on that plot die as if it were your own.
