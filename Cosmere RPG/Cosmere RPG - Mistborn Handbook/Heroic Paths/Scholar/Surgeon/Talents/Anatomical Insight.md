# Anatomical Insight

**Activation:** **\***

*Your anatomical knowledge aids you in striking vital points to incapacitate enemies while minimizing permanent harm.*

When you hit a target of your size or smaller with an [[Unarmed Attack|unarmed attack]], you can spend 1 focus or O to apply the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Exhausted|Exhausted]] condition to your target. The penalty applied by this condition equals half your ranks in **Medicine**, rounded up.
