# Scholar

Ever chasing the next discovery, Scholars balance the confidence to pursue their ideas alongside the humility to pivot when needed. Their passion for their craft fuels long hours in ash-laden fields or in workshops that smell of gunpowder and half-eaten sandwiches. Never content with a basic understanding of the world, Scholars constantly explore, learn, and build. No curiosity is meritless; unexpected topics yield great breakthroughs.

The double-edged sword of scientific advancement both propels civilizations forward and threatens to bring them crashing down. Prestigious Scholars align themselves with entities who provide ample resources for conducting research—and some of these Scholars do so with little concern for their patron’s motives. Others feel protective of their work and prefer to save their best inventions for those they deem worthy.

## Scholar Specialty

The Scholar path presents talents in three specialties:

**Strategist.** Wielding cleverness like a blade, Strategists plan ahead and take their enemies by surprise.

**Surgeon.** Making split-second life or death decisions, Surgeons rely on their extensive training and steady hands to save lives.

**Inventor  (Era 2).** Always tinkering with new parts, Inventors take pride in optimizing and embellishing their creations. (Only available in Era 2.)

## Building a Scholar

The following “[[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/05 - Ch 4 Heroic Paths#Scholars of Scadrial|Scholars of Scadrial]]” section suggests example builds for iconic characters. If you have a different idea in mind, consider these general tips for building an effective Scholar:

**Attributes.** It’s usually a good idea to prioritize **Intellect**, then **Presence** plus either **Speed** or **Strength** (depending on your desired combat functionality).

**Skills.** Useful skills include **Crafting**, **Deduction**, **Lore**, and **Medicine**.

**Other Heroic Paths.** The [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Hunter/Hunter|Hunter]] and [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Leader/Leader|Leader]] paths make excellent multi-path choices.

**Rewards.** When choosing rewards, you can’t go wrong with a wealthy patron to fund your research, a companion to test your inventions, or an [[Allomantic Grenade|Allomantic grenade]].

## Scholar Key Talent

The Scholar key talent, [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Scholar/Erudition|Erudition]], unlocks access to the Scholar specialties.

**Starting Skill: Lore.** If you choose Scholar as your starting path, gain a free skill rank in **Lore**.
