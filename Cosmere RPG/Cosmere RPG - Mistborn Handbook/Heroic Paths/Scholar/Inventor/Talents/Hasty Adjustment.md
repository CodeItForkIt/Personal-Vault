# Hasty Adjustment

**Activation:** **1**

*You’re a master of improvisational mechanics, even when time is tight. Where others see rubbish, you see a treasure trove of possibilities.*

Make a *Test: `skill=cra`* either to improvise a trap or similar device, or to alter or improve a tool or machine’s utility beyond what’s typically possible. You can combine components of another tool or machine you have to gain an advantage on this test. On a success, work with the GM to determine the new item’s effect.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, you learn to use the **Survival** skill to forage for scrap materials and spare parts while in an industrial area such as a city.
