# Out of a Jam

**Activation:** **r**

*Tending to firearms has become second nature. You coolly attend to weapon malfunctions without missing a beat.*

After a weapon within your reach runs out of loaded ammunition or breaks, you can use this reaction and spend 1 focus to reload the weapon or make a *Test: `skill=cra`* to repair it.
