# Gunsmith

**Activation:** **8**

*Crafting firearms with bells and whistles is a fickle art but one you have an affinity for.*

When you make a test to craft an item, your Opportunity range on that test expands by 2, and any cost to acquire raw materials is reduced by half.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, gain the Munitions Crafting utility expertise, which you can use to invent firearms using prototype designs (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/08 - Ch 7 Items|Inventing Firearms]]” in chapter 7).
