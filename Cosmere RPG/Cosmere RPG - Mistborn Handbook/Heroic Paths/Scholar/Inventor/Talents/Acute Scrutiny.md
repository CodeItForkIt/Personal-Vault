# Acute Scrutiny

**Activation:** **r**

*Your expert eye quickly spots shoddy construction and malfunctions waiting to happen. Through misdirection or outright sabotage, you ensure misfortune strikes at the perfect moment.*

When a character you can sense attempts to use a non-Invested object that either belongs to them or that was present at the start of the scene, you can use this reaction and spend 1 focus to make a *Test: `skill=cra`* against that character’s Cognitive defense. On a success, the item is no longer functional; the GM determines what happens when it malfunctions.
