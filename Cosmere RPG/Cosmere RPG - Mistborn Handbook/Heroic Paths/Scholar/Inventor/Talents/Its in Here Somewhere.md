# It's in Here Somewhere

**Activation:** **3**

*Your bag is full of random creations, but moments like this demonstrate exactly why you haul everything around.*

You attempt to create an item that would be useful in this scene, either using materials you have on hand or common materials you have enough boxings to purchase (as if you’d purchased them in an earlier scene). Make a *Test: `skill=cra`* to determine whether you succeed; for most items, the DC for this test is 15, but if you’re using nonstandard materials, the DC is 20 (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/08 - Ch 7 Items#Crafting Items With Nonstandard Materials|Crafting Items with Nonstandard Materials]]” in chapter 7).
