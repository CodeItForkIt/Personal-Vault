# Defensive Position

**Activation:** **8**

*You’ve learned when to withdraw and when to commit to a position, holding it unflinchingly.*

The [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Brace|Brace]] action adds two disadvantages to attacks against you, instead of one.

Additionally, while you’re using a shield to [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Brace|Brace]], allies within 5 feet of you can [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Brace|Brace]] as if they had cover or a shield.
