# Swift Strikes

**Activation:** **1**

*You instinctively follow the tempo of battle, keeping the pressure on your foes at critical moments.*

Spend 1 focus to make a second [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Strike|Strike]] action with a hand you already used for a [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Strike|Strike]] this turn.
