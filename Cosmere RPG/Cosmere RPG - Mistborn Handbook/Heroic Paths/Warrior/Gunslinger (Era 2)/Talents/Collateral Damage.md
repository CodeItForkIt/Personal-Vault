# Collateral Damage

**Activation:** **0**

*You skillfully aim your shots to eviscerate cover, tossing splinters or chips of stone to even hit entrenched foes.*

When you make a ranged attack that deals keen damage, choose another target within 5 feet of your target. Your attack also grazes that target.
