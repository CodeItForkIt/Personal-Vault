# Fire Discipline

**Activation:** **\***

*You know how to wield and maintain projectile weapons safely and with precision.*

Once per round, when you miss on an attack with a **Loaded** weapon, you can graze one target of that attack without spending focus.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, gain a weapon expertise in one **Loaded** weapon, and gain a utility expertise in either Crossbow Maintenance or Gun Maintenance  (Era 2).
