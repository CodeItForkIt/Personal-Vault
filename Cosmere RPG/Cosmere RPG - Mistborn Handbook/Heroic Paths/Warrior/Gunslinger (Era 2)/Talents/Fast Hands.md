# Fast Hands

**Activation:** **0**

*You’re faster on the draw than most—and thus far, that skill has kept you among the living.*

Spend 2 focus to gain 2, which you can use only for drawing or reloading a weapon using the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Interact|Interact]] action. Until the end of your turn, you gain an advantage on tests using that weapon to attack targets who haven’t yet taken a turn this round.
