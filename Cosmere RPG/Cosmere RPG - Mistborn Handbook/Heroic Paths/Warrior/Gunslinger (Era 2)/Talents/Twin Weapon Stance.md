# Twin Weapon Stance

**Activation:** **1**

*You adopt a balanced stance with a weapon in each hand, presenting multiple threats to keep your enemies guessing.*

When you acquire this talent, you learn a new stance, which you can enter as 1:

**Twin Weapon Stance.** While in this stance, you gain an advantage on **Agility** tests, and your Opportunity range expands by 2 for attack tests with one-handed ranged weapons. Additionally, choose a one-handed weapon you’re wielding to temporarily gain the **Offhand** trait.
