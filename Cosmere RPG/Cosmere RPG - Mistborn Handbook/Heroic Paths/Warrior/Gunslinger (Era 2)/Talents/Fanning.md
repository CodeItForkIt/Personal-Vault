# Fanning

**Activation:** **3**

*You rapidly depress the hammer of your gun multiple times, discharging a volley into foes around you.*

While you have a hand free, make a ranged attack test using a weapon with a **Loaded** [2+] trait, and choose a point within that weapon’s range. Within a 15-foot radius of that point, you can target a number of enemies up to the amount of ammunition loaded in your weapon (to a maximum of 6). You expend ammunition equal to the number of targets.

The DC for this test equals the highest Physical defense among your targets. If you target more enemies than your ranks in **Agility**, you gain a disadvantage on this test for each enemy beyond that number. On a success, you hit one target of your choice and graze each other target. You can spend O to inflict a critical hit on a target you hit, or to inflict a hit on a target you grazed.
