# Bruiser's Stance

**Activation:** **1**

*You shift your weight and position to fend off multiple foes, staying alert to threats from all directions.*

When you acquire this talent, you learn a new stance, which you can enter as 1:

**Bruiser’s Stance.** While in this stance, increase your Physical defense by 1 for each enemy within your reach (maximum of +5). Additionally, when an enemy triggers a [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Reactive Strike|Reactive Strike]], you can make that [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Reactive Strike|Reactive Strike]] without spending r. When you do, you must spend focus as usual, and you must attack them with an [[Unarmed Attack|unarmed strike]] or **improvised weapon**. You can only attack each enemy in this way once per round.
