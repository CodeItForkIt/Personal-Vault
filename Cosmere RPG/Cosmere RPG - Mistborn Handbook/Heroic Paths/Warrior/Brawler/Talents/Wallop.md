# Wallop

**Activation:** **r**

*You slide past an attack to deliver a devastating counterpunch that disorients your foe, disarms them, or even knocks them off their feet.*

After an enemy within 10 feet of you misses or grazes you with an attack, you can use this reaction and spend 2 focus to move up to half your movement rate, then make an [[Actions/Basic/Unarmed Attack|unarmed attack]] against the Spiritual defense of your attacker. When you roll damage for this attack, add an extra `2d6` damage. This attack ignores your target’s deflect value.

You can spend O from this attack to apply one of the following effects:

**Bellringer.** The target becomes [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Disoriented|Disoriented]] until the end of your next turn.

**Disarm.** The target must succeed on an *Test: `skill=agi`* (DC equals your attack test result) or drop one weapon they’re holding. You can either pick up that weapon or push it up to 10 feet away (no action needed).

**Knockdown.** The target is knocked [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]].

Your **Wallop** becomes more powerful as your tier increases: at tier 3, you can target an enemy within 15 feet and you roll an extra 4d6 damage (instead of 2d6); at tier 4, you can target an enemy within 20 feet and you roll an extra 6d6 damage; and at tier 5, you can target an enemy within 25 feet and you roll an extra 8d6 damage.
