# Battle-Hardened

**Activation:** **\***

*You’ve spilled blood—both yours and your foes—across many arenas, and you’ve survived through ferocity, grit, and hard-earned instincts.*

Once per round, you can reroll one of the following dice when its result is lower than your ranks in **Athletics**: a recovery die, a damage die for an [[Actions/Basic/Unarmed Attack|unarmed attack]] or **improvised weapon**, or an injury roll’s d20.
