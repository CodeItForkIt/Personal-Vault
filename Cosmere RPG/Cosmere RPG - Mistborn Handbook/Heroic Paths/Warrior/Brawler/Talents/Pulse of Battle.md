# Pulse of Battle

**Activation:** **\***

*When you get into the flow of the fight, time seems to slow. You instinctively deflect glancing blows and sense when to lean in for a counterattack.*

Before you’re grazed by an attack, you can choose one of the following effects (no action needed):

- Increase your deflect value against that attack by 1, and recover 1 focus.

- Spend 2 focus to gain an additional r.
