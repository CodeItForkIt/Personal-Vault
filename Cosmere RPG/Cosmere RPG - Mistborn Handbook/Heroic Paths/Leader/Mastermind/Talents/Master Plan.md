# Master Plan

**Activation:** **2**

*With a lot of forethought and a little luck, your master plan comes together like clockwork.*

Once per scene, you can reveal a master plan. Work with the GM to create an event (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring|Events]]” in chapter 9) with a positive outcome for you and your allies. This **Master Plan** event represents your off-screen efforts to plan and bring a significant narrative boon to fruition. You can only have one such event active at a time.

By default, your **Master Plan** has an event track with three Opportunity slots, which you and your allies can contribute to. When you create this event, you can choose to increase the event track by a number of slots up to your ranks in **Deduction**; this makes the event harder to trigger but more impactful. You and your allies can trigger it by filling the track before your next long rest. If not triggered by then, the event fails.

For example, an event with three Opportunity slots might represent your efforts to ensnare your political rival in a social trap, depriving them of a key ally. An event with five Opportunity slots might represent your efforts to bribe an entire shift of guards to conveniently take a break at a specific time.
