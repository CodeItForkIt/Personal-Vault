# I've Got Just the Thing

**Activation:** **1**

*You’ve been known to overpack, but you always have the right tool for the job—even when the job isn’t quite right.*

Once per scene, spend 1 focus to choose an item you don’t currently possess but reasonably could’ve obtained in the past, such as a piece of equipment, building blueprints, a letter, or something similar you can store on your person. You then produce that item from your belongings, as if you’d previously purchased or otherwise obtained it off-screen. If the item usually has a cost (as listed in [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/08 - Ch 7 Items|chapter 7]]), you must also pay that cost when you use this talent to obtain that item. The GM is the final arbiter of what item you can produce.
