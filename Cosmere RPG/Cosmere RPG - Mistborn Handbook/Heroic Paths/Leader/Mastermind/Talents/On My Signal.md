# On My Signal

**Activation:** **\***

*You and your crew are careful and efficient, preparing in advance so you can strike at the perfect moment.*

When you and your allies fill all Opportunity slots in your [[Heroic Paths/Leader/Mastermind/Talents/Master Plan|Master Plan]] event, you can choose not to trigger it immediately. Instead, during the current scene, you can decide to trigger it (no action required) after any test is resolved, or at the end of any character’s turn.
