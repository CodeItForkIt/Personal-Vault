# Precise Command

**Activation:** **\***

*You’ve already devised the means to your desired end and calculated every step along the way. Now you attentively guide your allies and follow through on your careful plan.*

When you make a **Deduction**, **Leadership**, or **Lore** test, you can spend 1 focus to roll your command die and add the result to your d20 roll.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, increase the size of your command die by one size (such as from a d4 to a d6).
