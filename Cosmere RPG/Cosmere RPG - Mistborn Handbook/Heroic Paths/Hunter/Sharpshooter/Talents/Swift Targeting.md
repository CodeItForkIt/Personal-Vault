# Swift Targeting

**Activation:** **0**

*You quickly focus on your target, filtering out all unnecessary detail in your environment to seek this new quarry.*

Spend 2 focus to use the [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Gain Advantage|Gain Advantage]] action as 0 against an enemy you can sense. If you’re [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]], using this talent instead costs no focus. After you successfully [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Actions/Basic/Gain Advantage|Gain Advantage]] in this way, that test’s target also becomes your quarry if you don’t already have one.
