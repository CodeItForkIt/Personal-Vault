# Deft Drop

**Activation:** **1**

*You drop to the ground and prepare for the dangers ahead.*

Gain the [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]] condition, safely dismounting from your mount if you’re riding one, then do one of the following:

- Use the [[Actions/Basic/Ready|Ready]] action, spending action points as usual for the action you’re readying.

- Reload a **Loaded** weapon to full ammunition.
