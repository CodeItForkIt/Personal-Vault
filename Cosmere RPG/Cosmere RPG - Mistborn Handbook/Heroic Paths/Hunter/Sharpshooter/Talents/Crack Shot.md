# Crack Shot

**Activation:** **\***

*As you aim, you tune out all distractions, control your breath to steady the shot, then fire with devastating accuracy.*

Before you make a test with a **Two-Handed** ranged weapon while [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]], you can spend 1 focus to expand that test’s Opportunity range by 2. If you have a disadvantage on that test, you can also choose one die; when you do, the GM can’t choose that die for your disadvantage, and must choose a different die instead.
