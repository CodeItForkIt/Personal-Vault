# Hazekiller Weaponry

**Activation:** **8**

*Bullets aren’t always what they seem. You’ve meticulously prepared knives and ammunition to trick brazen Allomancers who act on instinct.*

When you wield a weapon designed to counter Invested Arts (such as a glass dagger, obsidian axe, dueling cane, soundstick, or firearm loaded with a hazekiller round), it has the **Quickdraw** weapon trait. When you hit or graze with that weapon, you deal extra damage equal to your ranks in Insight.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, you either gain a utility expertise in Munitions Crafting, or you gain one weapon expertise of your choice.
