# Reckless Ferocity

**Activation:** **2**

*After your attack lands, you unhesitatingly strike again. Against a wounded foe, even a sloppy attack is a worthy one.*

Make two melee weapon attacks against the Physical defense of a target. The second attack gains a disadvantage.
