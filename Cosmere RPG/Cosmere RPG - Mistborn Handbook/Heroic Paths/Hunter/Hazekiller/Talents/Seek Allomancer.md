# Seek Allomancer

**Activation:** **r**

*When an Allomancer reveals themself, your adrenaline kicks in, heightening your senses as you study their movements and techniques.*

After a character who you can sense spends Investiture, if you’re aware they did so, you can use this reaction and spend 1 focus to make them your quarry.

Additionally, after you acquire this talent, your senses range doubles while a quarry who is your enemy is within 200 feet of you.
