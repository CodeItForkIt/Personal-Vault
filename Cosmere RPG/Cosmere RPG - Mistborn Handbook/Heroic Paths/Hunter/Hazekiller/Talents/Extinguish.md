# Extinguish

**Activation:** **r**

*You know the odds were against you from the start, but the uneven playing field sparks a fighting spirit in you that refuses to go down easy.*

Before your quarry recovers Investiture while within your reach, you can use the [[Actions/Basic/Reactive Strike|Reactive Strike]] reaction against them as if they’d voluntarily left your reach. If you hit or graze them with that attack, they only recover half as much Investiture and are [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]] until the end of their next turn.
