# Delicate Touch

**Activation:** **8**

*Eschewing durable weapons made of superior materials, you instead specialize in the unconventional. Your weapons and ammunition are fragile, yet deadly, even against Metalborn who are practiced in defying death.*

You ignore the effects of the **Fragile** trait. Additionally, when you make an attack test with a **Fragile** weapon, your Opportunity range expands by 2 for that attack.
