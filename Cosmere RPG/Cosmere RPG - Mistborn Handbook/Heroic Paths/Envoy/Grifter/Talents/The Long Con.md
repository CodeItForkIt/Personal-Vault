# The Long Con

**Activation:** **8**

*You can achieve anything with the right connections. Through years of careful planning, you’ve ingratiated yourself with the rich and powerful.*

When you acquire this talent, you gain a patron. Work with the GM to determine who this person is and what your history is with them. When determining patron amenities, you count as one tier higher (see “[[Mistborn Handbook/09 - Ch 8 Goals and Rewards#Patron Benefits|Patron Benefits]]” in chapter 8).

Additionally, while you have access to that patron’s amenities, those include clothing and accessories to make any mundane disguise.
