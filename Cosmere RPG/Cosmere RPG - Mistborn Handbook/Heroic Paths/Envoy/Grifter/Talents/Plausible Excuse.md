# Plausible Excuse

**Activation:** **r**

*Years of covert operations have taught you to dissemble on the spot, concocting a reasonable excuse for the most incriminating behavior.*

After you’re discovered while hiding or otherwise skulking about, you can use this reaction and spend 2 focus to pass yourself off as doing something innocent. You influence each character who can sense you to believe your excuse.

A character can resist this influence, but after they do, they lose additional focus equal to your ranks in **Deception**.

Additionally, when you acquire this talent, gain a utility expertise in Sleight of Hand.
