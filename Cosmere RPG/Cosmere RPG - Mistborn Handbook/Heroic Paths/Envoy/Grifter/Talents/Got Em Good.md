# Got ’Em Good!

**Activation:** **r**

*You patiently wait for your allies to best someone in a battle of wits, then you further fluster the unfortunate soul by cleverly pointing out their mistakes.*

After an ally succeeds on a test against the Cognitive or Spiritual defense of a target you can influence, you can use this reaction to make a clever jab. The target loses 2 focus, and if they have 0 focus afterward, they also become [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Disoriented|Disoriented]] until the start of your next turn.
