# Exceptional Bluff

**Activation:** **\***

*You feign unsurpassed authority on topics you’re only vaguely familiar with. As you sprinkle relevant buzzwords into a lengthy spiel, the uninitiated often become lost in your torrent of information.*

Before making a cognitive or spiritual test to influence a character, you can spend 2 focus to make a *Test: `skill=dec`* instead of using the other skill.
