# False Flattery

**Activation:** **8**

*Your smooth words disarm even those who ought to know better, giving you the upper hand when things get dicey.*

You can use your [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Envoy/Rousing Presence|Rousing Presence]] on a character who is not your ally and who is not already [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Determined|Determined]]. The next time that character fails a test, they must use their [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Determined|Determined]] condition to add an Opportunity, but you gain that Opportunity, not them.
