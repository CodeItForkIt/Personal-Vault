# This One’s on Me

**Activation:** **r**

*Sure, you’re a con artist, but you’re a loyal one. You’ll even take a bullet—or coin—to protect an ally in harm’s way.*

Before an ally is hit or grazed by a ranged attack, you can spend 1 focus to use the [[Actions/Basic/Move|Move]] action as a reaction; when you do, you must end that movement in a space between that ally and their attacker. You become the new target of that attack, which automatically hits you regardless of your defense, and the ally you protected becomes [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Determined|Determined]].
