# Envoy

When hope is flimsy and the future bleak, Envoys across Scadrial offer comfort and inspire others to press on. They know the power of the mind and heart can overcome most anything. With impassioned words, they guide rather than command, helping others confront inner struggles and realize their fullest potential.

Each Envoy’s moral code shapes the way they interact with the world around them. These ethics may be informed by established belief systems or by a nontraditional system of their own design. Even more important than what they believe in, Envoys must choose carefully who to believe in. Their guidance is a forge that can transform normal people into permanent fixtures in history. 

## Envoy Specialties

The Envoy path presents talents within three specialties:

**Faithful.** Searching for meaning and direction from deities, Faithful know the path forward isn’t always easy, but it must be pursued. They find miracles in mundane places and cling to the example of the divine.

**Grifter.** Untethered by standard moral codes, Grifters know the best lies have truth to them. Trust in a Grifter is generally misplaced, but their trust in others is as valuable as it is rare.

**Mentor.** Ever observant, Mentors work to understand the many facets of their pupils, with the intent to reconfigure and build them up to greatness.

## Building an Envoy

The following “[[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/05 - Ch 4 Heroic Paths#Envoys of Scadrial|Envoys of Scadrial]]” section suggests example builds for iconic characters. If you have a different idea in mind, consider these general tips for building an effective Envoy:

**Attributes.** It’s usually a good idea to prioritize **Presence** and **Willpower**.

**Skills.** **Discipline** is an essential skill for most Envoys. Other useful skills include **Deception**, **Leadership**, **Lore**, and **Persuasion**.

**Other Heroic Paths.** The [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Agent/Agent|Agent]], [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Leader/Leader|Leader]], and [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Scholar/Scholar|Scholar]] paths make excellent multi-path choices.

**Rewards.** When choosing rewards, you can’t go wrong with influential patrons.

## Envoy Key Talent

The Envoy key talent, [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Envoy/Rousing Presence|Rousing Presence]], unlocks access to the Envoy specialties.

**Starting Skill: Discipline.** If you choose Envoy as your starting path, gain a free skill rank in **Discipline**.
