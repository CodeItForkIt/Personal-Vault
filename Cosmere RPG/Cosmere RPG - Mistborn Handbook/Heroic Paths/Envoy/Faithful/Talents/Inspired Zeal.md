# Inspired Zeal

**Activation:** **\***

*When an ally achieves new heights, your proclamations of their success inspire those around you.*

After an ally you can sense uses their [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Determined|Determined]] condition to add an Opportunity to a test, you can choose a number of other allies you can influence up to your ranks in **Discipline**. Each target recovers 1 focus (no action required).
