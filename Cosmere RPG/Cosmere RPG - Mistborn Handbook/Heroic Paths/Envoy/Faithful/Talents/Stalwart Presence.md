# Stalwart Presence

**Activation:** **\***

*Your words carry the certainty of your beliefs, bolstering the endurance of even those who don’t share your faith.*

When you use your [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Envoy/Rousing Presence|Rousing Presence]], you can spend 1 focus to increase one of the target’s defenses (your choice) by 2 until the end of the next round.
