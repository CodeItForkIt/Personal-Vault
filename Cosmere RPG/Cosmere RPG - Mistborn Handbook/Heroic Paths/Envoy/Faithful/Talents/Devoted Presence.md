# Devoted Presence

**Activation:** **\***

*Your words fill your compatriots with purpose, helping them shrug off discomfort or surprise.*

When you use your [[Heroic Paths/Envoy/Rousing Presence|Rousing Presence]] on one or more allies, you can spend 1 focus per target to remove any number of the following conditions from them: [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Prone|Prone]], [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Slowed|Slowed]], [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Stunned|Stunned]], and [[Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Surprised|Surprised]].
