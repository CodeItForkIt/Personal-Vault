# Sound Advice

**Activation:** **r**

*You know failure can be an excellent teacher, but only if its origin is understood, so you encourage your allies to grow from their inevitable setbacks.*

After an ally you can influence fails a skill test, you can spend 1 focus to use your [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Envoy/Rousing Presence|Rousing Presence]] on them as r.
