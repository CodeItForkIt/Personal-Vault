# Rallying Shout

**Activation:** **8**

*Your words cut through pain, fatigue, and even unconsciousness, helping your allies regain their feet at critical moments.*

When you use your [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Heroic Paths/Envoy/Rousing Presence|Rousing Presence]], you can target an [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]] ally you can sense. When you do, the target is no longer [[Cosmere RPG/Cosmere RPG - Mistborn Handbook/Mistborn Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Unconscious|Unconscious]], and if they have 0 health, they roll their recovery die and recover health equal to the result + your ranks in **Leadership**.
