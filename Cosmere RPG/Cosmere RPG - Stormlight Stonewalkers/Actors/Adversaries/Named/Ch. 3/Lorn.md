##### Tactics

Lorn is a simple but efficient attacker. They attack with their Shortbow from a distance, chipping away at an unsuspecting caravanner's health as they move in. Once within range, Lorn charges their target to gain Momentum, then ruthlessly debilitates them with their Mace}. After overcoming the civilians (and usually ending their lives), Lorn makes off with their ill-gotten gains.

### Minion
The Lorn's attacks can't critically hit, and they're immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Momentum
If the Lorn moves at least 10 feet in a straight line toward a target then makes a Mace attack against them on that turn, the Lorn gains an advantage on that attack.

### Trip
*1 act*

The Lorn makes an *Test: `skill=ath`* against a target within 5 feet of them, opposed by the target's choice of Athletics or Agility. If the Lorn succeeds on this test, the target is knocked Prone.
