### Nimble
The Mink can move through enemy spaces and can end their turn in the same space as a willing character. While occupying the same space as another character, the Mink can’t be [[Stormlight Handbook/11 - Ch 10 Combat#Step 3: Resolve Damage|Grazed]] and attacks against the Mink gain a [[Stormlight Handbook/01 - Introduction#Adding Additional Dice|Disadvantage]].

### Pickpocket
*1 act*

The Mink makes a *Test: `skill=thv defence=phy`* against the [[Stormlight Handbook/04 - Ch 3 Character Statistics|Physical defense]] of a character within 5 feet of them. On a success, the Mink secretly takes an object from the character that weighs less than 5 pounds and that the character isn’t wearing or holding. On a failure, the Mink doesn’t take an object, and the target notices the attempted theft unless the Mink spends an Opportunity or 1 focus to avoid notice.

### Strike: Bite
*1 act*

**Attack** +4; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 4 Keen average`

### Furtive
Sneaking doesn't cause the Mink to become [[Stormlight Handbook/10 - Ch 9 Adventuring#Slowed|Slowed]].

### Enhanced Senses (smell)
The Mink gains an advantage on non-attack tests that rely on smell.
