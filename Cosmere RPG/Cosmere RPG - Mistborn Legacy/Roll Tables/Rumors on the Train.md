# Rumors on the Train

*Roll: *

| Range | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | A koloss army is gathering in an abandoned Roughs town. They’ll march on Elendel next. (False) |
| 2 | Opposing or competing with Tekiel’s railway expansion brings bad luck—cargo missing, destroyed tracks, and mysterious deaths. (True) |
| 3 | Tekiel trains to the Roughs are being attacked. Constables are covering it up. (True) |
| 4 | Life in the Roughs is worse than ever. The Tekiels ship less and charge more. (True) |
| 5 | A koloss tore open a Tekiel train like tin foil, leaving the grain for the town. Folks call them a hero. (True) |
| 6 | The genius gunsmith of the Last Round Armory in Weathering is making a gun that shoots wooden bullets. (*False*) |
| 7 | The ghost of Bloody Tan, a notorious Roughs killer, haunts Survivorist churches in the Roughs. (*False*) |
| 8 | On a successful DC 10 Perception test, a PC hears an odd noise from the compartment next door (see “[[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|Bayron’s Operative]]”). On a failure, reroll a different rumor. |
