# Unreliable Trap

*Roll: *

| Range | Result |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | The trap works as usual. |
| 3 | The trap hasn’t triggered when the PCs enter the room. Each time a character would trigger it during a scene, roll the plot die; the trap only triggers on C. |
| 4 | The natural environment has changed over the years, and the trap hasn’t triggered when the PCs enter the room. Choose one: The trap is submerged in water, its location has moved up to 15 feet, or it’s now home to four angry **snakes** (see the “Animal Companions” appendix in the *Mistborn Handbook*). |
