# Appendix A: Adversaries

This appendix lists all the adversaries that appear in the *Mistborn Legacy* adventure, presenting them alphabetically.

## Advisor

The need for shrewd advice is common from academia and industry to crime and politics. In any endeavor, a savvy advisor can make the difference between success and failure.

Advisors are specialists in their fields and possess expert knowledge the party can employ. In the Final Empire, advisors are often Steel Ministry officials, Terris stewards, skilled craftspeople, or minor nobility with scholarly interests. In the Basin, advisors might be academics, business consultants, or political aides.

![[pg189_MBL01_Murn_Just Hieu.webp]]

**Murn, former obligator turned Hemalurgist - JUST HIEU**

|  |
|----|
| **Advisor Tactics** |
| Rather than getting involved in a conflict directly, an **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Common Adversaries/Advisor|advisor]]** uses their abilities to support their clients. The advisor’s Timely Advice feature makes them a valuable companion in a conversation, while their At Your Service action can anticipate the needs of their client in combat. Advisors are particularly effective alongside Metalborn clients, enabling the Metalborn to save actions when using Drink Vial to refill their Investiture. Otherwise, advisors spend the remainder of their focus to offer Timely Advice, then Recover to regain focus. |

## Anastas Elariel (era 1)

Anastas Elariel is a Mistborn noble who, despite his talents and position, never developed the reputation expected of his bloodline and house. A lasting injury—sustained during a failed assassination attempt against Elend Venture—has left him with the use of only one hand, shaping both his movement and how others judge his capabilities. He avoids overt displays of Allomancy, presenting himself instead as a courtly figure known for poetry, conversation, and careful manners.

**JUST HIEU**

|  |

| **Anastas Elariel’s Tactics** |
| **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Anastas Elariel|Anastas Elariel's]]** Allomancy manifests in short, forceful bursts under pressure. If attacked, he uses Untrained Tinsight to spoil the strike, instinctively capitalizing on any hit or graze. If surrounded, Anastas uses Untrained Steelpush to disarm his opponents or knock them Prone. He relies on Shall We Dance? to push through exhaustion and to reposition or escape danger at the first opportunity. |

## The Anvil (era 2)

The Anvil is the feared outlaw leader of the Ironbrands, a gang operating out of the Roughs. Born Elanda Wilko, she cast aside her family name after the death of her father, reemerging under her new identity with an axe to grind. Once a lawkeeper herself, Elanda moved from sanctioned authority into criminal leadership, forging a reputation that commands loyalty among her followers. Her ambitions extend beyond banditry, aiming to reshape the Roughs into something independent and enduring.

|  |
|----|
| **The Anvil’s Tactics** |
| **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 7/Elanda The Anvil Wilko|The Anvil]]** uses Burn Iron and her Hand Cannon attack against the most immediate threat. Her Fortress Shield aids her in holding her position while continuing to shoot her Hand Cannon from range. When a metal-bearing enemy advances or attempts to move past her, she uses Ironpull to draw them in, then Strikes with Shield Bash to push them back. ## Ashweather Cett (era 1)

Ashweather Cett is a noble from Fadrex City who has come to Luthadel to press his claim to the throne. He operates openly from Keep Hasting, receiving Assembly members and other political figures in person. Cett wears worn clothing, ignoring the fashions and etiquette of the capital, and he speaks bluntly and with utter confidence. Having lost the use of his legs as a child, he’s carried everywhere on a simple wooden chair by servants.

## Azmine Wilko (era 2)

Azmine Wilko, a koloss-blooded human, is a professor of history at the University of Elendel. She’s known as one of the city’s foremost scholars of pre-Catacendre Scadrial. Wilko maintains extensive personal journals documenting contemporary events, preserving the present to inform the future. She also works closely with independent researchers and treasure hunters who share her goals of gaining and preserving knowledge.

Recently inducted into the Guardians, Wilko makes an invaluable ally to anyone dedicated to the search for truth—and an obvious target for those who’d prefer to rewrite history and claim the future for themselves. For more information about Wilko, see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction.

In addition to founding Conrad Industries, a company involved in weapons development and surveillance technology, Bayron is a member of the Gilded Trio—which has drawn him into even more dangerous ambitions. For more information on Bayron, see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction.

Bayron has two Hemalurgic steel spikes in his torso, which he implanted himself. They give him access to Pewter Allomancy and Steel Allomancy.

## Bezryl (era 1)

Bezryl is a Terris Keeper employed by Kandor Construction. She came to Luthadel seeking information about her missing brother, Fadwal. Bezryl favors honesty, plain speech, and fairness in all her dealings. Her training in architecture and engineering has honed a sharp eye for detail and inconsistencies. A talented Feruchemist with multiple metalminds, Bezryl is well equipped for most situations that come her way.

## Buvidas Twins (era 1)

Silla and Caridus Buvidas are noble siblings, Mistings, and members of the Gilded Few who act at the direction of Delina’s leadership. Full of aristocratic confidence and casual cruelty, they speak to others as inferiors and see defiance as a personal slight.

## Caridus

Caridus Buvidas, a steel Misting, carries himself with restless energy and rarely remains still for long. He rushes through conversation, speaking quickly and interrupting freely, then abandoning it when he loses interest. Caridus is drawn to excess, whether in drink, wagers, or violence, and he grows visibly bored when denied stimulation. In tense moments, he escalates without hesitation, putting on the heat just to see things burn.

## Commoner

The people of Scadrial have endured a world of hardship, revolution, and change after a long period of cultural stagnation. Though forced to suffer, adapt, and repeatedly rebuild in the wake of world-reshaping upheavals, each Scadrian has their own hopes, dreams, and capabilities.

Commoners are the typical citizens found in any city across Scadrial. Called skaa in Era 1 and citizens in Era 2, most commoners simply seek to live their lives. They strive to avoid violence, protect their loved ones, and pursue whatever prosperity they can find.

## Conrad Employees (era 2)

Employees of Conrad Industries serve one of Elendel’s most powerful industrial interests, carrying out the company’s work far beyond factory floors and counting houses. Publicly, Conrad Industries is associated with innovation, protection, and surveillance. Its employees are trained to operate within a rigid internal hierarchy with discipline and discretion. Assignments range from security and field work to spycraft and technical deployments. Conrad Industries places capable personnel wherever they’re needed, extending the company’s reach into the many corners of the Basin while maintaining plausible deniability in case of scandal.

## Freedom Fighter (era 2)

Scadrial’s history is rife with movements so momentous they inspired fiery beliefs. Though these causes vary widely, there are always a few true believers who turn their convictions to impassioned action.

Freedom fighters are willing to fight and bleed for the cause. In the Northern Roughs, the corrupt and cruel business practices of the Tekiel train lines have pushed some to seek radical solutions. Those who do are drawn to the Ironbrands. Led by the Anvil, this group takes action—often violently—to enact their goal of Roughs freedom. They envision a future where the Roughs escape from under the thumb of Elendel’s elites and exists as an independent nation.

## Garrisoner

Garrisoners are skilled, versatile combatants who are deadliest in groups and at range. Whether fighting against a corrupt despot or defending a governor’s power, garrisoners are trained to move and fight as large, formidable groups. Instrumental in both general defense and aggressive warfare, these soldiers can be employed to great effect in protecting important people and holding vital locations—or they can move forward in overwhelming ranks to break the enemy’s lines.

These trained fighters are typically employed by the powerful or by others wealthy enough to deploy large squads. ## Socialite

Whether a product of high birth or fortunate circumstance, socialites ooze wealth and influence wherever they go. A direct fight is hardly the only way to destroy one’s enemies, and a well-connected socialite can make a dangerous enemy indeed.

While many of the games socialites play can be frivolous and unimportant, not all of their concerns are petty ones. Wisely navigating the etiquette and politics of noble houses can mean the difference between a life of comfort and total personal ruin.

## Society Member (era 2)

It’s not uncommon for entitled nobles to gather in secret to pursue goals political, societal, or esoteric. Such is true of the Order of a Thousand Eyes, a fraternal secret society for elite students of the University of Elendel. Membership is determined by bloodline and reinforced by familial spikes, passed down through generations.

Before a pledge becomes a society initiate, they must undergo the Midnight Trials, a demanding competition where only the worthy are fully inducted into the Order and its secrets. Over time, access to knowledge and influence within the Order have tightened, leaving most members aware only of the role they’re expected to play.
