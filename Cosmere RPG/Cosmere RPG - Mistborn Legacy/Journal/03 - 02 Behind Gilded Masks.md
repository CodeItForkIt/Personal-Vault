# 02: Behind Gilded Masks

## Behind Gilded Masks

The Era 1 PCs return to Luthadel with dangerous cargo aboard. As rumors ripple through the city that Straff Venture is plotting a siege, a secret ball offers a chance to learn more about the Eternity Spike, drawing the party perilously close to the Gilded Few.

## Running This Chapter

The plot thickens in chapter 2, as the stakes for the PCs escalate—from just trying to get paid for a job to taking responsibility for the fate of an artifact too powerful to destroy. Freshly rescued from Urteau, Enge stays hidden on the boat (with the Eternity Spike) throughout this chapter.

## Chapter Flow

The party makes the 21-day trip back to Luthadel,during which Enge explains her upbringing, Vadilek, and the Gilded Few. Review “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Engeanthe’s Story]]” and “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling NeBaal|Handling NeBaal]]” in this book’s introduction. Also prepare the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Ball Invitation]]” handout (located in [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]) for the players.

Once in Luthadel, the PCs meet Cadme, Enge’s fixer and long-time friend. Cadme delivers the ball invitation, explaining the PCs must attend a secret masquerade thrown by her client, Guiverre Seeris, to deliver the urn and collect payment.

Enge debriefs the PCs on what to expect at the ball, and the party has another fourteen days of downtime to prepare. Review “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Downtime Before the Ball|Downtime Before the Ball]]” in this chapter and “[[Journal/15 - Appendix E Downtime|Downtime in Luthadel]]” in [[Journal/15 - Appendix E Downtime|appendix E]] to familiarize yourself with the party’s options.

At the ball, the PCs must attempt to make a good impression on Guiverre. Review the “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Running the Ball|Running the Ball]]” section carefully, especially the “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Guest List|Guest List]]” and the [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Event: Rising Reputations|Rising Reputations]] event, which tracks the party’s social status as they dance, debate, and help Cadme resolve a love triangle with Anastas Elariel and Delina Tekiel.

After Delina’s engagement announcement, the PCs are invited to Guiverre’s private study to trade secrets, learn more about the Eternity Spike, and potentially win over Guiverre as an ally. The night ends with the party making a hasty exit through a hedge maze full of hazekillers, fleeing an enraged Delina.

By the end of the chapter, the PCs learn the Gilded Few will never stop pursuing the Eternity Spike, and the Spike itself is indestructible—meaning the party must find an enduring way to keep the artifact safely hidden and out of Gilded hands.

## Character Advancement

The Era 1 characters start this chapter at 2nd level and advance to 3rd level after fleeing from Delina.

PCs can pursue personal goals during downtime. PCs who obtained Vadilek’s Hemalurgy notes (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction) have a chance to progress a “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal.

## Downtime on the Canal

During the uneventful twenty-one-day canal trip from Urteau to Luthadel, the PCs can engage in downtime activities (as described in the “Downtime” section of chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*). If the party retrieved Vadilek’s Hemalurgic texts, they can study them during this downtime (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction). The PCs can also examine the Eternity Spike (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Weight of Eternity|Weight of Eternity]]”) or interact with NPCs on the boat, potentially including Cynwise and the other Urteau refugees.

Enge has information to share with the party (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#A Deadly Graduation|A Deadly Graduation]]”). She also welcomes companionship with the PCs, but she declines any invitations to leave the boat during [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|chapter 2]] (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling NeBaal|Handling NeBaal]]” in this book’s introduction). Enge primarily spends downtime working on her secret project, the unique bold blue mistcloak.

## Weight of Eternity

During the journey, the mists react strangely to the Eternity Spike, occasionally gusting away and staying repelled—leaving the boat oddly exposed in an empty bubble of air. If a PC spends a few hours examining the Eternity Spike, they learn it has the Mist-Repellant and Reactive to Touch traits (see [[Journal/13 - Appendix C Eternity Spike|Eternity Spike]] in appendix C).

## A Deadly Graduation

Enge wants to talk. When the PCs strike up a conversation or on the last night of the voyage (whichever comes first), Enge tells the party the following:

- Enge apologizes for hiding her noble background from the crew. It’s a part of her past she hoped to forget.

- Enge’s uncle, Vadilek, was a noble supremacist who wanted to mentor “the next Lord Ruler.” To achieve this, he pitted his secret cadre of noble Allomancer mentees—the Gilded Few—against each other in tests called the Midnight Trials.

- As a Smoker, Enge’s job was to keep the trials hidden. But when Vadilek came to believe that the Steel Ministry had hidden metallurgic science, he ordered Enge to infiltrate the Ministry and hoped she would become an Inquisitor. She refused, renouncing her title, and left.

- Enge is apologetic for bringing the Gilded Few down on the party, warning the PCs are sure to be hunted from here on.

- Enge wasn’t aware Vadilek eventually became an Inquisitor himself, or that he was planning to torture and kill a thousand people. She was shocked when Vadilek chose her as his heir; his favorite had been Shan Elariel (until Shan died), with Delina Tekiel as his second choice.

## Same Old City

As the canal boat reaches Luthadel, read:

Luthadel towers on the horizon, a city of extremes— skaa and nobles, downtrodden and privileged, hope and despair. Your smuggling boat glides into port and the world continues, unaware of the secret you bear. Everyone thinks the Lord Ruler is gone for good—but with the Eternity Spike, a new one may rise.

The PCs can dock and secure the canal boat. If the party brought the prisoners to Luthadel, Cynwise finds the skaa accommodation in an empty warehouse nearby. The PCs can follow up with them during “Downtime Before the Ball.”

When the PCs are ready to proceed into the city, continue to “A Cordial Invitation."

## Getting Paid: Guiverre’s Ball Invitation

Cadme is pleased to see the silvery urn the PCs smuggled out of Urteau. She explains the next step of their job:

- The buyer is a noble named Guiverre Seeris (he/him), who’s asked the party to deliver the urn personally—at a secret ball he’s holding two weeks from now. He’ll provide the promised payment of 600 boxings.

- The ball circuit stopped during the house war and never resumed under Elend Venture’s rule, so the whole affiair is hush-hush. It’s hosted in Fellise, an upper-class suburb an hour outside Luthadel.

- The covert soiree celebrates the rumored engagement of Delina Tekiel to Anastas Elariel (he/him). Cadme makes a sour face while imparting this (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|Cadme’s Complication]]”).

Give the players the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Ball Invitation]]” handout (from [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]) or read:

Dearest Engeanthe (and Friends),\
You are cordially invited to a secret masquerade at the Seeris Estate in Fellise (details enclosed). I look forward to enjoying your company at the most exclusive and clandestine event of the season.\
Present this invitation to secure admittance. Your entire contingent is welcome, regardless of rank or station. There is no dress code, to suit our new era— but do come wearing a mask.\
Be sure to bring the curio you retrieved from Urteau. Await my summons after the second dancing set concludes, and your reward will be as handsome as I, if not more so!\
Your utmost discretion is appreciated, to avoid unwanted attention. With Great Affection,\
—Lord Guiverre Seeris\
P.S. A little mist-sprite told me you may have brought an unwanted problem back with you from Urteau. If so, I might have some insights to consider.

Cadme will attend alongside the PCs under her noble alias, Sarafine Casuana. She’ll arrange travel to Fellise for the group to deliver the urn.

### Guiverre and Idren: Familiar Names

The PCs may recognize that at Velstane’s in Urteau, Guiverre Seeris was the name circled in the tailor’s ledger. Additionally, the PCs have likely seen the Seeris name elsewhere: A corpse in the Gilded Few’s compound held a letter written to Lord Idren Seeris from his father, beseeching him to come home (see area [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C13: Warehouse|C13]] in chapter 1).

If the party mentions the letter addressed to Idren, Cadme confirms that Guiverre’s son, Idren Seeris, recently died. She doesn’t know the exact circumstances, as the grieving Lord Seeris refuses to speak on the matter.

### Enge’s Insight

After the PCs review the invitation, Enge sighs heavily, saying the invitation is more than it seems. She explains the following:

- If Enge has the party’s blessing, she fills Cadme in on Vadilek, the Gilded Few, and the Eternity Spike, swearing her to secrecy.

- Enge suspects Delina will use the ball to consolidate Gilded Few support among the Luthadel nobility.

- Enge guesses Delina’s fiancé, Anastas Elariel, is either already a member of the Gilded Few or will soon become one, under Delina’s influence.

- Enge is unsure whether Guiverre Seeris is involved with the Gilded Few, but she notes he’s a Seeker, so the party should be careful using Allomancy around him.

### Cadme’s Complication

Cadme is clearly shocked at Enge’s news, and if the PCs inquire, Cadme confesses she’s romantically involved with Anastas, though he only knows her as her alias Sarafine. She knew Anastas was betrothed for political reasons and was studying under Vadilek, but she had no idea about Vadilek’s sinister ambitions.

## Chalk Talk

After Cadme departs, Enge produces a chalkboard and prompts the PCs to plan. She says:

“It’s time to get paid! And I think the famously learned Lord Seeris is offering information. With his fancy-pants esoteric knowledge, he might actually have a solution to our spike issue. But Guiverre is savvy. He won’t risk his neck if we seem like a lost cause. If we make a good impression at the ball, we’ll be in a stronger position to negotiate.\
“Now, Delina and the Gilded Few are sure to be stirring the pot—they can’t murder us on the dance floor, but walking into that ballroom unprepared is like strolling unarmed into an ambush. We have two weeks to prepare, so let’s plan.”

During planning, Enge suggests the following goals if the PCs don’t, writing them on the chalkboard:

**Get paid!** This is non-negotiable. The crew won’t be able to survive or maintain the boat without the 600 boxings.

**Make an impression.** The PCs need to dance, socialize, and come off as competent and strong, or Guiverre and his guests will likely align with Delina.

**Learn what Guiverre knows.** Enge emphasizes that when the PCs meet privately with Guiverre to collect payment for the urn, that’s the perfect opportunity to discuss the Eternity Spike with him.

While the PCs take the carriage to Fellise with Cadme, Enge plans to stay with the Eternity Spike on the canal boat in Luthadel, pointing out that Delina already tried to kill her once. (If the PCs press, refer to “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling NeBaal|Handling NeBaal]]” in this book’s introduction.) She enthusiastically offers to help prepare during downtime, assisting with tasks like upgrading the hideout, crafting outfits, and providing dancing lessons.

**Dancing Lessons.** Enge teaches the PCs to dance.

## Soiree of Secrets

The day of the ball arrives. At the masquerade in Fellise, the PCs must survive two dance sets, make a good impression on Guiverre, and prevent Delina from recruiting more nobles for the Gilded Few.

## Cadme, Carriages, and Contingencies

Cadme arranges a carriage to the Seeris Estate in Fellise, an hour outside of Luthadel. She wears a flowing amethyst gown that suits her round figure and a mask that looks like ravens in flight.

Cadme gives each PC a small black raven whistle (detailed in appendix B) that sounds like a raven’s caw when blown. If they need to leave the ball in a hurry, the whistle is the signal to rendezvous at the front gate.

During the carriage trip, Cadme is distracted and uncharacteristically quiet; if asked, she shares her predicament (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Mist-Crossed Lovers|Mist-Crossed Lovers]]”).

## Arriving at the Ball

The PCs arrive to find the manor grounds dark, surrounded by a makeshift wall topped with broken glass, with hazekillers patrolling the perimeter. The estate appears more prepared for defense than celebration. A guard (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Garrisoner Era 1|garrisoner]]**) greets the PCs and asks for identification. Cadme hands over the urn for delivery to the “gracious host.” After a glance at the ball invitation, the guard opens the doors. Read:

Warmth, light, and music spill out onto the mist-shrouded steps. Guests in glittering finery and extravagant masks mingle behind windows covered with colorful tapestries, concealing the attendees’ mirth from prying eyes.\
Holding court is Lord Seeris, a finely dressed middle-aged man wearing a fox mask. He gesticulates with hands covered in rings. Nearby stands a dark-haired woman, taut and imposing in a gown of gold and white. Her mask is feline—predatory.

As the PCs enter, ask them to describe their outfits and masquerade masks. The PCs can make an entrance and mingle before waiters direct the party to their table.

## Seeris Manor

Seeris Manor includes the following areas:

**Grounds.** The gardens feature a crimson hedge maze and exotic plants with yellow and orange leaves, free of ash. A carriage house and stables stand near the front gate.

**First Floor and Ballroom.** Guests congregate in the grand ballroom. A string orchestra plays while couples glide below an impressive crystal chandelier. The north wall features a mural of fantastical animals. Tables where guests are served food and drink surround the dance floor.

**Second Floor.** The second floor contains empty guestrooms, a modest library, and a balcony overlooking the ballroom.

**Third Floor.** This floor houses bedrooms, Seeris’s private library, and his study—all off limits and guarded by four **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Hazekiller Era 1|hazekillers]]**.

## Guest List

The following notable guests are attending the ball.

### Lord of the Manor: Guiverre Seeris

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Guiverre Seeris|Guiverre Seeris]]** is an ally to the Gilded Few, but his allegiance is tenuous following the sacrifice of his son, Idren (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#A Casual Affair|Roleplaying Guiverre Seeris]]” later in this chapter). Guiverre spends both dance sets socializing and hosting. He doesn’t reveal what he knows to the PCs until their private audience after the second dance set.

### Guest of Honor: Delina Tekiel

**Delina Tekiel** doesn’t approach the PCs, but she may accept a dance invitation. The ball is in her honor, and she’s always surrounded by other eager nobles. Delina is bold and direct, but she withholds information about herself or the Gilded Few, spending focus to resist influence if necessary.

Delina wants to investigate rumors that Enge isn’t dead, reclaim the Eternity Spike, and draw Anastas into the Gilded Few by solidifying their engagement. See “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction for more information on roleplaying Delina.

### Gilded Few

Several young nobles from the Gilded Few number among the guests, including:

- **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Duvall Haught|Duvall Haught]]** (he/him), a Hemalurgy expert angling to be Delina’s second in command (and the main antagonist of [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]]).

- **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Yestal Haught|Yestal Haught]]** (advisor, he/him), friend of Idren and brother to Duvall.

- **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Triss Urbain|Triss Urbain]]** (socialite, she/her), friend of the late Idren Seeris.

- **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 1/Silla Buvidas|Silla Buvidas]]** (she/her) and **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 1/Caridus Buvidas|Caridus Buvidas]]** (he/him), if they survived [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|chapter 1]].

Everyone is under strict orders from Delina to be civil—at least until her engagement is announced—but the Gilded Few are still eager to embarrass the PCs socially (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Mingling|Mingling]]”).

### Other Guests

Other members of high society fill out the ballroom, including Cadme, **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Anastas Elariel|Anastas Elariel]]** (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Mist-Crossed Lovers|Mist-Crossed Lovers]]”), and court gossip **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Kliss Gardre|Kliss]]** (informant, she/her), a short blond woman who zips around the party spreading rumors (see the Rumors table earlier in this chapter). Assembly member Lord **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Haltier Habren|Haltier Habren]]** (socialite, he/him), deeply in the Gilded Few’s pocket, also hovers near Delina.

### Staff and Security

The manor’s servants (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Commoner Era 1|commoners]]**) are mostly skaa, now paid minuscule salaries in compliance with recently enacted labor laws. A fleet of Seeris guards (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Garrisoner Era 1|garrisoners]]**), led by elites (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Hazekiller Era 1|hazekillers]]**), patrol the manor and grounds.

## Event: Rising Reputations

When the PCs arrive at the ball, introduce the Rising Reputations event to the players (see “Events” in chapter 9 of the Mistborn Handbook). This positive event, which has five slots, tracks the PCs’ progress toward earning the respect of Guiverre Seeris and his guests.

![[pg048_Event_Rising-Reputations.webp]]

### Progressing the Event

When a PC gains an Opportunity during the ball, they can spend it to progress the event by 1 if they explain how they improve their own social standing or damagethat of their enemies. In addition, each of the following progresses the event by 1:

- All PCs successfully dressing to impress (see “[[Journal/15 - Appendix E Downtime#Suiting Up|Suiting Up]]” in appendix E).

- Reuniting Cadme and Anastas in “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Mist-Crossed Lovers|Mist-Crossed Lovers.]]”

- Each time a PC dances impressively (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Dancing|Dancing]]”).

- Winning when “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Debating Duvall|Debating Duvall.]]”

- Winning when “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Dueling|Dueling.]]”

If you fill all five event slots, the event is triggered, and the social temperature of the room shifts. The nobles regard the party with interest and envy. Guests begin asking the PCs to dance, and all Presence tests the PCs make gain an advantage for the rest of “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|Soiree of Secrets.]]”

Guiverre, though busy, stops by to comment to the party, “You’re making quite the splash! I’m looking forward to our little business meeting later.” Kliss approaches to compliment the PCs on their outfits; she asks for the inspiration behind them and where they got them.

## Mist-Crossed Lovers

If the PCs approach Cadme, or during phase two of the first dance set (whichever comes first), read:

“There’s Anastas,” Cadme says, blushing subtly. She tips her mask in the direction of a young nobleman dressed in green and gold. He stands on the sidelines, his right arm resting in an emerald-studded sling.

If she hasn’t already, Cadme shares about her romantic involvement with Anastas (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|Cadme’s Complication]]” earlier in this chapter). Cadme believed Anastas hadchosen to follow his family’s wishes and marry Delina…until last night, when he asked Cadme—as Sarafine—to elope. Cadme concludes:

“I panicked and said no! I didn’t want to ruin tonight’s job. And I’m still not sure whether he’s in the Gilded Few.” Eyes on Anastas, Cadme sighs. “But I love him. Or… I think I do! But… what do I know—I’ve been lying to him for a year. He doesn’t even know my real name. But if I do nothing, he’ll end up married to Delina, a murderous cult leader!”

### Running the Endeavor

If the PCs agree to help, run the attempt to reunite Cadme and Anastas as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”) that spans both dance sets. During phase two of each dance set (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Running the Ball|Running the Ball]]”), each participating PC can make two or three contributions toward this endeavor. By the end of the second dance set, the PCs must complete the three following objectives by obtaining at least 2 successes per objective:

**Confirm Anastas’s Loyalties.** It takes persuasive arguments (DC 15 Persuasion) to convince Anastas to talk. Alternatively, the PCs might gather this information elsewhere at the ball. Trading rumors with notorious gossip Kliss—or buying a rumor for 25 boxings—also yields useful information. After two successes, the PCs learn the full truth (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|Anastas’s Allegiance]]”).

**Answer Anastas’s Concerns.** If the PCs don’t reveal Cadme’s true identity, Anastas is crushed, wondering why Sarafine rejected him—was it his failure as a Mistborn? If the PCs do reveal Cadme’s true identity, Anastas is shaken—was the whole relationship just an act? The party must provide some sort of answer to address Anastas’s concerns and make a relevant test. They might help keep Cadme’s secret (DC 14 Deception), explain her love is pure no matter her name (DC 14 Persuasion), or try to empathize with Anastas’s feelings (DC 14 Insight). Arguments appealing to Anastas’s romanticism (DC 13 Insight or Lore) gain an advantage, but answers that make Anastas feel intimidated or pitied for his injury automatically fail. This is a good place to raise the stakes.

**Cadme’s Secret Proposal.** For this objective, the PCs must find the perfect location for a romantic proposal by scouting the manor (DC 12 Insight or DC 11 Survival) or asking around (DC 14 Leadership); the earlier “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Seeris Manor|Seeris Manor]]” section lists options the PCs can choose from. With a location chosen, the PCs can help Cadme stage the proposal. She could use help ducking out of the party (DC 13 Stealth or Deception) and making the proposal feel special (DC 12 Leadership, Lore, or Crafting). The PCs can also give Cadme advice or help keep her calm (DC 13 Insight).

### Trading Tidbits: Boxing for Boxing

Guiverre suggests an old party game: “Boxing for Boxing,” inviting each PC to ask one question, provided they first answer a question in exchange. He explains the rules. Each PC takes one turn, proceeding as follows:

1.  The PC asks a question.

2.  Guiverre asks a question.

3.  The PC chooses to answer or drink.

4.  Guiverre chooses to answer or drink.

A PC can lie or mislead Guiverre (DC 15 Deception), or they can answer truthfully and try to win him over (DC 13 Persuasion). Each PC gains an advantage on this test if the party triggered the Rising Reputations event. Attempts to deceive Guiverre are good places to raise the stakes.

### Special Rule: Closing the Distance

As the PCs and Cadme (and possibly Anastas) flee in their carriage, Delina chases them; run this as a Pursuit endeavor (see “Pursuit Endeavors” in chapter 12 of the *Mistborn Handbook*). Instead of tracking successes and failures, track the distance between pursuer andtarget. For this Pursuit, the starting distance is 3, and the escape distance is 6. Each time the PCs succeed on a test, the distance increases by 1. Each time Delina succeeds on a test, the distance decreases by 1.

If the current distance reaches 6 (the escape distance), the party gets away. If the current distance reaches 0, Delina closes with them (see “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”).

### Evading the Hunter

Delina employs expert tracking techniques and a skillful command of Steel and Iron Allomancy to hunt the PCs as they attempt to flee back to Luthadel in their carriage. To escape Delina, the PCs must overcome the following obstacles:

**The Gate.** The PCs can argue or threaten the gate guards (Intimidation vs. her Leadership +7) or blend in with other departing carriages (Thievery vs. her Insight +6).

**Fellise Suburbs.** Once past the gate, the PCs roll through the sleepy cobblestone streets of Fellise. The PCs might try clever navigation (Survival vs. her Perception +6) or convincing a noble acquaintance to briefly hide them (Persuasion vs. her Intimidation +6).

**Road to Luthadel.** The party’s carriage races along the dark road to Luthadel as Delina flies behind, using the nearby Allomantic roadway. They can go as fast as possible (Agility vs. her Allomancy +7) or create a desperate distraction (Deception vs. her Insight +6). This is a good place to raise the stakes.

## Journeying Onward

After this chapter, the Era 1 PCs advance to 3rd level.

As the chapter closes, the PCs are left with a conundrum: What should they do with the Eternity Spike? Having learned they can’t destroy it, and aware the Gilded Few will never stop coming after it, they must devise a solution to keep the dangerous artifact from falling into Delina’s hands.

The PCs need new allies and a plan to keep the Eternity Spike safe—and they can acquire both in [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]].
