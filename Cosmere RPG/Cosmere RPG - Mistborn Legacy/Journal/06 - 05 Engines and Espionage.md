# 05: Engines and Espionage

## Engines and Espionage

T hree centuries after the fall of the Lord Ruler, the Era 2 arc of Mistborn Legacy opens. Scadrial has transformed from a world of ash and mist into an industrial marvel, powered by engines and gunpowder. As the eras change, so does our cast. Era 2 introduces a new party of heroes—and a new Gilded threat—as Vadilek’s bloodstained legacy ripples forward through time.

## Running This Chapter

If you haven’t already, have your players make their Era 2 characters; these characters start at 3rd level and follow the instructions in the “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Creating Characters]]” section of this book’s introduction.

Despite the vast societal, technological, and economic changes Scadrial has seen over the past three centuries, the nobility’s excesses still endure. In this chapter, the new Era 2 party steals a piece of that privilege back— via screeching tires and a very public heist.

## Chapter Flow

Before starting, print or prepare the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|National Archives Heist Map]]” handout (see [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]). Either print and cut out the “Race Cars” (found later in this chapter) or gather nine tokens, then print or prepare the “Race Position Tracker.”

The PCs begin in the midst of an illegal motorcar race celebrating Lysarra Tekiel’s birthday. The race is hosted by ambitious young industrialist Bayron Conrad, and Kwylliam Elariel is the favorite to win. These three NPCs make up the Gilded Trio, the main antagonists of Era 2, so be sure to review their sections under “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction.

During a mid-race flashback, the PCs play out a scene from the day before, in which they meet their longtime co-conspirator, Professor Azmine Wilko (also detailed in “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]”). Kwylliam has bribed the National Archives to redirect a crate of artifacts meant for Wilko, so Wilko enlists the PCs to steal the crate before Kwylliam can take it.

The first phase of the heist is the motorcar race. The top three racers earn access to Lysarra’s exclusive birthday celebration, an after-party at the National Archives. There, the PCs attempt to steal an Archives badge from Kwylliam.

Next, the PCs infiltrate the Archives’ lower level to recover the crate—and a negative event tracks whether the PCs evade Archives security or trigger a lockdown. The PCs discover the crate has been redirected to a heavily alarmed vault, adding a final wrinkle to the party’s plan.

After grabbing the crate, the PCs flee the Archives with guards in hot pursuit, all culminating in a battle against Eliane Vorn, a relentless Twinborn security captain. The PCs’ successes during the Archives heist determine how long they must hold out against the constables.

With a final flashback scene, the PCs narrate the terms of their cinematic escape plan, then the chapter concludes with delivering the crate to Wilko. The crate contains a “[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Founders Key|Founder’s Key]]” and a masquerade mask—the first piece of the so-called Gilded Map, which is later revealed to be NeBaal’s map from [[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault|chapter 4]].

## Wilko’s Secrets

Unbeknownst to the PCs, Wilko is a newly initiated member of the covert Guardians organization, an era-spanning secret society tasked with keeping the Eternity Spike safe (see [[Journal/16 - Appendix F The Guardians|appendix F]]). Wilko doesn’t know much more than the PCs—and for good reason. Wilko is a great academic, but she’s a terrible liar. Charged with acquiring the mask and key, Wilko quickly reveals more to the PCs than she should, just by admitting these items exist (see the upcoming “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|How Did We Get Here?]]” section).

If the PCs press Wilko for more information during this chapter, she quickly crumbles, sharing that the mask and key have something to do with the Gilded Map, which she believes is an ancient, secret treasure hunt. She knows she absolutely must keep the mask and key away from people like Kwylliam.

There are a few things she won’t divulge: Though concealing it visibly pains her, Wilko staunchly refuses to name the Guardians or confess her membership in any sort of secret organization (at least during [[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|chapter 5]]).

## Character Advancement

The Era 2 characters start this adventure at 3rd level and advance to 4th level upon reaching Wilko’s office after the heist.

PCs can start a “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal after finding the Era 1 party’s Hemalurgy notes in crate 38.4B (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction). They can progress (and potentially complete) this goal during downtime in [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|chapter 6]].

## Rev Your Engines

This chapter starts in the midst of the action, with the PCs already in their motorcar, alongside eight other racers, all waiting for the starting gun. Motorcar racing through the streets of Elendel is illegal, but tonight, Bayron has bought off the constables.

## Starting Line

To begin the adventure, read the following:

It’s a crisp spring night, and the air reeks of oil, gasoline, and anticipation. Somewhere across the steel metropolis of Elendel, a clocktower strikes eleven, muffled beneath the growl of surrounding motorcars.\
To your right, lounging in a polished black racer, Kwylliam Elariel surveys the competition. The favorite to win the race, he wears a casual smirk, his sardonic eyes hidden behind mirrored goggles. Something golden glints on his lapel—something important.\
Three… Your motorcar rumbles eagerly… Two… Keep focus on your mark, the reason you’re here… One…

Have the players decide which PC is driving and where the rest begin in the vehicle. During the race, the driver makes skill tests to maneuver the motorcar while passengers take actions to support the driver. PCs can assist by navigating, shooting out tires, hindering rivals via Metalborn abilities, and even hurling well-timed insults.

### Driving

Each round, the driver must contribute by making a test directly related to driving. At the start of each round, ask the players if they want to swap drivers. If so, the driver spends 1 focus and swaps positions with another PC. Raise the stakes for tests made by the new driver that round. If the car doesn’t have a driver, each PC gains a disadvantage on tests during that round.

## Floor It

This scene covers the first half of the race, in which the party clashes with three rival racers: [[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Bayron Conrad|Bayron Conrad]] (he/him), then **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Lysarra Tekiel|Lysarra Tekiel ]]**(she/her), and finally **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Kwylliam Elariel|Kwylliam Elariel]]** (he/him). Run this scene as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). Each rival serves as an obstacle, which the PCs must overcome by making two successful tests to hold position in the race.

On a failure, describe how the rival leaves the party in the dust, and move to the next rival.

![[pg094_MBL05_Gilded Trio_Jordan Kerbow.webp]]

**Bayron, Kwylliam, and Lysarra prepare for the race. - JORDAN KERBOW**

### Bayron’s Assault

As the race begins, read:

At the starting gun, screeching tires and smoke fill the air. To your left, Bayron Conrad—industrialist, inventor, and the race’s organizer—roars forward in a steel-plated motorcar with a deep-green riveted hull.\
Bayron’s voice crackles through a loudspeaker bolted to his hood: “That’s what you’re driving? That model has an embarrassing design flaw. Hope you reinforced your axles!” Bayron’s heavy vehicle aggressively swerves toward yours. He shouts, “Boldness builds tomorrow!”

The party must either evade or withstand Bayron’s assault. They could shout directions to the driver to outmaneuver Bayron (DC 12 Leadership), brace for impact (DC 14 Athletics), or identify hairpin turns too tight for Bayron’s larger vehicle (DC 15 Deduction).

After 2 successes or 1 failure, proceed to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|Lysarra’s Crossing.]]”

### Lysarra’s Crossing

After the PCs navigate Bayron’s obstacle, they face Lyssara. Read:

Lysarra Tekiel’s sleek silver motorcar slices toward the head of the pack. This race is in her honor, and she maneuvers with cunning precision. Famous for her philanthropy, sharp wits, and ambition, the Tekiel heiress is also formidable behind the wheel.\
Ahead, the street tightens, multistory buildings closing in on both sides. The straightaway is bisected by a rail crossing, and the rumble of an approaching train grows loud. At the last second, Lysarra guns it. The train’s whistle blares as the Tekiel-stamped crossing gates begin descending, perfectly timed to close right behind Lysarra—and in front of you.

Lysarra’s maneuver perfectly traps the party on the wrong side of the tracks. The PCs could search for alternate routes (DC 14 Survival), accelerate before the gates slam down (DC 16 Agility), or distract Lysarra with a jeer about the Tekiels’ failing business (DC 16 Lore), allowing the party to overtake her.

After 2 successes or 1 failure, proceed to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|Kwylliam’s Riot.]]”

### Kwylliam’s Riot

After the PCs navigate Lysarra’s obstacle, they face Kwylliam. Read:

Kwylliam Elariel doesn’t race—he performs. His leisurely pace out in first place suggests he’s already won, and he’s just waiting for everyone else to realize it.\
At a crowded intersection, Kwylliam glances over his shoulder, lazily sipping from a silver flask. An instant later, chaos erupts. Shouts rise as spectators and bystanders boil into the street, jostling and screaming under the effects of Kwylliam’s Rioting.

To navigate the crowd of panicked pedestrians, PCs could counter with their own emotional Allomancy, intuit the flow of the crowd to find a safe path (DC 15 Insight), or command the masses to move with a booming shout (DC 16 Leadership).

After 2 successes or 1 failure, proceed to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor.]]”

### Resolving the Endeavor

Instead of this endeavor succeeding or failing, note down how many failures the PCs accrued. This number determines the party’s starting position in the second half of the race (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|Motorcar Chaos]]”).

## How Did We Get Here?

This flashback scene takes place in the office of Professor **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Professor Azmine Wilko|Azmine Wilko]]** (she/her) the day before the race. For tips on roleplaying Wilko, see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction.

To start the flashback, read:

Sunlight pours across Professor Wilko’s desk, strewn with maps and manifests. Wilko wears a tweed suit and a thoughtful frown as she pages through a folder stamped with the House Elariel seal.\
“It should’ve been here by now, but apparently Kwylliam Elariel ‘donated’ an absurd sum to the Archives’ Board of Directors. My contact, Wynn, says the crate’s now been ‘redirected’—moved to a shipment headed for the Elariel estate in just two days. Kwylliam must be stopped. Now.”\
Wilko’s intense gaze fixes on you—her colleagues and friends. “I need your help. I know you excel at ‘liberating’ Final Empire artifacts, and this crate needs liberating.”

Ask each player to describe their character’s appearance and what they’re known for in the group. Once they’ve finished, read:

“You’ll be stealing a crate from the National Archives: crate number 38.4B. It was entrusted to me, and I need it safe—before Kwylliam redirects it right onto his own doorstep.” She passes you a hand-drawn map.

Wilko’s sketch of the National Archives’ lower levels was drawn from memory. Give the players the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|National Archives Heist Map]]” handout (from [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]), then read:

“This is the Archives’ lower level. The crate was redirected to the estate transfer room. First, you’ll need to get into the Archives, and then you’ll need a badge to open the doors on the lower level. Normally, this would be quite difficult.”\
The professor offers a conspiratorial smile. “Fortunately, there’s an illegal motorcar street race tomorrow. If you place third or better in that race, you’ll secure an invitation to the after-party at the Archives— and a chance to steal Kwylliam’s badge.”

Wilko invites the PCs to ask questions, such as:

**What’s in the crate?** “Full contents? I’m… Well, um. I’m not… entirely sure.” She hesitates for a moment, clearly conflicted, then continues quickly, “But I do know two items are vital: a mask and a key. I’m hoping I can research more once you retrieve the crate.” If the PCs press for more information, refer to “Wilko’s Secrets” in this chapter’s introduction.

**How do we get access?** “A board member’s badge will open the Archives’ locked doors, so you can avoid setting off the alarms. The most accessible badge is pinned to Kwylliam Elariel’s lapel. Take it, and you can get into the estate transfer room on the lower level.”

**How does the race work?** “Only the top three in the race get invited to the after-party in the Archives. While competition is encouraged, lethal violence is obviously unacceptable. I’m covering your entry fee—but you’ll have to provide the motorcar yourselves.”

**What else should we know?** “Kwylliam is never alone. He, Bayron Conrad, and Lysarra Tekiel are an inseparable trio. Trust me, I knew them at University: Tangle with one, face all three.”

When the PCs are done with questions, Wilko ends the discussion by asking, “What’s the plan?”

## Laying the Groundwork

Ask each PC to describe one way they contributed to the plan then make a relevant skill test. Possible contributions include:

**Secure a Vehicle.** Acquire a motorcar by any means necessary (DC 14 Thievery).

**Scout the Route.** Study the race route for shortcuts and dangers (DC 13 Deduction or DC 14 Survival).

**Mark the Mark.** Review Kwylliam’s habits or spy on Bayron or Lysarra (DC 15 Insight or DC 16 Stealth).

**Rig a Surprise.** Modify the motorcar with a one-shot gadget (DC 14 Crafting).

For each success, propose a benefi t the PC can apply once (and only once) during the “Motorcar Chaos” scene, such as:

**Vehicle Modification.** If the PC can explain how the modification they’ve made to the motorcar helps, treat one failed test as a success.

**Prior Knowledge.** Use gathered intel on the race route to ignore one Complication.

**Helping Hand.** Have a contact interfere with the race, preventing a rival from taking one of their actions.

**Driver Training.** After moving up 1 position, make an additional test with a different skill to attempt another advance immediately.

## Motorcar Chaos

With preparations made, the spotlight returns to the present, and the race’s second half. As the PCs compete for a place in the top three, Bayron and Lysarra actively attempt to take the PCs out, while Kwylliam—ever overconfident—coasts smugly in first place. Run this street race (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Running the Race|Running the Race]]”) as a special endeavor, outlined here.

## Starting Positions

Prepare the “Race Position Tracker” and “Race Cars” (or gather 9 tokens). Keep track of racers’ relative standings as they move around on the position track from 9th (last place) to 1st (leading). During the race, when a racer moves forward to a new position, the racer in that position moves back one, swapping places with them. The party’s starting position depends on howmany failures they accrued during “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Floor It|Floor It.]]”

## Special Rule: Other Racers

In addition to the Gilded Trio and the party, five other drivers fill out the track. These racers don’t take independent turns; instead, they move up and down the tracker in relation to the party’s vehicle.

Once all the racers are placed on the tracker, read:

That sunlit memory fades back into the roar of engines and the smell of burning rubber. Far ahead, Kwylliam extends his lead, untouchable. But you’ve got more immediate problems: Uncomfortably close on your left, Bayron’s armored, double-wide monstrosity barrels forward, while ahead on your right, Lysarra’s sleek silver racer maneuvers with ruthless precision.

## Special Rule: Rival Actions

At the start of each round, Bayron Conrad and Lysarra Tekiel each take one of their rival actions (detailed in this section).

If a rival action causes the PCs to move back 1 position, the driver can spend 2 focus to redirect the effect onto the nearest NPC racer, removing them from the race. This doesn’t use the PC’s contribution for the round.

Bayron and Lysarra can also spend 2 focus when a PC action would impact them, negating the effect and removing the closest NPC driver instead.

### Bayron’s Rival Actions

**Bayron** has a Cognitive defense of 17, a Spiritual defense of 16, 5 focus, and the following rival actions:

**Roadblock Reinforcement.** Bayron makes a hand signal and an unnamed NPC racer reveals themself as a Bayron plant. Move the racer to the position behind Bayron, who can redirect any action’s effects onto this driver for free.

**Fire in the Hole.** Bayron launches a flash grenade from his vehicle. Each PC must make a DC 13 Discipline test. If the driver fails, the PCs move back 1 position. Each non-driver PC who fails is Disoriented until the end of their next turn.

**Steelpush.** Bayron Pushes against the metal grip on the party’s steering wheel. The driver must succeed on a DC 15 Athletics test or the PCs move back 1 position.

### Lysarra’s Rival Actions

**Lysarra** has a Cognitive defense of 18, a Spiritual defense of 18, 6 focus, and the following rival actions:

**Zinc Shot.** With zinc-fueled calculations, Lysarra tilts her mirror and lands an impossible pistol shot. The driver must succeed on a DC 15 Agility test or their car is damaged, causing the driver’s tests to gain a disadvantage until the issue is fixed with a successful DC 13 Crafting test.

**Perfect Drift.** A flawless drift through a sharp turn forces racers to slow, change course, or risk collision. The driver must succeed on a DC 14 Deduction test or the PCs move back 1 position.

**Cutting the Line.** Lysarra darts into a narrow alley, suddenly reemerging ahead. The driver must succeed on a DC 15 Perception test or the PCs move back 1 position; Lysarra can optionally take the party’s previous position.

## Running the Race

![[pg099_Map_Motorcar Chaos Race Route_Isaac Stewart copy.webp]]

**Motorcar chaos race route - ISAAC STEWART**

The race lasts three rounds. Each round, Bayron and Lysarra take a new rival action, then each PC makes a contribution to improve the party’s position in the race. Each success moves the party up 1 position (if they’re not already in first place), and each failure moves them back 1 position. At the end of round three, proceed to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Across the Finish Line|Across the Finish Line.]]”

Potential obstacles the PCs may face include:

**Packed Traffic.** Packed traffic makes it difficult to gain any ground. A PC driver could swerve between two vehicles (DC 15 Agility), Tap Zinc to calculate split-second positioning, or scan ahead for openings (DC 15 Perception).

**Skilled Competitors.** An aggressive racer tries to sideswipe the party’s car. A PC might toss loose items into the road (DC 14 Athletics), shoot a tire or other vulnerable spot (DC 14 Light Weaponry), or taunt a driver into making a critical error (DC 15 Intimidation).

**Damage to the Motorcar.** Another racer or the party’s own risky driving damages their car. A PC could tighten the steering linkage (DC 14 Crafting), patch a leaking line (DC 15 Thievery), or hold a malfunctioning part in place with brute strength (DC 15 Athletics).

## Across the Finish Line

After three rounds, the race is complete. The outcome depends on the PCs’ position on the tracker as the race ends:

**First Through Third Place:** Victory. Smiling, Kwylliam invites the PCs to the after-party: “I suppose we’ll see you at the party later? You’re a fun bunch. If you treat relics the way you treat the road, the Archives won’t survive the night.” Proceed to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|High Society Heist.]]”

**Fourth Through Ninth Place:** Defeat. If the party fails to place within the top three, they aren’t invited to the after-party and must find another way into the National Archives. Continue to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Invitationless Infiltration|Invitationless Infiltration.]]”

## Invitationless Infiltration

Without an invitation, the PCs must find a creative way into the party, and stealing the medal becomes more difficult. The PCs could try the following:

**Front Doors.** Thanks to security, the PCs can’t go through the front doors without succeeding on a difficult DC 22 Stealth test; failure gets them escorted right back outside.

**Staff Entrance.** It’s possible to enter through a side door with a successful test (DC 18 Stealth or Deception), but the PCs also need to dress up as waitstaff to traverse the crowded staff’s area to reach the main gallery. While the PCs are dressed as staff, every social test during “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|High Society Heist]]” gains a disadvantage, until the PCs can find a chance to change into party-appropriate clothes.

**Wilko’s Contingency Plan.** If all else fails, an archivist in green robes shows up to help; this is Professor Wilko’s contact, **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 5/Archivist Wynn|Archivist Wynn Letrelle]]** (advisor, they/them). Wynn eventually gets the PCs inside, but the group shows up late, attracting unwanted attention from the Archives’ security captain (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Interception: Security Captain Vorn|Interception: Security Captain Vorn]]”).

With entry secured, proceed to “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|High Society Heist.]]”

## High Society Heist

With access secured, the PCs must infiltrate the Archives’ lower levels, locate crate 38.4B, and escape. Stealing Kwylliam’s badge isn’t mandatory, but it makes the heist much easier. Refer to map 5.1 for the layout of the Archives’ ground floor. The PCs have ample time to interact with the gallery’s guests and exhibits in area G1 before proceeding with the job (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Head to the Heist|Head to the Heist]]”).

As the party enters, read:

Stone columns tower to the National Archives’ ceiling, giving the string quartet’s notes a cavernous sound. Elendel’s elite sip cocktails and take in the current exhibit, Stories From the Siege, open to Lysarra’s esteemed birthday guests.\
The woman of the hour, radiant in green silk and furs, stands by a display of busts. Young nobles surround her, hanging on her words. Nearby is a tiered chocolate fountain centerpiece complete with a Malwish attendant.\
Meanwhile, Bayron stands alone, writing in a notebook, and Kwylliam’s laughter can be heard as he poses on an exhibit, to the visible chagrin of a nearby archivist. The golden badge glints on his lapel.

The party serves as a chance for the PCs to meet the Gilded Trio and inspect the main exhibit’s artifacts.

## Interception: Security Captain Vorn

The Archives’ impressive security force is led by **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 5/Captain Eliane Vorn|Captain Eliane Vorn]]** (she/her), who wears her slate-gray duster with an air of understated menace and authority. She shadows Lysarra.

If the PCs used Wynn’s help to get into the Archives (during “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Invitationless Infiltration|Invitationless Infiltration]]”), or if they fail a test while engaging in any suspicious behavior, they catch Vorn’s eye immediately. She approaches, questioning the party about who they are and what they’re doing here. If the PCs name-drop Wynn, a legitimate Archives employee, this is enough to satisfy Vorn, or the PCs can succeed on a DC 18 Deception test to talk their way out. Vorn is eager to get back to Lysarra and eventually lets the PCs off with a warning either way—but if she’s unsatisfied or if the PCs mention Wilko, Vorn warns her security guards to keep a close eye on the party: As a result, PCs gain a disadvantage on Stealth and Thievery tests in the gallery area ([[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#G1: Gallery of the Past|G1]]).

### Exhibit: Stories From the Siege

The Archives’ current exhibit—Stories From the Siege: Tragedy and Triumph—features “historical items and portraits from a time of hope, tragedy, and political upheaval mere years before the Catacendre,” including:

- Fragments from Steel Gate, the last gate to fall during the Battle of Luthadel.

- Handmade pins showing a handprint, attributed to a skaa workers’ movement.

- Chipped busts of Shan Elariel, Delina Tekiel, Yestal Haught, Idren Seeris, Triss Urbain, Duvall Haught, Silla Buvidas, and Caridus Buvidas.

- Early proposals and writings from Luthadel’s Assembly.

- One of Lord Cett’s chairs.

- An exhibit on Luthadel’s skaa “crews,” displaying illegal metallurgy equipment, noble impersonation garb, faded false papers and arrest warrants, and a sketch of the Era 1 PCs, with Enge circled repeatedly in dark ink. (This is the sketch from the Secret Crucible’s study in [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|chapter 1]]). Below the sketch, a placard titled “Engeanthe Elariel and Crew” describes her as a noble-turned-smuggler who became a folk hero.

- A reconstructed five-panel stained-glass window showing the dominances of the Final Empire, captioned “Fellise Noble’s Window.”

- An obsidian axe, chipped in several places, owner unknown.

If the PCs examine the sketch of the Era 1 party, invite the players to describe what their characters know about these historical figures, based on PC legacies and personal connections.

### Fountain Gossip

## Stealing the Badge

To steal the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/National Archives Badge Era 2|National Archives badge]]*** (detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]), the party can attempt to distract Kwylliam with a conversation (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Resolving the Conversation|Resolving the Conversation]]”).

If the party placed third or higher in the race, Kwylliam is intrigued by the newcomers who upstaged his friends, welcoming conversation.

If not, he’s bemused to see the PCs at the party, becoming instantly suspicious. He might idly threaten to have Vorn throw them out, but he’s happy to keep talking as long as the party-crashers keep his interest. This attention makes stealing the badge harder: All tests to distract him or take the badge gain a disadvantage.

### Resisting Influence

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Kwylliam Elariel|Kwylliam]]** has a Cognitive defense of 16, a Spiritual defense of 19, and 6 focus.

He spends focus to resist interactions he finds dull or predictable. He’s easily amused by shocking, provocative, or absurd behavior, and he won’t resist influence when the PCs create chaos or disruption, fully entertained by these diversions.

### Conversation Approaches

The party must distract Kwylliam to steal the badge (see “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Special Rule: Attempted Theft|Special Rule: Attempted Theft]]”). They might discuss the following:

**Courting Chaos.** Kwylliam, a broadsheet mogul, craves intrigue, impropriety, and upheaval, and has a nose for gossip. Successfully spin a tale of scandal or hint at an explosive rumor, and he’s rapt. If it’s too tame or obviously fabricated, he declares himself bored and loses interest.

**Risk and Reward.** Kwylliam loves a risky move, particularly if it has shock value and might play well in the papers. If the PCs successfully pull off a reckless stunt or propose a dangerous game, he buys in immediately. This is a good place to raise the stakes.

**Connections.** If the party asks about his friends, he only shares his thoughts after the PCs spill dirt or impress him. If they do, he shares that Lysarra is audacious; only a few years ago her family was in financial ruin and a laughingstock. He also observes that Bayron is too smart for his own good, and Kwylliam likes pushing his buttons.

Pay close attention to what the PCs say to Kwylliam. Anything incriminating they tell him now could appear again later—published in a broadsheet in [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|chapter 6]].

### Special Rule: Attempted Theft

During the conversation, a PC can use their contribution to attempt to steal Kwylliam’s badge by making a DC 15 Thievery test. How advantageous the attempt is and what happens on a failure depend on how entertained Kwylliam is:

**While Kwylliam is entertained** (or if he no longer has enough focus to resist), each attempt to steal the badge gains an advantage, and he doesn’t notice the first two failed attempts. Each time the PCs fail, he must be entertained anew.

**While Kwylliam isn’t entertained**, each attempt to steal the badge gains a disadvantage, and he immediately notices the first failed attempt.

On a successful test, a PC steals Kwylliam’s badge without him noticing—but if Kwylliam notices a failure, the conversation is over.

### Resolving the Conversation

The conversation ends once a PC successfully steals the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/National Archives Badge Era 2|National Archives badge]]***, or once Kwylliam notices a failed attempt.

**Success.** The PCs cleanly steal the badge, and Kwylliam is none the wiser.

**Failure.** Kwylliam catches the thief’s hand, saying they don’t know each other well enough for that. After a mocking laugh, he casually calls Vorn over (see the earlier “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Interception: Security Captain Vorn|Interception: Security Captain Vorn]]” section), then leaves while Vorn harasses the party for several minutes. No further attempts to steal the badge can be made.

Either way, the PCs can continue to explore the party, or proceed to “Head to the Heist” to continue the mission.

## Event: Archives Lockdown

Archives Lockdown is a negative event (see “Events” in chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*) with four slots representing escalating suspicion. The event continues until triggered, or until the party exits the Archives. Raise the stakes liberally throughout this event.

![[pg104_Event_Archives-Lockdown.webp]]

### Progressing the Event

Immediately progress the Archives Lockdown event by 1 for each of the following:

- If the party entered the National Archives without winning the race.

- If the party failed the “[[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#Stealing the Badge|Stealing the Badge]]” conversation with Kwylliam or otherwise triggered “Interception: Security Captain Vorn.”

- If the party received a warning from security at the checkpoint ([[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage#G2: Gateway to the Heart|G2]]).

When a PC gains a Complication during the heist, you can choose to spend it to progress the event by 1, trading an immediate Complication for a potential lockdown.

### Triggering the Event

When you fill all four event slots, the event triggers and the National Archives immediately enters high alert. Read:

A shrill alarm bell rings. Nearby, you hear shouting: “Security alert in effect. All non-essential personnel, remain in place. Archives security teams are assessing the situation.”

All the doors on the lower level lock, and alarms sound on every level of the Archives. Social tests with archivists automatically fail and all Stealth tests gain a disadvantage. Additionally, four Archives security guards (use the **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Constable Era 2|constable]]** stat block) and their **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Lawkeeper Era 2|lawkeeper]]** chief intercept the party, attempting an arrest. They retreat if reduced to half health.

## L4: Vault 1616

### Getting Inside the Vault

If the PCs have Kwylliam’s National Archives badge, as they insert it and begin to open the door, have each PC make a Perception test; the PC with the highest test result notices an alarm mechanism just in time. A PC can disarm it with a successful DC 18 Thievery or Crafting test. Failure sets off an alarm and immediately triggers the Archives Lockdown event, but a success means the PCs have gained access to the vault without tripping the vault door alarm—no easy feat.
