# Appendix C: Eternity Spike

## The Eternity Spike

The Eternity Spike anchors the story of *Mistborn Legacy* in both eras. Created through a process involving the torture and murder of over a thousand people, the Eternity Spike is a heavily Invested Hemalurgic spike made of nicrosil. Vadilek Elariel (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Adventure Background|Adventure Background]]” in this book’s introduction) aspired to use the Spike to empower a worthy successor for the Lord Ruler, fathering a legacy of ambition, power, and secrecy destined to echo across centuries.

### Constructing a God

The Eternity Spike was intended to be used with a “latticework” of supporting spikes, created from sacrificing Allomancers and Feruchemists. Vadilek’s original latticework includes at least eight spikes: a combination of Vadilek’s own Steel Inquisitor spikes, and spikes born from Vadilek’s own secret cadre of students, the Gilded Few, who willingly sacrificed themselves for their mentor’s vision. In Era 2, the descendants of the original Gilded Few secretly carry these “familial” spikes, including Vadilek’s spikes and others created later.

#### **[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Eternity Spike GM ONLY|Eternity Spike]]**

*Special Equipment, 1 lb.*

This silvery-gray spike is about as long as an adult human’s hand. The shaft of the spike is formed in a pattern of twisted striations, and its head is a polygon with 16 facets. The Eternity Spike is made of nicrosil, supercharged with Investiture harvested from more than a thousand victims.

**Unique Traits.** The Eternity Spike has the following unique traits:

**Indestructible.** No known means in Era 1 or Era 2 can destroy the Eternity Spike. The sheer amount of Investiture in the Spike makes it nigh invulnerable; it can’t be bent, scratched, melted, or otherwise harmed.

**Highly Invested.** Because of the amount of Investiture stored within the Eternity Spike, it doesn’t function like normal Hemalurgic spikes and doesn’t lose power outside of a storage medium (such as blood).

**Mist-Repellant.** The Scadrian mists are repelled by the Eternity Spike.

**Reactive to Touch.** When touched with bare skin, the Spike sends out an Allomantic pulse. The wielder and each adjacent character feel a perceptible wave of energy, which can be detected without a test by any character burning bronze within 80 feet.

**Ascension Ritual.** To use the Spike, the bearer must already have an Inquisitor’s latticework of Allomantic and Feruchemical spikes. Once implanted, the Eternity Spike transforms its host into a **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 9/Hemalurgic Monstrosity|Hemalurgic monstrosity]]**.
