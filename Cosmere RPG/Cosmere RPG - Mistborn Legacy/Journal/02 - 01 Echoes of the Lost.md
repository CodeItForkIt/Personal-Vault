# 01: Echoes of the Lost

## Echos of the Lost

The fall of the Lord Ruler staggered Scadrial. The Final Empire crumbled, its Steel Ministry scattered. One man, Vadilek Elariel, foresaw this—and prepared. Obsessed with building a better empire, Vadilek turned to Hemalurgy, harvesting more than a thousand maimed souls to create the Eternity Spike, an artifact powerful enough to forge a new immortal ruler. To select a vessel worthy of such a relic, he started a secret society called the Gilded Few, a sinister organization whose bloodstained shadow stretches across the eras.

As the Era 1 story begins, Vadilek has chosen his heir: his estranged niece, Engeanthe Elariel. As Enge’s smuggling crew, the weight of Vadilek’s bloody legacy—and the harrowing responsibility that comes with it—now lands squarely on the party’s shoulders.

## Chapter Background

Vadilek has prepared a macabre ritual at the Gilded Few’s secret compound in Urteau. He created the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Eternity Spike GM ONLY|Eternity Spike]]*** (see [[Journal/13 - Appendix C Eternity Spike|appendix C]]) just months ago, and is now on the verge of using it on Enge, his niece and chosen heir.

Our adventure begins in Urteau, as the PCs complete a smuggling job with Enge—on the last night the party will see her alive.

## After the First Scene

In “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|A Quiet Evening,]]” Enge’s up-and-coming smuggling crew—the PCs—celebrates completing a job. Meanwhile, behind the scenes, Vadilek sends a message to lure Enge to his Gilded Few compound.

Vadilek’s Ascension ritual goes fatally awry when he surprises the Gilded Few by choosing the unwilling Enge for Ascension. He sacrifices himself to forcibly “gift” her his latticework—a collection of other Hemalurgic spikes needed to support the Eternity Spike. As Vadilek dies, another of his favored protégés, Delina Tekiel, jealously kills Enge and attempts to seize the Eternity Spike for herself. The Gilded Few turn on each other.

In the ensuing violence, Vadilek’s final contingency activates: NeBaal, a kandra in Vadilek’s employ, grabs the Spike, takes Enge’s body, and hides. Believing Enge to be dead and enraged by the Spike’s disappearance, Delina and the surviving Gilded Few collect Vadilek’s latticework and storm out, beginning their search for the Eternity Spike. NeBaal seeks to honor their contractual obligation to find a worthy vessel for the Eternity Spike, and the kandra decides to assume Enge’s form.

## Running This Chapter

This chapter takes place in Era 1. Before running this chapter, review this book’s introduction to familiarize yourself with Vadilek and Enge—two notable Era 1 NPCs—and have players create connections with Enge using the introduction’s “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Creating Characters]]” section.

## Chapter Flow

Before play begins, prepare the five chapter 1 handouts found in [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]. This chapter opens as the crew completes a smuggling job with Enge. The party must retrieve an artifact—a valuable silvery urn—and smuggle it out of Urteau. The job comes from a fixer and friend of Enge’s named Cadme, who appears in [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|chapter 2]].

After the job, Enge disappears, lured in by Vadilek. The PCs follow her trail to Velstane’s, a tailor shop concealing the Gilded Few’s compound, the Secret Crucible. The PCs search the complex halls, full of opportunities to learn about Vadilek and the Gilded Few. Familiarize yourself with the Secret Crucible dungeon (see maps 1.1 and 1.2). PCs can interact with Silla Buvidas, a Gilded Few member, and Murn, an ex-obligator who assisted with Vadilek’s heinous Hemalurgic experiments.

In the heart of the Crucible, the party fights a rampaging Hemalurgic beast before rescuing a hidden “Enge,” who shows the PCs the Eternity Spike. The PCs can also rescue a group of skaa prisoners, including a jaded teen named Cynwise, but doing so makes the party’s escape from the complex more harrowing.

The chapter culminates in a tense scene in which the PCs must sneak their dangerous new cargo through a checkpoint, smuggling the Eternity Spike out from under the surviving members of the Gilded Few.

## Character Advancement

The Era 1 characters start this adventure at 1st level and advance to 2nd level upon bypassing the canal checkpoint.

PCs can start a “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction) after finding Vadilek’s secret notes in the Gilded Few’s compound.

After a month of planning, the Urteau job is tonight. You reach the city’s outskirts on foot after docking your smuggling boat. The city sinks into the mist, its canals now bone-dry chasms crisscrossing the districts like scars.\
Enge, your captain and crew boss, tiptoes down a narrow stair carved into the nearest streetslot, crouching low at the base. “Drop’s here somewhere,” she mutters. “Behind one of these slabs. Help me find it, so we can get out before that damn patrol comes around again.”

## Grab and Go

Once the characters are introduced, read:

Above, lanternlight suddenly flares in the mist, accompanied by the sounds of an evening patrol. They’re close, but they haven’t spotted you—yet.

The crew can attempt to finish the smuggling job without being caught. This is an endeavor, in which the PCs try to overcome obstacles, primarily by successfully employing skills, expertises, and talents (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). Potential obstacles the PCs may face include:

**Find the Cache.** Spotting the uneven slab in the dark requires a good eye (DC 12 Perception). PCs can clear away ash (DC 11 Agility) or move the slab (DC 10 Athletics) to reveal “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#A Distinctive Urn|A Distinctive Urn.]]”

**Avoid Light.** Lanternlight pours into the streetslot. PCs can stick to the shadows (DC 11 Stealth) or camouflage themselves with soot (DC 13 Survival).

**Coming Closer.** One garrisoner approaches to investigate. PCs can lure them away with a distraction (DC 12 Persuasion) or concoct a story to waylay the patrol (DC 13 Deception). This is a good place to raise the stakes.

### Special Rule: You Just Mist Me

Enge always has her crew’s back. The first time a PC fails a test during this endeavor, she executes her signature smuggling move, the “You Just Mist Me,” distracting the garrisoners. Work with the player to narrate how Enge gives them the runaround, then the PC can reroll the test; they must use the new result.

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor succeeds if the party accrues 4 successes before 3 failures, or otherwise completes the objective.

**Success.** The party retrieves the urn undetected.

**Failure.** The party retrieves the urn, but it gets dented in the process (and the client won’t be pleased). Additionally, the upcoming canal checkpoint is more difficult to bypass (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#Canal Checkpoint|Canal Checkpoint]]”).

### A Distinctive Urn

The PCs recover a distinctive, three-foot-tall urn, silvery and decorated with plant motifs. A PC with the History expertise recognizes it as an artifact of the Larsta religion and the motif as extinct plants called “flowers.” With this objective completed, Enge suggests returning to the boat to celebrate with wine.

## A Quiet Evening

Enge is a friendly, offbeat leader with a loud mouth, a broad rebellious streak, and a genuine love for her crew. For information about roleplaying Enge, see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction.

## Canal Boat

This canal boat doubles as the party’s hideout (see “Hideouts” in chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*).

It’s a long, narrow barge of fine hardwood with a spacious cabin, cluttered with barrels and crates beneath sagging canvas tarps. Originally a noble’s pleasure cruiser, it’s been repurposed as a smuggling vessel since Enge acquired it a year ago. Two ash-stained brown horses, Willy and Bollocks, pull the boat along the towpath.

Ask each PC to describe a small aesthetic feature or customization they’ve added to the boat since joining Enge’s crew. Map 1.3 (near the end of this chapter) shows the inside of the canal boat, on which you can note their modifications.

## Enge’s Gifts

Enge offers the party a large bottle of wine and a small surprise: a new piece of hand-made clothing for each member of the crew. An attentive leader, Enge crafted a gift to suit each PC’s unique personality—a functional jacket, a decorative hat, and so on.

Once these gifts are handed out, Enge begins a new project this evening, stitching a scrap of bold blue fabric.

## What Enge Shares

Enge has kept her heritage as a noble of House Elariel secret, but she openly discusses other topics, such as:

**The Job.** Enge is pleased with the crew’s success and says they’ll leave for Luthadel tomorrow night. When they get back, she’ll contact her fixer, **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Cadme|Cadme]] (she/her)**, to arrange the delivery. Enge asks the PCs how they plan to spend their shares of the promised 600-boxing payout.

**The Post-Revolution.** A year ago, against all odds, the skaa rebellion won. Enge asks the PCs about their hopes for the future and their place in the new kingdoms. How did the revolution change their dreams?

**Family.** Enge asks the PCs about their family. She remarks it’s a shame you can’t choose your family like you can choose your crew.

**Rumors of a War.** Straff Venture’s armies are preparing to march against some enemy. Are the PCs worried about war in the countryside?

**Work in Progress.** If asked about the bold blue fabric she’s stitching on, Enge winks and says, “You’ll find out.” She asks if the PCs have any hobbies.

### A “Quick Errand”

At dawn, the PCs find a note from Enge, who left after receiving a message from Vadilek (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|Where’s the Boss]]”). Give the players the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Enge Letter]]” handout (from [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]) or read the following:

I’m out for a quick personal errand, but I’ll be back before nightfall—with spirits for the ride! Take the day to relax and rest up. You all deserve it.\
Here’s to many more jobs ahead—with the best bloody crew I could ask for! —Enge

The crew has planned this job for over a month. With it almost complete, ask each player to narrate what relaxing on the canal boat looks like for their PC. Do they play cards, swap old stories, work on a personal project, or just catch up on sleep?

## Where’s the Boss?

Nightfall nears, and Enge still hasn’t returned. The PCs know it’s unusual for her to break plans—especially plans involving schemes and spirits.

If the PCs search, they find a letter on her desk, written on fine paper.

Give the players the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Vadilek Letter 1]]” handout (from [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]) or read the following:

Engeanthe,\
I deeply regret our last words. What I asked of you is what I believed necessary. I did not understand what I was truly asking. I now know the full cost. You are owed an apology and an explanation.\
Allow me to make things right. Return to Velstane’s. Know that you are worthy, and there will always be a place for you here.\
—V.

Enge’s errand took her to Velstane’s, a tailor’s shop nearby in Urteau—and secretly a front for the Gilded Few’s hidden compound.

## Secret Crucible Locations

Investigating Urteau is easier at night, when the streets are dark and empty. The PCs know only that Velstane’s is an upscale tailor shop, popular among Urteau’s elite. Velstane’s is closed, but the front door hangs suspiciously ajar as the PCs arrive. Searching the shop reveals the way into the compound below.

## Velstane’s

The ground floor features a polished showroom, private fitting area, workshop, and warehouse. Map 1.1 shows these rooms and a warehouse one street over (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C13: Warehouse|C13: Warehouse]]”). Clues around the shop hint at an upcoming ball in Luthadel (see [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|chapter 2]]). The hidden compound below, known as the Secret Crucible, is shown on map 1.2.

The main entrance of Velstane’s opens into the showroom ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C1: Showroom|C1]]).

## C1: Showroom

The Velstane’s showroom is dark and empty. A door to the east connects to the fitting area ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C3: Fitting Area|C3]]), and the south door leads to the workshop ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C2: Workshop|C2]]).

The showroom gleams with polished mirrors and dark wood floors. Rows of mannequins adorned in flowing eveningwear stand poised beside cases of lace gloves and jeweled brooches. Doors lead east and south.

The eveningwear is ***fine clothing***. A PC can lockpick a case with a successful DC 10 Thievery test, obtaining lace gloves worth 25 boxings and a jeweled brooch worth 75 boxings.

## C2: Workshop

The workshop connects to the showroom ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C1: Showroom|C1]]). The room is cluttered with half-finished gowns. Incomplete masquerade masks, inlaid with delicate gold thread, lie on a table. Investigation uncovers the following:

**Tailor’s Ledger.** A leatherbound book tracks custom orders from Houses Elariel, Tekiel, and Buvidas. One name is underlined repeatedly: “Guiverre Seeris” (the host of the ball in [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|chapter 2]]).

**Rush Orders.** A stack of urgent commissions requests masquerade masks, dyed gloves, and cloaks. On a successful DC 8 Perception test, a PC finds a note: “Ensure the slit in the dress is wide enough. She may need access to a blade. —V.”

## C3: Fitting Area

The fitting area connects to the showroom ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C1: Showroom|C1]]), and a secret passage leads down to the guard room ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C4: Guard Room|C4]]) and the rest of the hidden complex.

Wood-paneled walls are bathed in orange by a single lantern. A central stage here is flanked by crushed velvet couches. Along the walls stand four curtained fitting rooms. One curtain is half-open, its fabric torn.

Behind the half-open curtain is a spacious fitting room. A tall mirror hangs on the wall. A fingerless knit glove, out of place among the finery, lies on the floor near the mirror. It is unmistakably Enge’s.

Characters who investigate discover the mirror swings outward to reveal hidden stairs descending underground. A faint light flickers below.

### Descending Into the Crucible

If the PCs attempt to move down the stairs quietly, each makes a DC 12 Stealth test. This is a good place to raise the stakes. If at least half of the PCs succeed, the garrisoners in the guard room ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C4: Guard Room|C4]]) gain the Surprised condition. Otherwise, the guards are prepared for the party’s arrival.

![[pg030_Map-1.2_The Secret Crucible_Eduardo Nonato.webp]]

## C4: Guard Room

This rectangular rough-stone chamber is dimly lit by a pair of torches in iron sconces. Stairs lead back up to the fitting area ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C3: Fitting Area|C3]]), while two garrisoners defend more stairs leading to a hallway deeper into the compound ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C5: Corridor|C5]]).

If the guards are Surprised, they scramble to grab their weapons, shouting, “You shouldn’t be in here!” Otherwise, the guards are alert, longbows nocked and aimed straight at the party.

### Battlefield Effects

The following effects are active during this combat:

**Portcullis.** The garrisoners can drop a rusty steel portcullis mid-room, potentially splitting the party. A character can Use a Skill (1) to make a DC 12 Athletics test to lift the barrier, or the portcullis can be bent enough to slip through by dealing 5 impact damage.

#### [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Guard Room Opportunities and Complications]]

### Aftermath

When one garrisoner is subdued, the other surrenders. If spared, they answer questions, but they don’t know much:

- **Vadilek Elariel (he/him)** is their employer, but they’ve never met him in person or gone deeper into the compound.

- Many nobles in especially fine clothes entered the complex today.

- The last person to enter arrived alone. She matches Enge’s description.

## C5: Corridor

As the PCs descend further past the guard room, read:

At the base of the stairs is a branching stone corridor with three doors. Harsh faces glare at you—marble busts, staring sightlessly from pedestals along the wall.

Each bust depicts a member of the Gilded Few. A PC with the Luthadel expertise or who succeeds on a DC 13 Lore test identifi es them as nobles and can name the most prominent: Shan Elariel, Delina Tekiel, and Duvall Haught. Additionally, anyone examining the busts spots one of Enge.

## C6: Assembly Room

The Gilded Few plan their operations in the assembly room, which connects to the corridor ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C5: Corridor|C5]]), study ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C7: Study|C7]]), and hallway ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C10: Hallway|C10]]). When the PCs enter this room, read:

A round wooden table commands the room while bookcases loom along the walls. Next to one is a thin blond woman in a cobalt gown, darkened with blood. She grabs a book, violently riffling through it, then throws it to the floor with a frustrated snarl. A lady’s handbag lies on a table nearby, unattended. One door stands tucked between the bookcases on the south wall, and another leads to the east.

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 1/Silla Buvidas|Silla Buvidas]] (she/her)**, a member of the Gilded Few, knows Vadilek kept rare Hemalurgic texts hidden somewhere in this room.

### Handbag

With Silla distracted by her search, a PC can grab her handbag with a successful DC 14 Stealth test. On a failure, Silla notices them (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#Silla|Silla]]”). The bag contains 25 boxings, common metal vials, and a note.

Give the players the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Vadilek Letter 2]]” handout (from [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]) or read the following:

Eternity awaits. The Ascension ceremony begins at nightfall. Only the worthy will endure what follows; the fire reveals the purity of the ore.\
—V.

### Silla

If Silla notices the PCs, she’s startled, demanding to know who the PCs are and how they got here. She shares little, beyond sardonically remarking the night couldn’t get any more off track.

If confronted or threatened, Silla flees toward the fitting area ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C3: Fitting Area|C3]]), using Ironpull (1) whenever it’s helpful. If she escapes successfully, she locks the hidden mirror door behind her, closing off that exit.

### Bookcases and Hidden Safe

Behind several nondescript books on a bookshelf along the south wall lies a hidden safe, which can be found on a successful DC 12 Perception test. Its combination is etched into Vadilek’s journal (found in [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C7: Study|C7]]), or it can be cracked on a successful DC 15 Thievery test. Inside are two leatherbound tomes encoded in cipher: forbidden Hemalurgic texts Vadilek stole from the Ministry. Thesetomes are annotated with Vadilek’s own notes in metallic ink, which the PCs can study during downtime (see“[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction).

## C7: Study

This side room holds a wide desk, wool rug, and unlit fireplace. A portrait hangs above the desk, and beneath it rests a locked bronze chest. This is Vadilek’s personal study, and all correspondence here is written with metallic ink. This area connects to the assembly room ([[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C6: Assembly Room|C6]]).

### Desk and Chest

Beneath the desk rests a locked bronze chest containing the code for the safe (found in [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C6: Assembly Room|C6]]). Characters who investigate uncover:

**Bronze Chest.** The locked chest can be opened on a successful DC 10 Thievery or DC 12 Athletics test. Inside are Vadilek’s journal (see “Journal”) and a sketched portrait (see “Sketch”).

**Journal.** Vadilek’s journal is filled with training logs comparing individual performances of Vadilek’s favorite Gilded Few protégés, each entry scoring members against one another. Shan Elariel’s name is crossed out. Delina Tekiel’s scores surpass all the others. The last page is torn out but can be found on Vadilek’s corpse (in [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#C10: Hallway|C10]]). Etched inside the journal cover is the combination for the safe: 16–50–36.

**Sketch.** A rough sketch depicts the PCs with Enge, her likeness circled repeatedly in dark ink. Notes on the back track the crew’s movements between Luthadel and Urteau over several months; this handwriting is obviously different from Vadilek’s. Secretly, these notes were made by NeBaal, whom Vadilek had assigned to spy on Enge.

### Portrait: Vadilek and Enge

The painting above the desk depicts a bald man with a black-and-red tattoo marking him as a member of the Canton of Inquisition. Beside him stands a young auburn-haired woman with bold eyebrows: Enge. A PC who succeeds on a DC 14 Lore test recognizes the man as Vadilek, a member of a lesser branch of House Elariel.

### Speaking With Murn

Murn assumes the PCs are part of the skaa rebellion and resigns himself to their justice. Guilt-ridden, Murn answers questions readily, except those about the Hemalurgic beast (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|Murn’s Mistake]]”).

Should the PCs attempt to absolve him of guilt, Murn spends focus to resist, believing he’s beyond absolution. He also spends focus to remain unswayed by threats of violence, as he’s unconcerned about dying.

Murn answers questions and shares the following:

**What happened here?** “Vadilek and I—fools, both—dreamed we could build a better Empire. We bled for that dream. Others bled more. We said it was for the greater good, but… By the Lord Ruler, I still hear them screaming. And for what? The Ascension ceremony failed. I didn’t see it, but I heard the chaos—we failed.”

**What is the Ascension ceremony?** “A way to make a god… and we don’t build gods gently.” If a PC pushes for specifics, have them make a DC 13 Persuasion or Intimidation test. On a success, Murn explains this laboratory was used for a process akin to creating an Inquisitor, producing an object Vadilek called the “Eternity Spike.” Vadilek alone knew the full, forbidden process behind the Eternity Spike’s creation, but Murn experimented with the other spikes Vadilek produced. Vadilek’s notes are somewhere in the building (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#Bookcases and Hidden Safe|Bookshelves and Hidden Safe]]” in C6). This information can also be found in Murn’s notebook (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|Murn’s Possessions]]”).

#### What Cynwise Wants

If the party wants Cynwise to share what they saw, run this scene as a conversation (see “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost#Resolving the Conversation|Resolving the Conversation]]”). To begin, Cynwise makes three requests of the party:

Cynwise might share the following:

#### Resisting Influence

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 1/Cynwise|Cynwise]]** has a Cognitive defense of 14, a Spiritual defense of 13, and 4 focus. Cynwise is deeply skeptical, and Deception tests made against them gain a disadvantage. Cynwise spends focus to resist intimidation or any offers requiring them to blindly trust the PCs.

#### Resolving the Conversation

The conversation succeeds if the party addresses “What Cynwise Wants,” either with honest offers or compelling deceptions. If Cynwise no longer has enough focus to resist influence, the conversation immediately ends in failure.

**Success.** Cynwise thinks highly of the PCs, warily thanking them for the help. They share everything they know and offer any assistance they can.

**Failure.** Cynwise crumbles, too scared and tired to bargain anymore. Emotionally broken, they share what they know and then turn away, saying, “Go save your own skins. We’ll find our own way to Luthadel. We always find a way.” They and the other prisoners reject further deals or help.

#### [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Fight for the Spike Opportunities and Complications]]

### Aftermath

Combat ends when both garrisoners or either twin is defeated. Any surviving adversaries retreat. A successful DC 8 Lore test identifies a House Buvidas insignia on the body of either fallen twin. The PCs can operate the winch manually to free the boat and begin the voyage to Luthadel with no further interruptions.

## Journeying Onward

Upon clearing the checkpoint, the PCs advance to 2nd level. Enge vows to search for leads on the Spike, while lying low in case the Gilded Few retaliate.
