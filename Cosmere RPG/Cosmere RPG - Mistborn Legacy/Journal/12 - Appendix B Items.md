# Appendix B: Items

## Items

This appendix details the unique items that appear in the *Mistborn Legacy* adventure. Other items mentioned in this adventure are detailed in chapter 7 of the *Mistborn Handbook*.

#### **[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Flamethrower|Flamethrower]]**  Era 2

*Special Weapon, 50 lb.*

**Skill:** Heavy Weaponry

**Damage:** 2d4 energy

**Range:** Ranged [10/20]

**Traits:** Dangerous, Loaded [5], Two-Handed, Unique

**Expert Traits:** Unique: loses Dangerous trait

This incendiary device consists of a metal tank, filled with flammable liquid fuel and worn like a backpack, hose, and handheld spray shaft. When triggered, fuel flows from the tank through the hose to the spray shaft, which ignites it, creating a jet of fire.

**Unique Traits.** Flamethrowers have the following unique trait:

**Blast of Fire.** When you hit a target with this weapon, you also graze every other character whose space is directly between you and the target (GM’s discretion). You can spend O from the attack to instead hit those grazed characters.

#### **[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Founders Key|Founder’s Key]]**

*Special Equipment, 0.25 lb.*

This large golden key is ornately decorated. The key’s bow depicts an eye with a star for an iris.

#### **[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Gas Mask Era 2|Gas Mask]]**  Era 2

*Equipment, 1 lb.*

This mask is made of rubberized fabric and khaki, inset with circular goggles. A long rubber hose connects a canister with chemical absorbents, providing protection against noxious fumes and smoke.

**Unique Traits.** Gas masks have the following unique traits:

**Unwieldly.** While wearing the mask, the Banter free action costs 1 and the wearer is Diminished [Awareness −2].

**Air Filter.** While wearing the gas mask, the wearer is immune to effects that require the target to breathe in a noxious substance.

#### **[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Landmine Era 2|Landmine]]**  Era 2

*Equipment, 4 lb.*

**Skill:** Heavy Weaponry

**Damage:** 2d6 impact

**Range:** Melee

**Traits:** Blast [10], Dangerous, Single-Use, Unique

**Expert Traits:** None

This metal cylinder has a six-inch diameter and consists of a pressure plate, trip wire, and explosives. Meant to be buried, the landmine is activated by pressing the plate or crossing the trip wire, which results in a deadly blast.

**Unique Traits.** Mines have the following unique traits:

**Trip Wire.** While touching this mine, you can use 2 to set it up, establishing a trip wire of 5 to 15 feet. A character can safely cross the trip wire if they Use a Skill (1) and succeed on a DC 15 Perception test. Otherwise, when a Medium or larger character enters a space with the mine or trip wire, the mine detonates. When it does, make a Crafting attack against the Physical defense of each character within 10 feet of the explosive. At the GM’s discretion, this blast can also damage objects within that area.

**Chain Reaction.** If the blast radius of a detonating mine overlaps with the space of another mine or its trip wire, that mine also detonates.

#### **[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Midnight Mistcloak|Midnight Mistcloak]]**

*Special Armor, 5 lb.*

**Deflect Value:** 1

**Traits:** Unique

**Expert Traits:** Unique

This one-of-a-kind mistcloak is especially well made. A unique, bold blue color, its mantle features complex embroidery in silver and blue thread.

**Unique Traits.** The Midnight Mistcloak has the following unique trait:

**Flowing Tassels.** While wearing this armor in an area that obscures vision, such as in darkness or mist, you gain an advantage on Stealth tests you make against characters whose primary sense is obscured by that area. Additionally, those characters gain a disadvantage on Perception tests against you.

If you have the Mistcloak specialist expertise, also add the following special expert trait:

**Mistborn Gravitas.** While wearing this armor, you gain an advantage on Intimidation tests against non- Mistborn characters who can sense you and who have no reason to believe you’re not Mistborn.
