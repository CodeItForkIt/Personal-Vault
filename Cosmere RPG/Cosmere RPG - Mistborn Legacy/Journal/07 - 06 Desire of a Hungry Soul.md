# 06: Desire of a Hungry Soul

## Desire of a Hungry Soul

An assassination attempt reveals that the Era 2 PCs have made dangerous enemies. To understand their attackers, the party must uncover dark secrets, some centuries old, below the University of Elendel.

## Running This Chapter

This chapter begins only days after the National Archives heist. Furious about the theft, the Gilded Trio sends assassins after the PCs—and after Professor Azmine Wilko.

## Chapter Flow

Before beginning, prepare several resources for the players—the “Wilko’s Note,” “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Midnight Trials Bid Letter,]]” and “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|University of Elendel Campus Map]]” handouts, along with a copy of the Steel Alphabet page from the back of the *Mistborn Handbook*. Whether the campus map is on printed paper or presented digitally, players need a way to draw on it during this chapter.

The chapter starts with a week of downtime, after which the party is interrupted by a coordinated bombing attack against them and Wilko. The PCs either save Professor Wilko, or she’s injured in an explosion.

The next day, the PCs head to the University of Elendel, where K.T., a student journalist, explains the attackers were after the party’s Founder’s Key: a key to unlock the Gilded Map below the University of Elendel, guarded by a campus fraternity (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Order of a Thousand Eyes|Order of a Thousand Eyes]]”). K.T. suggests infiltrating the Order’s masked initiation ceremony, the Midnight Trials, happening that night.

The PCs explore the campus to acquire masks to disguise themselves (potentially with help from a student named Marthis), acquire bones to present, and solve a puzzle to find the Midnight Trials’ starting location. Take note of the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Gilded Bickering|Gilded Bickering]]” scene, as this can trigger at multiple locations on campus.

At midnight, Kwylliam officiates the trials in the tunnels below the university. Be sure to review “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|The Midnight Trials]]” in advance, as each trial is unique. If the PCs pass the trials, they make it to the society’s inner sanctum, containing the Order’s secrets—most notably NeBaal, whom the Gilded Few has kept imprisoned for centuries, locked in a distinctive silvery urn. The chapter ends with the PCs rescuing NeBaal, potentially dueling Kwylliam on the way out.

## Order of a Thousand Eyes

Once a year, the scions of select noble bloodlines receive a bid letter inviting them to join the Order of a Thousand Eyes, the most elite fraternity at the University of Elendel. Only after passing the Midnight Trials do these young nobles learn the truth: Some or all of the members’ Metalborn powers come from “familial spikes,” Hemalurgic spikes handed down over generations—a few were even part of Vadilek’s original latticework.

These chosen few are taught the Eternity Spike is their birthright; unjustly stolen, it can only be found by following the legendary Gilded Map. They believe NeBaal knows that map’s location, and once a year, they temporarily release the kandra from imprisonment for ceremonial questionings. A few years ago, the Gilded Trio were the students who passed the trials and questioned NeBaal; they tricked the kandra into revealing what all three Gilded Map pieces were (though not their locations). The Trio made a pact together to find the map and cruelly locked NeBaal back in the urn.

Full of dark secrets, each class of the Order’s inductees become the next generation of the modern Gilded Few, inheriting positions of power throughout Elendel society.

## Character Advancement

The Era 2 characters start this chapter at 4th level and advance to 5th level upon escaping the university with NeBaal.

PCs with a “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal might progress it by studying the anatomical diagram during “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Induction.]]”

## Settling the Score

As the chapter begins, the Gilded Trio know that the PCs foiled Kwylliam’s bribes meant to redirect the crate out of Professor Wilko’s grasp. Willing to do whatever it takes to secure the key and the mask, the Trio searches Elendel for the party, preparing to deliver an explosive counterattack.

## Safe House

Wilko is ecstatic about the party’s successful heist. She plans to research the key and mask immediately, hoping to have more information soon. Wilko warns the PCs to lie low after their public getaway and sets them up with a safehouse: a warehouse in the docks.

The safehouse secretly belongs to the Guardians (see [[Journal/16 - Appendix F The Guardians|appendix F]]). Wilko blurts this out if pressed, then immediately flees the conversation. Depending on the Guardians’ focus, the warehouse is full of the following:

- **Wisdom.** Glass instruments.

- **Industry.** Soonie Pups (stuffed wolfhound toys).

- **Labor.** Frozen fish.

The PCs have a windowless private back room with one door, a table, and a cot for each person.

## Lying Low: Downtime in Elendel

The PCs have one week of downtime in Elendel. In addition to normal downtime activities (see “Downtime” in chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*), *Mistborn Legacy* also offers unique downtime options PCs can experience during this adventure. See “[[Journal/15 - Appendix E Downtime|Downtime in Elendel]]” in appendix E for a full list of the exclusive downtime options available for Era 2 Mistborn Legacy PCs.

If the party retrieved the Era 1 party’s Hemalurgy notes, they can study them during downtime (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction).

Tell the players Wilko has advised the party lie low, as the Trio are sure to be searching for them. If a PC ignores this advice and does downtime activities in public spaces—for example, researching at a library or recuperating in a hospital—have them roll the plot die. On a Complication, they feel watched; accrue an automatic failure toward the upcoming “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Racing to Wilko|Racing to Wilko]]” endeavor.

## A Ticking Clock

Downtime ends with an unmarked letter from Wilko. The uneasy peace is about to explode, as the PCs’ hideout (see map 6.1) is attacked, with a bomb in the furnace and a strike squad at the door. In the aftermath, the party must read the clues and rush to protect Wilko from a second bombing attack at her office at the University of Elendel.

To open the scene, read:

This evening, in the drafty back room of the warehouse safehouse, an unmarked letter awaits. Sitting next to it on the table, the bottle of your favorite libations signals the letter is from Wilko.

Give the players the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Wilko’s Note]]” handout (from [[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|appendix D]]) or read:

Good news: I’ve turned up some answers about the crate’s contents. I believe we have a “Founder’s Key.”\
As legend has it, three such keys were created by an early university teacher, who hid a precious treasure underneath the campus. The keys were handed down to noble students to safeguard. I believe this treasure is the “Gilded Map”… which we should talk about more in person.\
Right now, I have a more pressing problem: A former student of mine has been wolfhounding me about the key—Harmony knows how she found out. She’s a reporter for the Student Voice, and if she keeps digging, I fear she’ll learn about our recent activities.\
She claims to have important information about the key. Can you talk to her? I feel foolish for asking, but I’m a terrible liar.\
Sincerely,\
A. Wilko\
P.S. Speaking of feeling foolish—it’s probably just my nerves, but I keep feeling like I’m being watched. If anything happens to me, the mask and key are hidden in my copy of Trials of Monument. And please destroy this note!

## Conrad Strike Squad

After the party discusses the note (or when a PC begins to leave or go to sleep), have each PC make a Perception test. Read the following to the character with the highest result:

You notice an odd, tinny ticking sound coming from the northwest corner of the room, where your Tarcsel Electric furnace rattles.

Investigating reveals a ticking device placed inside the furnace control box. Each PC who went to investigate should make a DC 12 Deduction test. On a success, the PC knows this explosive is about to blow, and they have a split-second chance to warn the entire party to Brace (1) just before combat begins. Whether the PCs investigate or not, the bomb explodes, combat starts, and assassins attack.

### Starting Combat With a Bang

The bomb explodes at the start of the round, dealing 2d6 energy damage in a 25-foot radius and filling that area with thick smoke. Characters who used the Brace action instead take half damage. Every PC starts combat with the Surprised condition unless they succeeded on the Deduction test to identify the bomb.

Three plainclothes **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Operative Era 2|Conrad operatives]]** block the safehouse exit. The operatives take a slow turn, entering unharmed after the bomb goes off.

### Battlefield Effects

The following effects are active for this combat:

**Countdown.** At the start of the third round and each round that follows, the party accrues one failure toward the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Racing to Wilko|Racing to Wilko]]” endeavor.

**Smoke.** Thick smoke fills the area the bomb exploded in, obscuring sight.

### Aftermath

The operatives fight for four rounds or until one dies, at which point they retreat, saying, “They don’t have it; retreat to the second location.”

## Racing to Wilko

At this point, every moment counts. The PCs might investigate the defeated operatives for clues or search the wreckage for evidence. If at any time the PCs move to protect Wilko, proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Running the Endeavor|Running the Endeavor.]]”

If the party doesn’t attempt to rescue Wilko, jump ahead to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#A Long Night|A Long Night.]]” If the party remains at the scene for five minutes, accrue an automatic failure toward the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Racing to Wilko|Racing to Wilko]]” endeavor. Constabulary sirens can be heard coming this way. If the PCs don’t flee, four **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Constable Era 2|constables]]** arrive. While the PCs aren’t immediately recognized for their National Archives theft, the constables are suspicious and unhelpful (and deep in Bayron’s pocket).

### Operative Clues

Subdued operatives wish to be delivered to law enforcement. If a PC succeeds on a DC 13 Intimidation or Persuasion test, an operative spits, “You’re irrelevant. We’ll get what we need from your professor friend.”

The operatives’ firearms have silencers made by Conrad Industries. One operative’s jacket pocket contains a note reading “Office 141.” On a successful DC 12 Deduction test, a PC immediately recalls this is Wilko’s office number.

### Searching the Wreckage

A successful DC 12 Crafting or Perception test reveals the remnants of a bomb. Though the shrapnel is nearly unrecognizable as part of an explosive device, it’s clearly engraved with the Conrad Industries logo.

### Running the Endeavor

If the PCs realize Wilko is in danger, run an endeavor to reach her (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). The endeavor begins with up to 4 failures accrued, depending on how long the fight lasted, if the PCs dallied after, and Complications during downtime. Raise the stakes on all tests as the PCs face the following obstacles, in order:

**Navigating Elendel.** To get to Wilko in time, PCs might try running ahead (DC 15 Athletics), finding a shortcut (DC 14 Survival), or acquiring faster transportation (DC 13 Thievery).

**University Gate.** For the final endeavor test, the PCs arrive at the university gates and must either climb the wall (DC 17 Athletics), find a gap in the gate (DC 18 Perception), or deceive the night gate guard (DC 17 Deception).

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor ends when the party accrues 3 successes or 5 failures, or if they otherwise complete the objective. The endeavor’s outcome depends on the number of failures accrued.

**No More Than One Failure.** The PCs arrive at office 141 to find Azmine Wilko quietly studying in a plush chair, too absorbed to hear the ticking from her nearby furnace. A similar bomb explodes in three rounds if not deactivated with a successful DC 14 Crafting or Thievery test. Even if the bomb explodes, Wilko has been alerted to the danger; her wounds are superficial, and she remains conscious.

**Two or Three Failures.** The PCs arrive just as the bomb explodes. Wilko is conscious but requires medical attention within two rounds, such as a successful DC 10 Medicine test, or she falls unconscious; either way, she suffers an injury (make an injury roll, as described in chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*).

**Four or More Failures.** The PCs are at the university gates when the bomb explodes. In the chaos, the PCs can easily leave unnoticed or rush to Wilko’s destroyed office. Wilko is unconscious and severely hurt (make an injury roll with a −5 modifier).

### Wilko’s Lockbox: Trials of Monument

If the bomb at Wilko’s office went off, PCs searching for her copy of Trials of Monument find it unharmed in the rubble. The book is actually a disguised lockbox; inside are the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Founders Key|Founder’s Key]]***, the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Raven Mask|Raven Mask]]***, and a business card for K.T. Quiven, from the University Voice broadsheet’s office in the student center.

Searching the wreckage also reveals another bomb remnant stamped with the Conrad Industries logo.

When the PCs are ready to proceed, continue to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#A Long Night|A Long Night.]]”

## A Long Night

The party’s hideout has been destroyed, and Wilko may or may not be conscious.

If Wilko is conscious, she hosts the PCs with her family in the Tekiel Tower’s Clan Estates (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction). Wilko’s gregarious family members gossip about the fashionable Lysarra Tekiel showing around well-dressed businesspeople (foreshadowing the Elariel Spring Salon in [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|chapter 8]]), the Tekiels’ money troubles, and rumors saying crime connections may have been used to finish the Tower. If the PCs show or describe the bomb remnants left after either explosion, Wilko glumly speculates Bayron Conrad has bought off law enforcement to avoid consequences.

If Wilko is unconscious, her family takes her to the Clan Estates, and they don’t let the PCs visit. The PCs must find temporary accommodations.

### Reestablishing the Hideout

When the PCs wish to establish a new hideout, the characters can leverage their contacts to help find a suitable location. Use the New Safehouse table for the options available to the PCs, based on their legacies.

#### [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|New Safehouse]]

The contact (or in the case of the Heir, a member of the PC’s family) provides safe lodgings at the location. If the PC intends to make good, have them start an appropriate goal, gaining the location as a permanent hideout upon completion (see the “Hideouts” section of chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*).

### The Spin

The next day, the broadsheets (notably including Kwylliam’s paper, The Truth in Elendel) run a story reporting the explosions were caused by a Tarcsel Electric furnace manufacturing fault. This is the story authorities believe. If the PCs didn’t realize Wilko was in danger, they can read about the second explosion.

If the characters told incriminating stories to Kwylliam when they met in [[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|chapter 5]], these are also referenced in the broadsheet. No articles mention the Archives heist.

## Investigative Journalism

The next day, the PCs are due at the University of Elendel for a previously scheduled meeting with Wilko. If she’s conscious, she meets up with the party and introduces them to **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/KT Quiven|K.T. Quiven]]** (informant, she/ her). If Wilko is unconscious, K.T. finds the party instead.

## Campus Newshound

Dominating the University Voice office is K.T.’s corkboard, covered in clippings and notes carefully connected with string. K.T. waves the party toward seats between stacks of old broadsheets. Read:

“So, let’s talk about why you were attacked. I’ve been investigating an elite campus fraternity for young nobles, the Order of a Thousand Eyes. And as it happens, the Order uses a Founder’s Key in their yearly initiation ceremony, the Midnight Trials. A key that looks exactly like the one you and Wilko ‘acquired.’ These keys are said to unlock a treasure called the Gilded Map, hidden by the Order below the university. And now we know, whatever the Gilded Map is, the Order is willing to kill to keep it a secret.”\
K.T. smiles, eyes flashing with anticipation. “They tried to blow you up? Then let’s blow their secrets wide open.”

K.T. is convinced the party’s attacker was someone involved with the Order—someone trying to reclaim the Founder’s Key. K.T. hopes to recruit the PCs to help her investigate. She shares the following:

**Golden Opportunity.** K.T. presents a rare chance to go undercover. The yearly Midnight Trials are tonight, during which the hopeful pledges wear ceremonial masks to protect their noble identities.

**Order Invitation.** K.T. has a bid letter from the Order, listing instructions for student pledges to follow, should they intend to attempt the trials. Give the players both the “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|Midnight Trials Bid Letter]]” and “[[Journal/14 - Appendix D Handouts|University of Elendel Campus Map]]” handouts (found in **appendix D**). Alternatively, for the bid letter, you can read:

Child of Noblest Blood—\
The Midnight Trials are at the final bell on the 16th day of Cladence. Those judged Worthy shall inherit our Legacy. In preparation:\
• Don proudly the Mask of the Order.\
• Come bearing a Cazzian offering.\
The Worthy must seek the Trial’s starting location. On the map, chart a Nelazan Route, connecting the pairs of Strange Eyes. Read the Letters in Steel. Hidden at the Crossings are the directions you seek.\
The fire reveals the purity of the ore.

**Inside Man.** K.T. has an “inside man,” a fellow student and Order pledge named **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Marthis|Marthis]]** (he/him). He owes K.T. a favor and can help the party masquerade as fellow pledges.

Per the letter, the trials begin a few hours from now (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Event: Midnight Approaches|Event: Midnight Approaches]]”) at a secret starting location. The PCs must follow the letter’s instructions to infiltrate the trials (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Preparing for the Trials]]”). If the PCs decline to bring K.T. along, she conducts her own investigation and infiltration, undeterred.

The next portion of this chapter presents a nonlinear puzzle that unfolds as the PCs explore the university. It begins with an overview of “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|University of Elendel Locations.]]” Before running this portion, read through “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Preparing for the Trials]]”; at the end of that section, “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Map Puzzle|Map Puzzle]]” explains the puzzle clues encountered in some locations.

## U1: Gates and Grounds

![[pg117_MBL06_Elendel University_Erel Matita.webp]]

**Entering the hallowed grounds of the University of Elendel. - EREL MATITA**

A 15-foot brick wall surrounds the university. “Wasing the Always of Wanting of Knowing,” reads the inscrip tion above the grand gates. Two blackcoats guard the entrance. Just inside, students lounge on a manicured lawn between centuries-old buildings of brick and stained glass. The old fountain in the center shows a woman in a sheer dress, standing on ash, beseeching the sky.

The gates offer the only obvious entrance to university grounds. The two blackcoats (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Constable Era 2|constables]]**) check weapons and keep a visitor ledger. A row of classroom buildings known as the Electrum Houses stand to the west. If the bomb in Wilko’s office went off, House Six, the history department, has a 5-foot hole in the wall guarded by four more blackcoats.

## U2: Copper Hall

Maple trees flank Copper Hall, the university’s two-story administrative building. Its facade has a stained-glass window showing an apocryphal scene of debating historical scholars: Yomen, Tindwyl, Dilisteni, the Lord Mistborn, and others. A bored guard watches the door.

Copper Hall is the university’s administrative building. It contains the deans’ offices, a bursar’s office, and a dark, claustrophobic records room.

### Puzzle Clue

“LORD” refers to the Lord Mistborn depicted in the stained-glass window, where the word “Gaze” is written in chalk.

## U3: Student Center

The student center is a two-story brick building with columns supporting a large second-floor balcony. Inside, rooms house student organizations such as the yearbook club, a Model Assembly, the Young Ascendants, the Fine Arts club, and the University Voice student broadsheet. The building also offers a campus store and a small theater, where play rehearsals are underway. The Young Ascendants office bustles with excited students.

The Young Ascendants is a youth empowerment organization started by Lysarra. If the PCs approach the office, they see **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Lysarra Tekiel|Lysarra Tekiel]]** giving a presentation about the upcoming Elariel Spring Salon’s “Gilt and Glamour” charity auction (see [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|chapter 8]]) to a crowd of adoring students. She mentions that auction internships are available.

The campus store sells school supplies and university paraphernalia.

## U4: Advanced Science and Metallurgy Labs

A sign identifies this large brick building as holding the advanced science and metallurgy labs. Inside is an open-air main hall with anatomical models and academic awards on display. At the end of the medical wing lies an operating theater. The building also houses three levels of science classrooms and labs.

Classes end on the hour with professors teaching topics including biology, chemistry, and metallurgical science. The monotonous **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Professor Zhine|Professor Zhine]]** (advisor, he/him) teaches anatomy and Metalborn biology classes and may be found doing an instructional dissection in the operating theater.

### Puzzle Clue

PCs who follow the “Scholar Statue Gaze” (see area [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U7: Bell Tower|U7]]) to the operating theater discover a loose brick in the wall. On the brick is written, “Meet here at Midnight.” This location is where the trials are due to begin. At this point, if the PCs haven’t already seen it, the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Gilded Bickering|Gilded Bickering]]” scene (found later in this chapter) occurs now in the otherwise empty operating theater. Then Tiphiny approaches (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Map Puzzle|Map Puzzle]]”).

## U5: Library

Marble steps lead up to the massive university library. Scribbling pens, murmured conversation, and squeaking book carts can be heard throughout. A librarian immediately approaches to offer assistance, blinking at you from behind thick glasses. “Can I help you find anything? We have an informative public exhibition of the Words of Founding, Pledfellow’s edition. Or perhaps you wish to peruse our extensive broadsheet archive?”

The University of Elendel Library is the most extensive in Scadrial. **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Trudine|Trudine]]** (advisor, she/her) promotes various offerings. The public exhibition of the Words of Founding, Pledfellow’s edition, displays each volume on a pedestal beneath a softly glowing electric lamp.

### Puzzle Clue

“PLED” refers to the showcase, where the first book of the Words of Founding has a bookmark ribbon showing the word “Scholar.”

## U6: Chapel and Graveyard

The school’s interfaith chapel features a domed glass ceiling and pillars carved with Catacendre-inspired reliefs. A Pathian meditation room off the main chamber holds nothing but two chairs. Surrounding the chapel is a small graveyard dotted with weathered grave markers and mistwraith statues.

## U8: Dormitories: Lastrum and Pashadon Halls

Two monolithic three-story brick buildings serve as the Lastrum and Pashadon student dormitories. Both buildings have intimidating stonework accents, iron grates, and miniscule windows.

There are two main dormitories for students living on campus: Lastrum Hall (the men’s dorm) and Pashadon Hall (the women’s dorm). The first two floors contain bunk beds, while the top floor contains finely appointed singles for the richer students, including some nobles pledging the Order.

The dorms are only open to students and family, but the party has little trouble entering Lastrum Hall. **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Marthis|Marthis]]** is found around Lastrum (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Marthis and Masks|Marthis and Masks]]” later in this chapter).

Pashadon is harder to enter, with **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Madam Penfor|Madam Penfor]]** (bodyguard, she/her), the hall matron, stopping visitors at the front desk.

## U9: Society Row

Covered in ivy, prestigious society houses stand in a rambling row toward the north end of campus. Shared interest group housing here includes the Metallurgist’s Society, All Athletes’ Club, the Order of a Thousand Eyes, and the Cross-Cultural Village.

Only society members are allowed to enter the society houses. On a successful DC 13 Athletics test, a PC can climb the ivy of the Order’s manor wall to reach an open window on the upper floor.

### Exploring the Order’s Manor

The Order’s house has a gilded dining room, a shelldry parlor, a private library, and member bedrooms. The basement boasts a massive wine cellar. When a PC enters the house, if the PCs haven’t already seen the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Gilded Bickering|Gilded Bickering]]” scene (found later in this chapter), that scene now plays out.

While exploring, the PCs find a rosewood guitar (musical instrument) worth 100 boxings. Additionally, on a successful DC 12 Perception test, they discover the metal belts and blindfolds for the Midnight Trials’ “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Third Trial: Battle Royale|Battle Royale]]” later in this chapter.

On a failure, progress the Midnight Approaches event by 2 as **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Ylvwa|Ylvwa]]** (commoner, she/her) finds the party; the harassed Terris head maid is trapped in debt to the Order, and if the PCs lend a sympathetic ear to her long, tragic life story, she won’t call campus security.

## Preparing for the Trials

As the PCs begin preparing for the trials, introduce the Midnight Approaches event to the players. Note the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Gilded Bickering|Gilded Bickering]]” scene, which can trigger in different locations. Now three tasks await the party:

**Acquire Masks.** The PCs must acquire masks, and Marthis can help. The PCs can easily find him in Lastrum Hall ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U8: Dormitories: Lastrum and Pashadon Halls|U8]]). See “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Marthis and Masks|Marthis and Masks.]]”

**Procure a “Cazzian Offering.”** Wilko (if present), a PC with the Religion expertise, or a successful DC 18 Lore test reveals that the term “Cazzian offering” refers to the Cazzi people, who held a religious tradition of presenting a set of bones and telling their owner’s story. ## Event: Midnight Approaches

Announce you’re beginning an event (see “Events” in chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*). This negative event, Midnight Approaches, has six slots to loosely represent time running out before the Midnight Trials begin.

![[pg121_Event_Midnight-Approaches.webp]]

### Progressing the Event

When a PC gains a Complication during a scene on campus, you can progress the event by 1, trading an immediate Complication to reduce the time the PCs have left to prepare. Raise the stakes whenever the PCs do something suspicious. Attracting campus security is a sure way to get hassled, burning precious time. Inform the players this event also progresses in these special situations:

**Taking Time.** If a PC fails a test, the player can choose to turn the failure into a success by describing how their PC solves the problem, given plenty of time. (This rule can also be used to bypass a step of the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Map Puzzle|Map Puzzle.]]”) Each time they do this, progress the event by 1.

**Leaving Campus.** If PCs leave campus, it takes time to travel elsewhere in the city and back. Each time the PCs go off campus, progress the event by 1.

### Triggering the Event

If you fill all six event slots, the bell tower tolls midnight, and the PCs know time is up: The trials are beginning.

If the PCs have found masks and the trials’ starting location by midnight, they can join the trials immediately after the event triggers (even if they don’t have bones to present), arriving just in time. Otherwise, the PCs arrive late, scrambling to complete bare-minimum preparations (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Ill-Prepared|Ill-Prepared]]” later in this chapter). Either way, proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|The Midnight Trials.]]”

## Marthis and Masks

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Marthis|Marthis]]** (society pledge, he/him), who’s easy to find outside Lastrum Hall ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U8: Dormitories: Lastrum and Pashadon Halls|U8]]), is a noble student and one of this year’s pledges for the Order. He’s desperate to pass the trials by any means possible.

If the PCs mention K.T., Marthis is eager to talk. He shares the following:

- His dad will be so upset if Marthis doesn’t become an “Eye.”

- The Order only accepts “good families.”

- Some famous Eyes are on campus today. Marthis gushes over Kwylliam’s legendary reputation, saying he once rode a horse through the library.

If the PCs agree to help Marthis (or treat him with consistent kindness), he also shares:

- The trials require knowing university history. Studying campus locations may be helpful.

- Those who pass the trials must unmask for a party at the end.

Marthis provides the PCs with masks if they can offer any remotely plausible solution to help him pass the trials (whether honestly or by cheating). Alternatively, Marthis doesn’t have the trials’ starting location yet, and he’s happy to trade masks for information. The masks are skull masks with star-shaped Inquisitor spikes for eyes.

## A Cazzian Offering

The PCs must find suitable bones to offer during the trials. Run this search as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). The party’s options include the following:

**The Chapel ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U6: Chapel and Graveyard|U6]]).** Graverobbing from the chapel’s small graveyard is tricky due to the watchful groundskeeper, **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Fan|Fan]]** (they/them). PCs can distract Fan (DC 15 Deception), pick a story-worthy grave (DC 14 Lore), dig quickly (DC 16 Athletics), and gather what the party needs (DC 16 Medicine). This is a good place to raise the stakes.

**The Labs ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U4: Advanced Science and Metallurgy Labs|U4]]).** The labs have bones for teaching purposes, but they’re in well-observed areas. PCs can pose as guest faculty (DC 16 Deception), sneak into the back rooms (DC 14 Stealth), fi nd a suitable specimen (DC 10 Medicine), and smuggle bones out of the building (DC 16 Thievery).

**Student Center ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U3: Student Center|U3]]).** The student center’s theater has a fake skeleton, but play rehearsals are in progress. PCs can audition for the play (DC 14 Persuasion), impress students with their dramaturgical knowledge (DC 13 Lore), or make up a plausible explanation (DC 13 Deception).

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor succeeds if the party accrues 4 successes before 3 failures, or if they otherwise complete the objective.

**Success.** The party acquires bones.

**Failure.** The party acquires bones but attracts campus security; progress the Midnight Approaches event by 2.

## The Midnight Trials

Once preparations are complete (or the Midnight Approaches event triggers), the PCs have one chance to infiltrate the Midnight Trials. In the tunnels below the University of Elendel, answers about the Founder’s Key—and the Order’s darkest secrets—await.

## Ill-Prepared

If the PCs are either missing masks or don’t know the starting location when the trials begin, then moments after midnight, the party is approached by two **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Society Initiate Era 2|society initiates]]** who assume the PCs are lost pledges. The snickering initiates offer the party masks and the trials’ starting location—but only if one of the PCs completes a dare to first drink an entire bottle of sweet rum and sparkle tonic, then moon a blackcoat.

A PC who accepts must drink the rum and then make a DC 18 Discipline test. On a failure, they become Exhausted [−1] before mooning campus security. The blackcoats simply roll their eyes, unimpressed, but the story eventually reaches Kwylliam, who can leverage this for his Brutal Blackmail action.

If the PCs don’t accept, the society initiates can alternatively be bribed (500 boxings). They spend focus to resist Intimidation tests and fight back if attacked, but once either one runs out of focus or is reduced to half health, they both provide whatever the PCs need and flee in abject terror. If this occurs, Kwylliam eventually hears; he not only uses the information for his Brutal Blackmail action, but he also starts a nasty rumor about the PCs “attacking defenseless children.”

Once the PCs have masks and the trials’ starting location, continue to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Running the Trials|Running the Trials]]” (where the PCs arrive late).

## Running the Trials

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Kwylliam Elariel|Kwylliam Elariel]]** runs the Midnight Trials, a job he relishes, with the assistance of eight fourth-year society initiates. To participate, the PCs must wear masks and pose as initiates; two society initiates guard the entrance to the trials, refusing entry to any unmasked characters. Note how many trials they pass, for “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Judgment|Judgment]]” later.

### K.T. and Marthis

If K.T. infiltrates with the party, her confidence is inspiring, and the PCs each gain an advantage on their first test in the first trial. Otherwise, she infiltrates alone, and the party discovers her as the first trial ends. K.T. passes the trials unless the PCs interfere. If a PC rolls an Opportunity, K.T. might suavely create a distraction, allowing a PC to reroll a failed test using a different skill.

On a Complication, a society pledge might approach the PCs and try to make conversation, mistaking them for Marthis.

## Operating Theatrics

As the masked PCs enter the observation theater in the labs ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#U4: Advanced Science and Metallurgy Labs|U4]]), begin the Midnight Trials by reading:

The operating theater’s tiered observation semicircles lie in shadow. There, rows of figures stand. Each wears a skull mask with star-shaped Inquisitor spikes for eyes. In the center of the theater, illuminated by pale starlight, Kwylliam stands in crimson robes, identifiable despite his mask.

There are twenty pledges in the room. If the PCs arrived on time, read:

Kwylliam’s voice breaks the silence: “Welcome to the Midnight Trials. I am your nameless host, and tonight, Death himself shall judge your worth. We start tonight’s sacred tradition with a Cazzian offering.”

Otherwise, if the PCs arrive late, read:

## First Trial: Offering the Bones

For the first trial, the PCs must approach the operating table, gather their nerve, and present their bones as impressively as possible. Other groups of initiates offer family bones or skeletons “borrowed” from risky locations; one group even has lion bones.

### A Brave Face

First, as the party steps forward under the unnerving gaze of the masked pledges, have each PC make a DC 10 Discipline test (increase this to DC 16 if the party arrived late). On a failure, the PC loses 1 focus and, disquieted by the crowd, gains a disadvantage on their upcoming test to present the bones.

### A Bold Story

Next, Kwylliam prompts the party to tell the bones’ story. Ask for each PC’s contribution, having them make a relevant DC 15 skill test to help present the bones. Depending on which bones the party brought, the PCs can gain one or more of the following:

- **Advantages** for rare or noble bones.

- **Disadvantages** for fake bones, animal bones, or not bringing a complete skeleton.

### Completing the First Trial

If at least half of the PCs (rounded up) succeed on their test to present the bones, Kwylliam gravely says, “This offering is accepted,” takes the bones, and the PCs pass the first trial. Otherwise, he returns the bones, saying, “This offering is unworthy,” and the PCs fail this trial.

If the PCs brought no bones, they automatically fail this trial, earning a nasty laugh from Kwylliam and boos from the other pledges.

## Second Trial: Brains and Brawn

In the second trial, pledges answer questions about campus history while proving their physical endurance.

During this trial’s three rounds, Kwylliam theatrically asks questions while the PCs “wall sit,” braced with their backs against the wall and their knees deeply bent.

### Brawny Competition

At the start of each round, each PC attempts to maintain the wall sit by making an Athletics test with a DC of 10, 12, and 14 for rounds one, two, and three, respectively. Raise the stakes on this test, resolving the results using the [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Brains and Brawn Opportunities and Complications]] table.

### Brainy Trivia

After the Athletics tests, ask each PC a question (refer to the Kwylliam’s Questions table). If the player knows the answer to the question before rolling, their PC succeeds at answering the question. Otherwise, the PC must succeed on a DC 15 Lore test to remember the answer; they gain an advantage if they visited a related campus location during preparations. On a failure, the PC is out. Once each PC answers, a new round begins.

### T1: Old Aqueduct

This area connects to the stone room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]]). An uneven stone bridge stretches across this old underground aqueduct. As the pledges (including the party) arrive

at the bridge for the third trial, read:

The footsteps of many pledges echo off old stone. The sound of rushing water and an earthy musk hang in the damp air.\
Kwylliam’s words echo in the cavernous room: “Are you as nimble as the Lord Mistborn? The rules are simple. Your feet must stay on the stone. If you lose your belt or touch the water, you are disqualified. No drawing blood. No peeking. And no metal vials once we begin, so drink now. The last six standing pass the trial. We begin in ten, nine, eight…”

The PCs can Drink Vial (1) or take one single similar action to prepare before the trial begins. There are twenty hopefuls, but only eight **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Society Pledge Era 2|society pledges]]** actively fight near the PCs. One of these pledges is a **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Society Pledge Lurcher Era 2|Lurcher pledge]]** (who wields Iron Allomancy) and another is a **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Society Pledge Coinshot Era 2|Coinshot pledge]]** (who wields Steel Allomancy); you can find their statistics in the “[[Journal/11 - Appendix A Adversaries#Society Pledge Variants|Society Pledge Variants]]” section alongside that stat block. The non-Misting society pledges use their Invoke Status actions to press the PCs to target their rivals, and they aren’t above ganging up on the PCs. Kwylliam and the society initiates watch eagerly as combat begins.

### Special Rule: Fog of War

If a participant’s primary sense is sight, that sense is obscured by their blindfold and they’re Disoriented. Don’t reveal the map to a player whose character’s senses are obscured, unless their PC successfully peeks (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Battlefield Effects|Battlefield Effects]]”).

### Battlefield Effects

The following effects are active for this combat:

**Disqualification.** Characters who fall into the water, remove their belts or blindfolds, get caught cheating, deal keen damage (or otherwise draw blood), attempt to Drink Vial (1), or spend a full round not touching the walkway are disqualified.

**Hiding.** Characters can Use a Skill (1) to make a Stealth test against the Spiritual defense of each participant with obscured senses. On a success against a participant, that participant can’t sense the character until they reveal their location by attacking or performing another loud action.

**Peeking.** Characters can peek (0) with a DC 13 Thievery test. On a failure, they get caught cheating (see the “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#K.T. and Marthis|Troublemaking]]” sidebar earlier in this chapter). On a success, reveal the battle map to that PC’s player until the end of their turn; the PC ignores the Disoriented condition while the map is revealed in this way. Each time a PC succeeds on a test to peek, increase the DC by 3 for that PC.

**Sabotage.** To frame a participant for cheating via sabotage—such as removing their blindfold or faking bloodshed—a character can Use a Skill (1) to make a relevant test against the Spiritual defense (15) of the observing society initiates. On a success, the target is disqualified. On a failure, the saboteur is disqualified.

**Last Chance.** Characters at risk of falling into the water can use their reaction to Avoid Danger (R), even if they’re Disoriented and otherwise can’t use their reaction. They make an Agility test with a DC equal to the triggering test’s result (or 15 if there was no triggering test). On a success, they fall Prone at the bridge’s edge.

### Completing the Third Trial

The trial ends when only six participants remain. If at least half of the PCs are in the final six, the entire party passes the trial. Otherwise, they fail. Either way, proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#Judgment|Judgment]].”

## Judgment

Once the third trial is complete, society initiates collect all the pledges (and PCs) and lead them—still blindfolded—into the next room (area [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]] on map 6.3) for judgment.

## T2: Stone Room

This area connects to the old aqueduct ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T1: Old Aqueduct|T1]]) and the inner sanctum ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T3: Inner Sanctum|T3]]). If the PCs enter the stone room for judgment, they’re led to seats around a long stone table with the other pledges. Read:

Kwylliam declares, “Now, the crucible reveals the truth. The worthy among you will become an Eye, beholder of secrets, and our sibling immortal. Prepare for judgment.”

Ask the players to close their eyes, and have the characters repeat after Kwylliam, one line at a time:

“Let the fire reveal.”\
“May the Order judge me worthy.”\
“Let me learn the Gilded truths.”\
“Forge my eternal legacy.”

The outcome of the Midnight Trials depends on how many trials the party passed:

**3 Trials Passed.** Only the PCs have proved worthy. A society initiate removes their blindfolds and leads them further in; proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Induction.]]”

**1–2 Trials Passed.** The PCs and four other pledges have proved worthy. Proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Induction.]]”

**0 Trials Passed.** The PCs have proved unworthy. They and the other unworthy pledges remain at the table. The air is heavy with shame as the PCs hear eight other pledges being selected. Proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Dropping Out.]]”

Additionally, K.T. always passes regardless of the party’s result. Meanwhile, Marthis always fails the trials unless the PCs effectively intervened.

## Induction

If the party proves worthy, their induction begins. They and any NPCs who also passed the trials are accompanied by a society initiate to the inner sanctum (area [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T3: Inner Sanctum|T3]] on map 6.3). The PCs at last gain access to the Order’s deepest secrets—including NeBaal.

## T3: Inner Sanctum

This area connects to the stone room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]]) and the urn room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T4: The Urn Room|T4]]). As the PCs enter, read:

Three walls of this room are white marble, covered in carvings, inlaid with gold. One carving is a portrait of Death; one is a complex anatomical diagram; and one is a sprawling family tree. The far wall is smooth black marble, with a lone, large door standing closed in its center

### Judged Worthy

If the party passed the trials and enters the sanctum accompanied by a society initiate, also read:

The society initiate’s voice is solemn: “The Founder forged three keys to secure our inheritance. One was lost in the Catacendre’s fire. The second was deceitfully stolen. This is the third and last surviving Founder’s Key, and you are now its protector.” The society initiate presents a golden key—identical to the one you took from the Archives.

Ask the players which PC accepts the key from the society initiate; if none of the PCs want the key, K.T. takes it eagerly.

The masked society initiate gestures above. “The university exists to train us. We are the worthy.” She motions to the carvings. “Our power and strength come from our blood.” She points at the door. “Your inheritance lies beyond. Tonight, you join us as another Eye, searching for our Gilded treasure, our eternal legacy.” She hands over a small scroll. “Enter the final room. Use the key. And then speak these words.”

After handing over the scroll and key, the society initiate exits to the stone room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]]). The PCs are free to examine the carvings (especially important if the Heir is present) and can move on to the door to the urn room when ready.

### Scroll

The scroll reads: “We come, Scadrial’s noblest bloodlines, judged worthy of our inheritance. Where does our Legacy lie? Reveal to us the Gilded Map.”

### Carvings

Three carvings decorate the walls:

**Inquisitor.** An Inquisitor sits on a throne above a golden city. While the Inquisitor resembles Death, a successful DC 12 Lore test reveals that the eye tattoos and an Elariel signet pin means this is a specific individual: Vadilek Elariel.

**Diagram.** An anatomical diagram shows spikes inserted at different Hemalurgic bind points. A PC who succeeds on a DC 15 Lore or Medicine test and spends at least 5 minutes examining the diagram can mark a milestone toward the “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction).

**Family Tree.** Titled “The Gilded Few” in scrolling script, this family tree shows Gilded Few families, including Seeris, Haught, Buvidas, and Tekiel, with Vadilek Elariel at the top. The tree shows the creation and inheritance of familial Hemalurgic spikes—including Vadilek’s original latticework of spikes—handed down through the generations. Eight-pointed stars represent familial spikes, and golden names represent individuals who sacrificed themselves to create spikes. Idren Seeris’s name is golden. Bayron, Lysarra, and Kwylliam are all in the tree, each with stars for their spikes. On a successful DC 12 Deduction test, a PC realizes the stars are inherited, and a golden name “creates” a new star.

## T4: The Urn Room

This area connects to the inner sanctum ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T3: Inner Sanctum|T3]]). When the PCs enter, read:

Braziers burn in each corner of this marble room, yet the incense can’t cover the musky smell underneath. Two pedestals stand here. On the taller rests a distinctive silvery urn with a prominent lock. Stoneware pipes run from the urn into the back wall. The shorter pedestal holds a black marble bowl, filled with bones.

If the PCs passed the first trial, the bones are the ones they offered. Otherwise, the bones were submitted by another pledge.

The pipes connect to the fountains and dormitory refectory, bringing food and water to the urn’s kandra occupant. A PC who succeeds on a DC 12 Crafting or Deduction test can deduce the pipes’ purpose.

### A Distinctive Urn

The musky smell comes from a distinctive 3-foot-tall urn, silvery and decorated with plant motifs, worn smooth by time and handling. A successful DC 10 Crafting test on the urn reveals it’s made of aluminum and worth 1,000 boxings. It bears an obvious lock.

If the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Founders Key|Founder’s Key]]*** is used, read:

The key turns, opening a spigot on the urn’s side. A mass slurps out among the bones. It resembles writhing, translucent slugs pulsing with blood vessels. The mass moves, first slowly, then with hunger, to surround the bones. The bones spasm, some twitching upright. The mass expands, as though inhaling. Sinew and organs blossom as it contracts. Flesh webs between jawbones, a face forming. Lastly, with a wet plop, two eyes balloon into the skull’s sockets, rolling to look at you. Their gaze is filled with hatred.

This is NeBaal. Their form is hairless and based on the bones in the offering bowl. Describe what the body looks like, then read:

A Faceless Immortal. The hairless creature’s voice is a dry whisper. “A year already? Here for your ‘treasure,’ you entitled scum? Let’s get your pointless interrogation over with.”

## Meeting NeBaal

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/NeBaal|NeBaal]]** (they/them) has been imprisoned by the Gilded Few for centuries. The kandra is only allowed to reform during the annual Midnight Trials, when they endure a ritualistic questioning about the Gilded Map—and ultimately about where the Eternity Spike lies.

### NeBaal’s Demeanor

NeBaal initially assumes the PCs are pledges and responds with disdainful anger. As soon as the PCs make it clear they aren’t with the Order, NeBaal’s demeanor changes, imploring the party for help escaping.

A successful DC 10 Insight test confirms the truth in NeBaal’s words: They’ve been inhumanely imprisoned, and they won’t hurt anyone who helps them. If K.T. is with the party, she’s strongly in favor of freeing NeBaal.

![[pg131_MBL06_NeBaal Revealed_Anastassia Grigorieva.webp]]

**NeBaal revealed - ANA GRIGORIEVA**

### Talking to NeBaal

Until NeBaal understands the PCs aren’t pledges, they focus on two points: The pledges are unworthy, and they should renounce their noble families (who keep NeBaal trapped here). Use the following and NeBaal’s NPC profile (found under “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction|Notable Characters]]” in this book’s introduction) to guide the conversation:

**We come, Scadrial’s Noblest Bloodlines… Reveal to us the Gilded Map.** “You cowards come here year after year, begging for your so-called ‘legacy.’ I’ll tell you what I’ve told your parents and your parents’ parents: I judge you unworthy, Vadilek would judge you unworthy, so stop this torment and let me go.”

**Where is the Gilded Map/treasure?** “Hidden from unworthy eyes, remembered only by me. I am the Gilded Map.”

**What do you want?** “Freedom. Dignity. I’ve been here for centuries. I’m not a human, but I’m still a person.”

**Who did this to you?** “The Gilded Few and their descendants. Though I believe the specifics were Delina Tekiel’s idea.”

**Why should we trust you?** “I’m a kandra. You know of us? I can’t harm humans, and our promises, our Contracts, can’t be broken. If you oppose my captors, freeing me will help you and hurt them—I promise. So the question now is: Why should I trust you?”

### Helping NeBaal Escape

NeBaal desperately wants to escape. Nothing keeps them physically restrained beyond extreme weakness, but they’re Exhausted [−8] and can barely walk. They go along with the PCs’ plans to help them, but always refuse to harm humans. If NeBaal is in their boneless form, they can be smuggled under robes or similar garments.

When the PCs are ready for a daring escape with NeBaal, proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Dropping Out.]]”

## Dropping Out

Whatever happens during the Midnight Trials, the PCs must always make it to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T4: The Urn Room|T4: The Urn Room]]” and rescue NeBaal. The exact flow of events depends on when the party’s identity is revealed, as follows:

**Trials Passed.** If the PCs passed the Midnight Trials and became fully inducted Order initiates, they eventually exit the urn room to find **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Notable/Era 2/Kwylliam Elariel|Kwylliam Elariel]]** and eight unmasked **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Society Initiate Era 2|society initiates]]** waiting in the stone room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]]) with an expensive bottle of wine. Describe the NPCs insisting that the PCs unmask and celebrate. If the party refuses, Kwylliam eventually gets irritated and approaches to rip their masks off. Whenever the PCs are discovered, proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Stolen Secrets.]]”

**Discovered Early.** If the PCs are discovered early or fail the Midnight Trials, ensure they can still proceed to the urn room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T4: The Urn Room|T4]]) to rescue NeBaal. Best case, this happens during “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Induction]]” (when the PCs are already in T4). Worst case, if discovered early in the trials, they’re likely in a room with twenty society pledges, eight society initiates, and Kwylliam Elariel; should this happen, describe the chaos as the PCs are discovered, and give the party a chance to run for the urn room ([[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T4: The Urn Room|T4]]). Once the PCs reach the urn room, have Kwylliam, the society initiates, and the pledges retreat to block the exit, giving the PCs time to rescue NeBaal. When the PCs attack or attempt to leave, proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|Stolen Secrets.]]”

## Stolen Secrets

As the chapter hits a climax, the PCs’ identities have been uncovered, NeBaal desperately needs help escaping, and the party now faces a confrontation with Kwylliam—in a room packed with student witnesses.

## A Challenge

With the PCs’ cover blown, the society members and Kwylliam all move to block their exit, trapping the party (most likely in area [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]]). Thriving on the chaos, Kwylliam makes a play that offers a little risk to the Order members and a large boost to his own ego. Read:

Kwylliam looks your group up and down. “A bold move, I like it.” He glances at the surrounding Order members, then raises his voice, warming to his audience. “If you want a fight, I’ll bite—I challenge you to a crucible! A duel. But let’s make it fair.” He smirks darkly. “All of you, against me. If you dare.”

### Terms

The PCs must agree not to attack the students (pledges or society initiates) and swear to secrecy; the duel goes until one side concedes. If the PCs win, Kwylliam will let them go without calling security (and potentially agree to other stakes, even letting them take NeBaal). If they lose, they agree to owe Kwylliam a favor. To concede, a character must say, “The fire reveals, I cede to the worthy.” If one PC concedes, the entire party concedes.

If the PCs try to negotiate the terms, Kwylliam teases them for being scared.

Kwylliam obviously has the upper hand during this scene: The PCs are virtually guaranteed to lose if they fight the entire Order. However, Kwylliam prefers to keep the students and campus security out of it—though he does threaten to call security if the PCs “attack innocent students.”

### Beginning the Duel

To start the duel, Kwylliam Elariel moves combat to [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#T2: Stone Room|T2]] (if not already there), and the twenty society pledges and eight society initiates surround the perimeter, watching the proceedings. The spectators won’t join or interfere unless the PCs obviously break the terms of the duel. Kwylliam concedes if he’s reduced below half health.

### Aftermath

The combat ends if Kwylliam concedes (continue to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#A Worthy Victory|A Worthy Victory]]”), or if the PCs concede or flee (proceed to “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul#A Trying Defeat|A Trying Defeat]]”).

## A Worthy Victory

If Kwylliam concedes, read:

His beautiful face bloodied, Kwylliam breathes hard, giving an intense look before spitting out the words, “The fire reveals, I cede to the worthy.” After a pregnant pause, he laughs. “That was fun! All right, get out.”

If the party lets Kwylliam approach, he whispers, only for them, “Plans are going swimmingly for Bayron and Lysarra. It’s been ages since anyone outsmarted them. Keep things interesting for me, will you?” He winks, then shouts for the confused pledges and society initiates to move the celebration back to Society Row. No one stops the PCs from leaving with NeBaal.

## A Trying Defeat

If the PCs concede, Kwylliam hands them a card with an address in the Sixth Octant. If they follow up, they spend a week of downtime acting as newsies for The Truth in Elendel, hawking embarrassing stories about themselves.

If the PCs don’t follow up by the end of “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Downtime Before the Salon|Downtime Before the Salon]]” in chapter 8 (or if they fled from battle), Kwylliam spreads vicious rumors about the party’s cowardice, complicating “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|A Fashionable Infiltration]]” in chapter 8.

## Journeying Onward

With the mystery of the Founder’s Key solved, bigger questions about the Gilded Map lie ahead. Having uncovered the identity of their enemies—the modernday Gilded Few—the PCs now flee into the night with their new kandra ally. The Era 2 characters advance to 5th level.
