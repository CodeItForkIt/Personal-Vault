## Vault Traps

**Chapter 4**

The Vault Traps table and the Trap Modifications table list the traps and modifications you can build in your vault. Each has the following attributes:

**Prerequisites.** Requirements for building the trap or modification. Building doesn’t expend plans (unlike labor and materials); if you have a plans score of 2, you can build all items that require “Plans 2” or lower. For trap modifications, their prerequisites include the traps they modify.

**Materials.** The number of materials deducted from your materials total. Expend materials when you attempt to build the trap (succeed or fail).

**DC.** The test result you need to reach to successfully build the trap. Lower this by expending labor.

**Effect**. What the trap does.

**Size.** The space the trap covers on the map. This is expressed in both feet and grid squares. Sizes are approximate suggestions.

Base traps can be built any number of times, so long as you have the materials to do so. However, each modification (such as Acid Coating) can be added to a particular trap only once.
