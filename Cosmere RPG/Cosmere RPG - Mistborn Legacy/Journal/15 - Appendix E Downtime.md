# Appendix E: Downtime

## Downtime Overview

During the *Mistborn Legacy* adventure, the PCs have downtime between scenes to spend in Luthadel (in Era 1) and Elendel (in Era 2). During downtime, each PC can address their individual goals, projects, and interests, as described in the “Downtime” section in chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*. This appendix contains guidance for downtime activities that support the story of *Mistborn Legacy*.

Some downtime activities progress a PC’s personal goals, and others can provide rewards such as companions, patrons, hideouts, or status in organizations. For more information on these rewards, see “Rewards” in chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*.

## Downtime in Luthadel (Era 1)

The following section provides some additional options for downtime activities in Luthadel, during Era 1. Not all chapters offer downtime, and a few of these options are only available at certain points in the adventure. Optionally, consult “Luthadel” in chapter 6 of the *Mistborn World Guide* for more information on the locations mentioned in this section. ## Crafting

**Key Locations:** Hotel District, Kenton Street, PC Canal Boat Hideout, and Southbridge

Workshops and resources previously out of reach of the skaa become more accessible after the revolution, though the siege makes materials scarce. Crafting in Luthadel could involve:

**Canal Boat Maintenance.** The crew’s canal boat counts as a hideout, which the PCs could upgrade, repair, redesign, or improve.

**Metalmind Mentoring.** Feruchemists can make progress toward their “Construct Your Metalminds” goals as usual. If they search for a mentor, they may meet Bezryl from [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]].

## Dancing Lessons

**Key Locations:** PC Canal Boat Hideout

This special downtime activity is most useful during “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Downtime Before the Ball|Downtime Before the Ball]]” in chapter 2. If PCs don’t have the High Society expertise, they probably don’t know how to dance. Enge offers to teach them.

## Disrupting the Gilded Few

**Key Locations:** Fellise, Keep Haught, Keep Buvidas, and Hotel District

After the events in Urteau, the Gilded Few hunt the party. PCs can use downtime to lay false trails or disrupt Gilded resources; this is a good place to raise the stakes. Disrupting the Gilded Few could involve:

**Gaining Infamy.** PCs could progress a goal to “Intimidate the Gilded Few’s Forces.” Once complete, PCs gain an advantage whenever they attempt to convince rivals and minions from the Gilded Few to back down from a combat or conversation.

**Intercepting Resources.** Damaging trade negotiations, souring deals between noble houses, and hijacking supply shipments could disrupt Gilded Few members and their cash flows. PCs might gain boxings or narrative rewards from these activities.

## Improving Relationships

**Key Locations:** Brassgate, Kenton Street, and PC Canal Boat Hideout

New faces and new factions are gaining power in Luthadel. PCs can use downtime to gain status with organizations or develop relationships with NPCs. Improving relationships could involve:

**Spreading the Faith.** A PC can join the growing Church of the Survivor movement, rising in the organization’s ranks, or they could gain companions by attending services. A PC might even approach a notable member of the church (such as Demoux) for patronage.

**Repairing Relationships.** PCs who want to improve their relationship with an NPC (such as the Urteau refugees, Cynwise, Cadme, Anastas, Bezryl, Kandor, or Enge) could spend time with or do favors for an NPC. This could progress a goal to turn a sour relationship into a positive one, or unlock companions or patron benefits.

## Recuperating

**Key Locations:** Canton of Resource Headquarters, Kenton Street, Luthadel Garrison, and Keep Haught (see [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]])

Organized healing services are sparse in Luthadel, which only grows worse during the siege.

## Researching

**Key Locations:** Canton of Finance Headquarters, Kenton Street, Fourwell Crossroads, and Old Gate

In the revolution, libraries burned alongside noble keeps, but many repositories of knowledge remain. Researching in Luthadel could involve:

**Hidden Knowledge.** Procuring a rare tome could gain the PC an advantage on tests to learn about Hemalurgy (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction).

**Legalese.** Cross-referencing legal records could reveal Gilded Few connections and resources, giving advantage on a future effort to “Disrupt the Gilded Few” or foreshadowing future chapter events.

**Rumors.** PCs can research current events by hitting the streets and making a DC 12 Deduction, Insight, Perception, or Persuasion test. Succeed or fail, they always learn at least one rumor about upcoming chapter events, NPCs, or the siege. (During [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|chapter 2]], refer to the [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Rumors]] table.) On a success, they learn two rumors.

## Resettling Refugees

**Key Locations:** Kandor Construction (see [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]]) and Brassgate

If the party escorted Cynwise and the prisoners to Luthadel, PCs can use downtime in Luthadel to help these refugees settle in. Cynwise finds a job on their own (working for Kandor Construction—see [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]]), but the other refugees (Aime, Roben, Tuss, and Pella) could use assistance finding employment or making the empty warehouse Cynwise found them more hospitable. Twolocal groups—the Church of the Survivor and Kandor Construction—can potentially provide employment or community.

Ask what the PC does to assist and have them make a DC 12 test with a relevant skill; on a success, their efforts are helpful, and the refugees are forever grateful. Depending on the PC’s choices, the refugees might seek to return the favor by providing rumors from the skaa community, assistance during downtime, or help during later chapters.

Regardless of the outcome, one refugee, **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 1/Roben|Roben]]** (commoner, he/him), offers to stay with the PCs’ canal boat as a companion.

## Self-Reflection

**Key Locations:** Fountain Square and Kredik Shaw

Luthadel is finding its new identity after the revolution upset a thousand years of rule. As the city reinvents itself, so too might the PCs. Places of recent history might offer the PCs needed introspection.

## Suiting Up

**Key Locations:** Hotel District, Commercial District, and Kenton Street

PCs can use downtime to find suitable formal wear (see “Armor” and “Items” in chapter 7 of the *Mistborn Handbook*):

**Shopping.** Tailor and dress shops sell ***fine clothing*** (100 boxings) and ***breakaway clothing*** (150 boxings). Prices in Luthadel are high, but if PCs succeed on a DC 15 Persuasion test to negotiate with the tailor, they can earn a 25-boxing discount.

**Crafting.** The PCs can engage in crafting to manufacture outfits, improve purchased outfits, or make armor Presentable. See the “Crafting” rules in chapter 7 of the *Mistborn Handbook*. If Enge assists, PCs gain an advantage on their Crafting tests. Raw materials for clothing cost 20 boxings.

PCs who successfully acquire fine clothing gain an advantage on their first social test at parties or meetings with nobility, such as during chapter 2’s “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|Soiree of Secrets.]]” If all PCs have fine clothing, the [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Event: Rising Reputations|Rising Reputations]] event in chapter 2 starts with one slot already filled.

## Training

**Key Locations:** Kenton Street, Luthadel Garrison, and Southbridge

Skilled mentors can be found, but their time is in great demand due to the upheaval of the revolution and collapse of the Great Houses. Alternatively, experts can be found among friendly NPCs (or PCs). Training in Luthadel could involve:

**Becoming Cosmopolitan.** PCs can spend time in social situations that used to be barred to them, enabling them to gain the Underworld expertise, the High Society expertise, or other cultural expertises of interest.

**New Tricks.** When you use a mentor to train in a weapon expertise you already have, you gain the Focused condition for the next combat scene in which you use that weapon.

## Working a Profession

**Key Locations:** Commercial District, Industrial District, and River Channerel

From clearing the wrecks of burned noble keeps to manual labor in the streets, canals, and factories, there are many opportunities to make money in Luthadel. Working a profession in Luthadel could involve:

**Reputable Work.** PCs who engage in reputable work earn 4d6 boxings every five days. They can earn an additional 1d6 boxings if their work is related to a relevant expertise or if they return to the same job over multiple downtimes.

**Smuggling.** Smuggling pays more, but at least two PCs must be involved, and each involved PC must make a relevant DC 12 skill test with the stakes raised. On a success, that PC makes 5d10 boxings every five days. Unsuccessful PCs are caught, earn no boxings, and spend 1d4 days dealing with the consequences.

## Crafting

**Key Locations:** The Village (Fifth Octant), Independent Craftfolk (Third Octant), and University of Elendel (First Octant)

Laboratories and inventors abound in Elendel, and advances in the electrical, chemical, and industrial sectors happen every day. Crafting in Elendel could involve:

**Hazekilling.** The PCs could acquire or craft bespoke weapons and ammunition to counter Metalborn foes.

**Reverse Engineering.** If the PCs gained access to some of Bayron’s inventions, successfully investigating them could gain the PCs an advantage on tests to disable similar devices in future scenes.

**Safehouse Upgrades.** The crew could repair, redesign, and improve their hideout, which they may reestablish in [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|chapter 6]] (see “Hideouts” in chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*).

## Disrupting the Gilded Trio

**Key Locations:** Conrad Industries offices (Seventh Octant), Tekiel Trainyard (Eighth Octant), and The Truth in Elendel offices (Tekiel Tower and Sixth Octant)

After the National Archives heist, the Gilded Trio are on the lookout for the party. PCs can use downtime to lay false trails or disrupt the Trio’s resources; this is a good place to raise the stakes. Disrupting the Gilded Trio may involve:

**Expert Espionage.** PCs could gain access to Conrad Industries designs to learn about operatives’ gear (see [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|chapter 8]]) or armored cars (see [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]]) before they appear in later chapters.

**Dirty Laundry.** Looking into the Young Ascendants or poking around the Tekiel trainyard could provide evidence of Lysarra’s connection with the Morgenthax crime family.

**Redefine the Truth.** A PC could fight fire with fire by taking out ads in broadsheets owned by Kwylliam’s competitors. Appreciative noble houses might become patrons to any PCs who publicly stand up to Kwylliam.

## Improving Relationships

**Key Locations:** The Drunken Spur (Fourth Octant), Marehope Botanical Gardens (First Octant), Razzy Gillespie Carnival (Eighth Octant), and Warren Tresting’s Social Club (The Trustwarren, Fourth Octant)

Elendel is a massive city with residents from all walks of life. PCs can use downtime to gain status with organizations or improve or repair relationships with NPCs. Improving relationships could involve:

**Completing Goals.** PCs may have goals to help Marthis or NPCs related to their legacies; downtime is the perfect chance to foster those relationships.

**Repairing Relationships.** PCs who want to improve their relationship with an NPC (such as Professor Wilko, K.T., NeBaal, Marthis, or Merid) could do favors for or spend time with the NPC. This could turn a sour relationship into a positive one, or it could unlock companions or patron benefits.

**Show NeBaal Around.** A PC could introduce NeBaal to the post-Catacendre world, showing them technological or culinary innovations or otherwise acclimating them to Era 2. Kandra PCs may know of the Trustwarren, a kandra speakeasy.

## Recuperating

**Key Locations:** Galivan Memorial Hospital (First Octant), and the Village (Fifth Octant)

Healthcare services in Elendel are advanced and prevalent, with medical practices in every octant.

## Researching

**Key Locations:** Exquisite Excursions (Fourth Octant), University of Elendel (First Octant), and National Archives (Second Octant)

Higher learning is held sacred in Elendel, with museums, libraries, and experts in every field available in the city. Additionally, books aren’t the only places to research; hitting the streets and visiting various locations in Elendel is a great way to gather gossip. Researching in Elendel could involve:

**Appeals and Red Tape.** Engaging with the legal system could protect the PCs against repercussions from the constabulary, the National Archives, or University of Elendel blackcoats.

**Hidden History.** Studying historical records could help advance a goal to discover more about the Gilded Few’s history. If the PCs are members of the Guardians (see [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]]), Archivist Wynn can help with access to the Archives and research.

**Rumors.** PCs can research current events by hitting the streets and making a DC 12 Deduction, Insight, Perception, or Persuasion test. Succeed or fail, they always learn at least one rumor about upcoming chapter events or NPCs. On a success, they learn two rumors. Trellis Draam’s chocolate café, Exquisite Excursions, is a great place to catch high society gossip.

## Self-Reflection

**Key Locations:** Augur’s Well Soothing Parlor (Eighth Octant), Church of the Survivor (Fourth Octant), Field of Rebirth, and the Village (Fifth Octant)

A locus of culture and history for the entire Basin, Elendel is a city of contradictions, and its people must find their place in the tension between tradition and advancement. Self-reflection in Elendel could involve:

**Sacred Places.** The Field of Rebirth and various religious institutions throughout Elendel are excellent locations for soul-searching.

**Past Lives.** Locations related to the PC’s Era 1 legacy connection could reveal new self-revelations via stories or flashbacks from the Era 1 player. Superstitious PCs may visit the Augur’s Well, a Soothing parlor with a fortune teller who claims to connect people with their past lives.

## Training

**Key Locations:** The Silver Voyager (First Octant) and University of Elendel (First Octant)

Businesses, guilds, and noble houses provide training in all their disciplines—for a price. Training in Elendel could involve:

**New Tricks.** When you use a mentor to train in a weapon expertise you already have, you gain the Focused condition for the next combat scene in which you use that weapon.

**Higher Education.** If the PCs didn’t run afoul of campus security (see [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|chapter 6]]), the University of Elendel offers world-class instructors in many subjects. With Wilko’s help, PCs can audit a class or gain an audience with Elendel’s finest academics. This reduces the time needed for training to 7 days but increases the cost to 25 boxings a day.

**Guardian Resources.** If the PCs are inducted into the Guardians (see [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]]), they can find mentors in expertises related to the Guardians, and they gain training through the organization at no cost.

## Working a Profession

Key Locations: World-class Restaurants (Fifth Octant), Elendel’s Factories (Seventh Octant), Metalurgistics Allied (Seventh Octant), and Office Buildings (First or Second Octant)

In a city of millions, there’s always work to be done. Working a profession in Elendel could involve:

**Reputable Work.** PCs who engage in reputable work earn 4d6 × 2 boxings every five days. They can earn an additional 2d6 boxings if their work is related to a relevant expertise or if they return to the same job over multiple downtimes.

**Object Liberation.** Less scrupulous work pays more but at least two PCs must be involved, it takes 1d6 days, and each involved PC must make a relevant DC 12 test with the stakes raised. On a success, that PC can fence or keep a historical Era 1 item worth 6d10 × 2 boxings per five days. Unsuccessful PCs are caught, earn no boxings, and spend 1d4 days dealing with the consequences.
