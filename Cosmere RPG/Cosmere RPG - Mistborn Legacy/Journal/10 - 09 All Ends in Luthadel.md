# 09: All Ends in Luthadel

Throughout their Era 2 adventures, the PCs witnessed the lengths the Gilded Trio have gone to in order to achieve their baleful ambitions. In this final chapter, the party beholds the power—and decides the ultimate fate—of the Eternity Spike.

## Running This Chapter

The Gilded Trio travels north in a Breaknaught train with the stolen latticework and a captured Professor Azmine Wilko. They race to complete the Ascension ritual and use the Eternity Spike before the PCs catch up for one final, fatal confrontation.

## Chapter Flow

As the chapter begins, the party must assemble the Gilded Map and follow the Gilded Trio north to Old Luthadel, where they can rescue Professor Wilko at the Trio’s base camp before taking on a dungeon crawl through the Eternity Vault.

Before running this chapter, prepare the vault dungeon based on the choices (and dungeon map) the players made during [[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault|chapter 4]]. This GM setup work is covered step-by-step under “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Vault Dungeon Setup|Vault Dungeon Setup.]]” You must also decide which of the Gilded Trio is the “chosen one”—the main villain of your campaign. Choose whichever villain makes your players’ story feel the most exciting and personal; that villain will receive the Eternity Spike and “ascend.” This chapter’s read-aloud text assumes all three Gilded Trio members have survived this far, but if one or two of the Trio were killed before chapter 9, prepare to modify the provided read-aloud text to account for their absence.

PC legacies function differently during this final chapter. Retrieve the “My Legacy” handouts the players filled out during [[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault|chapter 4]], as the Era 1 and Era 2 PCs’ legacies interconnect during the final battle (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|Threads of Fate]]”). Also review the special “Chronicler Legacy” and “Convict Legacy” sidebars in this chapter.

Lastly, when preparing for the final battle in “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|Freedom for Fools,]]” review the **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 9/Hemalurgic Monstrosity|Hemalurgic monstrosity]]** stat block, which provides features and actions to represent your chosen main villain. The other surviving Trio members join the fight with modified stat blocks, functioning as rivals instead of bosses.

If the party strikes down the Hemalurgic monstrosity, the Gilded Few are defeated, and the PCs fulfill the legacies of the Eternity Spike’s original Era 1 protectors. What happens next is entirely up to the Era 2 PCs—but in “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Postlude|Postlude,]]” Ironeyes has a suggestion to make.

## Character Advancement

The Era 2 characters start this chapter at 8th level and advance to 9th level once they enter the vault dungeon. Upon defeating the Hemalurgic monstrosity and putting an end to the Gilded Few, the PCs advance to 10th level.

## Journey to the Past

The chapter starts immediately after the events of [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|chapter 8]]. Give the PCs a chance to quickly regroup for pursuing the Gilded Trio. If the PCs aren’t aware of any of the following, NeBaal shares:

- Professor Wilko mentioned rumors the Trio had found Old Luthadel, with Lysarra funding a secret excavation site there for almost a year.

- The purpose of the Gilded Map is to navigate the tunnels below Old Luthadel to find the vault entrance. Combined, the three map fragments show the way.

- With Wilko and her journal of notes on the Gilded Map, the Trio can now find the Eternity Vault holding the Eternity Spike in the Old Luthadel tunnels. It’s just a matter of time.

- The spikes Lysarra removed from the Gilded Few are the latticework needed for the Eternity Spike’s Ascension Ritual.

- The Breaknaught was headed to Old Luthadel. For both Scadrial’s future and Wilko’s safety, the PCs should follow as soon as possible.

### Friendly Faces

Both NeBaal and Merid (or, if Merid died, another member of the Guardians organization: Archivist Wynn Letrelle from [[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|chapter 5]]) proactively find the PCs before they leave. Several other NPCs are also available (if they survived), should the PCs seek them out:

**NeBaal.** NeBaal is adamant about traveling with the party. They’re worried for Wilko and feel responsible for everything leading to this moment. They recount the heroic actions taken by the Era 1 PCs, and say they can’t stand idly by.

**K.T. Quiven.** If the PCs seek out K.T., they can’t find her. Investigating her dorm room turns up stolen Conrad Industries uniforms as well as notes on Lysarra’s Breaknaught train car. It seems she’s gone undercover again. If the PCs didn’t find the Sublevel 1 platform, they can follow K.T.’s notes now to do so.

**Marthis Elariel/Tekiel.** If the PCs saved Marthis, he tells them everything he gleaned about Lysarra’s preparations for traveling to Old Luthadel, such as the planned route and the dry climate. This accrues 1 free success toward the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Ruins|To the Ruins]]” endeavor.

**Merid or Wynn.** If Merid survived, he provides the PCs with the blueprints for the Eternity Vault. Give the players the vault layout map they created during [[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault|chapter 4]], including the markings for all the traps. The Guardians pledge to do everything they can to help. If Merid is dead, Wynn still brings the PCs the Eternity Vault plans on behalf of the Guardians, but the secret society is in disarray and unable to provide any additional assistance.

## To the Ruins

While the PCs can follow Lysarra’s secret railway tracks underground, getting to Old Luthadel is still no small feat. The party must travel 600 miles by train or boat to the region around Callingfale in the Northern Roughs. After that, they must navigate around 75 miles of unforgiving rocky desert with no roads to reach the ruins of Old Luthadel. Run the party’s journey as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). There are three obstacles the PCs must overcome to reach the ruins: Deciphering the map pieces, securing fast travel, and surviving the frontier itself.

### Special Rule: Ascension Rising

The countdown to the Ascension event begins now (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Event: Ascension|Event: Ascension]]” later in this chapter). Don’t fully reveal the event yet, but do introduce an unnamed negative event to the players now, along with an event track with eight Complication slots. Progress the event by 1 for every Gilded Map piece the party never acquired. Show the players whenever you progress the event (but the purpose of the event won’t be fully revealed until “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|A Race to Eternity]]”). For now, only progress the event when directed, not on Complications.

Failures on the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Ruins|To the Ruins]]” endeavor represent delays, giving the Trio time to progress the Ascension. Each time the PCs accrue a failure during “To the Ruins,” fill a slot on the event track, and describe evidence of the Gilded Trio and their forces moving quickly ahead—for example, abandoned campsites, tire treads in the dirt, or rumors of a noble-led force moving northeast.

### Special Rule: Quiet Moments

Two roleplaying scenes interlace with this endeavor. The following order is suggested:

- Run “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Quiet Moment: Assembling the Map|Assembling the Map]]” after deciphering the map pieces.

- Run “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|We’ve Come So Far]]” after securing travel, while surviving the frontier, or if the PCs fail the endeavor.

### Running the Endeavor

The PCs face the following obstacles in order:

**Decipher the Map Pieces.** If the PCs have all three map pieces, they automatically gain one success, and the map is simple to assemble. Otherwise, to plot out the map, they can cross-reference their partial map with historical Luthadel maps (DC 20 Deduction) or use natural knowledge to decode it (DC 18 Survival). Progress to the next obstacle after 1 success or 2 failures.

**Secure Travel.** A PC might negotiate for express travel on a train or boat (DC 18 Persuasion) or identify quality horses for the overland trip in the Northern Roughs (DC 16 Survival). If Merid survived, PCs gain an advantage on these tests, and the Guardians offer an experimental blimp as transport—but it requires a dedicated mechanic (DC 16 Crafting) and is prone to breaking down. Progress to the next obstacle after 1 success.

**Survive the Frontier.** A PC could identify hazards in the Northern Roughs (DC 16 Survival), help avoid unwanted attention from bandits or koloss clans (DC 18 Perception), or keep morale up during the trek (DC 16 Leadership). This is a good place to raise the stakes.

### Quiet Moment: Assembling the Map

This scene happens shortly after the map is deciphered. If the PCs have the full map, NeBaal is hopeful. Read:

“The mask, the medal, and the mistcloak.” NeBaal moves them together, matching the stitching and engravings until the three pieces overlap and reveal a design. NeBaal steps back and folds their arms.\
“The Gilded Map.”

Otherwise, if the PCs don’t have the entire map, NeBaal is fearful. Read:

NeBaal stares at the fragmented map in their hands, eyes hard. They look up. “I won’t lie to you. This is going to be harder without the complete Gilded Map. We’ve learned what we can from what we have. The Trio doesn’t have the full map either, but they’ve got Azmine and her notes. I fear it’s truly going to be a race for the Spike.”

NeBaal wishes to travel with the party. If the PCs have a good relationship with NeBaal, the kandra quietly swears they will harm the Gilded Few if necessary (which breaks kandra taboos).

### Quiet Moment: We’ve Come So Far

One night during the journey, NeBaal initiates this conversation before everyone goes to sleep. NeBaal admits they’d like a distraction from worrying about Wilko’s safety; they may share regrets about harm that came to the Era 1 characters.

Use the following questions to get the characters talking. Go around the table once, asking each player to pick one of the questions for their PC to either answer or ask of another character:

- Which of the Gilded Trio do you think is the most dangerous?

- How do you feel about Lysarra’s slaughter of the Gilded Few?

- How does it feel to be heading to Old Luthadel, the heart of the Final Empire?

- If you obtained the Eternity Spike, what would you do with it?

- Is it a good idea to study ancient powers, or should they be left in the past?

- If faced with the choice of saving Professor Wilko or stopping the Gilded Trio, what should the party do?

- What do you think about the Guardians and the secret society they’ve built?

- If you don’t live through this, are there any requests you have for the party?

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor succeeds if the party accrues 5 successes before 3 failures, or if they otherwise bypass all three obstacles.

**Success.** The PCs make excellent time, arriving at Old Luthadel rested and ready for action, right on the Trio’s tail. Each PC gains the Determined condition.

**Failure.** The journey is taxing. As the PCs arrive, it’s clear the Gilded Trio are several steps ahead of the PCs.

## Old Luthadel

The PCs find that the cave entrance to the Old Luthadel ruins is surrounded by a Gilded Trio encampment crawling with enemy forces. As the PCs arrive, read:

Broken rock slopes down toward a yawning cavern maw; beyond it lie the tunnels of Old Luthadel and the ruins buried below the valley floor. Flickering gas lights reveal a full archaeological dig site is sprawled across your approach. Canvas tents, excavator rigs, and short rail lines crowd the area. Conrad Industries engineers weave between supply crates, and well-armed Morgenthax toughs patrol the perimeter.

The camp blocks the PCs’ decent into the Luthadel ruins, but it also offers a chance to learn what awaits below. NeBaal urges the PCs to search for Wilko.

Run the PCs sneaking through the camp as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). The PCs can complete optional objectives along the way to the tunnel entrance: investigate the Trio’s plans, sow chaos among the Gilded forces, and rescue Professor Wilko. As the endeavor starts, specifically ask the players whether they’re trying to rescue Wilko, as doing so makes this endeavor more difficult. Possible obstacles include:

**Recon Command Tents.** Creeping around canvas walls and supply stacks brings the party close to Morgenthax mercenaries (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Hazekiller Era 2|hazekillers]]**) and **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Engineer Era 2|Conrad engineers.]]** PCs can dart between tents unnoticed (DC 16 Stealth) or don a disguise (DC 17 Thievery) to overhear useful information: “Ascension preparation” has begun, the Trio are already in the tunnels, and the professor is being kept in a tent at the west edge of camp.

**Where’s Wilko.** A PC surveying the camp might notice a well-guarded tent (DC 15 Insight) or search through the engineers’ paperwork to find Wilko’s location (DC 18 Deduction).

**Strife Among Staff.** The Morgenthax criminal element is at odds with the corporate Conrad employees, causing tensions the party can exploit. PCs can mingle, drawing out grievances and loosening tongues (DC 15 Leadership), coaxing a stressed Conrad engineer into venting about missing personnel (DC 16 Persuasion), or posing as a Morgenthax runner, delivering “revised orders” to sow confusion (DC 16 Deception).

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor succeeds if the party accrues 4 successes before 3 failures. If the PCs opted to rescue Wilko, the number of successes needed increases to 5. If the PCs succeeded on the previous “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Ruins|To the Ruins]]” endeavor, then they arrived just after the Trio left in a rush. Describe how the camp is still askew; begin with 1 success accrued.

**Success.** The party gets through camp in good order. Whether through deception, theft, or spying, the PCs also learn extra information about the Gilded Trio’s forces (such as their makeup and number) and their locations in the vault (refer to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Cavern Crawl|Cavern Crawl]]”). If the PCs chose to rescue Wilko, continue to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Rescuing Wilko|Rescuing Wilko]].” Otherwise, NeBaal heads off to save the professor while the PCs proceed “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Vault|To the Vault.]]”

**Failure.** The PCs are discovered. To save the mission, NeBaal splits from the party and creates a risky distraction, telling the PCs to go on without them. If the PCs chose to rescue Wilko, continue to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Rescuing Wilko|Rescuing Wilko.]]” Otherwise, proceed straight “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Vault|To the Vault.]]”

## Rescuing Wilko

Wilko is in a tent guarded by Morgenthax thugs (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Hazekiller Era 2|hazekillers]]**). The exact situation depends on the outcome of the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Old Luthadel|Old Luthadel]]” endeavor.

If the PCs succeeded on the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Old Luthadel|Old Luthadel]]” endeavor, two bored Morgenthax thugs guard the tent, playing cards. Getting in only requires one successful test, such as tricking the guards (DC 14 Deception) or cutting a hole in the back of the tent (DC 13 Thievery); this test gains an advantage.

If the PCs failed “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Old Luthadel|Old Luthadel,]]” there’s an alert force of five Morgenthax thugs here. NeBaal’s distraction draws three away, but the other two attack the PCs on sight.

When the PCs get to Wilko, they find her attempting to escape by cutting through the tent with a file. She tells the PCs the Trio took her journal and headed into the tunnels; proceed “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Vault|To the Vault.]]”

## To the Vault

By this point, the PCs have always learned the Trio are headed toward the vault, accompanied by guards and engineers, preparing to use the Eternity Spike. If Wilko was rescued and NeBaal stayed with the party, they offer to help the PCs navigate the tunnels. If Wilko is present, she’s obviously terrified, but if the PCs ask, she accompanies the party.

The party may be close on the Gilded Trio’s tail, or far behind. They may be following the Gilded Map, or following their enemies’ tracks. And they may be entering the tunnel with friends by their side, or the fates of NeBaal and Wilko may be uncertain.

But no matter what, the PCs reach the cavern mouth, entering the tunnels down to Old Luthadel—where eternity awaits.

## Race Against Eternity

As the party navigates the tunnel system, the Gilded Trio enter the Eternity Vault with a mercenary excavation force, beginning the Ascension ritual. This vault dungeon is a co-designed map, incorporating the Era 1 PCs’ chosen defenses (which help the party) and the Trio’s forces (which hinder them).

If you haven’t already prepared the vault dungeon, take a moment to pause the game and complete the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Vault Dungeon Setup|Vault Dungeon Setup]]” steps now. (This may also be an opportune moment to handle “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Character Advancement|Character Advancement.]]”) When ready, continue to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Run the Dungeon|Run the Dungeon.]]”

## Character Advancement

The Era 2 characters advance to 9th level upon entering the vault dungeon. Let the players know this is the final level advancement of the Era 2 campaign, and work with them to finalize their Era 2 PCs.

After two eras, the ultimate confrontation with the Gilded Few and the Eternity Spike awaits just ahead.

## Vault Dungeon Setup

The vault dungeon requires some extra GM preparation to set up, as follows:

- **Place the Gilded Trio.** The Gilded Trio are in the final room, preparing for Ascension. Place the Trio in either cavern S1 or S2—wherever the Era 1 PCs put the Spike case.

- **Determine the Chosen One.** Determine which villain from the Trio is chosen for Ascension. All three have their own reasons for craving godhood—make the decision based on who would be the most exciting antagonist for your group.

- **Review the Dungeon Flow.** To finish preparations, read “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Special Rule: Handling Traps|Special Rule: Handling Traps]]” carefully, and scan map 9.1 and the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Cavern Crawl|Cavern Crawl]]” section. Don’t forget to describe the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Tunnel Features|Tunnel Features]]” to the players, when relevant.

## Run the Dungeon

When you’re ready to run the vault dungeon, do the following:

1.  **Explain the Event.** Go to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Event: Ascension|Event: Ascension,]]” fully reveal the negative event to the players, and follow the instructions there to determine how many slots have already been filled.

2.  **Pick a Dungeon Entrance.** The south entrance is a new tunnel, and deep tread marks indicate it was recently made (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T2: Drilling Operation|T2: Drilling Operation]]” below). If the PCs have the complete Gilded Map, they can choose any entrance to approach: the southwest ([[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T1: Outlaw Sentries|T1]]), south ([[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T2: Drilling Operation|T2]]), or southeast ([[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T3: Engineers|T3]]). Otherwise, place them at the south entrance, as they can only follow the drill tracks.

3.  **Run the Dungeon.** Run the dungeon using map 9.1 and the locations described in “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Cavern Crawl|Cavern Crawl.]]” Each time the Era 2 PCs enter a room where the Era 1 PCs constructed a trap, check that room’s Trap Setup instructions. Then refer to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Special Rule: Handling Traps|Special Rule: Handling Traps]]” to see how well the traps perform. Let the players roll any trap damage dice, and invite them to narrate how the triggered traps affect the room’s occupants. Finally, the Era 2 PCs enter that room in the dungeon, addressing whatever Gilded forces survive.

4.  **It Ends Here.** The dungeon ends either when the PCs reach the Trio (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Ascension, Interrupted|Ascension, Interrupted]]”), or the Ascension event triggers (see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Event: Ascension|Event: Ascension]]”).

### Special Rule: Complications Abound

With the characters racing to save Scadrial, the stakes couldn’t be higher. Outside combat, raise the stakes and add a disadvantage to any meaningful tests the PCs make to directly interact with the Trio’s forces. Each Complication automatically progresses the Ascension event.

### Special Rule: Handling Traps

Unless a trap has moved (see the [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Unreliable Trap]] table), the PCs know the trap’s exact position and don’t trigger it when willingly moving through its space. (Traps can be triggered by forced movement, such as if an enemy Shoves a PC into a trap.)

When directed to trigger a trap, do the following:

1.  Unless the PCs added the reinforced modification, the trap is unreliable. Roll on the [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Unreliable Trap]] table.

2.  When the trap triggers, use “[[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Vault Trap Effects]]” and the room’s Trap Setup to apply the effect to the characters in the room. Players roll the damage dice for traps.

## Cavern Crawl

The caverns contain Gilded Trio forces waiting to confront the PCs. If the Era 1 PCs placed traps in a room, consult the room’s Trap Setup instructions as the PCs enter. The PCs are rushing to reach cavern S1 or S2 (wherever the Era 1 PCs placed the Spike) before the Ascension event triggers.

### T1: Outlaw Sentries

This area connects to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T4: Explosives Experts|T4]]. If the PCs turned the Anvil against Bayron in [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]], this cavern is empty. Otherwise, the room contains three Ironbrands (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Freedom Fighter Era 2|freedom fighters]]**) and **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 7/Elanda The Anvil Wilko|the Anvil]]** (or if she was slain, a **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Koloss Clan Protector Era 2|koloss clan protector]]**). If the party fought the Anvil in [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]], the Ironbrands attack on sight. Otherwise, they’re sick of the Trio and willing to negotiate. The Anvil spends focus to resist arguments the PCs already made in [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]], but is open to new evidence and amenable to standing down or helping the PCs if they can give her a better deal.

**Trap Setup:** Trigger traps as usual. After the traps trigger, surviving Ironbrands are fearful, and PC tests to negotiate gain an advantage.

### T2: Drilling Operation

This area connects to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T3: Engineers|T3]] and [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T4: Explosives Experts|T4]]. Two **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Operative Era 2|Conrad operatives]]** guard the south entrance, adding a disadvantage on Stealth tests for PCs approaching from that direction. In the cavern’s center is a modified Tekiel steam engine, 25 feet by 10 feet with a massive drill head. It just bored a new tunnel into the vault. Upon startup, the engine’s coal boiler must be heated for one round, after which it can move, drilling through rock at a speed of up to 5 feet per round. The drill has a Physical defense of 18, 50 health, and a deflect value of 3. A PC can determine how to drive the drill if they have the Engineering expertise or succeed on a DC 18 Deduction test, gaining an advantage on the test if they saw the blueprints from [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|chapter 8]].

**Trap Setup:** Triggered traps only affect the drill. If the drill takes 10 or more damage, it jams, requiring a successful DC 16 Crafting test to become usable.

### T3: Engineers

This area connects to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T2: Drilling Operation|T2]] and [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T5, T7, and T9: Elite Snipers|T5]]. Three **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Engineer Era 2|Conrad engineers]]** scout through the tunnels, making notes and measurements. One engineer carries an ***Allomantic grenade*** that’s currently infused with Iron Allomancy (3 Investiture).

**Trap Setup:** Trigger traps as usual. After the traps trigger, surviving engineers are distracted; PC tests against the distracted engineers, such as Stealth tests, gain an advantage.

### T4: Explosives Experts

This area connects to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T1: Outlaw Sentries|T1]], [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T2: Drilling Operation|T2]], [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T6: Morgenthax Ambush|T6]], [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T5, T7, and T9: Elite Snipers|T7]], and [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T8: Disabling Under Duress|T8]]. Two **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Engineer Era 2|Conrad engineers]]** arrange three ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Landmine Era 2|landmines]]*** (detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]). 10 feet away, two more **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Operative Era 2|Conrad operatives]]** guard supplies, including two more ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Landmine Era 2|landmines]]***, two ***concussion grenades***, and a ***shotgun***.

**Trap Setup:** Trigger traps as usual. If either Conrad engineer dies, the three landmines also explode, partially collapsing the tunnel in area [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T4: Explosives Experts|T4]] and dealing 2d6 impact damage from the collapse, in addition to the damage from landmines and traps. After the traps trigger, surviving operatives fearfully guard the supplies.

### T5, T7, and T9: Elite Snipers

Each tunnel connects multiple areas:

- T5 connects to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T3: Engineers|T3]], [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T8: Disabling Under Duress|T8]], and T9.

- T7 connect to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T4: Explosives Experts|T4]] and S2.

- T9 connects to T5, [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T8: Disabling Under Duress|T8]], and S2.

In each of these tunnels, a sniper (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Elite Operative Era 2|Conrad elite operative]]**) awaits. The first time each PC enters the tunnel, the sniper makes a Stealth test against their Spiritual defense; on a success, the sniper is hidden from the PC and can’t be sensed by them until the operative attacks or otherwise exposes their position.

**Trap Setup:** Trigger traps as usual.

### T6: Morgenthax Ambush

This area connects to [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#T4: Explosives Experts|T4]] and S1. ## Event: Ascension

Ascension is a negative event with eight slots, representing the completion of the Gilded Trio’s Ascension ritual (see “Events” in chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*). During the Ascension event, one chosen Trio member is being implanted with the latticework—which includes Vadilek’s original spikes—preparing to receive the Eternity Spike itself.

The event is partially introduced to the players during “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Ruins|To the Ruins]]” and fully revealed during “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|Race Against Eternity.]]” By the time it’s fully revealed, the event may have already been progressed a few times (for missing Gilded Map pieces or failures during “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#To the Ruins|To the Ruins]]”).

At the start of “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|Race Against Eternity,]]” explain that this event tracks the Trio’s progress toward the Ascension ritual. Then remove 1 filled slot for each of the following that are true:

- If the Era 1 PCs put the Spike behind the waterfall in cavern S1.

- If the Era 1 PCs put a decoy case either in cavern S1 or cavern S2.

- If the Era 2 PCs had NeBaal’s help navigating the tunnels.

![[pg181_Event_Ascension.webp]]

### Progressing the Event

When a PC gains a Complication in the vault, progress the event by 1, trading an immediate Complication to progress the Ascension.

Let the players know this event is also progressed whenever the PCs engage in combat lasting more than one round.

Every time the event progresses, the chosen Gilded Trio member makes increasingly pained screams, echoing horribly through the cave tunnels, as another spike drives them closer to Ascension.

### Triggering the Event

If you fill all eight event slots before the party reaches the Eternity Spike, the event triggers; continue to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Ascension Arrives|Ascension Arrives.]]” Otherwise, if the PCs arrive at the final room before the Ascension event is completed, proceed to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Ascension, Interrupted|Ascension, Interrupted.]]”

## Ascension Arrives

When the Ascension event triggers, read:

Something changes. As if flipping a switch, the light gains a thick, unreal quality. The air itches and vibrates. Then you hear it: Terrified shouts. Running.\
Like an unseen black wave, a feeling rolls into you, immense and oppressive: Dread. The emotion drowns out all others, a frenzied, crawling blackness. Deep in your core, you know something has gone terribly, horribly wrong.\
The sounds of retreat give way to something else: guttural inhuman screams.

The Ascension has begun, unleashing a massive wave of emotional Rioting. As the wave hits, the Gilded Trio’s forces flee in abject terror, knowing instinctually that staying means annihilation. Each PC and friendly NPC must make a DC 20 Discipline test. On a failure, an NPC flees, and a PC must either spend 3 focus or gain the Diminished [Intellect −2] and Diminished [Willpower −2] conditions for the first round of combat.

When the PCs arrive at the Gilded Trio’s location, read:

The ritual room is empty. A mess of blood and rubble remains. A tunnel upward reveals strained sunlight filtering into a large cavern. The sound of monstrous, labored breathing echoes from above.

The chosen member of the Gilded Trio has turned into a Hemalurgic monstrosity and burst through the cavern’s ceiling into another larger chamber. The other Trio members climbed up after them. PCs who succeed on a DC 14 Deduction or Perception test uncover torn clothing from the monster’s transformation, revealing which of the Trio transformed. Following the Trio into the next cavern doesn’t require a test.

## Ascension, Interrupted

If the PCs arrive at the final room before the Ascension event triggers, describe the surviving Gilded Trio members kneeling around the “chosen one.” Then read or paraphrase, adjusting the text to match the identity of the character being sacrificed:

[*Bayron Conrad/Kwylliam Elariel/Lysarra Tekiel*] lies on the floor, head lolling, chest a mass of bloody wounds and silvery spike-heads. The latticework insertion— painfully interrupted. The chosen one convulses as [*he/she*] spots you, spike-filled eye sockets turned in your direction. “Do it now!”

If only the chosen Gilded Trio member survives, a terrified **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Conrad Operative Era 2|Conrad operative]]** places the Eternity Spike. Otherwise, a surviving Trio member assists, as follows:

**Bayron’s Assist:** “Boldness builds tomorrow!” With a trembling breath, Bayron stabs the Eternity Spike into [Kwylliam/Lysarra].

**Lysarra’s Assist:** “Time to rectify history.” Lysarra decisively slams the Eternity Spike into [Bayron/Kwylliam].

**Kwylliam’s Assist:** “Well, this should be interesting.” Without ceremony, Kwylliam slams the Eternity Spike into [Bayron/Lysarra].

Continue the scene by reading:

The chosen one writhes in agony. They begin to scream as bones pop and their skin stretches and bursts.\
Suddenly, the whole cavern shakes. Rocks crash to the floor, panicked shouts rise, and the air around the chosen one blurs with the distinctive shimmer of a speed bubble.

The bendalloy speed bubble remains for one round, during which the Hemalurgic monstrosity bursts through the cavern’s ceiling into the next chamber, and the other Trio members climb up after them. PCs who cross into the bubble must spend 2 focus or become Stunned. Following the Trio into the next cavern doesn’t require a test.

## Threads of Fate

## Special Rule: Legacy Intervention

The PCs gain the following ability during this combat:

**Legacy Intervention.** Once during the final combat, when your Era 2 legacy connection PC either makes a test or is the target of an enemy’s test, you can draw on your connected legacies to replace that test’s d20 result with one of your recorded legacy die results, potentially causing the Era 2 PC to succeed or their opponent to fail. You can choose to do this after you see their die roll and after applying any advantages or disadvantages, but you must do so before the effects of the test are resolved.

Any player can suggest using this ability, but the Era 1 player ultimately determines when to trigger it to aid their Era 2 counterpart.

Once a player uses the Legacy Intervention ability, the two connected players collaborate as follows:

1.  Give the Era 2 PC’s player the “My Legacy” handout the Era 1 PC’s player filled out.

2.  Ask the Era 2 player to read the recorded answer, to remind the group what the Era 1 PC’s final, lasting legacy was. (So, for instance, the Heir player reads the Noble player’s answer.)

3.  Ask the Era 1 player to explain how the Era 1 PC’s legacy rippled out into the future, sparking a chain of events—whether direct or indirect—affecting the Era 2 PC now, in this moment.

4.  Invite the two players to collaborate and weave the story together.

Creativity is encouraged. A player can explain how the Era 1 PC’s choices—perhaps a person they helped, an idea they championed, or an object they crafted—caused a chain reaction leading to this moment. The Era 1 PC can propose a short flashback if appropriate.

For example, the Official’s legacy of nonviolent leadership may have inspired a regulation limiting the use of certain arms by law enforcement, eventually limiting the Lawkeeper’s weaponry choices as a trainee, forcing the Lawkeeper to become a better shot with their own unusual chosen weapon, which they now fire with uncanny accuracy for a reality-defying trick shot—a far-reaching ripple effect of fate.

The connection can also be more succinct. Perhaps the Rookie’s ancestral armor—which the Veteran lovingly crafted—saves them from a fatal blow. The Era 2 PC can also simply be bolstered by a quote from or realization about the Era 1 PC’s life.

Once the players finish the explanation, the Legacy Intervention is complete. The Era 2 PC swaps in the Era 1 PC’s legacy die roll result (or one result of their choice, if they gained multiple legacy dice).

## Luthadel Ruins

The battle begins as the party emerges from the Hemalurgic monstrosity’s tunnel. Read:

You emerge from the tunnel below, up into an enormous cavern. Sunlight filters down from a fissure far above. Out of the mud around you rise ruined stone buildings. Timeworn facades and twisted metal peek through the natural rock at odd angles: the ruins of Old Luthadel.\
To the east, a bronze statue and an old fountain lie in rubble. To the northeast, a waterfall cascades hundreds of feet down over the tilted carcass of an ancient noble keep. Water tumbles through long-shattered stained-glass windows, shards glinting like gems, before pooling below and streaming west. Due north, a massive, crumpled building threatens to collapse even further.\
An enormous form stands in the sunlight. Translucent skin reveals a biological jumble of too-long bones and unnatural, undulating muscles. Glinting, embedded spikes dot their misshapen body. Eyes freshly pierced with spikes, they turn their face sunward and scream.\
Flexing clawed hands, the monstrosity begins moving toward the waterfall: a three-hundred-foot climb to freedom.

If the PCs interrupted the [[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Event: Ascension|Ascension]] event, also describe the remaining Trio members moving into position: Bayron into the fountain rubble, Kwylliam to the collapsed ruins, and Lysarra into the ruined buildings near the tunnel.

If a PC heard Ironeyes’s words during chapter 8, they recall his warning now: This much power could destroy a city like Elendel. Make it clear to the PCs this creature can’t be allowed to escape Old Luthadel.

## Beginning the Combat

Knowing only hatred and coursing with unstable power, the creature rages toward the surface. Until the Hemalurgic monstrosity takes 30 damage (or a PC spends O to cause them to fall), the creature spends fast turns to move toward the waterfall and climb, with the following conditions:

- Each Move (1) to climb requires a successful DC 15 Athletics test. The monstrosity doesn’t fall on a failure.

- The exit is 300 feet above.

- PCs can spend O to cause the monstrosity to lose their footing and fall to the cavern floor, taking 1d6 impact damage for every 10 feet they fall.

- Raise the stakes on all PC tests while the monstrosity is climbing.

On slow turns, the monstrosity uses Gain Advantage (1) with Athletics and attacks any PCs within reach with their Claws (1), Backhand (1), or Propel Metal (1). If the creature wasn’t attacked, they might use a third action to Move (1) or Brace (1) behind a natural feature.

## Event: Disrupt the Latticework

To introduce the Disrupt the Latticework event, read:

The creature that was once [*Bayron/Kwylliam/Lysarra*] slowly turns, their molten rage settling on you. Every few seconds, like a heartbeat, the Spike at the center of the monstrosity pulses. As it does so, flesh ripples and reknits, revealing the glittering heads of other spikes throughout their body.

When a PC gains an Opportunity while attacking the monstrosity, they can choose to spend it to progress the event by 1.

Additionally, a character who can reach the monstrosity can Use a Skill (1) to make an Athletics, Medicine, or Thievery test against the monstrosity’s Physical defense (19). Characters with the Hemalurgist specialist expertise gain an advantage on this test. On a success, progress the event by 1.

The creature’s massive, misshapen frame shudders, and with panicked motions, they grasp at the Eternity Spike driven into their chest. Their flesh no longer reknits.

## Aftermath

The Hemalurgic monstrosity fights to the death and can’t be saved, reasoned with, or cured.

If the monstrosity is killed, their body shrinks like a Feruchemist who’s no longer tapping strength, and any remaining Gilded Trio surrender in exhausted defeat. The PCs can recover the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Eternity Spike GM ONLY|Eternity Spike]]*** (see [[Journal/13 - Appendix C Eternity Spike|appendix C]]) and gain two tier 3 ***Hemalurgic spikes*** (see “Hemalurgic Spikes” in chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*); choose spikes that provide one of the Metalborn powers from the Hemalurgic monstrosity’s [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Unstable Expulsion]] table. The party also discovers a silvery spike with a scarlet sheen, dotted with rust-like spots: a trellium ***Hemalurgic spike***. The Gilded Few’s equipment, inventions, and vehicles sell for at least 4,000 boxings. Proceed to “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|Epilogue.]]”

If the PCs are defeated, the monstrosity climbs out of the cavern and disappears into the wilds of the Northern Roughs. Perhaps, after a few weeks or months in their new body, they’ll bend the Eternity Spike to their will. Or perhaps they’ll become a roaming danger to Scadrial… until a new band of heroes arises.

## Epilogue

To begin closing the adventure, as the party leaves the cavern system, read:

The blistering heat of the Northern Roughs greets you as you emerge from the caverns of Old Luthadel. You uncovered the centuries-old mysteries of the Gilded Map and the Eternity Vault. You revealed a hidden chapter in Scadrial’s history, and came face to face with ancient powers—powers toyed with by unworthy hands.\
You faced the atrocities of the past, and for today, Scadrial is safe. However, the future is never a given. It is a fragile, beautiful thing. Hoped for. Fought for. Bled for. How will you live yours?

The party can take any remaining Gilded Few into custody and return them to Elendel. Of course, the question of what to do with the Eternity Spike remains. (See the optional “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Postlude|Postlude]]” scene for one possible answer.)

If NeBaal didn’t accompany the PCs into the Eternity Vault, they catch up with the party as they emerge; if the PCs didn’t rescue Wilko, she was able to escape once the Trio’s forces scattered. Alternatively, if the PCs made little or no attempt to help NeBaal or Wilko, you may optionally roll the plot die to determine that NPC’s fate: On a Complication, make an injury roll for the NPC (see chapter 9 of the *Mistborn Handbook*).

Before wrapping the adventure, run short scenes to help the PCs process their accomplishments and celebrate their victories with allies. Ask the players which NPCs their characters would like to speak to as the story closes, and use the following ideas to guide or inspire these scenes:

**NeBaal’s Contract.** The kandra is introspective. They’ve come a long way from the person who simply sought to fulfill Vadilek’s original Contract. They didn’t foresee how Scadrial would change, how their life would be forever altered by the Eternity Spike, or the terrible irony of its unwieldable power.

**Wilko’s Mission Complete.** Despite the danger faced along the way, Wilko believes she and the party have made a true difference in the world. She wants to continue working together, help expand the Guardians (especially if Merid perished), explore Old Luthadel, and find new history to unearth.

**Other NPCs.** If they survived, K.T., Marthis, and Merid are astonished the Gilded Few were defeated. Any of these characters would serve as an excellent tier 2 companion (see “Companions” in chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*). Merid may invite the PCs to become Guardians now, if he didn’t before.

## Future of the Eternity Spike

Optionally, “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Postlude|Postlude]]” presents the players a long-term solution for keeping the Eternity Spike safe and secure (which, in turn, provides future adventure hooks). You can use these or your own ideas to make the Eternity Spike a starting point for more adventures. Alternatively, you could allow the PCs the freedom to decide what to do with the Spike. Ensconce it in the National Archives? Entrust it to the Guardians? Every party will have a different answer.

The *Mistborn Legacy* adventure has come to a close—but your story need not stop here! See “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel|Continuing Your Campaign]]” for even more ideas.

## Postlude

This optional scene can take place any time after the PCs defeat the Hemalurgic monstrosity. At a moment when all the PCs are traveling together and carrying the Eternity Spike, a black-clad figure approaches. This is Ironeyes (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#A Brush With Death|A Brush with Death]]” in chapter 8), regarded by many across the world as the personification of death.

Read the following:

Death stands before you.\
Ironeyes, the last Inquisitor—a figure out of legend. He gazes at you with spiked eyes, his expression impossible to read.\
“Bearers of Eternity,” he addresses you. “You have done a great service for Scadrial. Now I ask another. Give up the Spike so I may preserve it for a time when its power can be safely used.”\
He extends one hand. “This is a request, not a command. But if you fulfill it, you will gain Harmony’s favor, and mine.”

If the PCs refuse to hand over the Spike, Ironeyes leaves, disappointed. Other forces, such as the Set and the Ghostbloods, will likely take an interest in it, potentially sparking further adventures.

If the characters agree to hand over the Eternity Spike, they can take Harmony himself as a patron. Harmony will assign other missions to the PCs in the future. Furthermore, Ironeyes takes an interest in the party, and the next time one of the PCs is in mortal peril, Ironeyes appears to assist them.

## Continuing Your Campaign

After defeating the Hemalurgic monstrosity, the characters advance to 10th level. Many exciting adventures await in Scadrial:

- Investigate the origins of the Trellium spike found in the Hemalurgic monstrosity’s latticework, and perhaps come to blows with the Set.

- Continue showing NeBaal the world, and help them rejoin kandra society in Era 2.

- Work with Professor Wilko to investigate other mysteries from Scadrial’s past.

- Team up with the Anvil to right the wrongs perpetrated by the Gilded Few.

- Join the Guardians, pilot a blimp, and advance the organization’s agenda across the Elendel Basin.
