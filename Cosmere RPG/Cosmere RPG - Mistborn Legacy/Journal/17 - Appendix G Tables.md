|  |  |
|----|----|
| d8 | Result |
| 1 | **Meteoric Push (Steel Allomancy).** A massive explosion of Allomantic might spreads outward from the Hemalurgic monstrosity. The monstrosity makes an Allomancy attack against the Physical defense of all characters wearing or carrying non-aluminum, non-Invested metals within 20 feet of them, rolling `2d6 + 7` impact damage. On a hit, a character is thrown 20 feet. On a miss or graze, a character is knocked Prone. The area within 20 feet of the monstrosity becomes difficult terrain for the rest of the scene. |

The monstrosity makes an Allomancy test against the Cognitive defense of each character (other than themself) in that area. On a success, a character is Stunned until the end of their next turn. An affected character can resist this power’s influence by spending 2 focus as 0. |
| 3 | **Abnormal Growth (Pewter Feruchemy).** The Hemalurgic monstrosity grows to Huge size and becomes Enhanced [Strength +4] until the start of their next slow turn. For the duration, when they make a Strike, they can roll `2d12` in place of their usual damage dice. |
| 4 | **Perfect Prescience (Electrum Allomancy).** The Hemalurgic monstrosity gains five electrum shadows which last until the start of the monstrosity’s next slow turn. When the monstrosity makes a test, they can spend one electrum shadow to roll a d8 along with the other dice for that test. After rolling, they can choose one die from that test (other than the plot die) and add the d8’s roll to that result. |
| 5 | **Burst of Health (Gold Feruchemy).** The Hemalurgic monstrosity ends the effects of all diseases, drugs, and poisons on themself, and they recover `3d12` health. |
| 6 | **Stuttering Time (Bendalloy Allomancy).** The Hemalurgic monstrosity gains an advantage on all tests until the end of this turn and can immediately use 2. After the monstrosity uses those actions, they pause their slow turn, and every other character within 20 feet of the monstrosity can spend a focus to take a fast turn (PCs act first, then NPCs). After these fast turns, the monstrosity continues with their slow turn. |
| 7 | **Blurring Speed (Steel Feruchemy).** The Hemalurgic monstrosity becomes Enhanced [Speed +4] until the start of their next slow turn. (This increases their movement rate to 60 feet.) In addition, they can immediately move as 0, and if they end this movement within 10 feet of an enemy, they can use their Ragdoll action as 0. |
| 8 | **Inured to Pain (Pewter Allomancy).** The Hemalurgic monstrosity doubles their deflect value until the start of their next slow turn. For the duration, the monstrosity’s deflect also applies to damage dealt by weapons with the Pierce trait, unless that attack has an Opportunity. |
