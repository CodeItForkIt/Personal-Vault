# 03: The Many and the Few

## The Many and the Few

After escaping Delina and returning to Luthadel, the party now sits on the hottest contraband in Scadrial, trapped in a city under siege. With the threat of the Gilded Few looming, the party needs allies and a plan to keep the Eternity Spike safe—and fast.

## Running This Chapter

As the characters struggle to remain one step ahead of the Gilded Few, Enge hatches a plan to safeguard the Eternity Spike in the secret tunnels beneath Luthadel. During this chapter, the PCs make new allies, disrupt the Gilded Few, and develop plans to secure the Spike for the foreseeable future.

Enge is available to accompany the PCs for this leg of the adventure, though she stays out of intense action and combat, citing an odd weakness since Vadilek’s ritual (review “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling NeBaal|Handling NeBaal]]” in this book’s introduction).

## Chapter Flow

After twenty-one days of downtime, the party sets out to find Kandor at Kandor Construction, where they’re quickly embroiled in a workers’ strike. After meeting Bezryl and potentially reuniting with Cynwise (review “[[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|Roleplaying Cynwise]]” in chapter 1), a building suspiciously ignites, and Kandor asks the party to investigate.

A nonlinear investigation reveals a sinister plot by Duvall Haught, a Gilded Few member the PCs may have met in [[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks|chapter 2]]. Duvall uses his charity hospital as cover for his real work: kidnapping and killing Mistings and Feruchemists, hoping to recreate Vadilek’s experiments and win Delina’s favor. Duvall is a master manipulator, Rioting the workers and sabotaging the PCs. Review “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Haught Infirmary|Roleplaying Duvall Haught]]” and “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Following in Footsteps]]” (later in this chapter) before running this section.

While investigating, the PCs must resist a kidnapping attempt, gather evidence, and optionally assist with Bezryl’s search for her brother, a missing Terris steward. Afterward, a demonstration unfolds (organized by Cynwise), and the PCs must choose between stopping Duvall’s Rioters or keeping Bezryl safe. The conflict comes to a head at Haught Manor, where the PCs face off with Duvall.

As the chapter closes, the PCs resolve the labor dispute by choosing a trusted NPC to run Kandor Construction—an important decision, as this NPC leads the vault construction in [[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault|chapter 4]], eventually founding the Guardians organization that protects the Eternity Spike for centuries to come (see [[Journal/16 - Appendix F The Guardians|appendix F]]).

## Character Advancement

The Era 1 characters start this chapter at 3rd level, advance to 4th level before “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Demonstration Day,]]” and advance to 5th level after deciding Kandor Construction’s future.

PCs who obtained Hemalurgy notes (Guiverre’s or Vadilek’s) can progress a “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal during downtime (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction).

## Smugglers on the Run

As Enge hatches a plan to keep the Eternity Spike secure, the PCs have three weeks of downtime in a besieged Luthadel.

## Downtime on the Down-Low

After narrowly escaping Delina, the PCs must lay low. Run this twenty-one-day downtime using “[[Journal/15 - Appendix E Downtime|Downtime in Luthadel]]” in appendix E. Raise the stakes on teststhe PCs make for downtime activities in public spaces, as the Gilded Few harass PCs who don’t keep a low profile; see “[[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Downtime in Luthadel Opportunities and Complications]]” in appendix E for inspiration.

If the PCs reach out to Cynwise or the refugees, they learn most are building fortifications for and training to defend the besieged city from Straff Venture. During this downtime, Allomancers attack Vin, King Elend’s protector, on the roofs of Luthadel. Most assume they were sent by Straff Venture, but they were actually sent by Cett, a warlord from the Western Dominance. Cett’s army arrives shortly after, joining the siege. Additionally, Edgard “Breeze” Ladrian, Elend’s advisor, returns to Luthadel.

## A Smuggler’s Plan

As downtime comes to a close, Enge gathers the PCs on the canal boat to discuss the fate of the Eternity Spike. Read:

“So far, we’ve been lucky. The siege has kept Delina out of Luthadel, but she has plenty of agents in the city. We need to find a safer place to hide this Spike.”\
Enge unfurls a map of Luthadel on the table. “It’s a legend among smugglers, the idea that secret tunnels and vaults are hidden beneath these streets. I always thought it was a tall tale. Until I found a man who helped build them.”

PCs with the Steel Ministry expertise have heard rumors about these tunnels: To keep their location secret, the skaa who excavated them were routinely executed.

Enge has tracked down **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 3/Kandor|Kandor]]** (merchant, he/him), a skaa man who labored in the tunnels just before the Final Empire was overthrown. She urges the PCs to head to Kandor Construction and solicit Kandor’s help—without revealing the existence of the Eternity Spike. This time, though Enge is weak and “missing” her Allomancy, she volunteers to tag along. This is a good opportunity for her to bond with the PCs, to whom NeBaal is becoming emotionally attached (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling NeBaal|Handling NeBaal]]” in this book’s introduction).

As the party arrives, read:

Kandor Construction occupies an office, warehouse, and lumber mill next to the River Channerel in Luthadel’s Industrial District. Furious workers surround the main office.\
“We’re dying while you line your pockets!” a woman shouts. Chants of “Kandor, no more!” rumble through the crowd. Another voice cries out, “If you won’t listen, we’re done asking nicely!”\
A stately woman in traditional Terris robes steps forward, raising her hands. She addresses the crowd with calm authority. “Friends, your voices should be heard! But let us seek understanding, not destruction.”

Bezryl’s words keep the crowd from violence, but the shouting continues as emotions run high. An astute PC who burns bronze or succeeds on a DC 19 Insight test realizes the crowd is spurred on by an instigator burning zinc; if confronted, the instigator flees, and the crowd’s intensity immediately abates.

The PCs also recognize one of the striking workers as Cynwise, the skaa teen they met in [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|chapter 1]].

## Catching Up With Cynwise

Cynwise’s arm, broken in the recent barracks collapse, has been poorly bandaged and hung in a shoddy sling.

If the PCs didn’t help Cynwise in Urteau, Cynwise greets them bitterly with a hard smile, saying, “Told you I’d find a way.” Any genuine apology from the PCs quickly softens their bad attitude, but Cynwise’s trust is hard to re-earn.

If the PCs rescued Cynwise, the teen is thrilled to see them and promises to talk more later (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Speaking With Cynwise|Speaking with Cynwise]]” later in this chapter). For now, they’re busy with the strike, which they helped organize.

## Bezryl the Mediator

While Kandor’s office remains surrounded by angry workers, Bezryl introduces herself and explains Kandor is occupied by a labor dispute:

“You arrived at a difficult time,” the Terris woman says, shaking her head. “People died yesterday in an avoidable accident. A tragedy. The workers grow angrier by the day, and Kandor is afraid. I must persuade him to listen.”

She explains the crisis from the workers’ perspective:

- For weeks, Kandor Construction worksites have been plagued by accidents and injuries.

- Yesterday, over a dozen people were lost in a deadly building collapse while constructing a new barracks.

- With the city under siege, the wages Kandor offers are barely higher than the skaa earned before the Empire fell, and the work hours are just as punishing.

- Workers are now refusing to work and preparing for a potential strike, with some openly threatening Kandor.

When Bezryl heads into the office, the crowd parts for her.

## Bitter Dispute

Kandor sits in his office, head down, writing in his ledgers. As the party enters, read:

The owner of Kandor Construction looks up from a stack of ledgers on his desk. Though he bears the grizzled look of a man who survived hard times, he wears an expensive tailored suit in a traditional noble style.\
“Finally someone reasonable to talk to!” Kandor stands with effort, leaning on a fine mahogany cane. “Customers are always welcome… but I’m afraid my employees are currently refusing to work.”\
Turning to Bezryl, his voice takes on a bitter edge. “They’ve no right to tell me how to run my business—they can’t even keep a damn building from collapsing!”

Kandor is polite to the PCs but derisive toward the skaa workers, whom he sees as insolent and entitled; he believes their work is far less punishing than his was under the Ministry. Kandor blames “lazy workers trying to cut corners” for the building collapse.

Bezryl insists the workers are doing all they can, struggling to work long hours with empty bellies. She’s frustrated Kandor refuses to make concessions after yesterday’s accident.

If a PC tries to ask about the tunnels under Luthadel, Kandor narrows his eyes, asking how they know about the tunnels. The conversation is interrupted by “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#An Explosive Development|An Explosive Development.]]”

## An Explosive Development

When the conversation in Kandor’s office lulls (or the PCs bring up the tunnels), an act of arson escalates the labor dispute. Read:

A huge whooshing bang rattles the windows. The workers’ chanting outside turns to screams: “Fire!”\
Kandor lurches toward the door in alarm. “Explosion in the warehouse,” he says, blanching. “The sawdust is highly flammable.”\
Bezryl’s fists clench, her brass and steel rings glinting on her fingers. “I was just in the warehouse. There are people inside!” She rushes out of Kandor’s office.

Kandor implores the PCs to help, prioritizing saving his workers. He does his best to hurry after Bezryl, leaning on his cane.

### Fire and Rescue

When the PCs exit the office, read:

A windowless warehouse burns, smoke pouring from the door. Workers scramble away from the inferno, some running frantically toward the river, while others inside the building scream for help.

To put out the fire and save the workers, run anendeavor (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). Potential obstacles include:

**Raging Fire.** Water from the nearby river can be hauled in barrels (DC 14 Athletics) or transported by reversing a sluice gate attached to the lumber mill (DC 13 Deduction).

**Trapped Victims.** Characters can help Bezryl find trapped workers (DC 13 Survival) and lead them out through the choking smoke (DC 14 Discipline). The first employee saved is Cynwise.

**The Rebels.** Unless the PCs drove away the hidden instigator in the crowd, inside the warehouse are three rebels, Rioted into belligerent violence. Each rebel can be disarmed (DC 13 Athletics), ordered to stand down (DC 14 Intimidation), or reasoned with (DC 15 Persuasion). This is a great place to raise the stakes.

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor succeeds if the party accrues 6 successes before 4 failures, or if they otherwise complete the objective. Additionally, if the rebels aren’t dealt withbefore the endeavor ends, the scene becomes a combat.

**Success.** The party stops the fire, mitigating the damage. Two people perish and several others are injured, but Bezryl says it could’ve been much, much worse.

**Failure.** The fire rages for nearly an hour before the party and Bezryl extinguish it. At least ten workers are lost in the fire (some dead, others disappeared), and the workers immediately blame Kandor for the casualties.

### Combat

Any rebels who weren’t subdued during the endeavor fight for two rounds before abruptly surrendering. Afterward, they recall feeling anger so white-hot it compelled them to act.

## Candid With Kandor

![[pg063_MBL03_Kandor_Nele Klumpe.webp]]

**Kandor - NELE KLUMPE**

In the aftermath of the warehouse fire, Kandor approaches the party to speak privately. He then answers questions and candidly shares what he knows:

**What do you know about the tunnels beneath Luthadel?** “That’s quite a question. It’s true the Steel Ministry maintained secret tunnels under the city. Tunnels so deep and hidden, the Ministry tried to kill every worker who knew of them. I paid a high price to escape with that knowledge.” Kandor taps the ground with his cane to emphasize the point.

**Will you help us?** “I’ll make you a deal. I can show you those tunnels—along with caverns that don’t exist on any Ministry maps. Help me stop these cursed accidents and get the workers in line, and I’ll tell you everything I know.”

**What do you want us to do?** “Investigate the accidents. Talk to Bezryl, to the workers. Find out why so many things are going wrong. And find some kind of evidence this isn’t my fault, so they stop blaming me!”

**Why aren’t you helping the workers more?** “Keeping them employed while they make careless mistakes is bleeding me dry. If Haught Infirmary weren’t accepting skaa free of cost, I’d be out of business already.”

Kandor only shares his information on the tunnels’ location after the strike is resolved (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Getting to the Table]]”).

## Following in Footsteps

Over the next ten days, the party can freely investigate the accidents and interact with Bezryl and Cynwise. Kandor identifies the burned warehouse and the collapsed barracks project as likely places to begin investigating. Behind the scenes, Cynwise leads the workers in planning a protest against Kandor, set to take place in ten days’ time.

While investigating, the PCs must face an unexpected kidnapping attempt from Duvall’s mercenaries. They can also choose to visit Haught Infirmary, where numerous bystanders make violent confrontation with Duvall virtually impossible—for now.

## Kandor Construction

Kandor Construction’s workers remain on strike,angry and grieving. The PCs can search the burned warehouse, where they encounter Bezryl and some mourning workers.

### Bezryl’s Search

Bezryl is searching through the burned warehouse. As the party approaches, she waves, striking up a conversation. Read:

“I wonder if it was a mistake to come here,” Bezryl says, sifting through the ashes. “I came to Kandor Construction searching for my brother, Fadwal. He never came home after the Final Empire fell, and I heard a rumor he might’ve found work here. He did not.” She straightens, brushing charcoal off her hands.\
“I won’t give up hope, but now his disappearance is just one among many. Not all the absent workers are dead. Some have simply… vanished. I fear whatever fate befell Fadwal may have also befallen those skaa.”

Bezryl offers to accompany the PCs. She provides generations of wisdom about architecture and engineering, sharing knowledge readily whenever it might help. During all interactions, Bezryl consistently proves herself to be fair and trustworthy.

If the PCs express interest in assisting Bezryl with her own investigation, refer to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Bezryl’s Brother.]]”

### Tears and Ash

Outside the burned warehouse, skaa workers gatherto mourn. Any rebels who survived the fire are also here, whispering together. If a PC succeeds on a DC 12 Leadership test, the rebels answer questions freely. They still harbor resentment at Kandor for wearing fancy new suits while they struggle to feed their children, but they express sorrow and shame over causing the fire. They explain they were just so angry, they lost control—and one little flame was all it took.

If the PCs are compassionate, the workers mention they’ve seen unusual comings and goings from the glassware shop across the street (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Spy’s Nest]]”).

## Spy’s Nest

Across the street from Kandor Construction, one of Duvall’s spies has been staking out Kandor’s office from the top story of a glassware shop. If the PCs investigate, they arrive at the stakeout spot just after an instigator flees. Evidence points to Allomancy: a vial with traces of metal and written orders instructing, “Workers—Anger. Kandor—Fear.” A successful DC 11 Lore test identifies the metal as zinc.

## Bezryl’s Brother

Bezryl is searching for a missing Terris steward: **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 3/Fadwal|Fadwal]]** (he/him), her brother. If the PCs don’t volunteer to help first, Bezryl approaches them at the end of the first week of the investigation.

If the PCs agree to help, Bezryl suggests they could search for physical clues, canvass for rumors, or investigate other missing persons cases around the city for connections. Unlike Bezryl, Fadwal isn’t a Feruchemist. Bezryl hasn’t seen him since childhood, and she can’t describe him as an adult, but she knows he’ll be wearing a family heirloom: a ring with a triangle pattern.

Ask how each PC contributes and have them make a relevant DC 13 skill test; each test represents a day of time the party spends investigating. With each success, the party learns one of the following pieces of information:

- Several other Terris people have gone missing around the city.

- Kidnappings have been reported, and Mistings seem to be the target.

- Workplace accidents have increased citywide, not just at Kandor’s.

- Outsiders have been spotted frequenting Kandor’s neighborhood (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Spy’s Nest]]”).

If at least half of the PCs succeed on their tests, the party ends the investigation by uncovering a major clue: Fadwal was seen two weeks ago at the free infirmary at Keep Haught.

## Attempted Kidnapping

The first time the PCs go out at night during the investigation, they run into Duvall’s mercenaries, out searching the city for Mistings to kidnap. Have two outlaws approach the party, and read:

A voice calls out, “Give us your coins, and any metal vials you’re carrying! Set them down, and no one gets hurt.” A man and woman step out of the mist, brandishing wicked obsidian daggers.

On a successful DC 13 Perception test, a PC also spots a mercenary **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Hazekiller Elite Era 1|hazekiller elite]]** hidden in the mist, who gains an additional action:

1 **Strike: Glass Dagger.** Attack +5, reach 5 ft., 1d4 + 5 keen damage. This attack gains an advantage if the target isn’t aware of her presence. *Effectual Poison:* The first time she hits with this attack, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Athletics test or take 2d8 vital damage and become Stunned for 1 hour.

The hazekiller elite attacks Bezryl (if present) or the first character she suspects of being an Allomancer; if Bezryl becomes poisoned, she takes a slow turn and uses Tap Health to end its effects. Her goal is to incapacitate her target and drag them away.

The three kidnappers fight until the first two fall, then the third retreats. If subdued, the kidnappers confess to being “Misting bounty hunters,” and on a successful DC 14 Intimidation test, they identify Keep Haught as the drop-off location for kidnapped victims.

## Collapsed Barracks

A flock of skaa children pick through the collapsed barracks building, gathering nails and discarded tools to resell. They flee when the PCs approach. An elderly man named **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 3/Rogier|Rogier]]** (commoner, he/him) lingers nearby, lonely and eager to talk. Rogier grieves for his son, whowas badly injured in the collapse and never came home from the hospital.

A PC who investigates the wreckage and succeedson a DC 11 Crafting or Deduction test discovers suspiciously cut support posts: clear signs of sabotage.

## Speaking With Cynwise

Cynwise can easily be found at Kandor Construction or the refugees’ warehouse. They speak passionately about the strike and confide they’re organizing a demonstration, determined to make Kandor listen to the workers’ complaints.

Cynwise’s broken arm is badly splinted, obviously not by anyone with proper medical training. If the PCs helped Cynwise in [[Journal/02 - 01 Echoes of the Lost|chapter 1]] and show them kindness now (such as by tending to their arm with a successful DC 11 Medicine test), Cynwise reveals their reluctance to go to the free hospital with the other skaa: It reminds them of the soup kitchen in Urteau where they once went to get “free” food, before getting drugged and kidnapped. They keep warning the other skaa to stay clear of Haught Infirmary, but no one listens.

## Haught Infirmary

Once the PCs investigate enough, the clues lead to Haught Infirmary at Keep Haught in Southbridge.

As the PCs approach, read:

Keep Haught stands proud and dominant, stonework spires reaching six stories into the ash-choked sky. This ancestral noble manor now offers a free infirmary for the citizens of Luthadel. Tall doors stand open to the public, and a guard in faded red-and-white Haught livery helps an elderly skaa woman inside.

Although four House Haught guards (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Garrisoner Era 1|garrisoners]]**) stand near the entrance, they don’t challenge anyone entering. Inside, an infirmary houses thirty patients:

Keep Haught’s grand foyer leads to a vaulted ballroom, now filled with rows of occupied cots. A hum of activity— murmuring patients, orderlies wheeling carts, and the clatter of medical instruments—echoes against the stained glass and stone.

![[pg064_MBL03_Haught Infirmary_Kevin ONeill.webp]]

KEVIN O’NEILL

In the center of the room, **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 2/Duvall Haught|Duvall Haught]]** consults with a pair of surgeons. Unless the PCs use stealth or disguises, he immediately recognizes them. If a PC took Duvall’s 50-boxing bet during “[[Journal/03 - 02 Behind Gilded Masks#Debating Duvall|Debating Duvall]]” in chapter 2, he smugly mentions Elend’s deposal.

The PC with the highest Perception notices that Duvall now wears a copper ring with a repeating triangle pattern: Fadwal’s ring. If Bezryl is present, she spots it but remains tactically silent, discussing it with the party after they leave. Duvall handles the PCs with patronizing disdain, smoothly defl ecting accusations:

**What are you doing here?** “We’re offering a service to the community, of course. After the rebellion, I’ve found my calling here, helping those who cannot help themselves.”

**People keep disappearing here!** “True, we accept cases others deem hopeless. Some die. Tragic, but unavoidable—a burden we healers must bear.”

**Can we look around?** “You’re welcome to speak with the patients here. The rest of Keep Haught, however, is a private residence.”

**You’re kidnapping people!** “Ah yes, I’ve heard the same rumor! Tall tales, as copious witnesses will confirm. Alas, one thing I cannot cure is skaa superstition.”

**Where did you get that ring?** “It was a gift from a friend, can’t remember who. Do you like it?”

PCs who succeed on a DC 14 Perception test notice the orderlies look unusually strong; a successful DC 14 Medicine test also reveals the nurses are administering an unusually high quantity of sedatives.

If the PCs threaten or accuse Duvall, he laughs, calling them absurd. If they don’t relent, his dark eyes flash and he says, “Leave my home now, or I’ll have you brought before the Assembly.” He summons his house guards to escort the party out of Keep Haught.

## Character Advancement

## Presenting the Case to Kandor

Kandor eagerly awaits the party in his office, sitting at his desk with his cane across his lap, sifting through ledgers. If the PCs try to convince Kandor he’s being manipulated and the workers aren’t at fault, run the scene as a conversation (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Resolving the Conversation|Resolving the Conversation]]”). Topics the PCs present might include:

**Spies and Sabotage.** Arguments saying Kandor Construction is being sabotaged gain an advantage if the PCs present evidence, such as the note found in “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|Spy’s Nest]]” or the cut timbers from the barracks. Each piece of evidence can only be used once in this way.

**Implicating Duvall.** If the PCs blame Duvall and succeed on their test to do so, Kandor spends focus to resist, sputtering, “Haught, the philanthropist? But he’s been covering the medical care after all these accidents!” This is a great place to raise the stakes.

**Cold Compassion.** Duvall’s Rioting has consumed Kandor with fear for his business, and he lays blame on his “careless” workers, whom he views as lucky to be employed. He only needs to spend 1 focus to resist arguments involving compassion for the skaa.

### Resisting Influence

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch 3/Kandor|Kandor]]** (merchant) has a Cognitive defense of 13, a Spiritual defense of 14, and 4 focus. He ignores the focus cost the first time he resists influence using his Business Acumen feature.

Kandor uses all his focus to avoid entertaining the idea Duvall is to blame, unwilling to admit he’s been manipulated (or lose the free medical care he’s come to rely on for his workers).

### Resolving the Conversation

The conversation succeeds if the party convinces Kandor he’s been played, either by making compelling arguments or because he no longer has enough focus to resist influence. If the PCs resort to threats, the conversation ends in failure.

**Success.** Kandor admits he was wrong and apologizes. If the PCs blamed Duvall, Kandor promises to stop trusting him; otherwise, Kandor himself deduces that Duvall is behind this, explaining the revelation to the PCs.

**Failure.** Kandor refuses to believe the truth: “You’re just making far-fetched excuses for these half-asleep shirkers. I already spoke with the Assembly’s merchant faction. You can tell Bezryl that the next time these malcontents stir up trouble, I’ll use my sway with the Assembly to bring in the city guard.”

### Escalation

As the conversation concludes, read:

Bezryl knocks, entering before Kandor can answer. “I need assistance—quickly.” Outside, a growing crowd begins to chant.

Expressing fears the workers will “flay him alive,” Kandor asks Bezryl to intercede. She agrees and requests the PCs accompany her. Proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Demonstration Endeavor|Demonstration Endeavor.]]”

## Demonstration Endeavor

Cynwise and the workers picket in the street, in front of a growing crowd. Duvall’s mercenaries are concealed within that crowd, subtly Rioting the protesters and looking for opportunities to turn the demonstration violent.

As the PCs exit Kandor’s office, read:

The labor demonstration is in full swing. Skaa workers stand shoulder to shoulder, their chants echoing down the street like hammer blows—angry, hopeful, afraid. Makeshift burlap banners sway above the crowd. A few protesters grip weapons, the same ones they used the night the Lord Ruler fell. Tensions run dangerously high.\
Bezryl turns to you, worriedly twisting a gold bracelet. “This is escalating rapidly. Perhaps I can calm them. I must try.”

Run the demonstration as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”). Bezryl reports there are mercenaries in the crowd, intent on turning things violent. She’s determined to speak, but she admits it may make her a target.

**Kandor Construction workers demand safer working conditions. - STEVEN SHAN**

### Special Rule: Dual Objectives

The PCs can choose between two primary objectives: stopping Duvall’s Rioters, or helping Bezryl. Each objective requires at least 3 successes to complete.

With each PC contribution, the player must specify which objective they aim to progress.

### Special Rule: A Message of Hope

After the second PC contribution, Bezryl steps up on an overturned crate and addresses the protesters. During her speech, contributions toward stopping Duvall’s Rioters gain an advantage as Bezryl tries to calm the crowd—but she’s exposed, and attempts to help her gain a disadvantage.

Now and after each ensuing PC contribution, read a paragraph from Bezryl’s speech:

\- “You are not wrong! You deserve better. And we must fight for a better future, for all of us.\
- “But look around you—it is not Kandor who causes your children to starve. It’s the armies at our gates!\
- “Our fight now is not just for better working conditions, but for our very survival. To survive, we must work together!\
- “We need each other. We need every one of us— even Kandor! So, raise your voices, but lay down your weapons.\
- “Reach out across the lines that divide us. Fight for the future we deserve! Stand together, and we shall not fall!”

### Running the Endeavor

Potential approaches include:

**Stop Duvall’s Rioters.** A PC can track Duvall’s mercenaries through the crowd (DC 15 Survival) or scan for suspicious individuals (DC 15 Insight). They could climb onto a roof (DC 14 Agility) to see from a higher vantage point (DC 12 Perception). Discovered mercenaries could be Restrained or run off (DC 16 Athletics or Intimidation). This is a good place to raise the stakes.

**Help Bezryl.** If the PCs stick with Bezryl, they can encourage her (DC 13 Persuasion) or clear space for her to speak (DC 14 Athletics or DC 15 Intimidation). During her speech, PCs might help Bezryl sway the crowd (DC 15 Leadership) or identify threats (DC 14 Insight).

#### [[Journal/17 - Appendix G Tables|Demonstration Endeavor Opportunities and Complications]]

### Resolving the Endeavor

To succeed on both objectives, the PCs must obtain at least 6 successes (3 per objective) before 3 failures, or otherwise complete both objectives. Completing just one objective before obtaining 3 failures results in a partial success.

**Success on Both Objectives.** If the PCs stop the Rioters and help Bezryl, proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Vigil of Hope|Vigil of Hope.]]”

**Partial Success (Stop Duvall’s Rioters).** The PCs find one of Duvall’s Rioters, just as Bezryl is attacked. Proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Instigators|Instigators.]]”

**Partial Success (Help Bezryl).** The PCs protect Bezryl, but a riot ensues. Proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Instigators|Instigators.]]”

**Failure.** If the PCs fail at both objectives, they lose track of Bezryl, and the crowd grows hazardously violent. Proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Instigators|Instigators.]]”

## Instigators

If the PCs failed one or both objectives, Duvall’s mercenaries start a fight in the crowded street (see map 3.1).

![[pg069_Map-3.1-Outside Kandor Construction_Dallin Bifano copy.webp]]

**Map 3.1: Outside Kandor Construction - DALLIN BIFANO**

The battle begins with either an attack on Bezryl or a violent riot (or both), depending on the preceding endeavor’s outcome.

First, if the PCs didn’t successfully help Bezryl, she’s attacked. Read:

As the crowd listens intently to Bezryl, torchlight catches the glint of glass. A dagger sails toward Bezryl’s heart. She tries to dodge. She fails. As she collapses, so does the fragile peace. The panicked crowd erupts.

In this case, a glass dagger strikes Bezryl, dealing `1d4` keen plus `2d8` vital damage, and Bezryl is Stunned for 1 hour. She prioritizes using Tap Health to end this effect.

Next, if the PCs didn’t successfully stop the Rioters, the crowd turns violent. Read:

Like a dam breaking, the crowd’s anger over flows. A riot begins in earnest, violent and chaotic. Workers push to surround, to protect, to fight. Screams echo through the street, blood hits the cobblestones, and a buzzing panic swells within you, urging you to flee.

In this case, the crowd is Rioted, and so is the party. Before combat starts, each PC must either spend 1 focus to resist infl uence or, in a panic, move half their movement rate (or as far as possible) in a direction of the GM’s choice. The street is crowded and dangerous(see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Battlefield Effects|Battlefield Effects]]”). Bezryl fights alongside the PCs, keeping herself safe by using Tap Health and retreating if necessary.

Finally, proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Combat in a Crowd|Combat in a Crowd.]]”

### Combat in a Crowd

Start combat with two mercenaries (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Rebel Era 1|rebels]]**) who rush the PCs as three **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Common Adversaries/Instigator|instigators]]** keep the crowd panicking. Bezryl’s **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Hazekiller Era 1|hazekiller]]** attacker immediately retreats, having either just attacked Bezryl or missed their chance. They only join the fight if attacked by PCs. The instigators in this encounter are hidden among the crowd. If the PCs succeeded in “Stop Duvall’s Rioters,” they’ve identified one instigator, and the other two remain hidden. Otherwise, all three are hidden.

Place three **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 1 Adversaries/Commoner Era 1|commoner]]** tokens on map 3.1 for every hidden instigator; one of these three represents the instigator in disguise, but don’t tell your players which is which. Explain that “empty” squares on the map are crowded with protesters (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Battlefield Effects|Battlefield Effects]]” below).

A PC can Use a Skill (1) to make a DC 12 Insight test on a commoner (or disguised instigator) they can sense; a success reveals whether that commoner is really an instigator. This is a great place to raise the stakes. Alternatively, a PC with Bronze Allomancy can Burn Bronze (1) to identify the Rioting instigators. Each instigator joins the combat against the PCs once their cover is blown.

### Battlefield Effects

The following effects may be active during this combat, depending on how the PCs handled previous situations:

**Crowded.** With so many people concentrated in one place, the entire street counts as difficult terrain.

**Dangerous (If Crowd is Rioted).** If the PCs didn’t successfully stop Duvall’s Rioters, the Rioted crowd is dangerous. At the end of each PC’s turn, that PC must succeed on a DC 12 Agility or Athletics test or take `1d4` impact damage from a stray weapon attack.

### Aftermath

If intercepted, the hazekiller fights until half health, then attempts to flee. If they didn’t attack Bezryl, they still have their dagger, thus gaining an additional action:

1 **Strike: Glass Dagger.** Attack +5, reach 5 ft., 1d4 + 5 keen damage. *Effectual Poison:* The first time the hazekiller hits with this attack, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Athletics test or take 2d8 vital damage and become Stunned for 1 hour.

If the PCs subdue the instigators, any remaining rebels flee or surrender, and the crowd’s fury wanes considerably.

Eventually, the demonstration begins to peter out. Subdued adversaries can easily be made to talk with a successful DC 10 Intimidation test. Proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Confessions|Confessions.]]”

## Vigil of Hope

If the party succeeds on both of their “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Demonstration Endeavor|Demonstration Endeavor]]” objectives, the protest is peaceful. The PCs neutralize Duvall’s instigators, Bezryl remains safe, and her speech is a resounding success. Read:

Bezryl’s message of hope rings through the crowd. They grow still, listening. After a pause, a worker stands and speaks out—this time not to shout, but to share. She speaks with sorrow about the friends she lost in the building collapse. Another speaker follows, a widower, voicing his hopes for his children once the siege ends. As the day fades and the shadows grow long, what began as a protest becomes a candlelit vigil. The most downcast souls in Luthadel raise their voices in song— to honor the dead, and to bring hope to the living.

Each PC who listened to Bezryl’s speech gains the Determined condition. The party is also approached by one of Duvall’s mercenaries, who has had a change of heart after witnessing the vigil. Proceed to “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Confessions|Confessions.]]”

Duvall’s mercenaries confess to the party that he hired them to start a riot, capture Bezryl, and deliver her to Keep Haught tonight. Bezryl feels obligated to stay with the workers, but she implores the PCs to stop whatever Duvall is planning. ### Showdown With Duvall

### Battlefield Effects

The following effects are active during this combat:

**Well of Dissolution.** In the center of the room is a stone pit, 20 feet deep and 15 feet in diameter. A character who falls into the pit takes `1d6` impact damage. Climbing out of the pit is difficult, requiring a DC 12 Athletics test; on a failure, the character falls Prone.

**Captive Mistwraith.** The pit contains a captive **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Common Adversaries/Mistwraith|mistwraith]]**. Though it isn’t aggressive, it slowly digests any bodies dropped into it, and a character who ends their turn inside the pit takes `1d6` vital damage from its Necrophage feature. Characters outside the pit aren’t affected by the mistwraith’s Disturbing Sight and Eerie Aura features.

### Aftermath

Duvall fights to the death; once defeated, any surviving guards surrender. After combat ends, Fadwal’s muffled shouts are audible from Duvall’s office, hidden behind a sliding bookshelf on the east wall (see “[[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few#Secret Chamber|Secret Chamber]]”). Duvall carries 200 boxings and the keys to Fadwal’s restraints. Fadwal’s ring adorns his finger.

Assuming Duvall is dead, a PC who has marked at least two milestones toward their “Learn About Hemalurgy” goal can remove Duvall’s bronze Hemalurgic spike (refer to “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction). If the PCs don’t collect this spike now, Enge stealthily doubles back later tonight to extract it herself (see “[[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault|Enge’s Modest Proposal]]” in chapter 4).

If captured alive, Duvall refuses to talk, but his laboratory contains enough evidence to thoroughly incriminate him.

Either way, Duvall’s library contains several items of value:

- A kandra ***True Body*** made of stone, which might be sold as a curiosity for 300 boxings. If present, Enge is especially unsettled by this True Body, as well as by the mistwraith.

- A rare copy of *Book of the False Dawn*, worth 100 boxings.

- Vials of medicine (five doses of ***anesthetic***, two doses of ***effectual poison***, and one dose of ***tonic***).

- A journal containing more of Vadilek’s notes on Hemalurgy, which can be studied during downtime.

- Copper and brass jewelry etched with traditional Terris designs, worth 30 boxings.

## Journeying Onward

The PCs advance to 5th level upon resolving the labor dispute.

With Duvall downed and the dispute decided, the PCs are now ready to bring their most trusted NPCs into confidence, revealing their mission to protect the Eternity Spike. As the chapter closes, the party is poised to construct a vault in the secret tunnels below Luthadel, in hopes of securing the Eternity Spike’s future.
