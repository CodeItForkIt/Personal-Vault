# Appendix F: The Guardians

## The Guardians

This appendix provides information on the Guardians organization featured in the *Mistborn Legacy* adventure (see “Organizations” in chapter 8 of the *Mistborn Handbook*). The Guardians are a secret society dedicated to protecting the Eternity Spike and opposing the Gilded Few—a mission spanning the centuries.

## Organizational Priority

The Guardians initially formed in the wake of the labor negotiations at Kandor Construction in the year 1024 FE, when the Era 1 party’s trusted NPCs became involved in opposing the Gilded Few. The primary purpose of the Guardians organization is always to protect and preserve the Vault Map across the eras—and with it, the Eternity Spike.

However, the Guardians’ core philosophy and values are determined by the Era 1 PCs’ choices in [[Journal/04 - 03 The Many and the Few|chapter 3]]. Each possible Kandor Construction leader (Kandor, Bezryl, or Cynwise) has their own lasting impact on the organization’s future path and priorities. As the vault’s project supervisor and (usually) the first protector of the Vault Map, this NPC becomes an influential leader within the Guardians. Era 1 PCs who survived the Battle of Luthadel may also become fundamental to founding the Guardians; the players can learn about this from Merid (or Wynn) if the Era 2 PCs are inducted into the Guardians.

The full name of the Guardians organization reflects the priorities of its founders: either the Guardians of Industry, the Guardians of Wisdom, or the Guardians of Labor. Each offers different membership benefits, and any organization goals the Guardians assign theEra 2 PCs should align with this priority.

## Organization Scale

The Guardians organization starts at scale 1. The organization’s scale then adjusts in response to the PCs’ actions during the adventure. The following events affect the scale of the Guardians organization:

- If the PCs successfully completed “[[Journal/05 - 04 Eternitys Vault#Red Dawn in Luthadel|Red Dawn in Luthadel]]” in chapter 4, increase the scale by 1.

- If the PCs secured the Unity Medal (and didn’t take Bayron’s deal) in [[Journal/08 - 07 Striking The Anvil|chapter 7]], increase the scale by 1.

- If Merid died, decrease the scale by 1 (to a minimum of 1).

The above determines the organization’s scale (a possible value of 1–3), which can change throughout the adventure.

## Guardians of Industry

If the party chose Kandor, the Guardians prioritize merchant skaa in the years to come. They hate the nobility, but they’re hierarchical and pragmatic, ignoring the plight of the less fortunate.

### Return on Investment

As an organization of merchants, the Guardians can provide interest-free loans to the party. Taking out a loan requires one day of downtime, the money is typically due back within a month, and only one loan can be made at a time.

In addition, if a PC uses three days of downtime in any major city, they can locate a Guardians-affiliated merchant for a particular type of good (guns, musical instruments, metallurgist supplies, etc.), who sells to the PCs at a discount.

## Guardians of Wisdom

If the party chose Bezryl, the Guardians prioritize information, historical context, and coalition building. They’re level-headed and knowledgeable, but bureaucratic and slow to act.

### We Can Find Out

The organization can find the answer to any one question, if it was ever recorded at any point in Scadrial’s history. The GM determines how long getting this information takes, but obscure information should generally take days, while esoteric information takes weeks.

## Guardians of Labor

If the party chose Cynwise and the workers, the Guardians take a collective, decentralized approach. Agile and adaptive, they favor retaliation, disruption, and anonymity.

### Common Cause

The Guardians have agents placed among laborers, service workers, and mercenaries. During downtime, the PCs can approach the Guardians to identify agents who have infiltrated the ranks of an enemy organization (such as the Gilded Few). During a subsequent scene involving that organization, the specified number of NPCs surrender or withdraw, rather than opposing the party.
