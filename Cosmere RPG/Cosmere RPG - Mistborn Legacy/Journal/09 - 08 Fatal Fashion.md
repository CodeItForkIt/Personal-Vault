# 08: Fatal Fashion

With the Roughs behind them, the crew returns to Elendel, now potentially holding two Gilded Map pieces. Using the final map piece as bait, Lysarra lures the heroes to a fatal fashion show, where the Gilded Trio executes a breathtaking power grab.

## Running This Chapter

This chapter is the party’s chance to obtain the third and final map piece: a unique bold blue mistcloak, once entrusted to a hero from the Era 1 party. Handed down over the generations, this Midnight Mistcloak has become an heirloom—one Lysarra intends to use as bait.

## Chapter Flow

NeBaal informs the PCs that Lysarra has acquired the mistcloak and plans to auction it off at a fashion show during the Elariel Spring Salon. This is the party’s best chance to intercept the relic.

The PCs investigate, learning they can pose as runway models to gain entry, and potentially uncovering information about Lysarra (review “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|Lysarra’s Long Ploy]]” before running this investigation). Up to ten days of downtime follow, capped off by an attack from a Morgenthax crew boss, before the PCs arrive at the fashion show in style.

On Floor 48 of Tekiel Tower, the PCs can grill Marthis for information and get intelligence from Merid before the auction begins; both provide hints something isn’t right. Unbeknownst to the party, Lysarra’s auction masks a deadly plot. Tonight, the Gilded Few die, so the Gilded Trio can collect their spikes. As gas floods the ballroom, the party must flee, only to be intercepted by Lysarra and her loyal security captain (Vorn, if she survived [[Journal/06 - 05 Engines and Espionage|chapter 5]]).

In a final twist, while the PCs fight for their lives, the Trio kidnaps Professor Azmine Wilko. Regardless of the battle’s outcome, the remaining Trio members escape aboard a Tekiel train bound for Old Luthadel, holding Wilko hostage—speeding toward the Eternity Spike.

## Lysarra’s Long Ploy

During Lysarra’s time at university, Augustin Tekiel’s corruption—and the depredations of the notorious Vanishers—drove the Tekiel family into financial ruin. Ever since, Lysarra’s sole aim has been to use the Eternity Spike to restore House Tekiel to its former glory.

First, Lysarra leveraged her paramour, Vorn, to connect with the Morgenthax crime family, acquiring funding and security. Lysarra then began building a private railway, locating Old Luthadel, and diverting resources to build a base camp at the cave system’s entrance. However, even with troops and supplies to search the tunnels, the vault still proved impossible to find without the Gilded Map.

And so, armed with the knowledge deceitfully taken from NeBaal, Lysarra tracked down the mask, medal, and mistcloak, assigning a Trio member to obtain each. Through charm and charity, Lysarra secured the mistcloak. She also devised a backup plan, in case Kwylliam and Bayron should fail.

Now, Lysarra’s years of elegant, tactical plans enable her bold bid for divinity. She’ll use the mistcloak to lure in both the PCs and her fellow Gilded Few. She’ll murder them all with a deadly surprise attack and harvest the “familial spikes,” recovering Vadilek’s latticework. And if the PCs survive, she’ll still have distracted them long enough for her lackeys to kidnap Professor Wilko, just a few floors away, and steal the professor’s notes on the party’s map pieces. Afterward, Lysarra plans to take a getaway train straight to her Old Luthadel base camp—with either the entire Gilded Map or a full, functional copy of it in hand. With everything required for the ascension ritual, Lysarra means to finally fulfill Vadilek’s long legacy, achieve divinity, and secure the ultimate prize: control over Scadrial’s destiny.

## Character Advancement

The Era 2 characters start this chapter at 6th level, advance to 7th level immediately after “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Downtime Before the Salon|Downtime Before the Salon,]]” and advance to 8th level after fighting Lysarra.

## Eyes of Tekiel

Upon returning to their safehouse in Elendel, the party finds a note from NeBaal, asking to meet at Wilko’s apartment in the Clan Estates at Tekiel Tower.

## Bad Press

As the PCs head to the meeting, read:

A shoeless street urchin waves the latest issue of a broadsheet. “Rail rascals run riot in the Roughs! Bayron Conrad brutalized! Get your *Truth in Elendel*, three clips a copy, five if you want one that ain’t soggy!”

The front page headline proclaims, “Armed and Dangerous!” above unflattering sketches of the PCs. The article paints them as the architects of an Elendel crime wave including illegal racing, theft, harassing students, and an attack on Bayron in the Roughs. If Bayron died, the broadsheets obscure this fact—Lysarra wishes to avoid scrutiny before the Salon.

Another article, “Gilt and Glamour,” features Lysarra’s fashion show and auction. Taking place at Tekiel Tower during the Elariel Spring Salon ten days from now, the auction benefits Lysarra’s charity, the Young Ascendants. The auction’s centerpiece is the Midnight Mistcloak, a unique relic noted for its unusual blue color.

## Kindred Spirits

Professor Wilko welcomes the PCs to her family’s Tekiel Tower apartment (which they may have visited in [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|chapter 6]]). She steps into the kitchen to make tea.

Alone with the party, NeBaal thanks them, then expresses appreciation for Professor Wilko. NeBaal admires her passion and open mind, grateful to be treated as a person rather than an oddity. They beseech the party to promise that, if anything should happen to NeBaal, the PCs will protect Azmine.

### Of Maps and Mistcloaks

When conversation peters out, Wilko returns, and NeBaal gets down to business. Read:

“The final piece of the Gilded Map is a mistcloak, made by a dear friend. She began it, I finished it, and heroes kept it safe for generations. But now, unfortunately, it seems it’s fallen into Gilded hands.” NeBaal shows the “Glit and Glamour” broadsheet article. “This is our map piece—I’m certain. And Lysarra’s putting it up for auction at the Elariel Spring Salon.”\
Delicately pouring spiced tea, Wilko nods. “Lysarra’s lying low. No meetings, no appearances, but we know the mistcloak will be the centerpiece of that auction— meaning we have ten days to figure out a way in.

NeBaal and Wilko answer questions and share the following:

**The mistcloak is the map piece?** NeBaal answers, “This mistcloak is unique—bold blue, and subtly embroidered with a complex pattern of blue and silver thread inside the mantle. That’s part of the map, if you know how to read it.”

**How does the Gilded Map work?** NeBaal answers, “The three pieces form a map of the cave system below Old Luthadel, where the Eternity Spike is hidden in a secure vault.” Wilko adds, “The landscape has changed since the Catacendre, but those tunnels—the ‘wombs of the earth,’ they’re called—should remain unaltered.”

**What’s Lysarra’s plan?** Wilko answers, “It makes sense Lysarra would show off a mistcloak at her fancy fashion show, trying to evoke her connection to the Ascendant Warrior, Vin, and draw attention to the Young Ascendants. But… if Lysarra is putting the cloak up for auction, then either she’s decided she no longer needs it, or she has no intention of actually letting it go. My guess is the latter.” Frowning, NeBaal adds, “I fear you may be walking into a trap, but I don’t see a better option.”

**Who are the Young Ascendants?** Wilko answers, “The Young Ascendants are a university club and charity Lysarra’s run for years, trying to cash in on the Ascendant Warrior’s reputation for clout. The auction supposedly benefits the charity.” NeBaal adds a warning: “Be careful. Since most of the club members are young nobles, there are sure to be Gilded Few parents in the crowd.”

**How do we get the mistcloak?** Wilko answers, “You can bid on the cloak in the auction, or maybe just grab it and get out… but getting in is tricky. Security is tight. The Salon is exclusive, and invitations already went out weeks ago. So we’ll need to come up with a creative way in.”

Once questions are answered, NeBaal suggests the PCs investigate around Elendel to find a way into the Spring Salon. They offer to accompany the party, ever curious to see more of the city.

## Tekiel Investigation

To get access to the mistcloak, the PCs must infiltrate the Elariel Spring Salon. Investigation reveals a route to infiltrate the fashion show by posing as models, along with details from “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|Lysarra’s Long Ploy.]]”

## Investigative Web

Run the investigation as a Discovery endeavor, as described in chapter 12 of the *Mistborn Handbook*. The following locations each present a short scene with leads to other locations. The party can follow leads and visit locations in any order.

Don’t track successes and failures in this endeavor. However, bad luck and poor detective skills cut into the party’s downtime, should the PCs fail to gather at least one lead upon visiting each new location (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”).

## Starting Leads

The PCs know the mistcloak is the centerpiece of the Elariel Spring Salon auction, benefiting the Young Ascendants. Present the following leads:

**Lead: Young Ascendants Office.** Wilko notes the Young Ascendants operate out of the University of Elendel’s student center (see “[[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|University of Elendel Locations]]” in chapter 6).

**Lead: Tekiel Tower.** NeBaal suggests that since Tekiel Tower is the venue for the Elariel Spring Salon, it may be worth looking around.

### Young Ascendants Office (First Octant)

The Young Ascendants office in the University of Elendel student center is a busy base of operations for a staff of idealistic young women and Young Ascendants scholarship recipients. Enthusiastic but overwhelmed, they’re quick to defend Lysarra despite the intense demands she places on them. PCs speaking with them (DC 15 Persuasion) or glancing at their paperwork (DC 16 Deduction) can pick up leads.

**Lead: Rielle Talent.** Short on runway models, Madam Rielle is urgently seeking new talent.

**Lead: Rail Courier’s Office.** Lysarra’s packages were accidentally redirected to the Tekiel Railyard courier’s office instead of Tekiel Tower.

### Tekiel Tower (Fifth Octant)

A gleaming skyscraper of steel and concrete, Tekiel Tower dominates the Fifth Octant skyline. Tekiel guards patrol everywhere except the Clan Estates on floors 6 through 8. A PC might notice these guards have a more dangerous air than normal guards (DC 14 Insight)—a hint they’re all actually Morgenthax mobsters (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Outlaw Era 2|outlaws]]**). They’re tight-lipped, but a sneaky PC (DC 17 Stealth) can overhear (DC 15 Perception) the leads and some other helpful information: Only guests with verified invitations, Lysarra’s personal staff, and signed models for the fashion show will be allowed on Floor 48 during the Salon. PCs who examine the elevator carefully (DC 16 Crafting or Deduction) can determine some floors are only accessible when a code is entered into the elevator console; this includes Sublevel 1 (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Sublevel 1 Train Platform|Sublevel 1 Train Platform]]” at the end of this chapter).

**Lead: Orlen’s Stitch.** Guards joke that so many models have quit, they might be fitted for costumes at Orlen’s Stitch themselves.

**Lead: Young Ascendants Office.** Instructions from the Young Ascendants office are unusually chaotic, suggesting Lysarra’s focus is elsewhere.

### Rielle Talent (Second Octant)

Nestled between law offices and cafés, a small brickwork building is marked by a golden placard: Rielle Talent. The stylish **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 8/Madam Rielle|Madam Rielle]]** (commoner, she/her) communicates in rapid hand signs with **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 8/Uvina|Uvina]]** (commoner, she/her), a member of the Young Ascendants who interprets for the PCs. PCs who charm (DC 17 Persuasion), bluff confidence (DC 15 Deception), or command the room (DC 16 Leadership) catch Rielle’s interest—she prizes bold individuality over looks. Uvina and Rielle explain many models have quit lately, amid “unfounded” concerns about the Tekiels’ financial troubles and “wild rumors” about House Tekiel guards becoming violent.

![[pg157_MBL08_Rielle Talent_Joshua Chia.webp]]

**Rielle and her interpreter, Uvina - JOSHUA CHIA**

**Lead: Orlen’s Stitch.** If a PC catches Rielle’s interest, she offers to contract them for the Spring Salon, confirming only signed models are eligible for the fashion show. If they agree, she directs them to Orlen’s Stitch for outfitting, paying 25 boxings each up front, promising 50 more after the show.

### Orlen’s Stitch (Second Octant)

Packed with stressed-out models and gorgeous historical Scadrian fashions, Orlen’s Stitch is a tailor shop run under the sharp eye of the koloss-blooded **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 8/Orlen|Orlen]]** (merchant, he/him).

If the PCs already signed the contract with Rielle and need to assemble outfits for the show, continue to “A Fitting at Orlen’s” later in this chapter.

Otherwise, PCs can assist apprentices with sewing tips (DC 14 Crafting), eavesdrop on fittings (DC 16 Perception), or notice Orlen quietly fuming (DC 14 Insight) to find a lead in the chaos. Successfully sneaking a peek through a tower of packages (DC 15 Thievery) also reveals Lysarra’s recent commissions: light, breathable robes and headscarves, all suitable for desert wear.

**Lead: Rielle Talent.** Orlen just lost another batch of models after an intimidating security briefing from “Lysarra’s goons.” He needs more signed models from Rielle Talent.

### Rail Courier’s Office (Eighth Octant)

Cramped and chaotic, the courier’s office in the Tekiel Railyard bustles with shouting clerks and shuffling crates. **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Marthis|Marthis]]** (the society pledge from [[Journal/07 - 06 Desire of a Hungry Soul|chapter 6]]) argues with a red-faced clerk, demanding packages be rerouted to Tekiel Tower. Assuming they parted on good terms, PCs can speak with Marthis (DC 14 Persuasion) to learn he’s interning for Lysarra; Marthis doesn’t know what’s in Lysarra’s “super important” packages, but he can provide leads. PCs can also speak with the clerk (DC 14 Persuasion) to dig up leads, gaining an advantage on the test if they made a positive impression on the Tekiel railway workers in chapter 7.

**Lead: Orlen’s Stitch.** The packages are from Orlen’s Stitch, where Marthis says “models are doing important fashion stuff for the show.”

**Lead: Tekiel Tower.** The packages need to be delivered to “a special loading platform” in Tekiel Tower (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Sublevel 1 Train Platform|Sublevel 1 Train Platform]]” at the end of this chapter).

**Lead: Freight Warehouse.** Lysarra’s packages are waiting in the freight warehouse and need rerouting.

### Freight Warehouse (Eighth Octant)

The warehouse is a vast, echoing space filled with cargo, grinding carts, and shouting workers. PCs can stay unseen (DC 17 Stealth) or pose as workers carrying cargo (DC 16 Athletics) while searching for Lysarra’s packages. Grouped together are clearly labeled shipments for Lysarra containing warm-weather clothing, common metal vials, two ***revolvers***, a ***boot pistol***, and 10 rounds of ***aluminum ammunition***. A rolled-up blueprint depicts plans for an enormous vehicle, a steam-engine drill. Manifests direct these items to “Platform T1: Tekiel Tower, Sublevel 1.”

The PCs can notice a large man with a pinched face and a sharp suit. This is **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 8/Teddy|Teddy]]** (outlaw boss, he/him), a minor boss for the Morgenthax family, the crime syndicate funding the Tekiels. If the PCs pique Teddy’s suspicion, he approaches. If the PCs don’t leave after several clear threats, Teddy attacks, along with five other mobsters working the warehouse (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Outlaw Era 2|outlaws]]**). If the PCs defeat Teddy here, he doesn’t attack them later (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Menacing Morgenthax|Menacing Morgenthax]]”).

**Lead: None.** Though PCs learn about Lysarra’s plans at this location, there are no new leads here.

### Resolving the Endeavor

Instead of succeeding or failing, this open-ended investigation concludes whenever the PCs settle on a plan to infiltrate the Spring Salon. The party loses one day of downtime before the Salon for each time they fail to find any leads when visiting a new location, chasing bad hunches and hitting dead ends.

One revelation should always be clear: Rielle Talent is actively hiring models for the Spring Salon fashion show, making this a potential infiltration option. If the PCs take this approach, proceed to “Dressed to Kill.” If the PCs discover this information but make a different plan, proceed to “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Unsigned Talent|Unsigned Talent.]]”

## Unsigned Talent

Though modeling is the easiest path, the PCs may decide on another infiltration approach. Some options include:

**Friends in High Places.** Convincing a noble ally to part with their invitation requires a successful DC 19 Persuasion test and a promise to bid and win the Fan of the Bloom item at the auction.

**Charitable Endeavors.** If the PCs donated to the Young Ascendants in chapter 5 or now pledge a donation of at least 1,000 boxings, they can make a DC 15 Persuasion test, and on a success, earn VIP invitations as thanks.

**Party Crashers.** Sneaking in through service shafts requires a success on a DC 18 Deduction or Thievery test to discover a viable entry point. These routes are cramped, and PCs can’t bring large weapons or bulky gear through the narrow crawlspaces.

Once the PCs decide on an approach, proceed to “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|Dressed to Kill.]]”

## Dressed to Kill

After investigating, the party now has up to ten days of downtime before the Elariel Spring Salon (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Downtime Before the Salon|Downtime Before the Salon]]”). If the PCs haven’t already, they can obtain disguises as fashion models during downtime; when they do, run “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|A Fitting at Orlen’s.]]” The party’s preparations are punctuated by a visit from Ironeyes and, on the day of the Salon, an assassination attempt on the PCs.

## A Fitting at Orlen’s

If the PCs enter Orlen’s with contracts from Rielle, read:

The shop is chaos; fabric bolts spill across wooden floors while apprentices zigzag between mannequins and models. Someone yells about a torn hem, another about a stain, and somewhere in the back, someone is definitely crying.\
“You’re the new models?” Orlen barks, gesturing wildly with sharp-looking scissors. “You’re late. Find an apprentice, select an ensemble—and for the love of Preservation, don’t touch anything unless you want to be stitched to it!”

While the apprentices take their measurements, the PCs learn the outfits are all inspired by the historical Fellise Masque style. This fashion movement after the fall of the Lord Ruler featured the following:

**Patterned Accents.** Cuffs and linings with bold architectural lines, reminiscent of noble keeps’ spires.

**Curtain Dresses.** Voluminous skirts and sleeves with ornate wooden tassels.

**Crest Brooches.** Hooded outerwear, capable of hiding one’s face, secured by an ostentatious brooch with a family crest.

**Terris Long Vests.** Elongated vests with mixed-metal embellishments stitched into angular lapels.

Have each PC attempt to work with their tailor, making a DC 14 Crafting or Lore test. On a success, the PC gains an advantage on their first test during “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Caper on the Catwalk|Caper on the Catwalk,]]” and the outfit gains the Presentable trait. On a failure, the apprentice grumbles about amateurs ruining their hems and pokes the PC with a needle.

After fittings, Orlen instructs the PCs to return to pick up their finished outfits on the day of the Salon.

This optional downtime scene is available if you plan to run the “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Postlude|Postlude]]” scene at the end of chapter 9. During this downtime, choose a PC who either is interested in the Guardians organization, would find it especially meaningful to interact with this canonical character, or hasn’t been highlighted recently. A man in a black trenchcoat steps out of the shadows. Under the brim of his hat, steel spike-heads glint where his eyes should be. The left spike is pushed deeply into the socket, scars marring the thick black tattoos around that eye.\
“I don’t mean to startle you.” He speaks in a low, gravelly voice. “I’ve come to say… We appreciate your actions. Harmony and I. We aren’t supposed to interfere, but you should know that what you’re doing is important.”

## Salon Prep

The afternoon of the Spring Salon, Wilko visits the party to share what she and NeBaal have uncovered:

**Fashion Runway.** The evening opens with the show “Gilt and Glamour: Scadrian Fashion Through the Ages.” Models walk the runway, displaying items up for auction.

**Auction.** Modeled outfits are auctioned, along with a few era-original pieces, including the Midnight Mistcloak.

**Socializing.** The evening ends with drinks and a chance to socialize.

After laying out the schedule, Wilko asks the PCs for their opinion of NeBaal. She admits a fascination with the kandra as living history, but she’s pained by all the suffering they’ve endured. Concerned about what lies ahead, Wilko gravely requests that, should anything happen to her, the PCs ensure NeBaal doesn’t face the future alone.

When the PCs finish discussing, they’re due to pick up their outfits at Orlen’s (if they’re infiltrating as models) before heading to Tekiel Tower. Proceed to “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Menacing Morgenthax|Menacing Morgenthax.]]”

## Menacing Morgenthax

If the PCs already defeated Teddy in the freight warehouse during “Tekiel Investigation,” skip to “A Fashionable Infiltration.”

Otherwise, as the party makes their way to Tekiel Tower, they’re intercepted by **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 8/Teddy|Teddy]]** (outlaw boss) and his entourage of five mobsters (**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Various/Era 2 Adversaries/Outlaw Era 2|outlaws]]**).

Have each PC make a DC 17 Perception test. If at least half the PCs succeed, they spot Teddy following them with practiced nonchalance; if the PCs try to confront or evade him, he whistles, and five other mobsters appear right behind the party. If all the PCs fail, they spot nothing amiss until they suddenly find themselves surrounded. Either way, read:

A man with a pinched face and a heavy fur-lined coat steps forward, putting out his cigar. Surrounding him is an intimidating entourage, their hands not-so-subtly clutching concealed weapons, tattoos peeking out from under their rolled-up suit sleeves.\
“You’ve been causing a lot of trouble, what do they call yous, the ‘rail rascals’ of the Roughs?” The man clicks his tongue disapprovingly. “It’s an important night for our Lady friend, so I’m glad we just happened to bump into yous.”\
The mobsters draw weapons. “I’m sorry to say, you ain’t going to no party tonight. You’ve been disinvited."

![[pg160_MBL08_Morgenthax Mobster_Ignaszewski Pierre.webp]]

**Teddy Morgenthax - PIERRE IGNASZEWSKI**

Teddy and his crew advance to capture or kill the PCs. If over half the party failed their earlier Perception test, they’re surrounded, with the mobsters Bracing behind cover at the start of combat. Otherwise, the mobsters face them in a line.

### Battlefield Effects

The following effect is active during this combat:

**Thread the Needle.** When a PC who is posing as a model is hit by an attack, they must make a DC 13 Agility test to protect their outfit. On a failure, they must choose to either take maximum damage from the attack or have their outfit damaged.

### Aftermath

The mobsters won’t fight to the death; once Teddy is downed, they retreat. Subdued mobsters refuse to speak, but they all bear Morgenthax tattoos (a book with the number 9 on the right page). Teddy has a sketch of the PCs, ripped from Kwylliam’s The Truth in Elendel broadsheet, and a crumpled, unsigned note with orders to eliminate the PCs.

If a PC’s outfit was damaged, they must succeed on a DC 18 Crafting test to make a hasty repair or improvise a quick replacement before the fashion show, or else every test they make during “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Strut or Stumble|Strut or Stumble]]” gains a disadvantage.

## A Fashionable Infiltration

Security at Tekiel Tower is heavy, and the venue is crowded with nobles in finery. Morgenthax enforcers dressed in House Tekiel uniforms patrol the lobby and monitor all visitors.

If the party signed a contract with Rielle, their names are on the list. They’re escorted to a private elevator by two enforcers and taken to Floor 48, where organized mayhem reigns. An opulent ballroom here is in the final stages of setup, a large catwalk dominating the floor (see map 8.1 later in this chapter). Marthis is here coordinating setup. Enforcers lead the party backstage, where models are applying makeup, adjusting expensive outfits, and bickering with harried assistants.

If the PCs aren’t posing as models, this is a good place to determine how they enter the Floor 48 ballroom, where they can talk to Marthis before taking seats in the crowd.

If the PCs lost the duel with Kwylliam in chapter 6 and didn’t make good on their favor, the PCs now find themselves social outcasts among the nobles at the Salon, gaining a disadvantage on all social tests during “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Marthis the Intern|Marthis the Intern]]” and “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|Auction Action]]” as the elite guests snicker at their expense.

## Marthis the Intern

His reaction when the PCs approach depends on how the party treated him in chapter 6.

Marthis catches sight of you, then quickly looks away. “Didn’t expect to see you here,” he says. Conflict flickers over his face. “I guess… well, I hope you have fun at the auction. Big finale, I hear.”

### Conversation Approaches

A successful Insight test against Marthis’s Spiritual defense (14) reveals his anxiety. To get him to say more (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Resolving the Conversation|Resolving the Conversation]]”), the PCs might try the following:

**Expressing Concern.** The PCs can check in with Marthis empathetically. If a PC succeeds on a test, Marthis finds their kindness calming; the next test a PC makes to influence him gains an advantage, and he admits he’s been anxious all evening. He’s not sure what he’s gotten himself into since the trials—he’s just following Lysarra’s lead and trying to make his family proud.

**Probing for Information.** If the party asks for details about Lysarra or the auction, Marthis mumbles that he can’t say much. On a successful test, he spends focus to resist and admits Lysarra asked him to lead this technical setup at the last minute, and she’s been snappy all night. He says she’s meticulous with preparations and believes she’s just in a hurry because she’s leaving later this evening (he doesn’t know where).

**Applying Pressure.** If the PCs demand answers or accuse Marthis of hiding information, have a PC make a DC 18 Intimidation test. On a success, Marthis cracks, and the conversation succeeds. On a failure, Marthis refuses to engage for the rest of the auction, and the conversation ends in failure. This is a good place to raise the stakes.

### Resisting Influence

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Marthis|Marthis]]** (society pledge) has a Cognitive defense of 13, a Spiritual defense of 14, and 4 focus. He spends focus to resist questioning about the auction or pressure to betray Lysarra. He’s receptive to genuine emotional connection, especially if the party helped him previously.

### Resolving the Conversation

The conversation succeeds if the party builds enough trust to overcome Marthis’s hesitations or if his focus is reduced to 0. If the PCs treated him poorly in chapter 6, he’s wary, and a single failed test ends the conversation in failure.

**Success.** Marthis is visibly conflicted, caution quickly spiraling into rambling. Overwhelmed, he insists he doesn’t know what’s happening but says something feels wrong, and he’s scared. He overheard Lysarra mentioning a surprise beyond the fashion show pageantry. He feels stupid sharing this, but when she said “surprise,” it sounded threatening.

**Failure.** Whether from guilt or the fear of betraying Lysarra, Marthis shuts down completely. He mutters something about “leaving him and his family alone” and exits the backstage area, avoiding the PCs for the rest of the evening.

Either way, once the conversation concludes, if the PCs are posing as models, continue to “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Caper on the Catwalk|Caper on the Catwalk.]]” Otherwise, proceed directly to “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|Auction Action.]]”

### Auction Flow

PCs can participate in the auction by bidding on items as a group. Introduce the Dominate the Auction event to the players, and explain how the “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Rules for Bidding|Rules for Bidding]]” work. - The bidding ends on the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Robe of Silenced Steel|Robe of Silenced Steel]]*** (detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]). Allow the PCs to receive the robe, if they won.

- The [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Event: Dominate the Auction|Dominate the Auction]] event triggers.

- The PCs discover the trap. See “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Discovering the Trap|Discovering the Trap.]]”

### Auction Items

The following items are presented in order:

**Fan of the Bloom.** A delicate stained-glass fan crafted during the turn-of-the-century “Glassgarden” fashion wave, popularized in salons and horticultural clubs. Opening bid: 75 boxings. Value: 500 boxings. Rival bidders: 2.

**Robe of Silenced Steel.** This robe shields objects beneath it from the effects of Allomancy (as detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]). Opening bid: 100 boxings. Value: 2,000 boxings. Rival bidders: 3.

**Midnight Mistcloak.** This is (ostensibly) the final item up for bidding.

PCs can make a DC 15 Lore or DC 17 Crafting test to appraise an item as it comes up for bidding. On a success, they learn the item’s value and gain an advantage on tests to bid on it.

### Rules for Bidding

Bidding takes place in rounds. Each round, the following happens:

**Bid or Withdraw.** The party must bid or withdraw. If they bid, they must raise the bid by at least 25 boxings.

**Make a Test.** PCs bidding on behalf of the group must make a DC 16 test with a relevant cognitive or spiritual skill. If the players raised their bid by at least 50 boxings, raise the stakes. On a success, a rival bidder drops out; on a failure, a rival retaliates with a 100-boxing raise.

**Rivals Bid.** The current bid increases by 50 boxings for each rival still bidding. Then a new round starts.

The bidding for an item ends when the PCs withdraw or all rivals drop out. When the PCs win a bid, progress the Dominate the Auction event by 1, and have a PC immediately head to the stage to pay, receive the item, and possibly notice the trap door (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Trap Door and Dumbwaiter|Trapdoor and Dumbwaiter]]”).

### Event: Dominate the Auction

When the auction begins, create a Dominate the Auction positive event with four slots. Tell the players the event represents their control of the mood of the room, and triggering it represents their outright dominance in the battle of wits between bidders. However, in truth, the event is tracking whether the PCs discover Lysarra’s trap before it is sprung.

#### Progressing the Event

A PC can spend an Opportunity to progress the event by 1. The event also progresses by 1 when the PCs win an auction item.

- Lysarra is nowhere to be found.

## Hold Your Breath

The PCs must get through the locked, reinforced doors before being overtaken by the viridian mist. Run this scene as an endeavor (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Resolving the Endeavor|Resolving the Endeavor]]”).

If the PCs completed the [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Event: Dominate the Auction|Dominate the Auction]] event or otherwise discovered the room was a trap, they begin this endeavor with 2 successes already accrued.

### Special Rule: The Viridian Mists

Each character takes 1d4 vital damage at the start of each round of the endeavor. Each character who fails a test during the endeavor takes an extra 1d8 vital damage. A character who doesn’t need to breathe (such as a Cadmium Ferring using Tap Breath) doesn’t take damage.

### Special Rule: Abandoned Auction

Consult map 8.1 for the ballroom layout. Any items still waiting to be auctioned are left unattended on the stage (at the south end of area [[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Floor 48 and Floor 40 Locations|T1]]), including the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Midnight Mistcloak|Midnight Mistcloak]]*** (detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]).

Lysarra intends to reclaim the mistcloak after the mist clears. A PC can make a contribution, such as a DC 22 Agility test or a DC 20 Discipline test, to grab the mistcloak (or any other unclaimed auction item), taking 2d8 vital damage from the mists when they do. If they succeed, they gain the item but don’t count the success toward the endeavor. If they fail, they don’t gain the item.

### Special Rule: Other Guests

**[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 7/Merid|Merid]]** (lawkeeper), **[[Actors/Legacy Adversaries/Named/Ch. 6/Marthis|Marthis]]** (society pledge), and any other friendly NPCs immediately inhale the gas. Without assistance, they perish after the PCs accrue 4 failures. A PC can make a contribution, such as a DC 12 Medicine test or DC 15 Athletics test, to help a specific NPC. If they succeed, don’t count the success toward the endeavor, but roll 1d4 and add the result to the number of failures remaining before that NPC perishes.

If a PC seeks out Merid, he begins assisting one PC per round, adding an advantage on their test.

### Breach the Doors

The PCs must breach the ballroom doors to escape the flood of poisonous mists. Potential approaches include:

**Shatter the Mechanisms.** Characters can break the hinges and reinforcements on the doors with an appropriate weapon and a precise application of ordnance (DC 17 Light Weaponry or Heavy Weaponry). This is a good place to raise the stakes.

**Heave!** Characters can use a banquet table or lectern as a battering ram (DC 17 Athletics) or help coordinate efforts to break down the doors (DC 17 Leadership).

**Unlock the Doors.** A character with lockpicks can work to unjam the well-made lock (DC 17 Thievery).

### Resolving the Endeavor

The endeavor succeeds when the party accrues 6 successes, or if they otherwise complete the objective. If the PCs triggered the Dominate the Auction event or otherwise discovered the room was a trap, they begin the endeavor with 2 successes already accrued.

**Success.** As the mists swallow the entire ballroom, the PCs emerge into the corridor, gasping but alive. If any other NPCs survived (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Special Rule: Other Guests|Special Rule: Other Guests]]”), they’re too weak to assist further. The rest of the Spring Salon attendees lie dead.

**Failure.** Unlike many other endeavors, no fixed number of failures ends this endeavor. The PCs continue to take damage from the viridian mist until they escape or they die.

### Spike Extraction

During the fight, within the mist-filled ballroom, the five gas-masked Conrad operatives harvest spikes from the bodies of the Gilded Few. Their task is to send the spikes down the dumbwaiter to Sublevel 1 (see “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion#Sublevel 1 Train Platform|Sub-level 1 Train Platform]]”). They complete the extraction after three rounds. If the PCs approach, one operative attempts to grab the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Midnight Mistcloak|Midnight Mistcloak]]*** and distract the PCs while the rest of the operatives continue working.

### Battlefield Effects

The following effects are active during combat:

**Glass Curtain.** The windows are easily shattered; even if not targeted directly, attacks near them might warrant raising the stakes. The GM can spend C to cause a window to shatter. Each character directly adjacent to a shattering window must Avoid Danger (r) or take 1d6 keen damage from flying glass. The area within 10 feet of a shattered window becomes dangerous terrain [1d4 keen].

**Viridian Mists.** If the ballroom door is open, the mists spread 15 feet at the start of each round. A character who starts their turn in or enters the mists for the first time on their turn takes 2d8 vital damage. A broken window disperses mists within 10 feet and stops the mists from progressing.

**Freefall.** If a character adjacent to a shattered window is pushed (via a Shove or otherwise), they can Avoid Danger (r) by succeeding on a DC 15 Agility test. If the character fails to Avoid Danger, they fall from the window and plummet eight stories down to the Floor 40 ledge (T3), taking 8d6 impact damage and landing Prone.

**Wall-Mounted Transmitter.** A character can Interact (1) with the wall-mounted devices to enable ongoing Banter (0) between the transmitters in the hallway (T2) and ballroom (T1).

**Elevator.** Characters inside the elevator can Interact (1) to select a floor; at the start of the next round, the elevator arrives at that floor. If any adversaries fall to Floor 40, they use the elevator to ascend back to the fight.

### Aftermath

Lysarra fights ferociously and with deadly intent, but she doesn’t wish to die needlessly. If still alive when the operatives arrive at the start of round 4, she attempts to retreat via the elevator, using her key code to get to the sublevel floor. Two outcomes are possible:

**Lysarra Escapes.** If Lysarra successfully flees via the elevator, the Conrad operatives break through glass windows and rappel away. Vorn (or the replacement security captain) fights to the death, at which point any surviving Morgenthax toughs flee or surrender. A few minutes later, the building rumbles as the Breaknaught leaves the Sublevel 1 platform.

**Lysarra Defeated.** If the PCs defeat Lysarra, a moment before she expires, the entire building rumbles as the Breaknaught leaves the Sublevel 1 platform. Lysarra gives a bitter smile, saying, “You’re too late. Your professor friend has already been an enormous help—she made such careful, detailed notes… Just as good as the originals.” With her dying words, Lysarra insists her plans will outlive her: Bayron or Kwylliam will achieve godhood and wipe the PCs, scum that they are, off the face of Scadrial. After the fighting ends, if a PC has the Hemalurgist expertise, they can attempt to remove Lysarra’s ***bronze Hemalurgic spike***, which grants Brass Allomancy, from her corpse (see “[[Journal/01 - 00 Introduction#Handling Hemalurgy|Handling Hemalurgy]]” in this book’s introduction).

The ballroom is empty of life if the PCs reenter afterward. Investigation reveals silence and spike- stripped corpses. Defeated Conrad operatives each have a ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Gas Mask Era 2|gas mask]]*** (detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]). The PCs find 1,000 boxings on the security captain and a note reading “Elevator: 1024,” the code to access Sublevel 1. Any auction items the PCs didn’t already claim were delivered down the dumbwaiter with the spikes. If Merid survived, he finds the elevator code if the PCs don’t, and suggests using it.

## Sublevel 1 Train Platform

After a character enters 1024 in the elevator console, the elevator jolts, dropping with surprising speed. It lets the PCs out on a secret basement-level floor, Sublevel 1. Read:

The lift doors open on a cavernous room smelling of oil and cement. Harsh electric lights illuminate a train platform. Crates are stacked nearby in disarray, and a small hatch in the wall stands ajar. The empty train tracks disappear down a long, dark tunnel.

These sublevel tracks are part of Lysarra’s secret railway project leading to Old Luthadel. The Breaknaught left in a hurry and without all its supplies, leaving the tracks empty but warm. PCs who investigate the hatch find a dumbwaiter smelling of blood. It connects to the ballroom (among other floors). It contains a ***zinc Hemalurgic spike*** and a ***copper Hemalurgic spike*** inside a blood-filled metal vial.

## Wilko Abducted

When the PCs return to Wilko’s apartment, the door is ajar, splintered from forced entry. The interior is silent. As they enter, read:

The door hangs partially open on damaged hinges, its lock twisted. Inside, the apartment is a ruin. Drawers gutted, shelves half-emptied, books torn and trampled. A delicate tea service for two lies shattered near a small table, cups in pieces, spiced tea dripping from the shards. In the devastated room’s epicenter lies a melted puddle of transparent flesh and bones.

![[pg171_MBL08_Wilko Abducted_Kevin ONeill.webp]]

**Wilko’s apartment is in disarray after the Gilded Trio strike. - KEVIN O’NEILL**

Anyone examining the puddle finds NeBaal, who is suffering the effects of a kandra-disrupting compound that prevents them from holding shape. For a few minutes, NeBaal can only helplessly mouth, “Azmine.” Professor Azmine Wilko is nowhere in sight.

As the toxin begins to clear, NeBaal’s form stabilizes enough to speak. Their voice is laden with tension and fear. NeBaal shares the following:

**The Break-In.** NeBaal describes a forced entry by Morgenthax thugs shortly before the PCs’ arrival.

**Kandra Poison.** NeBaal attempted to intercept the intruders but was stuck with a needle—one coated in a substance that destroyed their ability to hold shape. Their body failed instantly. Though conscious, they couldn’t move or speak, and they helplessly watched the thugs tear the room apart and leave with Wilko.

**Wilko’s Abduction.** The intruders went straight for Wilko, then started looking for her journal. NeBaal’s voice tightens with barely contained emotion as they recount her being led away.

**The Stolen Journal.** Before leaving, the intruders located Wilko’s color-coded journal, containing her extensive notes and sketches detailing the party’s collected Gilded Map fragments. Combined with what Lysarra almost certainly gleaned this past month while in possession of the Midnight Mistcloak, the Trio can now navigate the tunnels to reach the Eternity Vault.

## Journeying Onward

The Era 2 characters advance to 8th level.

At this point, the PCs may have finally assembled all three pieces of the Gilded Map: the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Raven Mask|Raven Mask]]***, the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Unity Medal|Unity Medal]]***, and the ***[[Items/Legacy Unique Items/Midnight Mistcloak|Midnight Mistcloak]]*** (detailed in [[Journal/12 - Appendix B Items|appendix B]]). However, with Wilko kidnapped, the Trio has a clear path to their ultimate goal, and the party is a step behind. The PCs now have a clear trail to follow—and a friend to rescue.

If Merid survived “[[Journal/09 - 08 Fatal Fashion|An Emerald Ending,]]” he pledges the full support of the Guardians (see [[Journal/16 - Appendix F The Guardians|appendix F]]). If Merid died, instead decrease the Guardians organization’s scale by 1. If Marthis survived, he expresses heartfelt gratitude, offering 1,000 boxings and whatever favors he can provide. For additional information and benefits offered by Merid and Marthis, see “[[Journal/10 - 09 All Ends in Luthadel#Friendly Faces|A Matter of Time]]” in chapter 9.

From here, the Era 2 PCs race to Old Luthadel—and to the end of all things.
