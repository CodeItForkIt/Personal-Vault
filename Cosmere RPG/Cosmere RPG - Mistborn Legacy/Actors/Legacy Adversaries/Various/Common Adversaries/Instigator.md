# Instigator

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 20/20, Focus 5/5, Investiture 5/5  
**Skills:** ATH 2, LWP 2, DIS 2, INM 2, LEA 2, PRS 2, SUR 2

##### Tactics

An **instigator** uses **To Ashes** on terrain occupied by enemies or on routes they’re likely to cross. They then **Burn Zinc** to Riot fear or anger in nearby targets, maintaining the effect each round. The instigator avoids direct engagement, striking with their **Sidesword** only when an enemy is already threatened by others. If targeted directly, the instigator disengages and repositions, attempting to stay on the outskirts and destabilize the fight as long as possible.

### Blood Bath
When a character the instigator can sense is reduced to 0 health, the instigator recovers 1 focus and gains an advantage on their next attack.

### Strike: Sidesword
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, if the target is being affected by Burn Zinc, the instigator deals an extra `1d6 Keen`.

### Strike: Improvised Weapon
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or 15/30 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`. If the attack also hits against the target’s Cognitive defense, they’re **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

### To Ashes
*2 act*

The Instigator chooses a Large (10-foot) space within 10 feet of them, igniting the ground and unattended objects in that area. While on fire, that area becomes dangerous terrain that deals `1d8 Energy`. A character can use 1 to extinguish a 5-foot area.

### Burn Zinc
*2 act*

The Instigator chooses an emotion and chooses one or more targets in a Large (10-foot) area within 40 feet of them, then they make an *Test: `skill=all`* against the Spiritual defense of each target. On a success, the chosen emotion is Rioted in that target, influencing them to act in alignment with that emotion until the end of their next turn. Before this effect ends, the Instigator can maintain it on all affected targets in that area by spending 1 or more Investiture as 0.

An affected target can resist as 0, spending focus equal to the Investiture spent to **Burn Zinc** (instead of the usual cost to resist influence). After they do, they ignore their altered emotions until the start of their next turn.

### White-Hot Rage
When a character the instigator can sense within 40 feet of them misses with an attack, the instigator can momentarily Riot the attacker’s ferocity, causing them to graze themself.
