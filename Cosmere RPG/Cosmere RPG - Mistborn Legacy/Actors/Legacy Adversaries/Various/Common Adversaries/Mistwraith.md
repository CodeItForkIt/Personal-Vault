# Mistwraith

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** huge  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 0, Intellect 1, Willpower 2, Awareness 5, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 26/26, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** ATH 2, PRC 3, SUR 3

##### Tactics

In combat encounters, a **mistwraith** is more akin to an environmental hazard than a true enemy. Until they can see a corpse to devour, mistwraiths can only follow living creatures—as detailed in their Haunting feature. Though mistwraiths are passive and don’t typically react if attacked, their Eerie Aura and Disturbing Sight features usually still discourage potential foes from getting too close.

### Haunting
On each of the mistwraith’s turns, they must spend their actions to first **Move** as close as possible to the nearest corpse they can sense. Once within 5 feet of a corpse, they must **Devour Corpse** as soon as they can. If they can’t sense a corpse, they instead **Move** to follow living characters.

### Disturbing Sight
A character with 0 focus who senses the Mistwraith can’t chose to move toward them.

### Eerie Aura
Each time a character starts their turn within 30 feet of one or more Mistwraiths they can sense, that character must resist influence or become **Exhausted** [−1].

### Harmless
A character who either succeeds on a *Test: `skill=lor dc=18`* or spends O recognizes that mistwraiths are harmless. That character ignores the mistwraith’s **Disturbing Sight** and **Eerie Aura** features.

### Necrophage
When a character ends their turn touching the mistwraith, that character takes `1d6 Vital`.

### Devour Corpse
*2 act*

The Mistwraith devours a corpse within 5 feet of them and recovers 10 health.

### Scavenger
After a character the Mistwraith can sense is reduced to 0 health or suffers an injury, the Mistwraith moves up to their movement rate toward that character.
