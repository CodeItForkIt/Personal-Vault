# Outlaw (Era 2)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 0, Speed 2, Intellect 0, Willpower 1, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 11/11, Focus 3/3, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 2, LWP 1, STL 1, THV 2, DIS 1, INM 1, PRC 1, SUR 1

##### Tactics

**Outlaws** rarely fight fair, preferring to ambush and outnumber their targets. Their Ambusher feature gives them an immediate upper hand, allowing them to take shots before their enemies can react. If a fight closes to melee, outlaws use their Roughhousing action to disorient their targets. An outlaw who begins a combat often fights to the death, but if they were caught unawares, they may try to escape—so they can strike again when the advantage is theirs.

### Roughhousing
*2 act*

The Outlaw (Era 2) makes an *Test: `skill=inm`* against the Physical defense of a target within 5 feet of them. On a success, the target loses 1 focus and is **Disoriented** until the end of the outlaw’s next turn.

### Ambusher
At the start of each scene, if the Outlaw (Era 2) isn’t **Surprised**, they make a *Test: `skill=stl`* against the enemy they can sense who has the highest Spiritual defense. On a success, the Outlaw (Era 2) can use 1 before the first round. On a failure, or if they can’t sense any enemies, they can use **Gain Advantag**e as 0 on their first turn.

### Minion
The Outlaw (Era 2)’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.
