# Society Pledge (Lurcher) (Era 2)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 1, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 11/11, Focus 4/4, Investiture 4/4  
**Skills:** ATH 2, LWP 1, STL 2, INM 4, LOR 2, DEC 2

##### Tactics

A **society pledge** uses **Invoke Status** to force movement or strip focus before attacking (or if they’re a Coinshot or Lurcher, they use **Steelpush** or **Ironpull**). Once a foe is reduced to 0 focus, the pledge attacks them with **Gilded Cane** strikes.

### Minion
The Society Pledge (Lurcher) (Era 2)’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Life of Privilege
Once per test, the society pledge can reroll all natural 1s from a roll.

### Powerful Name
The society pledge gains an advantage on **Intimidation** and **Persuasion** tests made while invoking their family name.

### Strike: Gilded Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, if the target has no focus, they take an extra `2d4 Impact`.

### Ironpull
*1 act*

The pledge chooses a metal object they sense, and they move themself or the object straight toward the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward the pledge, whereas the pledge moves toward objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size. This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at an enemy in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the pledge must succeed on an **Allomancy** test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, they must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d6 + 4 Impact`.

### Burn Iron
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, the pledge senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 40 feet of them.
