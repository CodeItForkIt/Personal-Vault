# Freedom Fighter (Era 2)

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 1, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 21/21, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 3, LWP 3, DIS 3, PRC 3

##### Tactics

**Freedom fighters** work together and stoke each other’s boldness. During a fight, their **Harsh Judgment** ability allows a group of freedom fighters to completely swarm their enemies, and their **Conviction** feature makes freedom fighters more difficult to injure when in the presence of something they fight for. When low on focus, a freedom fighter can still use their **Knife** or **Shotgun** and support their allies with **Enkindled Passion**.

### Minion
The Freedom Fighter (Era 2)’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Conviction
While the freedom fighter is within 120 feet of something that inspires their conviction, their deflect value increases by 1.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

### Strike: Shotgun
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d10 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d10 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`;

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [2]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Harsh Judgment
*2 act*

The freedom fighter proclaims judgment against an enemy within 5 feet of the freedom fighter. Every other freedom fighter within 5 feet of the target can use r to join the judgment. The target takes `1d6 Keen` per freedom fighter who proclaims judgment.

### Enkindled Passion
*2 act*

The Freedom Fighter (Era 2) uses the **Gain Advantage** action (no action required). On a successful test, every other freedom fighter within 5 feet of them also gains an advantage on their next test against that target.
