# Koloss Clan Protector (Era 2)

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 6, Speed 3, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 1, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 50/50, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** ATH 2, HWP 2, INM 3, PRC 3, SUR 3

##### Tactics

A **koloss clan protector** strikes with their **Koloss Spike Maul** to knock a victim **Prone**, then they **Pulverize** their **Prone** target. At range, they can **Demoralize** their target and attack with their **Hand Cannon**.

### Malleable Will
The Koloss Clan Protector (Era 2) can only be affected by emotional Allomancy if the Allomancer spends 6 Investiture or more to **Burn Brass** or **Burn Zinc**. Once affected, the Koloss Clan Protector (Era 2) can’t end those effects on themself.

### Unyielding
The Koloss Clan Protector (Era 2) gains an advantage on tests to resist being moved or knocked **Prone**.

### Strike: Hand Cannon
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`.

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [6]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Strike: Koloss Spike Maul
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d10 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d10 + @skills.hwp.mod Impact average`, if the target is Large or smaller, the koloss can also spend 1 focus to knock them **Prone**.

### Pulverize
*2 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `2d8 + @skills.ath.mod Impact average`, this attack deals an extra `1d8 Impact` to a **Prone** target. On a hit, each character within 5 feet of the target who has a Physical defense equal to or lower than the attack result is also grazed by the attack.

### Demoralize
*1 act*

The Koloss Clan Protector (Era 2) makes an *Test: `skill=inm`* against the Cognitive defense of a character they can influence within 30 feet of them. On a success, the target loses 1 focus and their deflect value becomes 0 until the end of their next turn.
