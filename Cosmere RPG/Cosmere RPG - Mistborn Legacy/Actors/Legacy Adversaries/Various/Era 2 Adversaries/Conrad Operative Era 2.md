# Conrad Operative (Era 2)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 30/30, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** AGI 1, LWP 2, STL 2, CRA 2, DIS 2, PRC 2

### Strike: Baton
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, if the target is **Surprised** or **Disoriented**, this attack deals an extra `1d6 Impact`.

### Strike: Silenced Pistol
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + 3 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, if the target is **Surprised** or **Disoriented**, this attack deals an extra `1d6 Impact`.

*Pierce:* This damage can't be reduced by the target's deflect.

*Loaded [6]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Strike: Poison Gas Grenade
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/40 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the target is also **Afflicted** [ `1d4` vital] until they recover at least 1 health.

*Blast [5]:* On a hit, this attack grazes each character within 5 feet of the target; the operative can spend 2 focus to instead hit each of them.

### Deploy Devious Device
*2 act*

The Conrad operative makes a **Crafting** attack test against the Cognitive defense of a character they can sense within 5 feet of them, rolling `1d6 + 4 Energy`. On a hit, the target is also **Disoriented** or **Immobilized** (operative’s choice) until the end of the target’s next turn.

### Run Interference
Before an enemy the Conrad operative can sense within 5 feet of them makes a test using a physical skill, the operative can make an **Agility** test against the enemy’s Cognitive defense. On a success, the enemy gains a disadvantage on damage rolls until the end of their next turn.
