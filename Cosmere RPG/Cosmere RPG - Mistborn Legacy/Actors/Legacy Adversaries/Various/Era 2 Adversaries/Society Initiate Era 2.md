# Society Initiate (Era 2)

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 2, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 35/35, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** ATH 3, LWP 3, STL 3, DIS 3, INM 3, LOR 3, DEC 3, LEA 3, FER 1

##### Tactics

A **society initiate** uses **This Is Beneath Me** against an enemy they think is likely to make several skill tests, then they attack enemies with their **Revolver** or **Gilded Sidesword**. Once a foe is reduced to 0 focus, the society initiate focuses on them with **Eradicate the Weak** and with Strikes.

### Life of Privilege
Once per test, the society pledge can reroll all natural 1s from a roll.

### Powerful Name
The society pledge gains an advantage on **Intimidation** and **Persuasion** tests made while invoking their family name.

### Untouchable
The society initiate can spend 1 focus to turn a graze into a miss.

### Strike: Revolver
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 +@skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can't be reduced by the target's deflect.

*Loaded [6]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Strike: Gilded Sidesword
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, if the target has no focus, they take an extra `2d6 Keen`.

### Eradicate the Weak
*2 act*

The society initiate makes an additional Strike against a target with no focus (even if the initiate has already used that Strike action).

### This is Beneath Me
*2 act*

The society initiate makes an **Intimidation** test against the Cognitive defense of an enemy they can influence within 30 feet of them. On a success, the target experiences the following effects until the end of their next turn:

- They gain a disadvantage on all tests.

- After they fail a test, they lose 2 focus.

A character can resist this influence, but after they do, they lose an additional 2 focus.

### Tap Health
*1 act*

Now and at the start of each of the society initiate’s turns until the end of the scene, they recover .
