# Conrad Elite Operative (Era 2)

**Tier:** 3  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 5, Intellect 4, Willpower 3, Awareness 5, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 54/54, Focus 5/5, Investiture 7/7  
**Skills:** AGI 3, LWP 3, STL 2, CRA 2, DIS 3, LEA 2, PRC 2, ALL 4

### Strike: Hunting Rifle (Explosive Rounds)
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 150/600 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average` and `1d8 Energy`, every other character within 5 feet of the target also takes `1d8 Energy`

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [3]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

**FOUNDRY NOTE**: Currently multiple damages types are not supported, the Energy damage will need to be rolled separately by simply clicking the button in the chat card.

### Strike: Fragmentation Grenade
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `2d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Blast [5]:* On a hit, this attack grazes each character within 5 feet of the target; the elite operative can spend 2 focus to instead hit each of them.

### Burn Tin
*1 act*

The Conrad Elite Operative (Era 2) enhances their senses, experiencing the following effects until the end of the scene or until they end them on a later turn as 0:

- They become **Enhanced** [**Awareness**]. The bonus to this attribute equals half the Investiture they spent to **Burn Tin** (rounded up).

- All their senses are supernatural senses. The first time each round they experience heightened sensory input, they must succeed on a *Test: `skill=dis`* (DC equals 12 + the value of their **Enhanced** [**Awareness**] condition) or be **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

- When the Conrad Elite Operative (Era 2) successfully uses **Gain Advantage** with **Deduction** or an **Awareness** skill, their next attack against that target deals an extra `1d10` damage.

### Locate Target
While burning tin, the elite operative targets a character they can sense within 160 feet of them. An ally the elite operative can influence can immediately make a ranged Strike against that target as r. That attack gains an advantage and uses the elite operative’s **Perception** skill for the attack roll instead of the ally’s usual skill.
