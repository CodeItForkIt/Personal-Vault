# Conrad Engineer (Era 2)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 2, Intellect 3, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 30/30, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 2, LWP 2, CRA 2, DED 2, DIS 2, PRC 2

### Capable Engineer
The engineer gains an advantage on tests related to machines.

### Strike: Baton
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`

### Strike: Experimental Pistol
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`,the engineer can spend 1 focus to also push the target 15 feet away from them.

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [8]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Deploy Prototype Device
*2 act*

The Conrad operative makes a **Crafting** attack test against the Cognitive defense of a character they can sense within 15 feet of them, rolling `1d6 + 4 Energy`. On a hit, the device traps the target, making them **Immobilized** and **Afflicted** [ `2d6` energy] until they’re freed. The trapped target or a character who can reach them can **Use a Skill** to make a DC 14 **Agility** or **Athletics** test. On a success, the trapped target is freed.

### Siren Grenade
*2 act*

The engineer throws a sonic device with a loud, distracting siren at a point they can sense within 30 feet of them. Each time a character spends focus within 10 feet of that device, they must spend an additional 1 focus. A character who can reach the siren can **Use a Skill** to make a DC 14 **Athletics** or **Thievery** test, ending the siren’s effects on a success.
