# Constable (Era 2)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 0, Willpower 1, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 17/17, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 2, ATH 2, LWP 2, DIS 1, INM 1, INS 2, PRC 2

##### Tactics

When attempting to make an arrest, **constables** take advantage of numbers, making ranged Revolver attacks to give their fellow constables an opening to close the distance. Once they close in, they use their Dogpile feature to help them Grapple their targets. When they have a target under control, constables then use Escort to drag the target back behind police lines, working to keep each suspect separated so they can’t help each other escape.

### Strike: Baton
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`

### Strike: Revolver
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + 3 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can't be reduced by the target's deflect.

*Loaded [6]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Dogpile
The Constable (Era 2) gains an advantage on **Athletics** tests to **Grapple** or **Shove** a character who is within 5 feet of another Constable (Era 2).

### Minion
The Constable (Era 2)’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Escort
*2 act*

The Constable (Era 2) moves up to their movement rate, dragging a target they’ve **Restrained** behind them. Any **Reactive Strikes** triggered by this movement gain a Complication, which the GM can spend to cause the **Restrained** target to take damage as if they’d been grazed by that attack.
