# Enforcer (Era 1)

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 5, Speed 3, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 45/45, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** ATH 2, HWP 1, INM 3, PRC 3, SUR 2

##### Tactics

Fast, reckless, and effective, an **enforcer** never runs from a fight. They protect the weaker members of their crew, using their Bull Rush feature to move quickly through the field. If they’re stuck at a distance, an enforcer in Era 2 can use their Shotgun, but every enforcer’s priority is getting into melee to use their Unarmed Strike to restrain a foe.

Once their enemy is restrained, an enforcer uses their Breaking Bones action to devastate a single target, or they leverage their Improvising ability to wreck multiple enemies at once. An enforcer struggling to restrain a target can use Knockdown to set up their next Unarmed Strike.

### Bull Rush
The Enforcer (Era 1) can move through areas occupied by enemies and isn’t **Slowed** by difficult terrain created by Medium or smaller objects.

### Strike: Unarmed
*1 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.ath.mod Impact average`, if the target is Medium or smaller, the Enforcer (Era 1) can choose for them to become **Restrained**, but the Enforcer (Era 1) can’t attack other targets until that condition is removed. As 2, the **Restrained** target can make a DC 15 **Agility** or **Athletics** test, removing the **Restrained** condition on a success.

### Knockdown
After a Large or smaller enemy within 5 feet of the Enforcer (Era 1) hits them with a melee attack, the Enforcer (Era 1) knocks the attacker **Prone**.

### Improvising
*2 act*

The Enforcer (Era 1) throws an enemy they’re restraining into another enemy within 20 feet of the Enforcer (Era 1). The Enforcer (Era 1) makes a melee Athletics attack against the Physical defense of both targets, rolling `3d8 + 7 Impact`. Hit or miss, the thrown enemy also falls **Prone** in the nearest unoccupied space next to the other target.

### Breaking Bones
*2 act*

The Enforcer (Era 1) makes an *Test: `skill=ath`* against the Physical defense of a target they’re restraining. On a success, the target gains one of the following conditions until the end of the Enforcer (Era 1)’s next turn: **Disoriented**, **Exhausted** [−1], or **Slowed**. The Enforcer (Era 1) can spend 3 focus to instead inflict an injury.
