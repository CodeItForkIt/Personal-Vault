# Outlaw Boss (Era 1)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 2, Intellect 2, Willpower 4, Awareness 4, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 55/55, Focus 6/6, Investiture 0  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** ATH 2, HWP 2, LWP 2, DIS 2, INM 2, LEA 2, SUR 2

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to prevent the outlaw boss from using their Rile ’Em Up action and Don’t Disappoint Me reaction until the end of the outlaw boss’s next turn.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have the outlaw boss use their Get ’Em, Boys! action as r.

##### Tactics

An **outlaw boss** coordinates their subordinate outlaws with their Get ’Em, Boys! action. Outlaw bosses with low regard for their minions aren’t afraid to fire into a busy melee with Reckless Menace. Later in a combat, outlaw bosses use their Rile ’Em Up action to help allied outlaws recover enough focus to graze or to use their Roughhousing action.

### Ambusher
At the start of each scene, if the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) isn’t **Surprised**, they make a *Test: `skill=stl`* against the enemy they can sense who has the highest Spiritual defense. On a success, the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) can use 1 before the first round. On a failure, or if they can’t sense any enemies, they can use **Gain Advantag**e as 0 on their first turn.

### Boss
The Outlaw Boss (Era 1) can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) can also spend 1 focus to make the target **Afflicted** [ `1d8` ] until the target recovers at least 1 health.

### Strike: Longbow
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 150/600 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) can spend 1 focus to make the target **Slowed** until the end of the target’s next turn.

### Don’t Disappoint Me
When an ally the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) can influence misses on an attack, the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) allows them to reroll the d20 on the attack roll, potentially changing the miss into a hit. The ally must use the new result, and they can choose whether to graze after seeing the result.

### Get ’Em, Boys!
*2 act*

The Outlaw Boss (Era 1) orders each ally they can influence within 30 feet of them to use r to make a melee attack against an enemy, if they can.

### Reckless Menace
*1 act*

The Outlaw Boss (Era 1) makes an attack with a disadvantage, spending an additional 1 ammunition if they’re using a **Loaded** weapon. On a hit or graze, the attack also damages all characters (other than the Outlaw Boss (Era 1)) within 5 feet of the target as if they’d been grazed by that attack.

### Rile ’Em Up
*2 act*

The Outlaw Boss (Era 1) makes an *Test: `skill=inm`* against the Cognitive defense of a target they can influence. On a success, all allies within 60 feet of the Outlaw Boss (Era 1) recover `1d4` focus.
