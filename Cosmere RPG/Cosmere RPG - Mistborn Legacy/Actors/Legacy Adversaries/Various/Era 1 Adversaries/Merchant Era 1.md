# Merchant (Era 1)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 1, Intellect 1, Willpower 2, Awareness 2, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 18/18, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** LWP 1, CRA 2, DED 1, INS 2, PRC 1, PRS 2

##### Tactics

A **merchant** wants to come out of any conversation with a favorable deal. They use their Business Acumen feature to maintain the upper hand under pressure, then whittle the characters’ focus down with their Counterargument action. However, merchants aren’t keen on making enemies—so if things aren’t going the merchant’s way, they prefer to compromise rather than lose a contact.

Merchants are rarely well-equipped for combat. In the event of a fight, they immediately attempt to flee, using attacks to cover their escape.

### Business Acumen
The first time the Merchant (Era 1) resists influence during a conversation, it costs them no focus.

### Stock-in-Trade
The merchant gains an advantage on tests related to their specific inventory specialization, such as firearms, metals, or jewelry.

### Unexpected Acquisition
Once per scene, if a PC rolls O while conversing with the merchant, the merchant’s stock includes something rare or needed by the PCs.

### Counterargument
*2 act*

The Merchant (Era 1) makes a *Test: `skill=prs`* against a character’s Spiritual defense. On a success, the target loses 2 focus.

### Strike: Improvised Weapon
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`
