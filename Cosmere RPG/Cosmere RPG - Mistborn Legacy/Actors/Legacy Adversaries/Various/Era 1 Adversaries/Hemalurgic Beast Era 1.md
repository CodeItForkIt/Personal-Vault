# Hemalurgic Beast (Era 1)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 2, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 36/36, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 2, ATH 2, LWP 3, DIS 1, INM 2, PRC 2, SUR 1

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to steel themself against the horrors they’ve witnessed, becoming immune to the Hemalurgic beast’s Bloody Visage.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have the Hemalurgic beast leap up to 15 feet in any direction as r.

##### Tactics

The **Hemalurgic beast** opens combat with **Bloody Visage**, knocking foes **Prone** and draining their focus before advancing. He moves into reach of the nearest enemy and strikes with his **Metal Bar** or **Claws**. If a target withdraws, the beast uses **Chain Grab** to pull them back into reach. When multiple enemies are within 10 feet of the beast, he uses **Rampage**, leaping into the group and striking everything nearby. Once engaged, the **Hemalurgic Beast** doesn’t retreat, fighting to the death.

### Boss
The Hemalurgic Beast (Era 1) can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Hemalurgic Beast (Era 1) can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Desperate Strike
After the first time the Hemalurgic beast is reduced to 12 health (a third of their maximum health), if they can sense the enemy who damaged them, the beast makes a **Metal Bar** attack (no action required) against the enemy who dealt the damage. They can make this attack with the normal 5-foot reach, or they can throw the bar with a range of 20/60 feet. If the Hemalurgic beast can’t sense the enemy who damaged them, they instead target the nearest enemy they can sense within 60 feet of them.

### Strike: Claws
*1 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 3 Impact average`.

### Strike: Metal Bar
*1 act*

**Attack** +AGI; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + 2 Impact average`

### Chain Grab
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 20 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + 1 Impact average`, the Hemalurgic beast pulls the target up to 20 feet toward them.

### Bloody Visage
*2 act*

The Hemalurgic beast makes an **Intimidation** test against the Cognitive defense of each character they can influence. On a success, a character loses 1 focus and falls **Prone**.

### Rampage
*3 act*

The Hemalurgic beast leaps to a location within 15 feet without triggering **Reactive Strikes**, then makes a Strike against each enemy within 10 feet of the beast.

### Hemalurgic Spike
If a character is aware of the Hemalurgic Beast (Era 1)’s critical spike, they can spend O to allow themself or an ally of theirs to attempt to remove it once before the end of the scene. While that character can reach the beast, they can **Use a Skill** (1) to make an *Test: `skill=ath`* against the beast’s Physical defense. On a success, the spike is removed, the beast loses their **Regenerative Skin** action, and the beast takes `1d10 Keen`.
