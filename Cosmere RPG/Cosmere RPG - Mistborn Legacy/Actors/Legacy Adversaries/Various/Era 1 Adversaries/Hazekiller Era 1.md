# Hazekiller (Era 1)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 1, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 26/26, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 1, ATH 3, LWP 2, DED 2, DIS 1, INS 1, PRC 1, SUR 1

##### Tactics

**Hazekillers** fight in careful, coordinated teams, often led by a hazekiller elite. The hazekillers rush Allomancers in a collective charge using their Engage Allomancer reaction, then stick together to take advantage of teamwork with Kill Squad. When a hazekiller manages to knock a foe prone with Shield Bash, the hazekiller’s allies follow with Break Spirit.

### Strike: Dueling Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, if the Hazekiller (Era 1)} has at least one advantage on this attack, they can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `1d8 Impact`.

### Defensive Tactics
The Hazekiller (Era 1) carries a wooden shield, allowing them to use the **Brace** action.

### Kill Squad
The Hazekiller (Era 1) gains a bonus to attack and damage rolls equal to the number of allied Hazekiller (Era 1)s within 20 feet of them (maximum of +5).

### Brace
*1 act*

You hide behind cover within 5 feet of you (see “Terrain” in the *Mistborn Handbook*) to defend yourself against incoming attacks. All attacks against you gain a disadvantage for as long as you stay behind cover. If you attack or move, the benefits of this action end.

You’re only protected against attacks from the other side of your cover, and it must be substantial enough to realistically defend you against the attack. For example, you could press yourself against a wall or duck behind an obstacle, but if an enemy rounds the corner toward you, they can attack you without disadvantage.

If you have a weapon with the **Defensive** trait (such as a shield), you can wield it to create mobile cover for yourself. When you do, you must keep that weapon raised and between yourself and any potential attackers.

### Break Spirit
*2 act*

The Hazekiller (Era 1) makes an attack against a **Prone** character. On a hit, instead of dealing the usual damage, the target loses `1d4` focus.

### Engage Allomancer
After a character the Hazekiller (Era 1) can sense uses **Drink Vial** or an action that costs Investiture, if the Hazekiller (Era 1) is aware of it, the Hazekiller (Era 1) moves up to half their movement rate toward that character.
