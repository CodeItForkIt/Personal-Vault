# Bodyguard (Era 1)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 2, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 24/24, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** ATH 2, HWP 2, LWP 2, DIS 1, INM 2, INS 1, PRC 2

##### Tactics

A **bodyguard** must be decisive and fast, operating skillfully in close-range combat. They Close the Distance as swiftly as possible, then use Protective Duty to keep their charges safe. In Era 2, a bodyguard may attack with their Boot Pistol before reaching their charge, then swap to their Sidesword to continue the fight. They use their Subdue action as necessary to give their charge a chance to flee. Meanwhile, their Hold the Line feature ensures the bodyguard is di cult to take out of the fight and can stay where they need to be on the battlefield.

### Hold the Line
The Bodyguard (Era 1) gains an advantage on tests against being knocked **Prone**. Additionally, before the Bodyguard (Era 1) is unwillingly moved, they reduce that movement by 5 feet.

### Strike: Sidesword
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, the target takes an extra `1d6 Keen` if the Bodyguard (Era 1) is within 5 feet of an ally.

### Protective Duty
Before an ally within 5 feet of the Bodyguard (Era 1) is attacked, the Bodyguard (Era 1) becomes the target of the attack instead, as if they were in the ally’s space.

### Close the Distance
While within 30 feet of an ally, the Bodyguard (Era 1) can use the **Move** action as 0 once per turn.

### Subdue
*2 act*

The Bodyguard (Era 1) targets a Large or smaller character within 5 feet of them, knocking them **Prone** then making an *Test: `skill=ath`* against their Physical defense.

On a success, the Bodyguard (Era 1) can also make the target **Restrained** until the end of the bodyguard’s next turn.
