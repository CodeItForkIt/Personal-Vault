# Hazekiller Elite (Era 1)

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 4, Intellect 3, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 47/47, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 1, ATH 2, LWP 2, DED 2, DIS 3, INS 2, PRC 3, SUR 2

##### Tactics

With their Heightened Senses, a **hazekiller elite** is uniquely prepared to fight Allomancers, able to detect Allomancy and resist Allomantic manipulation. In close combat, a hazekiller elite denies their Allomancer opponent Investiture by using Devitalizing Feint. The hazekiller elite then attempts to knock their target Prone with Shield Bash, enabling the hazekiller elite to inflict extra damage with their Dueling Cane.

### Defensive Tactics
The Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) carries a wooden shield, allowing them to use the **Brace** action.

### Heightened Senses
The Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) gains an advantage on tests to recognize the use of Allomancy or to identify someone as an Allomancer. Additionally, if a Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) knows they’ve been affected by emotional Allomancy, they can spend 1 focus to end the effect as 0.

### Kill Squad
The Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) gains a bonus to attack and damage rolls equal to the number of allied Hazekiller Elite (Era 1)s within 20 feet of them (maximum of +5).

### Strike: Dueling Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, if the Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) had at least one advantage on this attack, they can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `2d8 Impact`.

### Brace
*1 act*

You hide behind cover within 5 feet of you (see “Terrain” in the *Mistborn Handbook*) to defend yourself against incoming attacks. All attacks against you gain a disadvantage for as long as you stay behind cover. If you attack or move, the benefits of this action end.

You’re only protected against attacks from the other side of your cover, and it must be substantial enough to realistically defend you against the attack. For example, you could press yourself against a wall or duck behind an obstacle, but if an enemy rounds the corner toward you, they can attack you without disadvantage.

If you have a weapon with the **Defensive** trait (such as a shield), you can wield it to create mobile cover for yourself. When you do, you must keep that weapon raised and between yourself and any potential attackers.

### Devitalizing Feint
*2 act*

The Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) makes a **Strike** to distract an Allomancer. On a hit, instead of dealing the usual damage, the target can’t recover Investiture until the end of their next turn.

### Honed Instincts
The Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) gains an advantage on their next attack against a character they know is an Allomancer.

### Deflect
Before the Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) is hit by a ranged weapon attack made by an enemy they can sense within 60 feet of them, the Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) raises their shield and the hit becomes a miss.

### Shield Bash
*1 act*

The Hazekiller Elite (Era 1) makes an *Test: `skill=ath`* against the Physical defense of a Medium or smaller character within 5 feet of them, knocking the target **Prone** on a success.
