# Con Artist (Era 1)

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 3, Intellect 2, Willpower 1, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 20/20, Focus 3/3, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** LWP 2, THV 2, DIS 2, DEC 2, INS 1, PRC 2, PRS 2

##### Tactics

A **con artist** is excellent in social situations, using their Inscrutable Expression feature to control conversations. Their Underhanded Sales ability works well to weaken their foes, whether in social situations or combat. In a fight, a con artist relies on deception to aid themselves and their allies, using Mislead to hinder their opponents and Create Distraction to get their allies to safety. With their Knife, a con artist can Disengage and flee if things start to look deadly.

### Inscrutable Expression
The Con Artist (Era 1) gains a bonus to their Spiritual defense equal to their **Deception** modifier against tests to discern whether they’re lying.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the Con Artist (Era 1) can also **Disengage** as 0.

### Mislead
*2 act*

The Con Artist (Era 1) makes a *Test: `skill=dec`* against the Spiritual defense of a target they can influence within 5 feet of them. On a success, they stab the target, dealing `1d4 + 10 Keen`. On a failure, the target loses 1 focus.

### Underhanded Sales
*2 act*

The Con Artist (Era 1) chooses up to five targets they can influence within 30 feet of them and makes a *Test: `skill=prs`* against their Spiritual defenses. On a success, the target loses 2 focus.

### Create Distraction
When an enemy the Con Artist (Era 1) can influence ends their turn within 5 feet of the Con Artist (Era 1) or of an ally the Con Artist (Era 1) can influence, the Con Artist (Era 1) can make a *Test: `skill=prs`* against the target’s Spiritual Defense. On a success, the Con Artist (Era 1) or their ally (whichever one the enemy moved next to) can move up to their movement rate (no action required) without triggering a **Reactive Strike**.
