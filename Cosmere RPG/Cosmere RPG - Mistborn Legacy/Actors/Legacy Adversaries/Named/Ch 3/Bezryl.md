# Bezryl

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 1, Intellect 3, Willpower 2, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 42/42, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 3, ATH 3, DED 3, DIS 3, LOR 3, INS 2, LEA 3, PRC 2, FER 3

##### Tactics

**Bezryl** avoids violent confrontation, disengaging quickly when threatened. If grappled or otherwise cornered, she uses **Tap Warmth** to shake her attackers. She then uses **Tap Speed** to stay out of harm’s way and escape. If she takes 10 or more damage, she uses **Tap Health** to stay in the fight.

### Strike: Unarmed
*1 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.ath.mod Impact average`

### Tap Warmth
*1 act*

Berzyl taps warmth until the end of the scene or until she uses **Store Warmth**. For the duration, when she takes energy damage, she increases it by 2. Additionally, at the end of each of Berzyl’s turns, if she’s been touching another character for more than a few seconds, Berzyl can use 0 to deal them 6 energy damage.

### Store Warmth
*1 act*

Bezryl stores warmth until the end of the scene or until she uses **Tap Warmth**. For the duration, before Berzyl takes energy damage, she reduces it by 2.

### Useful Memory
When an ally rolls O, Bezryl taps her coppermind to retrieve a memory relevant to her allies’ current mission or personal goals.

### Tap Memory
*1 act*

Bezryl chooses an expertise to gain until the end of the scene. For the duration, she also gains an advantage on tests related to that expertise.

### Tap Speed
*1 act*

Bezryl gains the following benefits until the end of the scene:

- She becomes **Enhanced** [**Speed** +2].

- She can use the **Move** action as 0 once per turn.

- She can spend 1 steelmind charge on her turn to gain 2, which she can only use to **Brace**, **Disengage**, **Interact**, **Move**, or make a physical test. During a conversation or endeavor, she can instead use 2 to make an additional contribution.

### Tap Health
*1 act*

Bezryl ends the effects of a single disease, drug, or poison on herself. Additionally, now and at the start of each of her turns until the end of the scene, she recovers .

### Steelmind
Bezryl has a steelmind that stores 6 charges.

### Goldmind
Bezryl has a goldmind that stores 6 charges.

### Metalminds
Bezryl has the following metalminds, each of which stores 6 charges: a coppermind (stores memory), a brassmind (stores warmth), a goldmind (stores health), and a steelmind (stores speed).

### Coppermind
Bezryl has a coppermind that stores 6 charges.

### Brassmind
Bezryl has a brassmind that stores 6 charges.
