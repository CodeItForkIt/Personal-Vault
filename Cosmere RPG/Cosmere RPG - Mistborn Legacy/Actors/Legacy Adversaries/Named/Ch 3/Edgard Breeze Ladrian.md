# Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 3, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 5  
**Resources:** Health 38/38, Focus 5/5, Investiture 7/7  
**Skills:** LWP 3, DED 1, DIS 1, DEC 3, INS 3, PRC 5, PRS 3

##### Tactics

A **socialite** shines in conversations, where their Charming Personality feature allows them to maintain composure while wearing down their opponents’ focus, and their Down the Grapevine reaction gains them further social leverage. Socialites aren’t likely to engage in combat unless forced, and when they must, they prioritize fleeing to safety. When cornered, they use their Beguiling Influence and Distracting Countenance actions to weaken foes (hoping for stronger allies to help finish them off), finally leaning on their Knife attack only as a last resort.

### Charming Personality
Once per conversation, after the Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian spends focus to resist influence, the Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian can choose one character in the conversation to lose 2 focus.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`. The first time the Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian uses this action during a scene, they gain an advantage.

### Distracting Countenance
*1 act*

The Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian chooses an enemy within 15 feet who can sense them. The target’s deflect value becomes 0 until the end of the Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian’s next turn.

### Beguiling Influence
*2 act*

The Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian makes a *Test: `skill=prs`* against the Spiritual defense of an enemy within 30 feet of them who can sense them. On a success, the target loses 2 focus.

### Down the Grapevine
When a character rolls a Complication during a conversation, the Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian reveals they know a compromising secret about a character involved in that conversation.

### Socialite Perception Bonus
Passive Effect to bring Socialite's Perception mod up to the 8 value in the book.

### Brass Allomancy Module
Brass Mistings, called Soothers, can dampen the emotions of others so they’re less likely to act on those emotions.

**Recommended Profiles.** Adversaries who rely on manipulation, such as the **con artist**, **detective**, **merchant**, and **socialite**, can make good use of this module.

### Burn Brass
*2 act*

The Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian chooses an emotion and chooses one or more targets in a Large (10-foot) area within 160 feet of them, then they make an *Test: `skill=all`* against the Spiritual defense of each target. On a success, the chosen emotion is Soothed in that target, influencing them to act in alignment with that emotion until the end of their next turn. Before this effect ends, the Edgard "Breeze" Ladrian can maintain it on all affected targets in that area by spending 1 or more Investiture as 0.

An affected target can resist as 0, spending focus equal to the Investiture spent to **Burn Brass** (instead of the usual cost to resist influence). After they do, they ignore their altered emotions until the start of their next turn.
