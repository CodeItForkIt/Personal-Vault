# Ironeyes

**Tier:** 4  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 6, Speed 5, Intellect 5, Willpower 6, Awareness 5, Presence 6  
**Resources:** Health 500/250, Focus 20/10, Investiture 20/10  
**Deflect:** 4  
**Skills:** ATH 5, HWP 5, DED 5, DIS 5, INM 5, DEC 5, INS 5, PRC 5, PRS 5, ALL 5, FER 5

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to end the effects of Ironeyes’s Burn Atium, Burn Bronze, and Burn Iron/Steel.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have Ironeyes use his Ironpull/Steelpush action as r.

##### Tactics

**Ironeyes** is difficult to defeat, especially since Burn Bronze and Inescapable Tracking make him hard to take by surprise; keep in mind that if he spends 6 Investiture to Burn Bronze, he can even detect and pierce copperclouds (see “Copper Allomancy” in chapter 6 of the Mistborn Handbook). During combat, he dominates enemies in melee with his Choke and Obsidian Axes actions. He can quickly react to a changing battlefield with Ironpull/Steelpush, and in dire situations, he uses Burn Atium to give himself an edge. This stat block doesn’t limit how many times Ironeyes can Burn Atium, but as the GM, you can limit this depending on his current access to the metal.

### Boss
The Ironeyes can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Ironeyes can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Death Is Among Us
Ironeyes gains a bonus to his Spiritual defense equal to his **Insight** modifier against tests to sense him.

### It Is a Fact
Each character of Ironeyes’s choice within 120 feet of him gains a −10 penalty to injury rolls.

### Unflappable
Ironeyes can resist influence without spending focus.

### Strike: Obsidian Axes
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `2d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, Ironeyes can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `2d6 Keen`.

### Burn Bronze
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, Ironeyes detects the following within 320 feet of him:

- Each time Investiture is spent.

- Each ongoing effect that’s fueled by Investiture, such as the Burn actions of Allomancers, even if that Investiture was spent before he used this action.

Ironeyes can sense the general direction of these effects’ sources. For as long as he can detect these effects, he can also choose to track the specific location and source of a number of effects equal to the Investiture spent. Ironeyes can make a *Test: `dc=15 skill=all`* as 1 to determine what power generated an effect he’s tracking.

### Inescapable Tracking
*1 act*

Ironeyes chooses an Invested effect he knows the current location of with his **Burn Bronze**, and he moves up to his movement rate toward it. If the effect is associated with a character, Ironeyes makes an *Test: `skill=all`* against their Spiritual defense. On a success, he gains an advantage on tests against that character and can continue to track their effect for up to one hour without spending additional Investiture to **Burn Bronze**.

### Burn Atium
*2 act*

For the next 5 rounds, when Ironeyes makes a test to physically interact with another character (including tests against Physical defense), don’t roll any d20s. Instead, set all d20s, including advantages and disadvantages, to a value of 15. Similarly, when an enemy makes a test to physically interact with Ironeyes (including tests against Physical defense), set the value of their d20s to 5. Before this effect ends, Ironeyes can maintain it as 0.

### Shoot Coin
*1 act*

**Attack** +ALL; **Range** 20 ft. per Investiture spent; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d12 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d12 + @skills.all.mod Impact average`

### Steelpush
*1 act*

The Ironeyes chooses a metal object they sense with Burn Steel, and they move themself or the object straight away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move away from the Ironeyes, whereas the Ironeyes moves away from objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or allow the avatar to “fly” for short distances). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the avatar must succeed on an Allomancy test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, they must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d12+@skills.all.mod Impact`.

### Ironpull
*1 act*

The Ironeyes chooses a metal object they sense with Burn Iron, and they move themself or the object straight toward the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward the Ironeyes, whereas the Ironeyes moves toward objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at one in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the Ironeyes must succeed on an Allomancy test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, they must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d12+@skills.all.mod Impact`.

### Burn Steel
*1 act*

Until the start of their next turn, the Ironeyes senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 320 feet of him.

### Burn Iron
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, the Ironeyes senses all non-aluminum sources of metal within 320 feet of him.

### Choke
*1 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.ath.mod Impact average`

This attack gains an advantage if Ironeyes successfully used his **Ironpull/Steelpush** against the target on this turn. On a hit, the target must also succeed on a *Test: `skill=dis dc=19`* or be **Stunned** until the end of their next turn.
