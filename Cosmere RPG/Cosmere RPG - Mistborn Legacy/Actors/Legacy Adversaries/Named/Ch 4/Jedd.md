# Jedd

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 2, Willpower 1, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 12/12, Focus 3/3, Investiture 0  
**Deflect:** 2  
**Skills:** ATH 1, HWP 2, LWP 2, DIS 2, INM 1, PRC 2

##### Tactics

A **garrisoner** is decisive and efficient. They can focus fire on a single powerful enemy, or use their Volley Shot reaction to spread damage and pick off multiple weaker targets simultaneously. When needed, a garrisoner’s Sidesword can allow them to reposition and hold the line.

Garrisoners work in tandem—they never leave another soldier behind, and they prioritize working together to bring down their foes, rather than aiming for individual glory.

### Minion
The Jedd’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Strike: Sidesword
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, the Jedd can also **Disengage** as 0.

### Strike: Longbow
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 150/600 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, if the Jedd had at least one advantage on this attack, they can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `1d6` damage.

### Volley Shot
After an allied Jedd within 10 feet of this Jedd hits a target with a ranged attack, this Jedd can immediately make a ranged attack against a different target within 5 feet of the first target.
