# Ashweather Cett

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 3, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 5  
**Resources:** Health 38/38, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** LWP 3, DED 1, DIS 1, INM 5, DEC 3, INS 3, PRC 5, PRS 3

##### Tactics

**Ashweather Cett** lets others speak first, responding with blunt questions and pointed remarks meant to provoke mistakes. When challenged, he holds his ground, resisting attempts to influence him and forcing opponents to press their case. He watches closely for hesitation or slips, using his **Well Informed** reaction to capitalize on Complications and expose what he knows. If violence breaks out, Ash weather targets already-weakened opponents, using **Crass Remark** to disrupt their composure. He won’t pursue a fight he can’t control, so he disengages once his advantage fades.

### Ashweather Cett Perception Boost
Effect to get Ashweather Cett's Perception skill to 8

### Tough Negotiator
Once during a conversation, after Ashweather spends focus to resist influence, he can choose one character in the conversation to lose 2 focus.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

The first time Ashweather makes this attack during a scene, he gains an advantage on it.

### Addling Jab
*1 act*

Ashweather chooses an enemy he can influence within 15 feet of him. The target’s deflect value becomes 0 until the end of Ashweather’s next turn.

### Crass Remark
*2 act*

Ashweather makes an **Intimidation** test against an enemy he can influence within 30 feet of him. On a success, the target becomes so disgusted or incensed that they commit a social blunder, gaining a disadvantage on all tests until they succeed on a contribution or until the scene ends.

### Well Informed
When a character rolls a Complication during a conversation, Ashweather reveals he knows a compromising secret about one of the characters involved in that conversation.
