# Zane

**Tier:** 3  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 5, Intellect 3, Willpower 4, Awareness 3, Presence 4  
**Resources:** Health 190/190, Focus 8/8, Investiture 4/8  
**Skills:** AGI 4, LWP 4, DIS 4, INM 4, INS 4, LEA 4, PRC 4, SUR 4, ALL 4

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to end the effects of the Mistborn’s Burn Atium, Burn Tin, and Burn Iron/Steel.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test either to have the Mistborn use the Drink Vial action as r, or to gain one additional use of Burn Atium.

##### Tactics

A **Mistborn assassin** is mobile, flexible, and hard to pin down in a fight. They’re di cult to surprise thanks to Burn Tin and Burn Iron/Steel, able to move quickly with Ironpull/Steelpush, and adept at overwhelming foes with Burn Atium.

### Boss
The Zane can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Zane can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Mistcloak
The Mistborn gains an advantage on **Intimidation** tests against non-Mistborn characters. Additionally, while in an area that obscures vision, they gain an advantage on **Stealth** tests against characters whose primary sense is obscured by that area, and those characters gain a disadvantage on **Perception** tests against the Mistborn.

### Strike: Obsidian Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the Zane can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `2d8 Keen`.

### Assassin's Dance
*2 act*

The Zane moves up to their movement rate and makes a **Strike**. On a hit, the target is also **Disoriented** until the end of the target’s next turn.

### Burn Tin
*1 act*

The Zane enhances their senses, experiencing the following effects until the end of the scene or until the Zane ends them on a later turn as 0:

- They become **Enhanced** [**Awareness**]. The bonus to this attribute equals half the Investiture they spent to **Burn Tin** (rounded up).

- All their senses are supernatural senses. The first time each round they experience heightened sensory input, they must succeed on a *Test: `skill=dis`* (DC equals 12 + the value of their **Enhanced** [**Awareness**] condition) or be **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

- When the Zane successfully uses **Gain Advantage** with **Deduction** or an **Awareness** skill, their next attack against that target deals an extra `1d10` damage.

### Burn Atium
*2 act*

For the next 5 rounds, when Zane makes a test to physically interact with another character (including tests against Physical defense), don’t roll any d20s. Instead, set all d20s, including advantages and disadvantages, to a value of 15. Similarly, when an enemy makes a test to physically interact with Zane (including tests against Physical defense), set the value of their d20s to 5. Before this effect ends, Zane can maintain it as 0 by spending an additional use of **Burn Atium** (which they can gain from a Complication, as described below).

### Shoot Coin
*1 act*

**Attack** +ALL; **Range** 20 ft. per Investiture spent; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d10 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d10 + @skills.all.mod Impact average`

### Projectile Lurch
Before an ally within 160 feet of the Zane is attacked with a metal projectile the Zane senses with Burn Iron/Burn Steel, the Zane becomes the target of the attack instead.

### Steelpush
*1 act*

The Zane chooses a metal object they sense with Burn Steel, and they move themself or the object straight away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move away from the Zane, whereas the Zane moves away from objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or allow the avatar to “fly” for short distances). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the avatar must succeed on an *Test: `skill=all`* against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, the Mistborn must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d10+@skills.all.mod Impact`.

### Ironpull
*1 act*

The Zane chooses a metal object they sense with **Burn Iron**, and they move themself or the object straight toward the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward the Zane, whereas the Zane moves toward objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at one in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the Zane must succeed on an *Test: `skill=all`* against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, they must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d10+@skills.all.mod Impact`.

### Burn Steel
*1 act*

Until the start of their next turn, the Zane senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 160 feet of them.

### Burn Iron
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, the Zane senses all non-aluminum sources of metal within 160 feet of them.
