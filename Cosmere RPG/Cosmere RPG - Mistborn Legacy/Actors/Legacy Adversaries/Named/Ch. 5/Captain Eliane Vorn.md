# Captain Eliane Vorn

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 4, Intellect 4, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 60/60, Focus 5/5, Investiture 5/5  
**Skills:** AGI 2, LWP 2, THV 1, DED 2, DIS 2, LOR 1, INS 2, LEA 2, PRC 2, ALL 3, FER 3

##### Tactics

**Captain Eliane Vorn** wields her Twinborn powers of Cadmium Allomancy and Steel Feruchemy with frightening lethality. Her primary goal is to disable and detain her foes. During the first round of combat, she favors a slow turn, usually including the following actions in order:

- **Tap Speed** (1).

- **Move** (0 due to tapping speed) to position herself near the most dangerous opponent she can.

- **Burn Cadmium** (2), spending 1 Investiture to create a Huge time bubble that envelops her, her chosen opponent, and as many other foes as possible.

On each turn while Vorn’s foes are slowed in her time bubble, she also takes a slow turn, usually including the following actions in order:

- Remove 1 delay point from herself (1). Because of her **Tap Speed**, the other delay point was already negated, so she has no more delay points.

- Use **Time Contortion** (1) to damage the highest priority target in her bubble. (Her **Enhanced** [**Speed** +2] bonus applies to all of her attack tests and her damage.)

- **Move** (0) toward that target and make a Strike with her **Revolver Butt** (1) to knock the target **Prone**.

- Maintain her **Burn Cadmium** (0).

If Vorn’s allies are in serious danger, she immediately shifts to prioritize their protection and uses **Tactical Withdrawal** to direct them out of harm’s way—but keep in mind that when she’s in a time bubble, she can’t influence allies on the outside.

### Strike: Vorn's Revolver
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can't be reduced by the target's deflect.

*Loaded [6]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Strike: Revolver Butt
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, the target is also knocked **Prone**.

### Time Contortion
*1 act*

Vorn uses **Vorn’s Revolver** to attack one target in her time bubble. This attack doesn’t count as a Strike.

### Burn Cadmium
*2 act*

Vorn creates a time bubble in a Huge (15-foot) area centered on her current location. Time within this bubble slows dramatically. Each character in the bubble gains 2 delay points. While a character has at least 1 delay point, they must use every available action and contribution to remove their delay points in one of the following ways, and they can’t use any other actions or reactions until they do:

- As 1, they can remove 1 delay point from themself. A character can use this action more than once per turn.

- They can use a free action. If such an effect grants one or more 1, these can be used to remove delay points.

- During a conversation or endeavor, they can use their contribution to remove 2 delay points from themself.

The bubble has the following additional effects:

- Effect durations elapse more slowly within the bubble. While a character has at least 1 delay point, this pauses the duration (though not the effects) of ongoing effects, so those effects ignore the passage of rounds and turns.

- Characters who willingly move through the bubble’s surface lose focus equal to the Investiture spent; if this reduces their focus to 0 (or if they already had 0 focus), they’re Stunned until the end of their next turn.

- Characters outside the bubble can’t communicate with or influence characters inside through mundane means, and vice versa.

- When a character makes an attack through the bubble’s surface, they gain a number of disadvantages equal to the Investiture spent to Burn Cadmium. The attack gains the Dangerous weapon trait, and it can’t hit or graze the target unless the attacker also spends O.

The time bubble ends at the end of the next round—but since rounds don’t count while Vorn has at least 1 delay point, the bubble lasts until the end of the round where Vorn removes her last delay point. When the bubble ends, remove its remaining delay points from affected characters.

Before the time bubble ends, Vorn can maintain it as 0 while within the bubble by spending the same Investiture as before, granting each character in it an additional 2 delay points.

### Tactical Withdrawal
After an ally Vorn can influence within 30 feet of her is hit with an attack, Vorn commands them to withdraw. That ally can immediately move up to half their movement rate without triggering a **Reactive Strike**.

### Tap Speed
*1 act*

Vorn gains the following benefits until the end of the scene:

- She become **Enhanced** [**Speed** +2].

- She can use the **Move** action as 0 once per turn.

- She gains 1 fewer delay points from the creation or maintenance of any cadmium time bubble.

### Steelmind
Vorn has a steelmind that stores 5 charges.
