# Hemalurgic Monstrosity

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** large  
**Attributes:** Strength 5, Speed 4, Intellect 3, Willpower 4, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 160/160, Focus 6/6, Investiture 7/7  
**Skills:** AGI 3, ATH 3, INM 3, PRC 3, SUR 3, ALL 3, FER 3

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to prevent the monstrosity from using the **Propel Metal** action until the end of the monstrosity’s next turn.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have the monstrosity use their **Ragdoll** action as r. The monstrosity gains an advantage on their **Athletics** test for this action.

### Boss
The Hemalurgic Monstrosity can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Hemalurgic Monstrosity can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Strike: Claws
*1 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 10 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d8 Keen average`;

**Hit** `2d8 + @skills.ath.mod Keen average`, the monstrosity can spend 1 focus to knock a Large or smaller target **Prone**.

### Strike: Backhand
*1 act*

**Attack** +ATH; **Reach** 10 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `2d6 + @skills.ath.mod Impact average`, the monstrosity also recovers `1d4` focus.

### Eternity Spike
The monstrosity has been pierced by the Eternity Spike. At the start of each of the monstrosity’s fast turns, they recover and all their Investiture (unless the Disrupt the Latticework event has been triggered).

### Unstable Expulsion
The extreme amount of Investiture within the Eternity Spike has supercharged the monstrosity. At the start of each of the monstrosity’s slow turns, roll a d8 on the [[Roll Tables/Unstable Expulsion|Unstable Expulsion]] table and apply the listed effect immediately. If the number rolled is greater than the number of marked slots in the Ascension event, the monstrosity is **Stunned** until the end of that turn.

### Propel Metal
*1 act*

**Attack** +ALL; **Reach** 20 ft. per Investiture spent; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d10 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d10 + @skills.all.mod Impact average`, the monstrosity can spend 1 focus to make the target **Restrained**. The target can end the **Restrained** condition by succeeding on a DC 17 **Agility** or **Athletics** test as 1.

### Ragdoll
*2 act*

The monstrosity grabs a Large or smaller character within 10 feet of them and throws the target with prodigious force, making an **Athletics** test against the target’s Physical Defense. On a success, the target is thrown 50 feet, takes `3d12 Impact`, and is knocked **Prone**. On a failure, the target is thrown 10 feet and takes `2d12 Impact`.

### Rapid Speed Bubble
Before an enemy attacks the monstrosity, the monstrosity reacts with supernatural speed as they warp time around themself. That attack gains a disadvantage, and after it’s resolved, the monstrosity can immediately use an action that costs 1.

### Modular Actions
The monstrosity can use only one of the following three actions, determined by what character was transformed into this monstrosity: Burn Pewter for Bayron, Foment Fear for Kwylliam, or Douse the Spark for Lysarra. They can’t use the other modular actions.

**FOUNDRY NOTE**: All three actions are provided for your convenience, simply delete the two you do not need.
