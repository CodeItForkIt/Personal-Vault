# Merid

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 2, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 27/22, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** ATH 1, HWP 2, LWP 2, DED 1, DIS 1, INM 1, INS 1, PRC 1, PRS 2

##### Tactics

**Lawkeepers** are an overwhelming force when they work in tandem—their Carbine attack allows them to knock enemies Prone, creating an opportunity for other lawkeepers to move in and surround a target with their In Pursuit reactions. On the lawkeeper’s next turn, they use their Arrest action to detain the target.

Should a lawkeeper question a suspect or detainee, the lawkeeper’s Eye of the Law feature puts pressure on anyone who tries to lie to them.

### Arrest
*2 act*

The Merid makes an *Test: `skill=ath`* against a **Prone** target’s Physical defense, binding them with a pair of handcuffs on a success. The handcuffs can be broken with a successful *Test: `skill=ath dc=30`* or by dealing 15 damage directly to them, or they can be unlocked with a successful *Test: `skill=thv dc=18`*. The Merid carries two sets of handcuffs.

### In Pursuit
After an enemy within 30 feet of the Merid falls **Prone**, the Merid moves up to their movement rate.

### Eye of the Law
If a character fails a **Deception** test against the Merid, that character loses 1 focus.

### Strike: Carbine
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 80/320 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, the Merid can also spend 1 focus to knock the target Prone.

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [8]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`
