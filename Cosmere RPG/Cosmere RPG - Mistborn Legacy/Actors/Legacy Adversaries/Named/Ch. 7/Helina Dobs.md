# Helina Dobs

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 0, Speed 1, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 22/22, Focus 4/4, Investiture 8/4  
**Skills:** LWP 1, STL 1, DED 2, LOR 2, PRC 2, PRS 2, ALL 2

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the Tineye informant can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `2d4 Keen`.

### Burn Tin
*1 act*

The Helina Dobs enhances their senses, experiencing the following effects until the end of the scene or until the Helina Dobs ends them on a later turn as 0:

- They become **Enhanced** [**Awareness**]. The bonus to this attribute equals half the Investiture they spent to **Burn Tin** (rounded up).

- All their senses are supernatural senses. The first time each round they experience heightened sensory input, they must succeed on a *Test: `skill=dis`* (DC equals 12 + the value of their **Enhanced** [**Awareness**] condition) or be **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

- When the Helina Dobs successfully uses **Gain Advantage** with **Deduction** or an **Awareness** skill, their next attack against that target deals an extra `1d6` damage.

### Informed
Before each scene, roll the plot die. On O, the Helina Dobs knows information useful to the PCs. On C, the Helina Dobs knows compromising information about one  of the PCs.

### Well-Connected
The Helina Dobs gains an advantage on tests related to the location and organizations they use to collect their information (such as the Elendel constabulary, markets, and industry).

### Barter
*1 act*

During a conversation, the Helina Dobs makes a *Test: `skill=prs`* against the character they can influence who has the highest Spiritual defense. On a success, each character of the Helina Dobs’s choice in the conversation loses 1 focus.
