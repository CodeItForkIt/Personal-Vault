# Elanda “The Anvil” Wilko

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 6, Speed 3, Intellect 2, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 59/52, Focus 5/5, Investiture 10/5  
**Deflect:** 2  
**Skills:** AGI 2, ATH 2, HWP 2, DIS 3, INM 3, LEA 2, PRS 1, ALL 3

### Strike: Hand Cannon
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d8 +@skills.hwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can't be reduced by the target's deflect.

*Loaded [6]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### Strike: Shield Bash
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.hwp.mod Impact average`, the target is also pushed up to 15 feet away and takes an extra `1d4 Impact` if they collide with a solid surface.

### Fortress Shield
While wielding a massive shield, the Anvil reduces the damage she takes from ranged attacks by 10 and can use the **Brace** action as 0.

### Unyielding
The Anvil gains an advantage on tests to resist being moved or knocked **Prone**.

### Projectile Lurch
Before an ally within 80 feet of the Anvil is attacked with a metal projectile the Anvil senses with **Burn Iron**, the Anvil becomes the target of the attack instead.

### Ironpull
*1 act*

The Anvil chooses a metal object she senses with **Burn Iron**, and she moves herself or the object straight toward the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward the Anvil, whereas the Anvil moves toward objects of her size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at one in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the Anvil must succeed on an **Allomancy** test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, she must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d8 + 6 Impact`.

### Burn Iron
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, the Elanda “The Anvil” Wilko senses all non-aluminum sources of metal within 80 feet of her.
