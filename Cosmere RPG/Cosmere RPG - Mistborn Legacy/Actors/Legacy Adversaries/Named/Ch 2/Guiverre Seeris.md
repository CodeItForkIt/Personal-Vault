# Guiverre Seeris

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 4, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 3  
**Resources:** Health 45/45, Focus 5/5, Investiture 5/5  
**Skills:** AGI 2, LWP 3, THV 2, DED 3, LOR 2, INS 2, PRC 3, PRS 2, ALL 3

##### Tactics

**Guiverre Seeris** begins by using **Burn Bronze** to identify and track Investiture use. He avoids direct engagement, using **Gain Advantage** whenever possible, then applying **Flexible Strategy** to pass that advantage to an ally. He relies on **Follow My Lead** to reposition allies and allow them to make powerful attacks. Guiverre only attacks when a target he’s tracking is exposed, striking with his **Knife** to capitalize on the extra damage. If he’s injured or targeted directly, he uses **Secret Stash** to recover and cleverly respond to the situation.

### Effortless Contingency
Guiverre Seeris can graze without spending focus.

### Flexible Strategy
When Guiverre Seeris successfully uses the **Gain Advantage** action, they can grant that advantage to one of his allies within 30 feet of him instead of to himself.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

If Guiverre is tracking the target using his **Burn Bronze**, this attack deals an extra `2d6 Keen`.

### Burn Bronze
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, Guiverre Seeris detects the following within 80 feet of him:

- Each time Investiture is spent.

- Each ongoing effect that’s fueled by Investiture, such as the Burn actions of Allomancers, even if that Investiture was spent before he used this action.

Guiverre Seeris can sense the general direction of these effects’ sources. For as long as he can detect these effects, he can also choose to track the specific location and source of a number of effects equal to the Investiture spent. Guiverre Seeris can make a *Test: `dc=15 skill=all`* as 1 to determine what power generated an effect he’s tracking.

### Secret Stash
*2 act*

Guiverre uses **Recover** (no action required). He then reveals a scene-relevant object he prepared earlier, such as an obsidian weapon, and he gains 1, which he can only use for an action involving the item.

### Follow My Lead
*1 act*

Guiverre instructs an ally he can influence within 60 feet of him. That target can use r to move up to their movement rate without triggering **Reactive Strikes**, then either make a **Strike** against a character or gain the **Focused** condition until the end of their next turn (Guiverre Seeris’s choice). If the ally makes a **Strike**, it deals an extra `1d8` damage on a hit or graze.
