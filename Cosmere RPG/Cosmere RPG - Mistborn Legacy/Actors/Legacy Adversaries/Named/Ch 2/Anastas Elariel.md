# Anastas Elariel

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 3, Willpower 2, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 102/51, Focus 8/4, Investiture 12/6  
**Skills:** AGI 3, LWP 2, DED 3, DIS 3, LOR 3, INS 2, PRC 2, ALL 1

##### Tactics

**Anastas Elariel**’s Allomancy manifests in short, forceful bursts under pressure. If attacked, he uses **Untrained Tinsight** to spoil the strike, instinctively capitalizing on any hit or graze. If surrounded, Anastas uses **Untrained Steelpush** to disarm his opponents or knock them **Prone**. He relies on **Shall We Dance?** to push through exhaustion and to reposition or escape danger at the first opportunity.

### Long-Term Injury
Anastas can only use one hand and arm. After he makes a physical skill test, he becomes **Exhausted** [−1] until the end of the scene.

### Poetic Reversal
The first time in a scene that Anastas would spend focus to resist influence, doing so costs him no focus and he gains an advantage on his next test.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, Anastas can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `2d4 Keen`

### Shall We Dance?
*1 act*

Anastas burns pewter to improve his steps. He moves up to half his movement rate without triggering **Reactive Strikes**, and until the end of his next turn, he ignores the **Exhausted** condition.

### Untrained Steelpush
*1 act*

Anastas makes an **Allomancy** test against the Physical defense of each character (other than himself) within 10 feet of him who is wearing or holding non-aluminum, non-Invested metal. On a success, a target must drop a metal object they are holding (if possible) or be knocked Prone.

### Untrained Soothing
*2 act*

Anastas makes an **Allomancy** test against the Spiritual defense of each character (other than himself) within 10 feet of him. On a success, a target’s distrust is Soothed, influencing them to let their guard down and making them **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

### Untrained Tinsight
Before Anastas is attacked by a character he can sense, he adds a disadvantage to that attack test. If the attack hits or grazes, he can immediately use **Gain Advantage** (no action required) against the attacker.
