# Kliss Gardre

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 0, Speed 1, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 2, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 22/22, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** LWP 1, STL 1, DED 2, LOR 2, DEC 1, INS 2, PRC 2, PRS 2

##### Tactics

An **informant** prefers to leverage information to keep a fight from coming to blows. In conversations, an informant rarely lacks the upper hand; their Informed feature ensures they enter each conversation with leverage. Informants trade information for equal information (or su cient payment), and they’re notoriously di cult to corner into giving away more than they desire. In fact, informants typically try to leave a conversation having given less than they received, using their Bartering action as necessary to cow the PCs.

In combat, informants generally prioritize selfpreservation and seek safety, but if forced to engage, they defend themselves with their Knife attack.

**Informant Encounter Opportunities and Complications**

### Informed
Before each scene, roll the plot die. On O, the Kliss Gardre knows information useful to the PCs. On C, the Kliss Gardre knows compromising information about one  of the PCs.

### Well-Connected
The Kliss Gardre gains an advantage on tests related to the location and organizations they use to collect their information (such as high society or the criminal underworld).

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the Kliss Gardre spend 1 focus to deal an extra `2d4 Keen`.

### Barter
*1 act*

During a conversation, the Kliss Gardre makes a *Test: `skill=prs`* against the character they can influence who has the highest Spiritual defense. On a success, each character of the Kliss Gardre’s choice in the conversation loses 1 focus.
