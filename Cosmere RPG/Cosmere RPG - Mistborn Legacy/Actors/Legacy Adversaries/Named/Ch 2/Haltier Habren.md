# Haltier Habren

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 3, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 5  
**Resources:** Health 38/38, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** LWP 3, DED 1, DIS 1, DEC 3, INS 3, PRC 5, PRS 3

##### Tactics

A **socialite** shines in conversations, where their Charming Personality feature allows them to maintain composure while wearing down their opponents’ focus, and their Down the Grapevine reaction gains them further social leverage. Socialites aren’t likely to engage in combat unless forced, and when they must, they prioritize fleeing to safety. When cornered, they use their Beguiling Influence and Distracting Countenance actions to weaken foes (hoping for stronger allies to help finish them off), finally leaning on their Knife attack only as a last resort.

### Charming Personality
Once per conversation, after the Haltier Habren spends focus to resist influence, the Haltier Habren can choose one character in the conversation to lose 2 focus.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`. The first time the Haltier Habren uses this action during a scene, they gain an advantage.

### Distracting Countenance
*1 act*

The Haltier Habren chooses an enemy within 15 feet who can sense them. The target’s deflect value becomes 0 until the end of the Haltier Habren’s next turn.

### Beguiling Influence
*2 act*

The Haltier Habren makes a *Test: `skill=prs`* against the Spiritual defense of an enemy within 30 feet of them who can sense them. On a success, the target loses 2 focus.

### Down the Grapevine
When a character rolls a Complication during a conversation, the Haltier Habren reveals they know a compromising secret about a character involved in that conversation.

### Socialite Perception Bonus
Passive Effect to bring Socialite's Perception mod up to the 8 value in the book.
