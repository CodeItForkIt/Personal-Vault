# Duvall Haught

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 4, Intellect 4, Willpower 2, Awareness 1, Presence 4  
**Resources:** Health 70/70, Focus 4/4, Investiture 6/6  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** AGI 2, LWP 2, DED 1, INM 3, LOR 2, MED 2, LEA 1, ALL 2

##### Tactics

**Duvall Haught** begins combat by using **Burn Steel** and identifying metal-bearing targets. He alternates between **Shoot Coin** and close-range strikes with his **Surgeon’s Knife**, acting without restraint whenever openings present themselves. When attacked with a metal weapon, he immediately reacts with **Not So Fast** to absorb the blow and stay in the fight. If threatened by multiple enemies at once, Duvall burns zinc by using **To Arms!**, forcing nearby combatants to strike out at those within reach. He uses his Boss feature to spend focus freely on extra actions and cond ition removal, continuing to fight until brought down.

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to push or pull Duvall up to 10 feet.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have Duvall use Steelpush as r.

### Boss
The Duvall Haught can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Duvall Haught can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Strike: Surgeon's Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

### Burn Zinc
*2 act*

Duvall Haught chooses an emotion and chooses one or more targets in a Large (10-foot) area within 40 feet of him, then makes an *Test: `skill=all`* against the Spiritual defense of each target. On a success, the chosen emotion is Rioted in that target, influencing them to act in alignment with that emotion until the end of their next turn. Before this effect ends, Duvall can maintain it on all affected targets in that area by spending 1 or more Investiture as 0.

An affected target can resist as 0, spending focus equal to the Investiture spent to **Burn Zinc** (instead of the usual cost to resist influence). After they do, they ignore their altered emotions until the start of their next turn.

### To Arms!
*2 act*

Duvall burns zinc to Riot the fear and anger of up to five creatures he can sense within 40 feet of him. He makes an **Allomancy** test against the Spiritual defense of each target. On a success, a target must use a reaction (if they can) to make a melee Strike against a character of their choice.

### Not So Fast
Before Duvall is attacked with a metal weapon, he flares steel to increase his deflect value by 5 until the start of his next turn.

### Shoot Coin
*1 act*

**Attack** +ALL; **Range** 20 ft. per Investiture spent; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d@scalar.modules.metallicarts.die Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d@scalar.modules.metallicarts.die + @skills.all.mod Impact average`

### Steelpush
*1 act*

Duvall chooses a metal object he senses with Burn Steel, and he moves himself or the object straight away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move away from Duvall, whereas Duvall moves away from objects of his size category or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or allow Duvall to “fly” for short distances). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, Duvall must succeed on an **Allomancy** test against the Physical defense of the target.

### Burn Steel
*1 act*

Until the start of their next turn, the Duvall Haught senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 40 feet of him.
