# Tiphiny

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 1, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 11/11, Focus 4/4, Investiture 4/4  
**Skills:** ATH 2, LWP 1, STL 2, INM 4, LOR 2, DEC 2

##### Tactics

A **society pledge** uses **Invoke Status** to force movement or strip focus before attacking (or if they’re a Coinshot or Lurcher, they use **Steelpush** or **Ironpull**). Once a foe is reduced to 0 focus, the pledge attacks them with **Gilded Cane** strikes.

### Minion
The Tiphiny’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Life of Privilege
Once per test, the society pledge can reroll all natural 1s from a roll.

### Powerful Name
The society pledge gains an advantage on **Intimidation** and **Persuasion** tests made while invoking their family name.

### Strike: Gilded Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, if the target has no focus, they take an extra `2d4 Impact`.

### Invoke Status
*2 act*

The society pledge makes an **Intimidation** test against the Spiritual defense of an enemy within 10 feet of them who can sense them. On a success, the target must (if they can) either spend 2 focus or use a reaction to move as far away as they safely can from the pledge, up to half their movement rate.
