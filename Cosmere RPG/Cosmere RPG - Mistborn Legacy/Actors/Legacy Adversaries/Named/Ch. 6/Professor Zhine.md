# Professor Zhine

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 2, Intellect 4, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 20/20, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** STL 3, DED 3, LOR 3, INS 3, PRS 3

##### Tactics

Rather than getting involved in a conflict directly, an **advisor** uses their abilities to support their clients. The advisor’s Timely Advice feature makes them a valuable companion in a conversation, while their At Your Service action can anticipate the needs of their client in combat. Advisors are particularly effective alongside Metalborn clients, enabling the Metalborn to save actions when using Drink Vial to refill their Investiture. Otherwise, advisors spend the remainder of their focus to offer Timely Advice, then Recover to regain focus.

### Minion
The Professor Zhine’s attacks can’t critically hit, and they’re immediately defeated when they suffer an injury.

### Strike: Dueling Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`

### At Your Service
*1 act*

The Professor Zhine chooses one character they can sense within 5 feet of them. That character gains 1 on their next turn, which they can use only for these actions: **Brace**, **Drink Vial**, **Gain Advantage**, or **Interact**.

### Timely Advice
Before a character the Professor Zhine can influence spends focus to graze or resist influence, the Professor Zhine can spend the focus on that character’s behalf.
