# Silla Buvidas

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 1, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 44/22, Focus 4/4, Investiture 5/5  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** AGI 1, LWP 2, DIS 1, INS 1, PRC 2, ALL 2

### Coordinated Effort
While Silla can sense Caridus Buvidas, she can use the Interact action as 0.

### Metallic Movement
When Silla Buvidas uses Ironpull to move, their movement doesn't trigger Reactive Strikes.

### Lurching Crash
*1 act*

Silla pulls herself up to 20 feet per Investiture spent toward a metal object she senses with Burn Iron, then makes a melee Allomancy attack against the Physical defense of a character within 5 feet of her, rolling 1d6 + 4 impact damage.

### Marked
*2 act*

Silla makes a Perception test against the Spiritual defense of a character she can sense. On a success, Silla discerns a point of interest related to the target, such as a hidden object or an escape route, and she can immediately move up to her movement rate toward that point.

### Push and Pull
When Caridus Buvidas makes a Shoot Coin attack that hits a target positioned along a straight line between him and Silla, Silla augments his attack, pulling the target 10 feet toward her.

### Strike: Dueling Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`

### Ironpull
*1 act*

The Silla Buvidas chooses a metal object they sense with Burn Iron, and they move themself or the object straight toward the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward the Silla Buvidas, whereas the Silla Buvidas moves toward objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at one in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the Silla Buvidas must succeed on an Allomancy test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, they must make an Allomancy attack, impact damage equal to their Metallic Art die + their **Allomancy** modifier.

### Burn Iron
*1 act*

Until the end of the scene, the Silla Buvidas senses all non-aluminum sources of metal within their Metallic Art range.
