# Caridus Buvidas

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 3, Intellect 1, Willpower 2, Awareness 3, Presence 1  
**Resources:** Health 22/22, Focus 4/4, Investiture 5/5  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** AGI 1, LWP 2, DIS 1, INS 1, PRC 2, ALL 2

### Push and Pull
When Silla Buvidas makes a Lurching Crash attack that hits a target positioned along a straight line between her and Caridus, Caridus augments her attack, knocking the target Prone.

### Marked
*2 act*

Caridus makes a Perception test against the Spiritual defense of a character he can sense. On a success, Caridus discerns a point of interest related to the target, such as a hidden object or an escape route, and he can immediately move up to his movement rate toward that point.

### Metallic Movement
When Caridus Buvidas uses Ironpull to move, their movement doesn't trigger Reactive Strikes.

### Coordinated Effort
While Caridus can sense Silla Buvidas, he can use the Interact action as 0.

### Strike: Dueling Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`

### Shoot Coin
*1 act*

**Attack** +ALL; **Range** 20 ft. per Investiture spent; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d@scalar.modules.metallicarts.die Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d@scalar.modules.metallicarts.die + @skills.all.mod Impact average`

### Steelpush
*1 act*

The Caridus Buvidas chooses a metal object they sense with Burn Steel, and they move themself or the object straight away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move away from the Caridus Buvidas, whereas the Caridus Buvidas moves away from objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or allow the avatar to “fly” for short distances). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the avatar must succeed on an Allomancy test against the Physical defense of the target.

### Burn Steel
*1 act*

Until the start of their next turn, the Caridus Buvidas senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within their Metallic Art range.
