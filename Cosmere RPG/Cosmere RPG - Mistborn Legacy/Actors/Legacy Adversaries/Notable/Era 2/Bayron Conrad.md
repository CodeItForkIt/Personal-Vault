# Bayron Conrad

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 4, Speed 3, Intellect 4, Willpower 3, Awareness 2, Presence 4  
**Resources:** Health 62/62, Focus 5/5, Investiture 6/6  
**Skills:** ATH 2, HWP 2, LWP 2, CRA 2, DIS 2, INM 2, LOR 2, LEA 2, PRC 2, PRS 2, ALL 2

##### Tactics

At the start of a fight, **Bayron Conrad** uses **Burn Pewter** and closes on the nearest threat, opening with his **Knucklebones**. When an attack misses him, he reacts with his **Belittling Boast**, mocking nearby foes for failed strikes. If a character attempts to intimidate him, he immediately moves closer and focuses the fight on them. If combat begins to turn against Bayron, he uses **Fire in the Hole** to damage and disorient his foes, then he repositions to gain some distance and switches to his **Shotgun**.

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to disrupt Bayron’s planted explosives, removing one use of his Fire in the Hole.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have Bayron use Steelpush or Fire in the Hole as r. He can use Fire in the Hole in this way even if he has no uses remaining.

### Boss
The Bayron Conrad can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Bayron Conrad can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Cage Fighter
**Persuasion** tests against Bayron gain a disadvantage. Additionally, Bayron gains 1 focus after he’s hit by a melee attack.

### Cheap Shot
When Bayron successfully uses the **Gain Advantage** action with **Deduction** or an **Awareness** skill, his next attack against that target deals an extra `1d8` damage.

### Strike: Knucklebones
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Impact average`. While Bayron is burning pewter, this damage die increases to `1d10`.

### Strike: Shotgun
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d10 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d10 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [2]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1 or by spending 1 focus.

### Burn Pewter
*1 act*

Until the end of the Bayron Conrad’s next turn, they gain the following benefits:

- They become **Enhanced** [**Speed**] and **Enhanced** [**Strength**]; the bonus to these attributes equals half the Investiture spent (rounded up).

- If they’re **Exhausted**, they don’t apply that penalty to their test results until their burn ends.

Before their pewter burn ends, they can maintain it by spending 1 or more Investiture as 0.

### Regenerate
Bayron Conrad recovers .

### Fire in the Hole
*2 act*

Bayron triggers a bomb he prepared earlier, which explodes in a space he can sense. He makes a ranged **Crafting** attack against the Physical defense of each character within 10 feet of that explosion, rolling `2d4 + 6 Energy`. On a hit, a character is also **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

### Belittling Boast
After an attack misses Bayron, he belittles nearby foes, making an **Intimidation** test against the Spiritual defense of each enemy he can influence within 10 feet of him. On a success, a character gains a disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn.

### Hotheaded
After a character makes an **Intimidation** test against Bayron, he can immediately move up to his movement rate toward that character.

### Steelpush
*1 act*

The Bayron Conrad chooses a metal object they sense with Burn Steel, and they move themself or the object straight away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move away from the Bayron Conrad, whereas the Bayron Conrad moves away from objects of their size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or allow the avatar to “fly” for short distances). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, the avatar must succeed on an Allomancy test against the Physical defense of the target.

### Burn Steel
*1 act*

Until the start of their next turn, Bayron Conrad senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 40 feet of him.
