# Professor Azmine Wilko

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 1, Intellect 4, Willpower 2, Awareness 1, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 18/18, Focus 4/4, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** DED 2, DIS 2, LOR 3, PRS 2

##### Tactics

**Azmine Wilko** attempts to stay out of the fight and behind cover. If a friend is threatened or wavering, she uses **Unshakable Resolve** to restore their focus and grant them the **Determined** condition. She only attacks with her **Snub Nose pistol** when an enemy closes on her position. If injured, Azmine uses **Koloss Recovery** to remain upright long enough to disengage.

### Terrible Liar
Azmine gains a disadvantage on all **Deception** tests.

### History Buff
Once per scene, if a PC rolls O while conversing with Azmine, she presents a helpful fact or a piece of advice derived from her vast historical knowledge. The first time a PC makes a test related to this fact or advice, they gain an advantage on their roll.

### Walking, Talking Library
After one of Azmine’s allies spends at least one hour speaking with her, they gain one temporary utility expertise related to their conversation, which lasts until their next long rest. If a PC has the **Erudition** talent, this conversation also allows them to reassign expertises and skill ranks as if they’d visited a library.
