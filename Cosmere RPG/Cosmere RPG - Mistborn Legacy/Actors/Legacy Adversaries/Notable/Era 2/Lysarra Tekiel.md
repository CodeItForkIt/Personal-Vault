# Lysarra Tekiel

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 4, Intellect 4, Willpower 4, Awareness 3, Presence 5  
**Resources:** Health 128/128, Focus 6/6, Investiture 7/7  
**Skills:** AGI 3, LWP 3, STL 3, DIS 3, INM 3, LOR 3, DEC 2, INS 4, PRC 3, PRS 3, ALL 3, FER 3

##### Tactics

As combat begins, **Lysarra Tekiel** keeps to pistol range, opening with **Douse the Spark** on as many opponents as possible. She targets the most dangerous foe with **Marked for Death**, then follows with **Rapid Fire** to concentrate fire and bring them down. If an enemy closes into melee, Lysarra uses **Ghost Assassin** to strike and reposition, relying on **Graceful Withdrawal** when she takes damage. If the tide seems to be turning against her, she uses **Contingency Plans** to introduce a Morganthax outlaw. She leverages **Mental Pressure** throughout the fight.

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to cause Lysarra’s Twin Pistols to jam, forcing her to reload before she can attack with them.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have Lysarra use Twin Pistols or Douse the Spark (no action required).

### Boss
The Lysarra Tekiel can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Lysarra Tekiel can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Graceful Withdrawal
After Lysarra takes damage from a melee attack, she can spend 1 focus to move up to half her movement rate without triggering **Reactive Strikes** (no action required).

### Mental Pressure
Lysarra seems to be several steps ahead, controlling the battlefield and cutting off options left and right. Each time an enemy who Lysarra can influence uses a single action costing 2 or 3, the enemy must raise the stakes and roll the plot die with a disadvantage.

### Strike: Twin Pistols
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 20/80 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `2d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, the target takes an extra `1d4 Keen` if the Lysarra Tekiel is within 5 feet of an ally.

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [12]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1 or by spending 1 focus.

### Strike: Hidden Blade
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d10 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d10 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

### Rapid Fire
*2 act*

Lysarra makes two **Twin Pistols** attacks. If both attacks hit the same target, that target also falls **Prone**.

### Douse the Spark
*1 act*

Lysarra pulses out a Soothing wave of emotional suppression. She makes an **Allomancy** test against the Spiritual defense of any number of characters she can sense within 40 feet of her. On a success, a target gains the **Slowed** condition until the end of their next turn. An affected target can resist as 0, spending focus equal to the Investiture spent (instead of the usual cost to resist influence) to end the effect.

### Marked for Death
*1 act*

Lysarra makes an **Allomancy** test against the Spiritual defense of a target she can sense within 40 feet of her. On a success, she Soothes their sense of self-preservation, influencing them to leave their weaknesses exposed until the end of her next turn. For the duration, when Lysarra hits the Soothed target with an attack, it ignores the target’s deflect and deals an extra `1d8` damage per Investiture spent on this Soothing. An affected target can resist as 0, spending focus equal to the Investiture spent (instead of the usual cost to resist influence) to end the effect.

### Ghost Assassin
*2 act*

Lysarra moves up to half her movement rate without triggering **Reactive Strikes**, then makes a **Hidden Blade** attack with an advantage. On a hit, the attack deals an extra `2d6` damage. After the attack, she again moves up to half her movement rate without triggering **Reactive Strikes**.

### Contingency Plans
*2 act*

Lysarra has plans within plans. She activates one of the following contingencies:

**Morgenthax Tough.** Introduce one Morgenthax **outlaw** to the scene. They can take a fast or slow turn this round.

**Secret Stash.** Lysarra uses **Recover** (no action required). She then reveals a scene-relevant object she prepared earlier, such as a weighted aluminum ornament or weapon, a crystal decanter with flammable contents to throw, or another unexpected asset suited to the current threat. Lysarra also gains 1, which she can only use for an action involving the item.

### Behavioral Recognition
When a character Lysarra can sense fails a test targeting her, her analysis of their movements and patterns allows her to predict their immediate actions. Until the character succeeds on a test against Lysarra, they gain a disadvantage on tests against her. A character can only be affected by this reaction once per scene.

### Zincmind
Lysarra has a zincmind that stores 6 charges.
