# Kwylliam Elariel

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 3, Speed 4, Intellect 2, Willpower 4, Awareness 4, Presence 5  
**Resources:** Health 130/130, Focus 6/6, Investiture 7/7  
**Skills:** AGI 3, ATH 3, LWP 4, THV 3, INM 4, DEC 3, INS 3, PRC 3, PRS 3, ALL 3, FER 3

##### Tactics

If combat breaks out, **Kwylliam Elariel** uses **Turn on the Charm** and moves to keep some distance between himself and any combatants. He taunts an opponent, using **Gain Dirt** before following up with **Brutal Blackmail** to strip away their focus. Once a foe is out of focus, Kwylliam closes in with his **Dueling Cane**, then uses **You Bore Me** to make repeated strikes against his target. If Kwylliam feels truly threatened by an attack, he immediately responds with **Fast Friends**. He uses **Shape the Narrative** every round to destabilize and demoralize.

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O to hit one of Kwylliam’s metalminds, ending the effects of either Edgelord, Turn on the Charm, or Fast Friends (enemy’s choice).

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to have Kwylliam witness them make an embarrassing blunder. The target is Disoriented until they use 1 to either spend 2 focus or succeed on a DC 14 **Discipline** test to end the condition.

### Boss
The Kwylliam Elariel can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Kwylliam Elariel can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Shape the Narrative
Once per round, Kwylliam can force a character he can sense to reroll the plot die. They must use the new result.

### Drama Fiend
After a character Kwylliam can sense gains a Complication, Kwylliam recovers 1 focus.

### Strike: Dueling Cane
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`, if the target has no focus, they take an extra `2d8 Impact`.

### Strike: “Plot Twist” Hunting Rifle (Aluminum Rounds)
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Range** 150/600 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`

*Pierce:* This damage can’t be reduced by the target’s deflect.

*Loaded [4]:* Reload this weapon to full ammunition as 1.

### You Bore Me
*1 act*

Kwylliam makes an additional Strike against a target with no focus (even if he has already used that Strike action).

### Edgelord
*1 act*

Until the end of Kwylliam’s next turn, when another character makes an attack test within 80 feet of him, they must raise the stakes and roll the plot die with a disadvantage.

### Brutal Blackmail
*1 act*

Kwylliam Riots a target’s shame and desperation, making an **Insight** test against the Spiritual defense of a target he can influence within 80 feet of him who he knows incriminating information about (such as information from his **Gain Dirt** action). On a success, the target becomes **Diminished** [**Willpower** −2] and **Diminished** [**Presence** −2] until the end of the scene, and they lose `1d4` focus. If the target doesn’t have that much focus to lose, they’re also **Stunned** for the round. Once Kwylliam has targeted a character with **Brutal Blackmail**, he can’t target them again unless he gains new blackmail material about them.

### Turn on the Charm
*1 act*

For the rest of the scene, Kwylliam gains the following effects:

- He becomes **Enhanced** [**Presence** +2].

- He can speak and understand every language used by characters within 80 feet of him.

- Attempting to resist Kwylliam’s influence costs an additional 2 focus.

### Gain Dirt
*2 act*

Kwylliam turns his full attention to a target he can influence within 80 feet of him, momentarily Rioting their impulsiveness. The target must either spend 2 focus to resist influence or share personal information that reveals a vulnerability, an incriminating detail, or an embarrassing fact.

### Fast Friends
Before a character Kwylliam can sense within 80 feet of him makes an attack against him, he attempts to build their Connection and Riot their guilt. Kwylliam makes a **Persuasion** test against the Spiritual defense of the target; on a success, before each time the target attacks Kywlliam until the end of the scene, the target must spend 2 focus to resist influence. This effect ends on a character if Kwylliam attacks them.

### Chromiummind
Kwylliam Elariel has a chromiummind that stores 5 charges.

### Duraluminmind
Kwylliam Elariel has a duraluminmind that stores 5 charges.

### Metalminds
Kwylliam has two metalminds, each of which stores 5 charges: a chromiummind (stores Fortune) and a duraluminmind (stores Connection).
