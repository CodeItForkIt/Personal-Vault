# NeBaal

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 4, Intellect 3, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 6  
**Resources:** Health 40/40, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** AGI 2, LWP 3, STL 3, THV 3, DED 2, DIS 3, LOR 2, DEC 2, INS 4, LEA 1, PRC 4, PRS 1

##### Tactics

**NeBaal** avoids direct confrontation and keeps out of reach whenever possible, using **Disquieting Form** to sap an opponent’s focus rather than trading blows. When wounded, NeBaal uses **Regenerate** to stay upright long enough to withdraw. If brought to the brink of defeat, they **Play Dead** and remain still until attackers leave.

NeBaal adheres to their Contract, which forbids killing humans (among other stipulations). However, they can attack non-humans with their **Knife** and may intervene if an ally is in mortal peril.

### Inscrutable Expression
NeBaal gains a bonus to their Spiritual defense equal to their **Deception** modifier against tests to discern whether they’re lying.

### Kandra Resistances
The kandra ignores the **Pierce** weapon trait when taking keen damage. Additionally, they can only be affected by emotional Allomancy if the Allomancer spends 6 Investiture or more to **Burn Brass** or **Burn Zinc**; once affected, the kandra can’t end those effects on themself.

### Natural Tools
NeBaal gains an advantage on all **Thievery** tests and on **Crafting** tests to create traps or hidden compartments.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft. or **Range** 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Keen average`, if NeBaal had at least one advantage on this attack, they deal an extra `2d8 Keen`.

### Secret Away
*1 act*

NeBaal retrieves or stores an item in an unseen compartment they create in their body, or they allow a character who touches them to do so. Only one item can be hidden at a time, and it can’t exceed 1.5 feet in any dimension. A character can’t affect the concealed item with Allomancy unless they have at least 6 ranks in it.

### Play Dead
Once per scene, before NeBaal is reduced to 0 health , they choose to be reduced to 1 health instead, and they convincingly feign defeat until they interact with their surroundings or use actions or reactions. Characters with a Spiritual defense of 19 or higher aren’t deceived by this ruse. Otherwise, a character who inspects NeBaal’s body can recognize NeBaal’s ruse by succeeding on a **Deduction** or **Medicine** test against NeBaal’s Cognitive defense.

### Disquieting Form
*1 act*

NeBaal alters their appearance to terrify a character within 30 feet of them who can see them, making a **Deception** test against the target’s Cognitive defense. On a success, the target loses 2 focus.

### Close Wound
*2 act*

NeBaal shapeshifts to heal their wounded flesh, recovering .
