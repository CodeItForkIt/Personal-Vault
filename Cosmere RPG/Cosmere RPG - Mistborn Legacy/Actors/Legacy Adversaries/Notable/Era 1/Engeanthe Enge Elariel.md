# Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel

**Tier:** 1  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 2, Speed 2, Intellect 1, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 20/20, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Skills:** ATH 2, LWP 2, DIS 2, INM 2, LEA 2, PRS 2, SUR 2

##### Tactics

**Rebels** band together during combat, inspiring each other to fight harder through their Stronger Together reaction and Sidesword attack. In a pinch, they use Improvised Weapon attacks to disorient enemies—which can provide an opportunity for fiery chaos caused by the rebel’s To Ashes action. Even if a rebel falls in battle, their sacrifice is never in vain, as their For the Cause feature bolsters their allies until the bitter end.

### For the Cause
When the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel is defeated, each ally within 30 feet of the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel who can sense them recovers 1 focus.

### Strike: Improvised Weapon
*1 act*

**Attack** +LWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + @skills.lwp.mod Impact average`. If the attack also hits against Cognitive defense, the target is **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

### Strike: Sidesword
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel can also spend 2 focus to grant an advantage to the next attack an ally makes while within 5 feet of the rebel.

### Stronger Together
When an ally within 5 feet of the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel grazes with an attack, the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel can turn that graze into a hit and allow the ally to immediately **Disengage** (no action needed).

### To Ashes
*2 act*

The Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel chooses a Large (10-foot) space within 10 feet of them, igniting the ground and unattended objects in that area. While on fire, that area becomes dangerous terrain that deals `1d8 Energy`. A character can use 1 to extinguish a 5-foot area.

### Copper Allomancy Module
Copper Mistings, called Smokers or Copperclouds, produce copperclouds that mask Investiture within.

**Recommended Profiles.** Adversaries who rely on subtlety, such as the **detective** and **socialite**, can use this module to mask the Allomancy of themselves and their allies.

### Burn Copper
*1 act*

The Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel creates a coppercloud centered on themself, covering an area up to a 30-foot radius. The coppercloud has the following effects:

- Invested powers and senses can’t detect the coppercloud. Additionally, they can’t detect characters or Investiture usage through the coppercloud.

- The Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel’s mind and emotions can’t be affected by Invested effects.

- The above two effects are ignored by Invested effects that cost at least 6 Investiture.

The coppercloud moves with the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel and lasts until the end of the scene or until they end it on a later turn as 0.

### Emotionally Inert
Each character within the Engeanthe “Enge” Elariel’s coppercloud is immune to Invested effects that impact their mind or emotions, unless the Invested effect costs at least 6 Investiture (or a similar resource like metalmind charges).
