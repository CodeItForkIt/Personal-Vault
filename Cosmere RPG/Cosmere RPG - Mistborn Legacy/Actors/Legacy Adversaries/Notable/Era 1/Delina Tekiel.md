# Delina Tekiel

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 4, Speed 4, Intellect 2, Willpower 4, Awareness 3, Presence 4  
**Resources:** Health 155/155, Focus 8/8, Investiture 8/8  
**Skills:** AGI 3, ATH 3, HWP 3, STL 3, DIS 2, INM 2, INS 3, LEA 3, PRC 3, ALL 3

##### Tactics

**Delina Tekiel** advances aggressively, using her first fast turn to **Burn Iron/Steel** (or if obviously outnumbered, she uses that turn to **Burn Atium**, then later uses a boss action to **Burn Iron/Steel**). Delina then uses **Ironpull/Steelpush** to close distance or drag enemies out of position before committing to a strike. Once engaged, she cuts through a single target with **Assassin’s Dance**, then turns on nearby foes to knock them off their feet with **Devastating Sweep**. Delina uses emotional Allomancy to scatter groups and break resolve, forcing enemies to retreat. If pressed by multiple threats at once, Delina uses **Burn Atium** and presses the attack relentlessly until resistance collapses.

##### Opportunities and Complications

**Opportunity.** An enemy can spend O either to halve the damage they take from Delina’s next attack against them or to end Delina’s ongoing Invested effects.

**Complication.** The GM can spend C from an enemy’s test to either have Delina use Drink Vial as r or recover 1d4 focus.

### Boss
The Delina Tekiel can take both a fast turn and a slow turn each round. After an enemy finishes a turn, the Delina Tekiel can spend 1 focus to immediately use an additional 1 or 0. Additionally, they can spend 1 focus on their turn to remove a condition from themself.

### Influential Bearing
The first time Delina Tekiel would spend focus to resist influence in a scene, it costs them no focus and each enemy in the scene loses `1d4` focus.

### Hunter's Instinct
Delina gains an advantage on Perception tests to locate any target she’s dealt damage to this scene.

### Strike: Vadilek's Obsidian Axe
*1 act*

**Attack** +HWP; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + @skills.hwp.mod Keen average`, she can spend 2 Investiture to deal an extra `2d6 Keen`.

### Assassin's Dance
*2 act*

Delina Tekiel moves up to their movement rate and makes a **Strike**. On a hit, the target is also **Disoriented** until the end of the target’s next turn.

### Devastating Sweep
*3 act*

Delina makes a Strike against each character within 5 feet of her (other than herself). On a hit, each target also falls **Prone**.

### Burn Atium
*2 act*

For the next 5 rounds, when Delina Tekiel makes a test to physically interact with another character (including tests against Physical defense), don’t roll any d20s. Instead, set all d20s, including advantages and disadvantages, to a value of 15. Similarly, when an enemy makes a test to physically interact with Delina Tekiel (including tests against Physical defense), set the value of their d20s to 5. Before this effect ends, Delina Tekiel can maintain it as 0.

### Burn Tin
*1 act*

Delina Tekiel enhances their senses, experiencing the following effects until the end of the scene or until the Delina Tekiel ends them on a later turn as 0:

- They become **Enhanced** [**Awareness**]. The bonus to this attribute equals half the Investiture they spent to **Burn Tin** (rounded up).

- All their senses are supernatural senses. The first time each round they experience heightened sensory input, they must succeed on a *Test: `skill=dis`* (DC equals 12 + the value of their **Enhanced** [**Awareness**] condition) or be **Disoriented** until the end of their next turn.

### Prey for the Hunter
*2 act*

Delina simultaneously Soothes and Riots all characters in a Huge (15-foot) area within 80 feet of her, making an **Allomancy** test against the Spiritual defense of each character (other than herself) in the area. On a success, a target must use a reaction (if they can) to move as far as they can away from Delina, up to their movement rate, and they’re **Diminished** [**Willpower**] until the end of their next turn. The penalty to this attribute equals the Investiture Delina spent.

### Brute Force
After Delina is grazed by a melee attack, she can make an **Athletics** test with a DC equal to that attack roll. If she succeeds, she disarms the attacker, and she can spend 1 Investiture to deal `1d6 Impact`. The attacker’s weapon falls into a space of Delina’s choosing within 5 feet of them.

### Shoot Coin
*1 act*

**Attack** +ALL; **Range** 20 ft. per Investiture spent; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d@scalar.modules.metallicarts.die Impact average`;

**Hit** `1d@scalar.modules.metallicarts.die + @skills.all.mod Impact average`

### Steelpush
*1 act*

Delina chooses a metal object she senses with **Burn Iron/Steel**, and she moves herself or the object straight toward or away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward or away from Delina, whereas Delina moves toward or away from objects of her size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at an enemy in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, Delina must succeed on an **Allomancy** test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, Delina must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d8 + 7 Impact`.

### Ironpull
*1 act*

Delina chooses a metal object she senses with **Burn Iron/Steel**, and she moves herself or the object straight toward or away from the other, up to 20 feet per Investiture spent. Objects of a smaller size move toward or away from Delina, whereas Delina moves toward or away from objects of her size or larger; if an object is attached to something else, that counts toward its size.

This ability can be used creatively (such as to disarm enemies or strike at an enemy in the object’s flight path). To affect an unwilling character in such a way, Delina must succeed on an **Allomancy** test against the Physical defense of the target, or if the effect deals damage, Delina must make an Allomancy attack, rolling `1d8 + 7 Impact`.

### Burn Steel
*1 act*

Until the start of her next turn, Delina Tekiel senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 80 feet of her.

### Burn Iron
*1 act*

Until the start of her next turn, Delina Tekiel senses all sources of non-aluminum, non-Invested metal within 80 feet of her.
