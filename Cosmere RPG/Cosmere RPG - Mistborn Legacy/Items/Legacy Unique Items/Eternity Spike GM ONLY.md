*Special Equipment, 1 lb.*

This silvery-gray spike is about as long as an adult human’s hand. The shaft of the spike is formed in a pattern of twisted striations, and its head is a polygon with 16 facets. The Eternity Spike is made of nicrosil, supercharged with Investiture harvested from more than a thousand victims.

**Unique Traits.** The Eternity Spike has the following unique traits:

**Indestructible.** No known means in Era 1 or Era 2 can destroy the Eternity Spike. The sheer amount of Investiture in the Spike makes it nigh invulnerable; it can’t be bent, scratched, melted, or otherwise harmed.

**Highly Invested.** Because of the amount of Investiture stored within the Eternity Spike, it doesn’t function like normal Hemalurgic spikes and doesn’t lose power outside of a storage medium (such as blood).

**Mist-Repellant.** The Scadrian mists are repelled by the Eternity Spike.

**Reactive to Touch.** When touched with bare skin, the Spike sends out an Allomantic pulse. The wielder and each adjacent character feel a perceptible wave of energy, which can be detected without a test by any character burning bronze within 80 feet.

**Ascension Ritual.** To use the Spike, the bearer must already have an Inquisitor’s latticework of Allomantic and Feruchemical spikes. Once implanted, the Eternity Spike transforms its host into a **Hemalurgic monstrosity.**
