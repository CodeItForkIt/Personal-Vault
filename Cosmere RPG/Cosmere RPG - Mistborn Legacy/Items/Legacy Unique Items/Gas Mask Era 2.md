# Gas Mask (Era 2)

*Equipment, 1 lb.*

This mask is made of rubberized fabric and khaki, inset with circular goggles. A long rubber hose connects a canister with chemical absorbents, providing protection against noxious fumes and smoke.

**Unique Traits.** Gas masks have the following unique traits:

**Unwieldly.** While wearing the mask, the Banter free action costs 1 and the wearer is **Diminished [Awareness −2]**.

**Air Filter.** While wearing the gas mask, the wearer is immune to effects that require the target to breathe in a noxious substance.
