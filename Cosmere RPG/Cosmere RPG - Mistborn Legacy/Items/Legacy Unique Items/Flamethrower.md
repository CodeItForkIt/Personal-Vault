# Flamethrower

**Damage** 2d4 energy; **Range** Ranged [10/20]; **Traits** Dangerous, Loaded [5], Two-Handed, Unique; **Expert Traits** Unique: loses Dangerous trait;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy; **Price** — bx; **Weight** 50 lb;\

This incendiary device consists of a metal tank, filled with flammable liquid fuel and worn like a backpack, hose, and handheld spray shaft. When triggered, fuel flows from the tank through the hose to the spray shaft, which ignites it, creating a jet of fire.

**Unique Traits.** Flamethrowers have the following unique trait:

**Blast of Fire.** When you hit a target with this weapon, you also graze every other character whose space is directly between you and the target (GM’s discretion). You can spend from O the attack to instead hit those grazed characters.
