# Landmine (Era 2)

*Equipment, 4 lb.*

**Skill:** Heavy Weaponry; **Damage:** 2d6 impact; **Range:** Melee; **Traits:** Blast [10], Dangerous, Single-Use, Unique **Expert Traits:** None

This metal cylinder has a six-inch diameter and consists of a pressure plate, trip wire, and explosives. Meant to be buried, the landmine is activated by pressing the plate or crossing the trip wire, which results in a deadly blast.

**Unique Traits.** Mines have the following unique traits:

**Trip Wire.** While touching this mine, you can use 2 to set it up, establishing a trip wire of 5 to 15 feet. A character can safely cross the trip wire if they Use a Skill (1) and succeed on a DC 15 Perception test. Otherwise, when a Medium or larger character enters a space with the mine or trip wire, the mine detonates. When it does, make a Crafting attack against the Physical defense of each character within 10 feet of the explosive. At the GM’s discretion, this blast can also damage objects within that area.

**Chain Reaction.** If the blast radius of a detonating mine overlaps with the space of another mine or its trip wire, that mine also detonates.
