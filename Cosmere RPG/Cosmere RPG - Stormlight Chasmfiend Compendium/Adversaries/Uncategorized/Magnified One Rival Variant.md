# Magnified One (Rival Variant)

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** large  
**Attributes:** Strength 5, Speed 1, Intellect 2, Willpower 2, Awareness 5, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 50/50, Focus 4/4, Investiture 7/7  
**Skills:** ATH 2, HWP 3, DIS 2, INM 2, LEA 2, PRC 3

### Regenerative Carapace
*1*

The Magnified One regains 3 health at the start of each of their turns if they have at least 1 health.

### Carapace Spikes
*1*

When the Magnified One is hit by an attack, they can choose to shatter their carapace spikes, dealing `1d10 Keen average` to each character within 5 feet of them. Once they shatter their spikes, they can’t do this again until they regrow the spikes using their Surge of Progression action.

### Strike: Shoot Spike
*1 act*

**Attack** +8; **Reach** range 60ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d8 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d8 + 8 Keen average`

### Brace: External Carapace
*1 act*

Costs 1 Focus or Investiture.

The Magnified One uses their carapace like a shield, gaining the benefits of the ****Brace**** action as if they were in cover.

### Shell Binding
*1*

When the Magnified One (Rival Variant) succeeds on the Grapple action against a target, they can spend 1 Investiture to grow a carapace shell over the target, causing the target to also become Immobilized. Even if the target is no longer in the Magnified One (Rival Variant)'s reach, the target remains Immobilized and Restrained until the target or a character who can reach them succeeds on a *Test: `skill=ath dc=15`* to free them from the carapace.

### Magnified Charge
*2 act*

The Magnified One (Rival Variant) moves up to 25 feet in a straight line without triggering Reactive Strikes. The first time the Magnified One (Rival Variant) comes within 5 feet of each enemy during this move, the Magnified One (Rival Variant) can make a Carapace Weapon attack against that enemy as Free Action. On a hit, the enemy must succeed on *Test: `skill=ath dc=15`* or be knocked Prone.

### Strike: Carapace Weapon
*1 act*

**Attack** +8; **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `2d6 Impact average`;

**Hit** `2d6 + 8 Impact average`

### Surge of Progression
*1 act*

The Magnified One (Rival Variant) can grow their carapace for defense or offense. If they choose defense, they gain a +3 bonues to their deflect value until the start of their next turn. If they choose offense, they regrow their Carapace Spikes.\
