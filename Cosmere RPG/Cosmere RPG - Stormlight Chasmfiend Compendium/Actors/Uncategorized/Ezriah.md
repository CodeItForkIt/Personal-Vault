##### Tactics

In combat, citizens generally flee and seek safety unless their loved ones are in immediate danger.

A Ezriah shines in conversations, where their **Charming Personality** allows them to maintain power and composure while shattering their opponent's. Their **Down the Grapevine** reaction gains them further leverage. Ezriahs are unlikely to engage in combat unless forced, and they prioritize fleeing to safety unless there's no other option. When cornered, they use their **Beguiling Influence** and **Distracting Countenance** to weaken foes for stronger allies, leaning on their **Knife** as a last resort.

### Charming Personality
*1 spe*

Once during a conversation, after the Ezriah spends focus to resist a character's influence, the Ezriah can choose one character involved in the conversation to lose 2 focus.

### Distracting Countenance
*1 act*

The Ezriah chooses an enemy within 15 feet who can sense them. The target's deflect value becomes 0 until the end of the Ezriah's next turn.

### Beguiling Influence
*2 act*

The Ezriah makes a *Test: `skill=prs defence=spi`* of an enemy within 30 feet who can sense them. On a success, the target loses 2 focus.

### Down the Grapevine
*1 rea*

When a character rolls a Complication during a conversation, the Ezriah reveals they know a compromising secret about one of the characters involved in that secret.

### Strike: Knife
*1 act*

**Attack** +6 **Reach** 5 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 6 Keen average`

The Ezriah gains an advantage on their first Knife attack during a scene.
