# Selik

**Tier:** 2  
**Size:** medium  
**Attributes:** Strength 1, Speed 5, Intellect 2, Willpower 3, Awareness 3, Presence 2  
**Resources:** Health 40/40, Focus 5/5, Investiture 0  
**Deflect:** 1  
**Skills:** AGI 3, ATH 3, LWP 3, STL 3, CRA 3, DED 3, INM 3, DEC 3, PRC 3

##### Tactics

When surprising opponents from the shadows, an Selik can Gain Advantage and use their **Shortbow** to hamstring their target. On their next turn, they pursue the Slowed foe, triggering **Killer Instinct** and following with a lethal **Dagger** strike. Should an Selik fail to hit, they have no qualms about using **Twist the Knife** to make up for it before slipping into the darkness to live another day.

### Skulking Approach
At the start of each scene, if the Selik is in cover or an area where their enemy’s primary sense is obscured, the Selik can make a *Test: `skill=stl`* (no action required) with an advantage against the Spiritual defense of each enemy who can sense them. Each enemy they succeed against loses track of them and can no longer sense them until the Selik attacks or takes another action that would expose them.

### Stay in the Shadows
While the Selik is in cover or an area where their enemy's primary sense is obscured, their Physical defense increases by 2 (to 18) against that enemy.

### Strike: Dagger
*1 act*

**Attack** +8; **Reach** 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft.; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d4 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d4 + 8 Keen average`, and if the Selik had at least one advantage on this attack, they can spend 1 focus to deal an extra `4d4 Keen average`.

### Killer Instinct
After the Selik ends a Move action within 5 feet of an enemy, if the Selik moved at least 20 feet this turn, they can use the Gain Advantage action as Free Action.

### Twist the Knife
Once per turn, when the Selik grazes, they can add their attack test modifier to that graze's damage.

### Strike: Shortbow
*1 act*

**Attack** +8; **Range** 80/320 ft; **Targets** one;

**Graze** `1d6 Keen average`;

**Hit** `1d6 + 8 Keen average`, and the target must succeed on a *Test: `skill=ath dc=13`* or be Slowed until the end of the target's next turn.
