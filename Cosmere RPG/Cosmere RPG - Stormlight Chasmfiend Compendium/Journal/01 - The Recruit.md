# The Recruit

An outspoken young Truthwatcher is urging the besieged city of Varikev to lay down arms—but is her message one of peace or despair? The Coalition of Monarchs and the Sons of Honor compete to recruit a reluctant Radiant in “The Recruit,” a Stormlight scenario for three to five 6th-level characters. Originally created for some of the Cosmere RPG’s earliest alpha testing, this short adventure can fit into any campaign set in the months after *Oathbringer.*

## Scenario Hooks

Use one of these hooks to introduce your players to the scenario, or create your own.

**Roion’s Plea.** The new Highprince of the Roion princedom seeks to defend his lands from Odium’s army, but an agitator within the city of Varikev is calling for her own people to surrender.

**Recruitment Mission.** The Coalition of Monarchs has sent the party to win over an elusive Truthwatcher who isn’t yet fighting alongside other Knights Radiant.

## Getting Started

Unbeknownst to the party, a Shardbearer named Eranniv seeks to abduct Ezriah on behalf of a secret society called the Sons of Honor.

Eranniv has been working with an assassin named Selik to intercept the PCs; Eranniv now awaits his signal to abduct Ezriah.

The confrontation with this Shardbearer will be especially exciting if one of the PCs has recently gained access to a Shardblade.

To start the scenario, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You've arrived at the remote city of Varikev, one of the few cities in the mighty nation of Alethkar that has not yet fallen to Odium's armies. However, reports indicate that warform singers are marching toward the city and will reach it in days.\
>      You seek a Truthwatcher named Ezriah. A woman with remarkable healing abilities, she could prove an asset in the city's defense—but instead, she's been urging citizens to surrender to the approaching army.\
>      A helpful local guide named Selik has led you to a market plaza surrounded by sturdy stone buildings. He’s told you Ezriah is due to speak here soon. A crowd is gathering.

Ask each player to establish whether their character hopes to recruit Ezriah to join the Knights Radiant, whether they’re just trying to persuade Ezriah to stop calling for surrender—or whether they have some other personal goal to pursue in the upcoming conversation scene.

## Conscientious Objector

When the PCs are ready to enter the plaza, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A young woman stands on a wooden stage overlooking the city square. She has short black-and-red hair, piercing green eyes, and a red havah.\
>      Ezriah’s voice rings out above the murmur of the gathered crowd. “Citizens of Varikev! An army approaches and we cannot hope to stand against it! You’ve heard stories of Voidbringers conquering all of Alethkar. Those stories are true, and I implore you not to throw away your lives.”
>
> ![[Ezriah_VALERIA CASALE.webp]]

**VALERIA CASALE**

The characters can listen to Ezriah’s speech or try to debate her. Either way, they can learn the following about her views:

- She argues that the Alethi have no hope against the singers and must surrender to avoid further bloodshed.

- She witnessed the terrible futility of Parshendi attempts to resist a superior military at the Shattered Plains. Now she believes the roles are reversed, and resisting Odium’s forces will only lead to unnecessary deaths.

- She mourns the fall of Alethkar but believes surrender or evacuation is the only way for the citizens of Varikev to survive.

To persuade Ezriah to consider joining the Knights Radiant or to otherwise stop her from convincing the citizens of Varikev to surrender, the party can engage Ezriah with a conversation (see [[Journal/01 - The Recruit|Resolving the Conversation]]). The PCs might discuss topics such as the following:

**Ideals Worth Fighting For.** Ezriah secretly fears her fate is to experience and chronicle tragedies as a helpless bystander, and she is receptive to successful arguments that she could be meant for more. PCs who describe the oaths and heroism of the Knights Radiant gain an advantage on related tests.

**War Stories.** Ezriah is deeply empathetic, and she responds emotionally to successful arguments that touch on the horrors of war. A character with the Military Life expertise can describe the cruelty of Odium’s forces without making a test, causing Ezriah to lose 1 focus.

If a character is not actively contributing to the conversation, ask them to make a Perception test (DC 13). On a success, they notice Selik pull a spanreed out of his pocket and twist its ruby—as if signaling someone.

If a character gains an Opportunity (Opportunity) or Complication (Complication), use the Conscientious Objector Opportunities and Complications table for inspiration.

## Resisting Influence

Ezriah has a Cognitive defense of 16, a Spiritual defense of 17, and 5 focus. She only must spend 1 focus to resist arguments that appeal to nationalism or distrust of the singers. Deeply concerned with saving lives, she doesn’t resist successful arguments related to healing innocent people or keeping civilians out of harm’s way.

## Resolving the Conversation

The conversation succeeds if the party convinces Ezriah to reconsider her stance, either because they make especially compelling arguments or because Ezriah no longer has enough focus to resist influence. If the PCs take a belligerent approach or their arguments fall flat, end the conversation in failure.

**Success.** Ezriah will stop calling for the humans of Varikev to surrender and is willing to learn more about the Knights Radiant and the Coalition of Monarchs.

**Failure.** Ezriah remains unconvinced. Any citizens who overheard the conversation stay nearby, vowing to surrender to Odium’s forces.

## Shardbearer Assault

Soon after receiving the signal from Selik, Eranniv (“er-RAN-niv," **[[Actors/Uncategorized/Erraniv|heavy Shardbearer]]**, he/him) arrives in the market square. Eranniv is a lighteyed warrior who lost his House’s Shardplate in an arena duel. Desperate to reclaim his status, he has accepted a mission—and a borrowed set of Shards—from the Sons of Honor.

When the conversation is complete, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Awespren, like rings of blue smoke, suddenly appear amidst the gathered citizens. A woman raises her freehand toward the edge of the plaza and cries out, “Shardbearer!”\
>      She points at a figure in bulky Shardplate, slate-gray with gold adornments. A deep voice echoes from behind the figure’s helmet visor. “Hand over the Truthwatcher!”\
>      The armored warrior thrusts his hand to the side and white mist begins to coalesce into a long, curved Shardblade. As it forms, he charges toward the stage.

Eranniv summons his Shardblade just before the first round of combat begins. During the combat, he takes care not to harm Ezriah, but has no compunctions about killing anyone else who gets in his way, including any **[[Actors/Uncategorized/Commoner|commoner]]**s nearby. Arrogant and overconfident, Eranniv takes fast turns whenever possible.

Selik (**[[Actors/Uncategorized/Selik|assassin]]**, he/him) uses the first NPC slow turn to draw a dagger and join the fight. He gains an advantage on his first attack against a character who was not already suspicious of him.

Ezriah uses the **[[Actors/Uncategorized/Ezriah|socialite]]** stat block but has the Progression (+4) and Illumination (+7) skills (two ranks in each skill), and 7 Investiture. She tries to avoid the fray, taking the Brace action behind any available cover, but might Aid the characters if they swayed her in the previous scene.

During combat, characters might try to interact with the following elements of this scene:

**Wooden Stage.** The stage is only 5 feet above the ground, and can be climbed without a test. Opportunity or Complication results during the battle can be spent to cause portions of the stage to collapse, knocking characters Prone and creating difficult terrain.

**Stone Buildings.** The homes in this area are 20 feet tall and relatively easy to climb (DC 10 Athletics). Any character on a rooftop gains an advantage on Perception tests targeting characters below. A character who falls from a rooftop takes `2d6` Impact damage.

If a character gains an Opportunity (Opportunity) or Complication (Complication), use the Shardbearer Assault table for inspiration.

**Shardbearer Assault Opportunities and Complications**

|  |  |
|----|----|
| **Result** | **Example** |
| Opportunity | Ezriah uses her Radiant abilities to heal herself or another character within reach, recovering `1d8 + 5` health. |
| Complication | A nearby citizen dies, slain by an errant slice from Eranniv’s Shardblade. |

## Conclusion and Rewards

The scenario's outcome depends on the party's decisions, successes, and failures, any of the following might apply:

**Borrowed Shards.** If the characters succeeded in the battle against Eranniv, they can claim his Shards—at least temporarily.

**Recruit Recruited.** If the party successfully persuaded Ezriah, PCs with goals related to the Knights Radiant or the Coalition of Monarchs may receive rewards.

## Expanding this Adventure

The aftermath of this scenario can be developed into further adventures.

- If someone claims Erraniv's borrowed Shards, that character becomes a high-priority target for assassination by the Sons of Honor.

- After the battle, the PCs have one more opportunity to recruit Ezriah to their cause. If she joins them, the party must lead her back to their headquarters in Urithiru.

- The PCs turn their attention to recruiting the Skyeels, a local group of talented mercenary archers.

- Varikev is still in danger; city leaders approach the heroes for help holding off a singer strike force while citizens evacuate.
