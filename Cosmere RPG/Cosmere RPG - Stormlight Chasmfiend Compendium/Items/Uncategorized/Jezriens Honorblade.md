**Damage** 2d10 spirit; **Range** Melee; **Skill** Heavy Weaponry or Light Weaponry; **Traits** Dangerous, Deadly, Unique; **Expert Traits** Unique (loses Dangerous trait)

**Unique Traits.** While you’re bonded to Jezrien’s Honorblade, it has the same unique traits as a Shard­ blade (see chapter 7 of the *Stormlight Handbook*), plus the following unique traits:

**Herald of Windrunners.** Your ranks in the **Adhesion** and **Gravitation** surge skills increase to 3. If you already have 3 ranks or more in a skill, that skill instead increases by 1 rank, to a maximum of 5. However, if you roll a Complication while making a **Adhesion** or **Gravitation** test, you must double the Investiture cost of that test.

**Wield Investiture.** If you don’t already have an Investiture pool, you gain one, and your maximum Investiture equals 2 + either your Awareness or Presence (whichever is higher). You can use the **Breathe Stormlight**, **Enhance**, and **Regenerate** actions.

**Surge Talents.** You gain the **Binding Strike** talent from the Adhesion talent tree, and you gain the **Gravitational Slam** talent from the Gravitation talent tree. When you gain a level and thus gain a new talent, you can choose it from the **Adhesion** and **Gravitation** paths.

If you lose your bond to Jezrien’s Honorblade, you lose all skills and talents granted by the Honorblade. If you no longer meet the prerequisites for any **Adhesion** or **Gravitation** talents you chose when you gained a level, you can replace them using the Self ­Reflection downtime activity (see chapter 9 of the *Stormlight Handbook*).
