**Damage** 1d8 keen; **Range** Ranged [100/400]; **Traits** Cumbersome[3], Loaded [1], Two-Handed; **Expert Traits** Unique;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy Weaponary; **Price** 800 mk; **Weight** 10 lb.;

Originally developed in Thaylenah to launch flaming oil and set enemy vessels’ sails alight, hand ballistae are heavier than normal crossbows and specially designed to deliver a payload. While they failed in their initial purpose and were once solely sought by enthusiasts, recent discoveries have given new utility to this weapon: firing gemstones containing anti-Light at Radiants and Fused. Though it’s incredibly impractical to do this in the heat of combat without rigorous training, this discovery threatens to forever change the millennia-long war between humans and singers.

**Unique Traits.** If you have the Hand Ballista specialist expertise, add the following unique expert trait:

**Payload Bolt.** Before you use *1* to reload this weapon, you can spend an additional *1* to attach a piece of equipment you have (see “Equipment” in chapter 7 of the *Stormlight Handbook*) to the bolt you’re loading. You are encouraged to find your own uses of this and work with the GM to resolve the effects, but some common uses could be:

- Attaching a bottle of oil or strong alcohol that douses the target in flammable liquid on a hit or graze that creates dangerous terrain when lit.

- Attaching a grappling hook to allow you to anchor it at any point within the hand ballista’s range, using Heavy Weaponry in place of Athletics or Agility (and gaining a disadvantage on the test if the anchor point is beyond the hand ballista’s short range).

- Attaching an anti-Light infused gemstone (anti-Stormlight for Radiants or anti-Voidlight for Fused and Regals) that when it hits a target with 1 Investiture or more reduces their Investiture to 0 and causes them to immediately suffer an injury.
