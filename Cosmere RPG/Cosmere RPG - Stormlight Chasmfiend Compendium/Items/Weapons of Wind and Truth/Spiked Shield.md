**Damage** 1d6 keen; **Range** Melee; **Traits** Cumbersome [3], Defensive; Two-Handed; **Expert Traits** Momentum, loses Two-Handed;

**Type** Heavy Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy; **Price** 50 mk; **Weight** 8 lb.;

A feature of the Riran military, embodying its purpose to protect Rira’s borders from Iri’s incursions, spiked shields are designed to discourage and block attacks while allowing retaliatory strikes. These massive tower shields are constructed with intimidation at the fore. Usually as tall as their intended wielder, their outer surface and edges are covered in brutal spikes.

Among singers, spiked shields have long been favored by physically powerful forms, such as direform Regals. The Fused passed this knowledge on to newly awakened singers, Soulcasting shields for direform shock troops.

**Expertise.** Treat Shield expertise as having expertise with spiked shields.\*

*\* Foundry Note:* The system currently does not automate this. If you have Shield expertise you should grant yourself Spiked Shield expertise as well.
