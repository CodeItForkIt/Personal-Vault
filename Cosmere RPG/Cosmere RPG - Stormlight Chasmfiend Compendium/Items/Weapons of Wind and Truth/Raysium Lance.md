**Damage** 1d8 keen; **Range** Melee [+5]; **Traits** Two-handed, Unique; **Expert Traits** Defensive;

**Type** Special Weapon; **Weapons Skill** Heavy Weaponary; **Price** Reward only; **Weight** 11 lb.;

Most often wielded by Heavenly Ones, these lances are around 8 feet long and are forged with a core of raysium – the god metal of the Shard Odium. They are coated in aluminum, with a gemstone inset at the base of the blade. Raysium possesses the unique quality of having an Investiture “polarity,” conducting Investiture in a particular direction along it. Consequently, when this lance strikes a Radiant, it siphons some of their Stormlight into its gem.

**Expertise.** Treat Longspear expertise as having expertise with raysium lances.\*

**Reward.** A raysium lance is a Tier 2 reward.

**Investiture Drain.** When you hit a target with this weapon, they lose 1 Investiture, and if they’re infused with at least one invested effect (such as a Basic Lashing from the Gravitation surge), each of those effects also loses 1 infused Investiture. An effect reduced to 0 infused Investiture immediately ends.

*\* Foundry Note:* The system currently does not automate this. If you have Longspear expertise you should grant yourself Raysium Lance expertise as well.
